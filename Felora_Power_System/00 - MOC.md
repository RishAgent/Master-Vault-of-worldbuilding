# Felora Power System MOC

Felora is a **Gold-Grade Origin World** roughly ten times larger than Earth, with excellent Mana density and no public knowledge of the Tower.

## Core Locks
- Same universal Tier, Level, EXP, Class, Personal Rank, Star/Circle, Skill and affinity rules as Tower-connected worlds.
- No visible System for ordinary Felorans.
- Felora once failed a Tower-linked Preparation phase; direct memories of the Void/Tower were erased and restructured.
- Civilization is stable, not post-apocalyptic.
- Great walls, wards, patrols and response forces keep settled regions safe.
- Gates can appear inside civilized territory and may break if not cleared.
- Beyond the walls, Silver and Gold monsters are normal ecological threats.
- Merit is granted for verified achievement.
- Class and Personal Rank indicate **potential**.
- Tier indicates **realized current power**.
- Merit indicates **proven accomplishment**.
- Commoners can rise into hereditary nobility through Tier + Merit.
- Inherited title and personal standing are separate.
