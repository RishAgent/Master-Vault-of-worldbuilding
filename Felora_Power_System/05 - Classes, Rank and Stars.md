# Felora Classes, Rank and Stars

## Classes
Classes exist naturally through Felora's hidden Tower framework.

Most civilizations reveal them through an adolescent awakening ceremony, variously called:
- The Calling
- Soul Revelation
- First Awakening
- Class Rite

The ceremony stabilizes and reveals a Class Seed influenced by:
- race
- bloodline
- training
- affinity
- personality
- profession
- achievements
- soul compatibility

Felorans believe this is a natural law.

## Personal Rank
Personal Rank reflects long-term growth potential and efficiency.

It does not substitute for Tier or Merit.

## Core Social Rule
> **Class and Rank = potential**
>
> **Tier = current realized power**
>
> **Merit = proven accomplishment**

A Mythic-Class Iron child does not become a Duke.

## Mage Stars
Feloran mage Stars are the same real Mana-Core Star architecture used by the wider setting.

Working regional titles:

| Stars | Local Title |
|---|---|
| ★ | Mage |
| ★★ | Senior Mage |
| ★★★ | **Grand Mage** |
| ★★★★ | **Great Mage** |
| ★★★★★ | **Archmage / Sage Mage** |

Star Rating remains separate from Tier.

## Starting Kingdom
The first kingdom Alok reaches may have:
- many One-Star Mages
- fewer Two-Star Senior Mages
- several Three-Star Grand Mages
- **one known Four-Star Great Mage**
- no publicly active Five-Star Archmage

Five-Star may be treated as the legendary known regional ceiling.

## Alok

# ★★★ Three-Star
