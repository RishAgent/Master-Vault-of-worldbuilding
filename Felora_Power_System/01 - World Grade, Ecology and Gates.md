# Felora World Grade, Ecology and Gates

# FELORA — GOLD-GRADE ORIGIN WORLD

Gold-grade means the world's unmanaged mature wilderness can naturally and repeatedly sustain **Gold-tier life**. It does not mean every creature is Gold.

| Region | Typical Ecology |
|---|---|
| Major cities | Ordinary–Iron; Gates mostly Iron–Bronze |
| Controlled countryside | Iron–Bronze |
| Interior monster zones | Bronze–Silver |
| Managed hunting regions | Bronze–Silver |
| Frontier | Silver common; Gold incursions |
| Beyond Great Walls | Silver–Gold |
| Deep Wilds | **Gold-dominant mature ecology** |
| Forbidden Territories | Peak Gold / Platinum+ possible |

## Civilization
Kingdoms maintain walls, wards, military outposts, patrol roads, hunting guilds, response teams, barrier towers and controlled monster zones.

Felora is dangerous, but civilization is stable.

## Gates
Gates can appear almost anywhere, including inside kingdoms.

They may contain:
- monsters
- cores and Mana crystals
- herbs and ores
- ruins
- self-contained biomes
- bosses
- advancement materials

If a Gate is not cleared or stabilized, it may undergo a **Gate Break** and release its inhabitants.

## Monster Tides
Large migrations may occur because of Mana surges, apex-predator movement, seasonal breeding, Gate phenomena or ecological displacement.

These are treated like major wars or natural disasters, not signs of an apocalypse.
