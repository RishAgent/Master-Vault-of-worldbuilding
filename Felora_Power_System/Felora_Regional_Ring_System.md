# Felora Regional Ring System

> [!summary]
> Felora is a **Gold-Grade Origin World** whose dangerous wilderness is classified through a regional **Ring System**.
>
> These Rings are **not mathematically perfect circles around the planet**.
>
> They form around major civilized regions and are distorted by terrain, Mana density, monster territories, rivers, mountains, Gates, ancient ruins, roads, and defensive infrastructure.
>
> The farther one travels beyond stabilized kingdom territory, the stronger the naturally self-sustaining monster ecology becomes.

---

# 1. Core Principle

A Ring is classified by the **Tier of monster population that can naturally reproduce, survive, and maintain a stable ecological presence there**.

A Bronze Ring is not merely an area where a Bronze monster has been seen.

It is an area where Bronze-tier monsters are a normal and self-sustaining part of the ecology.

Lower-Tier creatures may still exist within higher Rings.

Higher-Tier creatures may occasionally enter lower Rings.

The Ring indicates the **dominant mature threat ecology**, not an absolute limit.

---

# 2. Civilized Kingdom Territory

Inside a kingdom's defensive frontier, monster populations have been controlled for generations.

Typical threats include:

- Ordinary magical wildlife
- Iron monsters
- Bronze monsters
- occasional Beginner Bronze threats in remote regions
- Gates
- monster nests
- mutated wildlife
- old ruins
- managed hunting grounds

Civilized territory may contain:

- the capital
- cities
- towns
- villages
- farmland
- forests
- lakes
- mountains
- internal monster zones
- military territories
- controlled resource regions

> **Inside the kingdom, monsters are an ongoing management problem.**
>
> **Beyond the walls, monsters become the dominant ecology.**

---

# 3. Outer Defensive Frontier

A kingdom's "outer wall" does not need to be one perfectly circular stone wall.

The defensive frontier may combine:

- enormous constructed walls
- mountain ranges
- fortified passes
- rivers
- Mana barriers
- fortress chains
- defensive towers
- ward networks

Each major section of the frontier normally contains fortified towns, military bases, supply depots, and Merit registration facilities.

These settlements act as gateways between civilization and the Rings beyond.

---

# 4. Iron Ring

## Dominant Ecology

Typical threats:

- Ordinary monsters
- Iron monsters
- occasional Bronze monsters

Iron-tier hostile creatures are the normal serious threat.

## Civilized Presence

The Iron Ring is dangerous to ordinary civilians but manageable for trained forces.

Kingdoms may establish:

- frontier towns
- mines
- fortified farms
- logging settlements
- military camps
- relay stations
- protected roads
- resource-processing centers

It is the most heavily utilized wilderness Ring.

## Travel

Ordinary guarded caravans may travel established Iron Ring routes.

Patrols are frequent.

---

# 5. Bronze Ring

## Dominant Ecology

Typical threats:

- Iron monsters
- Bronze monsters
- occasional Silver monsters

Bronze creatures form stable populations.

## Expedition Requirements

Normal soldiers are insufficient by themselves.

Expeditions commonly require:

- Bronze officers
- trained formations
- mages
- healers
- barrier specialists
- scouts
- supply support
- fortified rest camps

## Economic Importance

The Bronze Ring is highly valuable because Bronze-grade cores, crystals, hides, blood, organs, magical plants, ores, and alchemical materials support large portions of Feloran progression and industry.

---

# 6. Silver Ring

## Dominant Ecology

Typical threats:

- Bronze monsters
- Silver monsters
- occasional Gold monsters

Silver creatures are established territorial predators.

## Travel

This is no longer ordinary patrol territory.

Convoys commonly require:

- Silver commanders
- multiple Bronze elites
- professional mages
- barrier specialists
- heavily reinforced wagons
- organized supply chains

## Strategic Value

Silver Ring resources support:

- Silver progression
- major advancement breakthroughs
- high-grade equipment
- national military growth
- noble House development

---

# 7. Gold Ring

## Dominant Ecology

Typical threats:

- Silver monsters
- Gold monsters
- occasional Platinum threats

Gold monsters maintain genuine territories and stable populations.

This is one of the defining traits of Felora as a **Gold-Grade World**.

## Civilized Presence

Permanent settlements are rare.

Structures are more likely to be:

- military citadels
- elite hunting bases
- ancient fortresses
- resource strongholds
- research stations
- secured route stations

A kingdom does not control the Gold Ring in the same way it controls its inner territory.

Instead, it controls:

- routes
- strongholds
- strategic zones
- resource claims

---

# 8. Platinum Frontier

## Dominant Ecology

Typical threats:

- Gold monsters
- Platinum monsters
- potentially beings above Platinum

Some regions may not be fully mapped.

They may simply be classified as:

# UNCHARTED HIGH-RISK TERRITORY

Possible contents include:

- ancient Dragons
- forgotten civilizations
- forbidden ruins
- catastrophic Gates
- unique species
- world anomalies
- high-grade Mana phenomena
- unknown apex entities

Feloran civilizations know such regions exist.

They do not meaningfully control them.

---

# 9. Secured Inter-Kingdom Routes

Kingdoms are not isolated.

They maintain secured corridors through dangerous Rings.

These routes may contain:

- fortified roads
- watchtowers
- military strongholds
- relay stations
- protected camps
- barrier checkpoints
- supply depots
- emergency shelters
- communication arrays

## Route Grades

### Iron Routes

Used by normal guarded caravans and regular trade.

### Bronze Routes

Require military escorts and scheduled patrol support.

### Silver Routes

Used by:

- major trade convoys
- noble delegations
- royal envoys
- strategic military movement

### Gold Routes

Rare and extraordinarily expensive to maintain.

Only major powers can sustain reliable Gold Ring corridors.

---

# 10. Strongholds

Strongholds are essential to Ring travel.

## Bronze Ring Stronghold

May contain:

- military barracks
- Merit Hall
- hospital
- monster-processing yard
- supply depot
- smithies
- alchemical workshops
- advancement-resource market
- convoy shelters

## Silver Ring Stronghold

Often resembles a heavily fortified town or small city.

May include:

- permanent Silver commanders
- large barrier formations
- high-grade communication arrays
- extensive warehouses
- major medical facilities
- specialized monster-processing infrastructure

## Gold Ring Stronghold

A nationally famous strategic asset.

Usually defended by powerful individuals and enormous formations.

Losing one can become a national crisis.

---

# 11. Diplomatic and International Travel

Travel between kingdoms is possible because major powers maintain secured routes.

Royal or diplomatic events may require coordinated travel windows.

Examples:

- coronations
- royal weddings
- tournaments
- alliance meetings
- trade summits
- magical conferences

For major events:

- routes may be cleared in advance
- patrols increase
- strongholds raise readiness
- multiple delegations travel together

Distance alone does not determine travel difficulty.

A nearby kingdom separated by a Gold Ring may be harder to reach than a more distant kingdom connected through a Bronze route.

---

# 12. Kingdom Scale

Major Feloran kingdoms are vast.

They may contain:

- multiple major cities
- dozens or hundreds of towns
- thousands of villages
- huge farming regions
- forests
- mountains
- lakes
- managed monster territories
- military regions

The kingdom's defended territory is therefore a complete civilization zone, not merely one city behind a wall.

---

# 13. First Expedition Relevance

Alok's first expedition enters Felora beyond the kingdom's outer defenses.

The first soldiers they are expected to meet may be:

- several days away from their own kingdom wall
- returning from an authorized monster-culling operation
- transporting harvested monster corpses
- travelling toward a fortified wall town
- moving through the Iron Ring or near the Bronze Ring boundary

This makes their presence believable.

It also explains why they immediately find Alok's group unusual.

Anyone travelling this far normally has:

- a Merit Crest
- route knowledge
- a registered stronghold or camp
- military or noble authorization
- knowledge of nearby kingdom infrastructure

Alok's explanation that they come from **very far away** is therefore plausible in a world of Felora's enormous scale.

---

# 14. Canon Locks

- Felora uses a **regional Ring System**.
- Rings are not perfect global circles.
- Ring shape is influenced by terrain, Mana density, monster territories, Gates, and civilization.
- Civilized kingdom territory contains mainly Ordinary through Bronze threats under active control.
- Beyond the defensive frontier, wilderness threat density rises sharply.
- Iron Ring = Iron-dominant ecology.
- Bronze Ring = Bronze-dominant ecology.
- Silver Ring = Silver-dominant ecology.
- Gold Ring = Gold-dominant ecology.
- Platinum Frontier = poorly controlled high-risk territory.
- Higher Rings still contain lower-Tier life.
- Higher-Tier monsters may occasionally enter lower Rings.
- Kingdoms maintain fortified roads, camps, strongholds, and secured corridors between one another.
- International travel is possible but becomes increasingly expensive and dangerous through higher Rings.
- Major kingdoms are geographically vast and contain many settlements within their defended territory.
- Ring classification describes **self-sustaining ecological threat level**, not merely sightings.
