# Felora Merit Economy

Merit is class-neutral. Mage, Knight, Archer, Healer, Summoner, Assassin, Beast Tamer or any other Class can earn the same Merit for the same verified accomplishment.

## Fixed Monster Merit

There is **no relative-strength multiplier**.

| Monster Tier |   Beginner | Intermediate |    Advanced |        Peak |
| ------------ | ---------: | -----------: | ----------: | ----------: |
| Ordinary     |          1 |            5 |          10 |          25 |
| Iron         |        100 |          250 |         500 |       1,000 |
| Bronze       |      2,500 |        5,000 |      10,000 |      25,000 |
| Silver       |     50,000 |      100,000 |     250,000 |     500,000 |
| Gold         |  1,000,000 |    2,500,000 |   5,000,000 |  10,000,000 |
| Platinum     | 25,000,000 |   50,000,000 | 100,000,000 | 250,000,000 |

Named monsters, Gate Lords, calamity beasts and other exceptional targets may carry additional fixed Merit.

## Lifetime Merit
Never decreases. Used for:
- advancement qualification
- social standing
- noble eligibility
- military authority
- guild rank
- succession qualification
- restricted access

## Merit Credits
Spendable currency derived from recognized service and kills. Used for:
- breakthrough resources
- government-owned cores
- Mana crystals
- medicine
- equipment
- techniques
- Gate permits
- academy access
- land
- hunting licenses

Spending Merit Credits does **not** reduce Lifetime Merit.

## Monster Loot
The corpse and resources remain physical property.

Hunters may retain or sell:
- cores
- crystals
- hides
- blood
- bones
- horns
- claws
- organs
- venom
- meat
- alchemical materials

Thus:

> **Merit = status and qualification**
>
> **Loot = wealth and physical resources**

## Kill Verification
Monster death leaves a temporary **Death Imprint** readable by Merit Obelisks, guild halls, military offices and royal assessment centers.

The imprint verifies:
- killer
- registered party
- major contributors
- monster Tier and substage
- whether Merit was already claimed

Buying a core does not grant the original killer's Merit.

Group Merit may be divided by recorded contribution, including damage, defense, healing, barriers, control and command.
