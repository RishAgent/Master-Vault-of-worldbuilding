# Felora Merit Crest System

> [!summary]
> The **Merit Crest** is Felora's standardized soul-bound identity and social-recognition card.
>
> Its physical appearance reflects **officially recognized social standing**, not raw Tier.
>
> Full registered information is shown by placing the card on a compatible **Merit Display Crystal**.
>
> Alok's System can synchronize with his own Crest and read the same legitimate registry information directly.

---

# 1. Core Principle

The Merit Crest answers:

> **What has society officially recognized this person as?**

It does not simply answer:

> **How powerful is this person?**

Therefore:

- Tier does not determine Crest color.
- Social standing determines the physical Crest.
- A powerful foreigner with no local Merit can still have a low-grade Crest.
- Noble birth and personal Merit remain separate.
- Eligibility for promotion does not automatically equal promotion.

---

# 2. Physical Form

The Crest is a rigid Mana-reactive card roughly the size of a bank card.

It contains or links to:

- soul-bound identity
- Mana signature
- legal registration
- kingdom registry mark
- social standing
- House or inherited-status markings
- anti-counterfeit protections

The card itself normally shows only its rank appearance and markings.

Detailed information requires a **Display Crystal**.

# 6. Qualification vs Promotion

A registry can show that a holder is **eligible** for promotion.

Formal promotion still requires the relevant authority.

Example for Baron:

- Beginner Silver Tier
- 500,000,000 Lifetime Merit
- Crown approval
- formal ennoblement
- usually land or territorial grant

Only after approval does the Crest become the **Silver Baron Crest**.

# 8. Automatic Merit Recording

Merit is recorded automatically by the hidden Feloran/Tower-compatible framework when it recognizes valid acts such as:

- monster kills
- registered group contribution
- military service
- Gate clearing
- wall defense
- civilian rescue
- Crown-sanctioned missions

The Crest is **not** the sole storage location of Merit.

It functions more like an identity card and account key.

Conceptually:

> Merit is deposited automatically.
>
> The Crest is used to access and verify the record.

---

# 9. Lifetime Merit and Merit Credits

## Lifetime Merit

- never decreases
- cannot be spent
- records recognized accomplishment
- determines promotion eligibility
- contributes to advancement qualification
- supports noble elevation

## Merit Credits

Spendable institutional reward used for:

- cores
- Mana crystals
- medicine
- equipment
- training
- techniques
- Gate permits
- advancement chambers
- land rights

Spending Merit Credits does not reduce Lifetime Merit.

---

# 10. Inherited Noble Status

Inherited status remains separate from personal standing.

A noble-born child may carry:

- a Gray-White Common Citizen Crest
- low personal Merit
- a House sigil or heir mark on the reverse

Example:

> **Gray-White Crest**
>
> **Common Citizen**
>
> **House Starlight Heir Mark**

The parent's Merit is never inherited.

---

# 11. Death, Theft and Replacement

Merit Crests are soul-bound.

If the holder dies:

- the Crest becomes inactive
- the record locks
- it cannot be legally reused
- another person cannot inherit the dead holder's Merit

If lost or destroyed:

- the Merit record remains intact
- a Merit Hall can issue a replacement after identity verification

Counterfeiting or tampering with a Crest is a serious crime.

Alok's System may reveal limited public/registered information:

- Name
- Age

- full Attributes
- Skills

- secret resources
- hidden unregistered information
