# Felora Lifespan and Society

Tier advancement reconstructs the vessel and dramatically extends life.

## Human Baseline

| Tier | Approximate Natural Lifespan |
|---|---:|
| Ordinary | 80–120 years |
| Iron | 120–180 years |
| Bronze | 200–300 years |
| Silver | 450–700 years |
| Gold | 1,200–2,000 years |
| Platinum | 3,500–6,000 years |

Race, bloodline, Stars, Skills, regeneration and Divinity may increase these values.

## Social Consequences
Because people live so long:
- Merit requirements are intentionally enormous.
- Bronze careers may span many decades or centuries.
- Silver nobles can govern for centuries.
- Gold figures may remain active for more than a millennium.
- families stockpile breakthrough materials for generations.
- monster territories become strategic national resources.
- succession may be delayed for centuries.
- political grudges can be personally remembered.

## Breakthrough as Longevity
People pursue advancement not only for power, but for life.

A Peak Bronze trying to reach Silver may be fighting for several additional centuries.

A Silver trying to reach Gold may be fighting for over a millennium of future life.

## Rejuvenation
A full Tier breakthrough partially rejuvenates the vessel by:
- renewing organs
- repairing tissue
- strengthening Mana channels
- restoring metabolic balance
- removing age-related weakness

Chronological age does not reverse.
