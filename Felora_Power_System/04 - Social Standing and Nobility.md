# Felora Social Standing and Nobility

Felora has a genuine commoner–noble divide, but the divide can be crossed through personal achievement.

## Baronetcy
Peak Bronze + 15M Lifetime Merit is the normal entry point into hereditary nobility.

A Baronet may gain:
- House recognition
- hereditary surname/status
- limited noble privilege
- estate rights
- better legal standing
- access to noble institutions

## Inherited Title vs Personal Merit
Birth status and personal achievement are separate.

A Duke's child may be a ducal heir while personally remaining an Iron-tier Meritor.

The parent's Tier and Merit are not inherited.

## Succession
Major titles require personal qualification to exercise full authority.

If an heir is underqualified, a regent may govern until the heir qualifies.

Possible regents:
- surviving spouse
- qualified relative
- House elder
- Crown appointee

## Noble Advantages
Nobles retain major structural advantages:
- tutors
- training
- advancement materials
- family techniques
- better equipment
- Gate access
- academies
- political connections
- bloodline resources

But a commoner can still rise legally through the **Merit Path**.

## Social Principle
> Blood grants opportunity.
>
> Tier grants capability.
>
> Merit grants recognized accomplishment.
