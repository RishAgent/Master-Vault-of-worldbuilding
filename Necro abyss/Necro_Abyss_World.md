# Necro Abyss World

## Core Classification

**Necro Abyss World** is a **High-Gold to Low-Platinum world** dominated by necromantic civilizations, corrupted ecosystems, undead military powers, Abyssal Mana, and ancient soul-based structures.

It is significantly harsher than Felora.

Felora can be treated broadly as a **Gold-grade world**, where Silver combatants are important, Golds are major political and military powers, and Platinum beings exist as rare upper-tier entities.

Necro Abyss World sits above that baseline.

Its ordinary dangerous regions can contain large numbers of Gold-tier entities, while Platinum beings exist as established rulers, commanders, apex predators, necromantic lords, and regional powers rather than impossible anomalies.

The world should still preserve a strong hierarchy.

> Platinum is common enough to shape the world.
>
> Platinum is **not** so common that it becomes mundane.

---

# 1. World Power Band

## General Combat Population

Working world distribution in dangerous inhabited or monster-dense regions:

- **Silver:** common lower military / predator population
- **Gold:** very common serious threats
- **Platinum:** high-ranking rulers, commanders, elites, apex creatures and major specialists
- **Diamond:** extremely rare world-level entities, ancient rulers, sealed horrors or exceptional necromancers

A useful working distribution for major hostile regions:

| Tier | Approximate Role |
|---|---|
| Bronze / Iron | degraded undead, weak wildlife, servant creatures, vermin |
| Silver | common combatants, lesser undead soldiers, hunters, beasts |
| Gold | elite troops, commanders, war-beasts, serious predators, necromancers |
| Platinum | regional rulers, great undead, major generals, fortress lords, apex monsters |
| Diamond | extremely rare world-level beings |

A rough population bias in major war zones may look like:

- **40–50% Silver**
- **30–35% Gold**
- **10–20% Platinum**
- **well under 1% Diamond**
- remainder lower or degraded creatures

This is a balancing reference rather than a hard population law.

# 2. Celestia Rescue Context

Alok enters Necro Abyss World after meeting the manifested projection of the Tower itself.

The Tower reveals that:

- Celestia was a Divine-Born created through Tower-guided circumstances.
- Celestia was once intended to become the next Tower.
- Her body and soul still exist.
- Both are currently in the hands of a necromancer.
- Alok asks whether Celestia can be restored through his Eternal Legion / soul-restoration authority.
- The Tower accepts his proposal and sends him to retrieve her.

The world therefore acts as a **Celestia Rescue Arc** and as Alok's first serious campaign in a world where his personal combat power is no longer the main strategic weapon.

---

# 3. Time State

Time in Alok's original location is completely stopped while he remains inside Necro Abyss World.

This means:

- no wedding delay;
- no outside-world time loss;
- no Felora progression time loss;
- no family members realize he has temporarily left;
- he may spend an extended subjective period inside the Abyss World and return to effectively the same moment.

This keeps the existing Advanced-Silver / wedding timeline intact.

---

# 4. Temporary Tower Artifact

The Tower gives Alok a temporary Mana artifact before sending him into Necro Abyss World.

## Temporary Mana Reservoir Ring

Purpose:

- extreme sustained Mana reserve;
- large-scale Shadow extraction;
- Soul Authority support;
- Legion deployment;
- restoration work;
- prolonged campaign operations.

Working effect:

> **Effective Mana Capacity: Beginner Diamond class**

Important limitation:

This does **not** make Alok a Beginner Diamond combatant.

Alok remains physically and structurally:

> **Intermediate Silver**

The ring enhances reserve and operating endurance.

It does not automatically give him:

- Diamond durability;
- Diamond reaction speed;
- Diamond physical strength;
- Diamond spell output;
- Diamond vessel stability;
- Diamond-tier body reconstruction.

His own vessel remains the limiting factor for personal output.

---

# 5. Alok's Personal Combat Position

Alok is still **Intermediate Silver** upon entry.

However, Necro Abyss World removes many of the restrictions that limited him during the Felora auction battle.

There are no unconscious nobles around him.

No royal district to protect.

No city he is trying not to destroy.

Therefore Alok may make much freer use of:

- large Divine attacks;
- Astrape;
- Aetheryn;
- Aegis;
- Corpse Explosion;
- large-area elemental spells;
- multi-affinity attacks;
- Fenridon;
- Gold Shadows;
- large Shadow deployments;
- Aeonmorta Legion forces.

## Working Personal Combat Rule

Against Gold:

- one Beginner Gold: manageable depending on matchup;
- several Beginner Golds: dangerous but realistic;
- Intermediate Gold: serious threat;
- Advanced Gold: extremely dangerous;
- Peak Gold: beyond comfortable direct combat;
- Platinum: not a normal solo fight.

Alok may be capable of hurting higher beings through Divine, Light, Soul, Life/Death or anti-corruption mechanics, but raw Tier gaps remain important.

---

# 6. The True Power Source — Aeonmorta Legion

Necro Abyss World is designed to showcase the **Aeonmorta Legion as Alok's principal strategic power**.

Alok is not merely the strongest individual present.

He is the Sovereign who makes the army function.

His Legion can provide:

- heavy combatants;
- commanders;
- scouts;
- soul specialists;
- mages;
- support units;
- healers;
- defensive specialists;
- high-tier strike forces;
- large-scale warfare capacity.

Legion members are restored / preserved souls and persistent individuals.

They are distinct from Shadows.

Members of the Legion may:

- grow;
- evolve;
- receive Classes through the Class Codex;
- operate independently;
- return to Alok's Soul Space after death;
- be restored again through Alok's authority.

The Legion is therefore a persistent civilization-scale military system rather than a disposable summon army.

---

# 7. Shadow Army

Alok's Shadow army remains a separate system.

Shadows are not the original souls.

They are combat imprints / corpse-derived constructs.

This distinction allows Necro Abyss World to feed both systems independently.

## Shadow Uses

- disposable assault forces;
- mass infantry;
- cavalry;
- aerial units;
- elite copied combatants;
- siege units;
- scouts;
- bodyguards;
- expendable battlefield screens;
- Platinum or Gold battlefield assets if extraction succeeds.

The Abyss World is an ideal environment for rapid Shadow growth because it contains:

- huge hostile populations;
- large numbers of high-tier corpses;
- necromantic war zones;
- entrenched military forces;
- large-scale battlefield casualties.

---

# 8. Soul Extraction vs Shadow Extraction

Necro Abyss World provides two separate forms of battlefield growth.

## Shadow Extraction

Used primarily on:

- ordinary hostile corpses;
- powerful slain monsters;
- undead war-beasts;
- defeated commanders;
- high-tier enemies whose combat imprint is useful.

Outcome:

> Shadow combat construct.

## Soul Extraction / Eternal Legion Restoration

Used primarily on:

- intact souls;
- imprisoned souls;
- enslaved people;
- corrupted warriors who can be purified;
- ancient heroes;
- intelligent beings worth preserving;
- rare species;
- high-value commanders;
- beings who may voluntarily join after restoration.

Outcome:

> Restored individual within Alok's Eternal Legion framework.

---

# 9. Dual Extraction Principle

Because Shadows are **not souls**, a sufficiently intact enemy may theoretically provide:

1. a recoverable soul for restoration / Legion recruitment;
2. a corpse-derived combat imprint for Shadow extraction.

This allows one exceptional defeated enemy to potentially yield:

> **a restored individual**
>
> plus
>
> **a Shadow representing their prior combat state**

The restored person remains the true individual.

The Shadow remains a construct.

This should be used selectively rather than on every enemy.

---

# 10. Moral Difference Between the Two Powers

A useful thematic distinction:

> **Shadow Summoning asks what the corpse can still do.**
>
> **Eternal Legion asks whether the person can still be saved.**

Most enemies in Necro Abyss World should become:

- corpses;
- resources;
- Shadows.

Only exceptional souls should become new Legion members.

This prevents the Legion from becoming a meaningless population dump while making every restored soul narratively important.

# 11. Campaign Structure

## Phase One — Arrival and Survival

Alok enters Necro Abyss World with no map and no confirmed location.

Immediate objectives:

- survive;
- assess corruption;
- identify local Mana behavior;
- locate hostile forces;
- establish a safe perimeter;
- summon an initial Legion expedition group;
- deploy scouting Shadows;
- determine whether local languages / soul signatures can be understood;
- identify large settlements, fortresses and necromantic territories.

Alok personally handles most Silver enemies and some Golds.

## Phase Two — First Stronghold

Alok captures or secures:

- an undead fortress;
- a ruined city;
- an abandoned necromantic citadel;
- or another defensible location.

This becomes the operational base.

From here:

- Legion deployment increases;
- Shadow extraction begins at scale;
- prisoner and soul recovery begins;
- local maps and intelligence are gathered;
- Abyss resources are catalogued;
- scouting fronts expand.

## Phase Three — Expansion

The Legion begins operating on multiple fronts.

Alok is no longer personally present at every engagement.

Commanders take independent objectives.

Shadows provide expendable mass forces.

Legion soldiers provide intelligence, command, complex magic and disciplined warfare.

Alok moves between high-value targets, strongholds, soul prisons, necromantic laboratories, resource sites and battlefield crises.

## Phase Four — Platinum Resistance

As Alok's presence becomes known, genuine Platinum forces respond.

Potential enemies:

- Platinum undead generals;
- ancient necromancers;
- bone dragons;
- soul-forged war-beasts;
- fortress guardians;
- death knights;
- abyssal liches;
- regional sovereigns;
- high-tier necromantic constructs.

At this point:

> **Alok's army fights Platinum.**

Not Alok alone.

Platinum victories may require multiple Gold Legion members, Gold Shadows, large Silver formations, Divine purification, Light, Soul authority, Corpse Explosion chains, battlefield traps, formation warfare and sacrificial Shadow tactics.

---

# 12. Platinum and Diamond Limits

Platinum should remain frightening.

Even with the temporary Mana ring, Alok is not meant to casually duel Platinum enemies.

A Platinum being should represent:

- regional military power;
- major fortress ruler;
- apex predator;
- strategic threat;
- battle requiring army coordination.

Diamond beings should be extremely rare.

Potential examples:

- the necromancer who possesses Celestia;
- an ancient undead monarch;
- a sealed Abyss sovereign;
- a world-level death entity.

If the central necromancer is Diamond-tier or above, Alok should not defeat them through direct physical superiority.

Possible victory methods:

- Legion warfare;
- infiltration;
- soul-system exploitation;
- necromantic counter-authority;
- destroying anchors / phylacteries;
- stealing Celestia rather than killing the ruler;
- environmental collapse;
- internal rebellion;
- using the enemy's own undead against them.

---

# 13. Self-Feeding War Loop

Necro Abyss World should demonstrate how terrifying Alok's military architecture becomes during a long campaign.

Working loop:

> defeat enemies  
> ↓  
> extract useful Shadows  
> ↓  
> recover viable souls  
> ↓  
> increase army size and quality  
> ↓  
> defeat stronger forces  
> ↓  
> capture stronger corpses and souls  
> ↓  
> expand again

This is not because Alok personally becomes Platinum.

It is because his **war infrastructure grows as the campaign progresses**.

---

# 14. Shadow Army Growth Potential

Before this arc, Alok possesses a large hidden Silver Shadow army.

Necro Abyss World can substantially alter its composition.

Possible direction after the campaign:

- thousands of Silver Shadows;
- hundreds of Gold Shadows;
- a limited number of Platinum Shadows;
- specialized Abyss / undead units;
- elite commanders;
- siege creatures;
- flying monsters;
- necromantic support constructs.

Exact post-arc counts should remain flexible until battles are written.

---

# 15. Legion Growth Potential

The Aeonmorta Legion should grow more selectively.

Possible recruits:

- enslaved souls;
- ancient heroes;
- high-tier warriors;
- necromancers who reject their rulers;
- purified undead whose original souls survive;
- Abyss races capable of redemption;
- intelligent magical beasts;
- imprisoned spirits;
- commanders who choose Alok after restoration.

New Legion members should retain individuality.

They should not become mindless puppets.

---

# 16. Celestia

Primary rescue target:

> **Celestia**

Known:

- Divine-Born;
- born roughly 33,000 years before Alok;
- child of Anarak, a Dragon God associated with Mana;
- child of a Crescent Elvan Goddess associated with Lunar Laws;
- once the Tower's favorite / apostle;
- intended successor to the Tower;
- killed before age 30;
- body never publicly recovered;
- soul never publicly recovered;
- body and soul now confirmed to exist in Necro Abyss World;
- both currently controlled by a necromancer.

Alok intends to restore her through Soul Authority, Divine Power, Eternal Legion framework, Soul Space and restoration mechanics.

---

# 17. Celestia Restoration Rule

Celestia should **not** return easily.

Her body and soul have existed in necromantic custody for approximately 33,000 years.

Potential complications:

- soul fragmentation;
- necromantic binding;
- phylactery splitting;
- memory damage;
- corruption;
- body modification;
- authority theft;
- soul-chain anchors;
- forced undead evolution;
- partial consciousness;
- emotional trauma;
- missing Law fragments.

Alok's restoration should be possible because his power is fundamentally different from ordinary necromancy.

However, the restoration should still be difficult, expensive, technically complex and emotionally significant.

---

# 18. Celestia and the Legion

If Alok restores Celestia through his Eternal Legion framework:

- she remains fully herself;
- she is not a puppet;
- her autonomy is preserved;
- the Legion acts as the metaphysical anchor allowing her restoration;
- she may technically become bound to Alok's cycle;
- if destroyed again, she may potentially return to his Soul Space for restoration.

This would make her an unprecedented Legion member.

She was once meant to become the next Tower.

Now she may become part of the Sovereign of Eternal Cycle.

---

# 19. Alok's Progression During This Arc

Alok should **not automatically advance to Advanced Silver** merely because of this campaign.

The outside timeline is frozen.

Therefore the existing Felora progression remains intact:

- auction battle recovery;
- Physical Vessel reaches roughly 99%;
- return from Necro Abyss World;
- normal refinement continues;
- saturation on Day 8;
- breakthrough quest appears afterward.

Necro Abyss World instead advances Alok through:

- combat experience;
- soul authority;
- Legion command;
- Shadow quality;
- battlefield leadership;
- necromantic understanding;
- Abyss materials;
- rare souls;
- new strategic knowledge.

# 20. Narrative Purpose

Necro Abyss World serves several purposes at once.

## Power-System Purpose

Demonstrates that Alok's greatest strength is no longer purely individual combat.

## Sovereign Purpose

Shows **Sovereign of Eternal Cycle** functioning as a true army / civilization-scale Class.

## Shadow Purpose

Provides an environment where high-tier Shadow extraction can occur rapidly.

## Legion Purpose

Provides recoverable souls and potential new Legion members.

## Celestia Purpose

Introduces and rescues the previous Divine-Born who was meant to inherit the Tower.

## Cosmology Purpose

Provides early direct exposure to:

- Abyss civilizations;
- extreme necromancy;
- higher-tier worlds;
- indirect Void influence;
- the deeper Tower conflict.

Necro Abyss World should feel:

- ancient;
- hostile;
- necromantic;
- militarized;
- corrupted;
- enormous;
- oppressive;
- full of forgotten civilizations;

A graveyard.

A kingdom of the dead.
