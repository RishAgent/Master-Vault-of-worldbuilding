# Necro Abyss Conspiracy and Celestia Rescue Plan

## Core Arc Premise

The **Necro Abyss World** is not only a rescue mission for Celestia.

It is also the stage for a wider assassination conspiracy against Alok.

The Tower deliberately sends Alok into Necro Abyss World at the exact moment multiple divine factions and surviving followers of the five gods Alok erased as an infant are moving to kill him before he grows further.

The timing is calculated.

The Tower is intentionally preventing a repeat of Celestia's tragedy while simultaneously using the assassination window to place Alok somewhere he can:

- grow;
- build his Shadow army;
- expand the Eternal Legion;
- uncover the conspiracy;
- rescue Celestia;
- and turn the assassination attempt into an ambush.

# 1. World Power Structure

Necro Abyss World remains:

> **High Gold → Low Platinum overall**

The world baseline is not changed.

General structure:

- Gold = common serious combat tier
- Platinum = established rulers, governors, commanders, apex entities
- Diamond = rare inner-circle world-level beings
- Supreme = exceptional central ruler class

# 7. Severed Apostleship

Apostles were once terrifying not only because of their personal Tier, but because of the active divine connection to their patron.

Now their patrons are dead.

Therefore they retain:

- Supreme-tier bodies
- old blessings
- sacred techniques
- divine relics
- fragments of authority
- inherited Divine Skills
- ancient combat experience
- faith-fed reserves
- apostolic physiology

But they have lost:

- active Divine Power replenishment
- direct Law reinforcement
- real-time divine guidance
- miracle intervention
- emergency god descent
- active patron protection
- continuous authority amplification

Working System-style condition:

[ APOSTOLIC STATE: SEVERED ]

[ DIVINE PATRON: DECEASED ]

[ AUTHORITY LINK: COLLAPSED ]

[ MIRACLE ACCESS: LOST ]

[ DIVINE REINFORCEMENT: SEVERELY REDUCED ]

This makes them:

> still Supreme-tier monsters

but no longer functioning as fully supported Apostles.

Their Apostleship becomes a major historical advantage, but no longer an active god-backed force multiplier.

# 15. Tower's Calculated Timing

The Tower sends Alok to Necro Abyss World deliberately.

This is not coincidence.

The Tower knows an assassination attempt is forming.

It recognizes the pattern.

Thirty-three thousand years ago:

- Celestia grew openly;
- divine enemies became afraid;
- gods acted through followers;
- schemes killed her.

Alok's infancy:

- five gods directly attacked him;
- Alok erased them;
- the Tower then hid and sealed him.

Now:

- Alok is visible again;
- his growth is accelerating;
- his power is becoming Tower-like;
- divine enemies are moving before he becomes too dangerous.

The Tower changes strategy again.

She removes Alok from the assassination window.

But rather than merely hiding him—

she sends him somewhere that forces growth.

# 26. Core Thematic Function

This arc is not merely:

> Alok rescues Celestia.

It is about:

- Sovereignty;
- inherited tragedy;
- refusing repetition;
- restoration vs replacement;
- self-generated power vs borrowed divine authority;
- Alok becoming a commander rather than merely a fighter;
- the Tower learning from Celestia;
- Alok turning assassination into growth;
- Celestia becoming proof that even the Tower's greatest failure can be reversed.

The strongest thematic contrast:

> The Tower once lost Celestia and tried to replace her with a new strategy.

Alok's instinct is:

> **Why replace her when I can bring her back?**

That is one of the defining differences between Alok and the Tower.
