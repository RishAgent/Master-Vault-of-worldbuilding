# Supreme Dragon Lineage

> [!important] Naming warning
> **Supreme Dragon Lineage** is a proper ancestral bloodline name. It is **not the same thing as Supreme Tier**.

## Core concept

The Supreme Dragon Lineage predates or stands extremely close to the ancestral draconic template from which the modern **Dragon** and **Dragonoid** branches diverged.

It represents unusually complete draconic potential rather than merely "a stronger species of Dragon."

A sufficiently pure bearer can potentially express traits associated with both branches:

- overwhelming Dragon vessel quality
- humanoid precision
- instinctive magic
- structured spellcraft
- exceptional Star/Core compatibility
- full draconic manifestation
- high compatibility with Classes
- ancient bloodline memory
- exceptionally broad evolutionary options

## Relationship to Dragons and Dragonoids

Supreme-lineage descendants may appear as:

- Dragons
- Dragonoids
- Draconic Ascendants
- rare higher expressions that do not cleanly fit either lower race

This helps explain why Dragons and Dragonoids begin converging at **Platinum** and normally complete Draconic Ascension at **Diamond**: the higher their evolution, the closer they return toward the complete ancestral draconic template. Supreme-lineage bearers may express this convergence unusually early or with far greater completeness.

## Alok

Alok carries ancestry connected to the **Supreme Dragon Lineage**.

This should remain separate from his current displayed Race and from his Life/Death divine heritage unless/until the narrative explicitly reveals how the lineages intersect.

Potential long-term implications:

- compatibility with draconic bloodline powers
- unusual vessel resilience
- affinity with Dragons/Dragonoids
- possible full draconic traits or form at later awakening
- access to ancestral draconic evolution routes

> [!warning] Reveal control
> Exact parentage, source, purity, concentration and awakening conditions are intentionally not fixed here unless separately canonized in the story.
