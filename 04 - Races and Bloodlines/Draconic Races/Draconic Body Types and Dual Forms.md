# Draconic Body Types and Dual Forms

Body type is anatomy, not Tier.

## Common Dragon body plans

- **True Dragon:** four legs plus wings; highly adaptable
- **Drake:** four-legged, usually wingless terrestrial form
- **Wyvern:** two rear legs plus wing-arms; aerial specialization
- **Wyrm:** elongated/serpentine body
- **Lindwyrm:** serpentine body with forelimbs
- **Amphiptere:** winged serpent
- **Leviathan:** massive aquatic draconic architecture
- **Burrow Dragon:** subterranean specialist
- **Shell Dragon:** heavily armored shell/turtle-like form
- **Feathered Dragon:** Mana-feathered celestial/wind/light forms
- **Construct Dragon:** artificial or forge-born draconic body
- **Spirit Dragon:** partially or wholly spiritual body

Size is independent of body type and Tier.

## Dragon humanoid manifestation

Humanoid form is a **racial development ability**, not a universal fixed-Tier spell.

Typical progression:

- **Silver:** exceptional/high-bloodline Dragons may show partial humanoid features or crude mimicry
- **Gold:** advanced Dragons may develop functional humanoid mimicry
- **Platinum:** stable humanoid manifestation becomes normal among high/civilized lineages; body and Mana pathways begin becoming genuinely compatible with the form
- **Diamond:** Draconic Ascension normally completes; Humanoid Form becomes an authentic body of the Draconic Ascendant
- **Supreme+:** increasingly effortless form shifting, mass control and lineage-specific dual-form techniques

Primitive lineages may unlock humanoid expression later. Magical/celestial lineages may unlock it earlier.

## Dragonoid draconic manifestation

Typical progression:

- **Silver:** scales, horns, claws, draconic eyes and pressure become easier to manifest
- **Gold:** stronger wings/breath/partial racial anatomy; early partial Dragon Form in gifted bloodlines
- **Platinum:** major draconic manifestation; near-complete or complete Dragon Form becomes possible in strong lineages
- **Diamond:** Draconic Ascension normally completes; Full Dragon Form becomes an authentic body rather than a temporary transformation
- **Supreme+:** increasingly complete ancestral manifestation, mass control and lineage-specific dual-form techniques

Exact timing varies by bloodline, Personal Rank, Class, affinity and evolution path.

## Dual-form rule after Diamond

A completed **Draconic Ascendant** possesses two genuine body expressions:

### Draconic Form
- maximum raw mass
- armor
- breath output
- physical force
- flight/locomotion advantages
- strongest racial anatomy

### Humanoid Form
- precision
- smaller target profile
- weapons/artifacts
- fine spellcasting
- civilization/social compatibility
- often better Mana efficiency

Neither form is universally superior.

## Partial manifestation remains useful

Even after Ascension, an individual may selectively manifest:

- horns
- claws
- scales
- wings
- tail
- draconic eyes
- breath organs
- localized armor

without changing fully into Draconic Form. Skilled Ascendants use partial manifestation tactically.
