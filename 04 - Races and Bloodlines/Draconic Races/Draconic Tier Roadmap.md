# Draconic Tier Roadmap

This is the broad racial/civilizational interpretation of V4 Tiers for draconic beings. Species and exceptional bloodlines may deviate.

| V4 Tier | Typical Dragon significance | Typical Dragonoid significance |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | Hatchling / young true Dragon | Normal birth baseline; young Dragonoid |
| Silver | Juvenile developed Dragon; strong racial magic | Trained youth; early draconic manifestations and Classes |
| Gold | Mature regional Dragon; humanoid mimicry possible | Adult combatant; strong partial draconic traits |
| **Platinum** | **High Dragon; stable humanoid form and structured magic; convergence begins** | **Elite Dragonoid; major draconic anatomy; near-complete Dragon Form begins** |
| **Diamond** | **Draconic Ascendant — Dragon-origin** | **Draconic Ascendant — Dragonoid-origin** |
| Supreme | High Draconic Ascendant; lineage authority rapidly deepens | Same converged race family |
| Noble | Noble/Ancient Ascendant | Same converged race family |
| Baron | Draconic Lord-grade Ascendant | Same converged race family |
| Count | Great Draconic Lord / clan-scale authority | Same converged race family |
| Duke | Archdragon / Imperial Ascendant-grade existence | Same converged race family |
| King | Dragon King / Draconic King | Same converged race family |
| Emperor | Dragon Emperor / Draconic Emperor | Same converged race family |
| Sovereign | Draconic Sovereign | Same converged race family |
| Transcendent | Transcendent draconic existence; racial limits begin breaking down | Same converged race family |
| Demigod | Concept-bearing draconic demigod | Same converged race family |
| Divine | Divine Dragon Ancestor / unique divine draconic race | Same converged race family |

> [!canon]
> **Platinum is the transition zone. Diamond is the normal completion point of Draconic Ascension.**

> [!note]
> Proper names such as **Dragon King**, **Dragon Emperor**, **Archdragon**, etc. are cultural/evolutionary titles. They often correlate with certain Tiers but are not automatically granted by Tier alone.
