# Draconic Ascension and Convergence

## Locked V4 placement

> [!canon]
> **Platinum Tier is the convergence transition. Diamond Tier is the completed Draconic Ascension.**
>
> Dragons and Dragonoids begin from opposite racial architectures, but by Diamond their bodies and souls have acquired the defining capabilities of both branches.

The term **Ascension** in draconic racial lore does **not** mean the universal Transcendent Tier. It is a specific draconic evolutionary event that normally completes during the breakthrough into **Diamond Tier**.

## Pre-Ascension — Platinum Tier

Platinum is the normal **convergence stage**.

At this point a high Dragon should be developing or stabilizing:

- civilization-grade sapience
- structured spellcraft
- stable humanoid manifestation
- compatibility with Classes where appropriate
- precision control of an enormous draconic vessel
- increasingly deliberate rather than purely instinctive racial magic

At the same time, a high Dragonoid should be developing:

- major scale/horn/claw manifestation
- true racial breath techniques
- wings or lineage-specific draconic anatomy
- huge vessel expansion
- partial to near-complete Dragon Form
- stronger instinctive draconic magic and bloodline memory

Platinum beings may already blur the distinction heavily, but their original branch still dominates their racial architecture.

## Completed Ascension — Diamond Tier

The breakthrough into **Diamond Tier** normally completes the convergence.

Both branches become a higher draconic Race broadly classified as:

# **Draconic Ascendant**

Their origin remains recorded:

- **Origin: Dragon**
- **Origin: Dragonoid**

but origin is now evolutionary history rather than a restriction on their present body.

A Draconic Ascendant possesses a **dual-form racial architecture**.

### Dragon-origin Ascendant

The former Dragon now possesses:

- a genuine humanoid body
- a genuine full draconic body
- structured magic
- refined fine-motor Mana control
- full sapience and civilization compatibility
- Class compatibility where the soul/path permits
- preserved racial breath, scales, physicality and instinctive draconic magic

### Dragonoid-origin Ascendant

The former Dragonoid now possesses:

- a genuine humanoid body
- a genuine full draconic body
- full racial breath architecture
- complete major draconic anatomy
- dramatically expanded vessel and Mana capacity
- retained Class compatibility and structured spellcraft
- awakened instinctive draconic magic and bloodline traits

## Two authentic forms

After completed Ascension, neither form is merely an illusion, disguise or temporary transformation.

Both are valid manifestations of the same soul-body template:

> **Humanoid Form** — precision, artifacts, social interaction, compact spellcasting and efficiency.
>
> **Draconic Form** — maximum mass, armor, breath output, flight, physical force and racial expression.

Lineage, Class and personal specialization determine which form is superior for a particular task.

## Species names after convergence

**Draconic Ascendant** is an umbrella racial classification, not a replacement for every lineage/species title.

For example, a Diamond-tier being may be recorded as:

> **Race:** Draconic Ascendant  
> **Origin:** Dragon  
> **Lineage / Evolved Form:** Sunscale Imperial Dragon

or:

> **Race:** Draconic Ascendant  
> **Origin:** Dragonoid  
> **Lineage / Evolved Form:** Imperial Storm Dragon

This allows the old species catalogue to remain useful while making the Dragon/Dragonoid convergence mechanically explicit.

## Post-Ascension reproduction

Draconic Ascendants can produce descendants that stabilize naturally into either lower branch.

Depending on:

- parental lineage
- partner
- bloodline purity
- bloodline concentration
- Mana environment
- inherited affinity
- egg/gestation conditions

children may be born as:

- Dragons
- Dragonoids
- rare hybrid/atavistic draconic forms

This is one of the major biological origins of Dragonoid civilization: ancient ascended Dragons produced descendants whose humanoid architecture stabilized from birth.

## Higher-Tier progression

After Diamond, the question is no longer **Dragon or Dragonoid?**

It becomes:

> **What kind of Draconic Ascendant is this being becoming?**

Typical interpretation:

- **Diamond:** completed Draconic Ascendant; dual-form architecture stabilized
- **Supreme:** High Ascendant; major lineage authority awakens
- **Noble–Duke:** lineage lord/archdragon/imperial ascendant stages
- **King:** Dragon King / Draconic King-grade lineage expression
- **Emperor:** Dragon Emperor / Draconic Emperor-grade expression
- **Sovereign:** Draconic Sovereign; extremely complete racial authority
- **Transcendent:** transcendent draconic existence; conventional racial limitations begin breaking down
- **Demigod:** concept-bearing draconic demigod
- **Divine:** Divine Dragon Ancestor, Divine Draconic Sovereign, or unique lineage-specific God Race

At upper Tiers, Race names become increasingly personal, ancestral and conceptual.
