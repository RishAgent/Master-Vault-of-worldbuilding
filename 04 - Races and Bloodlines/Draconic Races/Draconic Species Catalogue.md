## Apex classification rule

Entries marked **Transcendent+** require manual conceptual placement:

- **Transcendent** if the name describes an extraordinarily evolved but still fundamentally racial power.
- **Demigod** if the being possesses genuine conceptual Authority but is not a complete god-vessel existence.
- **Divine** if the species literally embodies/commands a fundamental concept, Law or divine Authority.
