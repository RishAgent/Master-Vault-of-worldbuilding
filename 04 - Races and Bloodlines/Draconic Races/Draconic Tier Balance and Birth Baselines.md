# Draconic Tier Balance and Birth Baselines

> [!important] Locked baseline
> **True draconic races are not Ordinary-tier creatures.** A healthy Dragon hatches at **Bronze Tier or above**. Most Dragonoids are likewise born around **Beginner Bronze**, making them one of the strongest naturally born civilized races known to the Tower.

This is a racial starting point, not Personal Rank. A B-Rank Dragon and B-Rank human have the same B-grade growth potential, but the Dragon begins with a vastly superior vessel.

## Dragon birth Tier

| Draconic lineage quality | Typical hatch Tier | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Weak/degraded true Dragon lineage | Beginner Bronze | Minimum stable baseline for a healthy true Dragon |
| Common Dragon lineage | Beginner–Intermediate Bronze | Most wild hatchlings |
| Greater/High lineage | Intermediate–Advanced Bronze | Strong clans and dangerous regional species |
| Ancient/Imperial lineage | Advanced–Peak Bronze | Rare eggs with dense inherited cores |
| Primordial/Supreme lineage | Peak Bronze–Beginner Silver | Exceptional births; usually require powerful parents or incubation conditions |

A dragon egg can undergo **Precocious Racial Evolution**, allowing it to hatch in a later Race form or higher Tier if bloodline purity, concentration, incubation and resources are exceptional.

## Dragonoid birth Tier

| Dragonoid lineage quality | Typical birth Tier | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Degraded/atavistic | Advanced Iron–Beginner Bronze | Rarely weak by draconic standards |
| Common Dragonoid | Beginner Bronze | Civilized racial baseline |
| Greater/High bloodline | Intermediate Bronze | Common among strong houses |
| Ancient/Imperial bloodline | Advanced Bronze | Elite noble houses |
| Primordial/Supreme bloodline | Peak Bronze, rarely Beginner Silver | Exceptional births |

Dragonoids are therefore dramatically stronger at birth than humans and most common Tower races.

## Same-Tier combat bias

Dragons are deliberately **overbuilt for their Tier**.

At equal Tier and similar Personal Rank, a Dragon commonly has:

- higher STR and VIT
- greater total Mana capacity
- stronger natural armor
- racial breath damage
- superior elemental resistance
- flight or specialized locomotion
- larger effective combat mass

A Dragonoid at the same Tier is usually less overwhelming physically than a Dragon, but gains:

- better fine Mana control
- earlier Class access
- weapon/artifact compatibility
- formal spellcraft
- tactical education
- humanoid efficiency

## Racial benchmark envelopes

These are **typical envelopes**, not qualification requirements.

### Beginner Bronze

Universal benchmark: ~250.

**Dragon hatchling / young Dragon:**
- STR: ~350–500
- AGI: ~250–400
- VIT: ~400–600
- INT: ~250–450

**Dragonoid:**
- STR: ~280–380
- AGI: ~280–400
- VIT: ~300–420
- INT: ~320–480

Specialized lineages can deviate heavily.

## Personal Rank distribution

Draconic races have unusually favorable Rank distributions.

### Dragons
- B Rank: common
- A Rank: common among strong lineages
- S Rank: rare but culturally significant
- SS/SSS: ancient, imperial or primordial prodigies
- Divine: extraordinary bloodline anomaly / divine lineage

### Dragonoids
- B Rank: common baseline
- A Rank: very common among trained noble or military houses
- S Rank: recognized prodigies, not mythical impossibilities
- SS/SSS: civilization-level talents
- Divine: extraordinarily rare

This high distribution is one reason draconic civilizations are major Tower powers.

## Why Dragons still have Tiers

Their racial advantage does not allow them to ignore the V4 Tier system.

A Beginner Bronze Dragon is still a Bronze vessel. Its racial anatomy simply lets it express the Tier more efficiently and dangerously than a typical human.


## Convergence balance point

The natural Dragon/Dragonoid contrast is strongest from **Bronze through Gold**.

- Dragons dominate raw racial body quality, instinctive magic and physical combat.
- Dragonoids compensate through Classes, structured spellcraft, equipment and civilization-based training.

At **Platinum**, the distinction starts collapsing as each branch acquires the other's defining advantages.

At **Diamond**, completed Draconic Ascendants possess both a genuine humanoid body and genuine draconic body. Balance from Diamond upward should therefore be determined primarily by **Personal Rank, lineage, Class, Tier development, Stars, affinities and individual mastery**, not by whether the being was originally born Dragon or Dragonoid.
