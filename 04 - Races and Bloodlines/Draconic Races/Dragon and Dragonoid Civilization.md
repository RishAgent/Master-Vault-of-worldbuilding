# Dragon and Dragonoid Civilization

## Dragonoid civilization

Dragonoids are recognized by the Tower as a **civilized climbing Race**.

They possess:

- kingdoms and empires
- guilds
- military orders
- magical academies
- Class institutions
- crafting traditions
- Dragon-partner houses
- clan bloodline registries
- Tower expedition teams

## Dragon participation

Sapient Dragons can be:

- citizens
- rulers
- clan elders
- military partners
- scholars
- guardians
- merchants
- independent Tower climbers

The culture rejects the assumption that a Dragon accompanying a Dragonoid is automatically a mount or pet.

## Partnership traditions

Common partnership models include:

- oath partners
- sibling-bond traditions
- rider/flight combat pairs
- mage/Dragon artillery pairs
- house guardian bonds
- ancestral patron relationships

Consent and status matter strongly in advanced draconic societies.

## Political structure

A single kingdom may contain:

- Dragonoid commoners and nobles
- Dragon clans
- mixed bloodline houses
- Ascended Draconic elders
- allied non-draconic races

Some states are Dragonoid-led, some Dragon-led, and some governed jointly.

## Tower identity

### Dragon
Tower classification usually begins as **Draconic Mana Beast / Sapient Draconic Race**, depending on intelligence and civilization status.

### Dragonoid
Recognized as a standard **Civilized Tower Race**.

### Draconic Ascendant
Normally recognized from **Diamond Tier onward** as an **Ascended Draconic Race**, with Dragon/Dragonoid origin recorded separately. Platinum-tier beings may already be legally/culturally treated as ascension candidates because the convergence process has begun.
