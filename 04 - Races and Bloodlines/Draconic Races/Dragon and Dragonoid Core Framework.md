# Dragon and Dragonoid Core Framework

## The two draconic races

### Dragon

A Dragon is a **beast of magic**: an organism whose body, Mana Core, bloodline and instincts are inseparable from magic.

Dragons begin with overwhelming natural advantages:

- powerful birth vessels
- very high Mana density
- racial breath weapons
- scales, claws, wings or other specialized anatomy
- exceptional affinity compatibility
- instinctive magic
- long natural lifespans
- strong bloodline memory and racial Skills

Early and mid-stage wild Dragons do **not** require Classes to be dangerous. Their Race itself supplies enormous combat functionality.

### Dragonoid

A Dragonoid is the **civilized humanoid branch of draconic evolution**.

Dragonoids are born humanoid and sapient. They are recognized by the Tower as a normal climbing civilization and can naturally participate in:

- Classes
- professions
- guilds
- kingdoms
- formal spell systems
- Tower contracts
- artifact crafting
- military organizations
- schools and magical academies

Dragonoids sacrifice some of the Dragon branch's immediate raw body superiority in exchange for precision, flexibility, Class access, culture and structured magic.

## Shared ancestry

Dragons and Dragonoids are not unrelated species. They are **sibling branches of one draconic ancestry**.

A useful analogy is not "human and lizard," but two evolutionary solutions derived from the same deep magical template:

- **Dragon route:** body and instinct first → civilization and humanoid control later.
- **Dragonoid route:** civilization and humanoid control first → complete draconic body later.

## Partnership culture

In mature draconic civilizations, Dragons are not pets and Dragonoids are not their masters.

They exist as:

- kin
- partners
- bonded companions
- clan members
- military partners
- ancestral elders
- political allies
- family-line protectors

A Dragon may outrank, outlive or politically command the Dragonoid beside it. A Dragonoid may be the strategist, mage or ruler paired with a physically superior Dragon. The relationship is culturally flexible.

## The convergence principle

At sufficient evolution, both races acquire what the other began with.

A Dragon gains:

- stable humanoid manifestation
- civilization-grade intelligence
- structured spellcraft
- potential Class compatibility
- precision body control

A Dragonoid gains:

- partial draconic manifestation
- wings, scales, horns and breath
- enormous vessel expansion
- full ancestral draconic form
- increasingly complete Dragon Authority

At **Platinum Tier**, the two racial routes enter their convergence stage. The breakthrough into **Diamond Tier** normally completes [[Draconic Ascension and Convergence|Draconic Ascension]], producing a **Draconic Ascendant**. From that point onward, Dragon or Dragonoid is primarily an origin designation rather than a limitation on present racial form.
