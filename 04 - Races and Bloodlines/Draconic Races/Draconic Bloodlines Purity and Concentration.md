# Draconic Bloodlines Purity and Concentration

Draconic Bloodline is separate from **Race**, **Personal Rank**, and **Tier**.

Example:

- Race: Flamehorn Dragon
- Tier: Intermediate Silver
- Personal Rank: A
- Bloodline: Imperial Fire Dragon Lineage
- Bloodline Purity: 91%
- Bloodline Concentration: High

## Bloodline Purity

Purity measures how cleanly the current lineage is expressed.

| Purity | Category | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–39% | Degraded | Heavy dilution/conflict; weakened inherited expression |
| 40–69% | Ordinary | Normal wild/civilized expression |
| 70–84% | Strong | Excellent specimen |
| 85–94% | Exceptional | Rare, highly stable lineage expression |
| 95–99% | Near-perfect | Exceptional ancestral fidelity |
| 100% | Perfect | Complete expression of the current lineage |

100% Purity does **not** force evolution. It improves stability and can reveal hidden evolutionary branches.

## Bloodline Concentration

Concentration measures the **quantity/density of ancestral essence** present.

A being may be:

- 98% pure but possess only a faint ancestral spark
- 85% pure but carry an enormous concentration of ancient blood

Both matter differently.

## Effects of strong draconic bloodline

Bloodline can influence:

- birth Tier
- Rank distribution
- racial Skills
- affinity strength
- Star formation
- evolution options
- body type
- breath type
- lifespan
- inherited memories
- Class options for Dragonoids
- conceptual compatibility at high Tiers

## Draconic lineage grades

This is a **lore classification**, not a power Tier and not Personal Rank.

- Lesser
- Common
- Greater
- High
- Noble
- Ancient
- Imperial
- Primordial
- Supreme
- Divine

A "Supreme Dragon Lineage" is therefore a named ancestry grade and **not the same thing as Supreme Tier**.
