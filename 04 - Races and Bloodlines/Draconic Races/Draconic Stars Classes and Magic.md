# Draconic Stars Classes and Magic

## Dragon Mana architecture

Dragons possess some of the densest natural Mana Cores among mortal races.

Their racial anatomy often includes:

- core-linked breath organs
- Mana channels embedded through scales and bones
- affinity-specialized glands/organs
- unusually resilient Star architecture
- bloodline memories that assist instinctive casting

## Stars

Dragons use the same V4 Star system as other races, but high-quality draconic lineages often form Stars more efficiently.

Possible advantages include:

- proto-Star structures formed in the egg
- naturally high Mana compression tolerance
- racial evolutions that reinforce Star stability
- breath organs directly interfacing with the Star/Core

A Dragon being born at Bronze does **not automatically mean it possesses a complete First Star**; however, most healthy true Dragons develop or stabilize their First Star extremely early because Bronze-tier draconic biology is built around dense Mana architecture.

## Dragons and Classes

Immature wild Dragons commonly rely on:

- Race Skills
- bloodline abilities
- instinctive magic
- affinities
- physical anatomy

They may remain Classless far longer than civilized races without being weak.

Sapient/civilized Dragons can later gain Classes through the normal V4 System when eligible.

Examples:

- Infernal Vanguard
- Storm Tyrant
- Rune Scholar
- Imperial Flame Knight
- Leviathan Warden

## Dragonoids and Classes

Dragonoids are Class-compatible from the same developmental stage as other civilized Tower races.

Their Race naturally synergizes with:

- Dragon Knight paths
- elemental mage paths
- runic crafting
- aerial warfare
- bloodline Classes
- transformation Classes

A Dragonoid's Class does not replace its Race Skills.

## Affinities

Draconic evolution families are strongly affinity-driven. Common roots include:

- Fire / Magma / Solar
- Lightning / Wind / Storm
- Water / Ice / Pressure
- Earth / Metal / Gravity
- Nature / Life
- Death / Soul
- Dream / Mind
- Rune / Alchemy
- Mirror / Space
- Sound / Resonance
- Cosmic / Stellar

High-Tier dragons may awaken conceptual affinities or Authorities, but these must be earned through evolution and comprehension rather than assumed from species name alone.
