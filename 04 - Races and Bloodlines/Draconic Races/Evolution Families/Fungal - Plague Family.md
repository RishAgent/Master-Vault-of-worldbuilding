# Fungal / Plague Draconic Family

## V4 identity

**Core affinities:** Poison, Nature, Disease  
**Archetype:** Blighted / Devourer

A horrifying, often highly restricted lineage that starts as simple swamp predators and evolves into civilization-ending ecological catastrophes.

## Body-type trend

Wyrms and Abyssal Dragons. They rarely develop true wings, instead floating on miasma or growing massive, grotesque fungal structures.

> [!important] Conversion rule
> The species below retain the original side-story names and evolutionary relationships, but old Bloodline Rank labels have been removed. The listed V4 Tier is a **recommended usual evolution point**, not an absolute species power lock.

- **Marshfang Wyrm** → **Venomcoil Wyrm**
- **Venomcoil Wyrm** → **Mirecrown Wyrm**
- **Mirecrown Wyrm** → **Plaguebloom Dragon** — *Fungal Symbiosis*
- **Plaguebloom Dragon** → **Rot-Crown Dragon**
- **Rot-Crown Dragon** → **Necrotic Spore Sovereign**
- **Necrotic Spore Sovereign** → **Pestilent Worldwyrm**
- **Pestilent Worldwyrm** → **Plague Sovereign Dragon**
- **Plague Sovereign Dragon** → **Unknown Mythical**

## V4 balancing notes

- All healthy true-Dragon root forms must be **Bronze Tier or higher**.
- Race evolution is conditional and may occur earlier/later than the recommendation.
- Personal Rank remains F→Divine and is independent of species.
- Conceptual apex forms listed as **Transcendent+** must be manually placed at Transcendent, Demigod or Divine depending on how literal their Authority becomes.
- A Dragonoid bloodline may inherit this family's affinities and transformation traits without being born in the corresponding beast-form species.
