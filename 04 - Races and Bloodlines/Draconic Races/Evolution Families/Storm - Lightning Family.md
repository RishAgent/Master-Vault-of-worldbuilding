# Storm / Lightning Draconic Family

## V4 identity

**Core affinities:** Lightning, Wind  
**Archetype:** Celestial / Tyrant

Apex predators of the sky, evolving from fragile gliders into regional weather dominators and conceptual thunder entities.

## Body-type trend

Begins exclusively as Wyverns (two legs, wing-arms) built for aerodynamics. Mid-tiers branch into True Dragons. Apex forms are Tyrant Dragons and Heavenly Dragons.

> [!important] Conversion rule
> The species below retain the original side-story names and evolutionary relationships, but old Bloodline Rank labels have been removed. The listed V4 Tier is a **recommended usual evolution point**, not an absolute species power lock.

## Evolution relationships

- **Breezetail Wyvern** → **Galecrest Wyvern**
- **Galecrest Wyvern** → **Stormwing Wyvern**
- **Stormwing Wyvern** → **Thunderjaw Wyvern**
- **Stormwing Wyvern** → **Tempest Wyvern**
- **Thunderjaw Wyvern** → **Thundercrest Dragon**
- **Tempest Wyvern** → **Stormheart Dragon**
- **Thundercrest Dragon** → **Thunder Emperor Dragon**
- **Thunder Emperor Dragon** → **Storm Tyrant Dragon** — *Authority Path*
- **Stormheart Dragon** → **Storm Tyrant Dragon**
- **Storm Tyrant Dragon** → **Heavenly Storm Dragon**
- **Heavenly Storm Dragon** → **Heavenbreaker Thunder Dragon** — *Destruction Mutation*
- **Heavenly Storm Dragon** → **Imperial Storm Dragon**
- **Imperial Storm Dragon** → **Celestial Thunder Emperor Dragon**
- **Celestial Thunder Emperor Dragon** → **Unknown Mythical**

## V4 balancing notes

- All healthy true-Dragon root forms must be **Bronze Tier or higher**.
- Race evolution is conditional and may occur earlier/later than the recommendation.
- Personal Rank remains F→Divine and is independent of species.
- Conceptual apex forms listed as **Transcendent+** must be manually placed at Transcendent, Demigod or Divine depending on how literal their Authority becomes.
- A Dragonoid bloodline may inherit this family's affinities and transformation traits without being born in the corresponding beast-form species.
