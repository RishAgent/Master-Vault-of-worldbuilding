# Fire / Imperial Draconic Family

## V4 identity

**Core affinities:** Fire, Authority  
**Archetype:** Imperial

The most well-known progression of draconic power, moving from simple volcanic scavengers to the highest authorities of flame and command.

## Body-type trend

Starts as Ground Drakes (wingless, terrestrial), transitioning into True Dragons (winged), and finally taking on Imperial and Emperor Dragon forms characterized by literal or magical crowns of horn and flame.

> [!important] Conversion rule
> The species below retain the original side-story names and evolutionary relationships, but old Bloodline Rank labels have been removed. The listed V4 Tier is a **recommended usual evolution point**, not an absolute species power lock.

## Evolution relationships

- **Ashscale Drake** → **Emberclaw Drake**
- **Emberclaw Drake** → **Flamehorn Dragon**
- **Flamehorn Dragon** → **Magmaheart Dragon**
- **Flamehorn Dragon** → **Infernal Horn Dragon** — *Demonic Contamination*
- **Infernal Horn Dragon** → **Abyssflame Dragon**
- **Abyssflame Dragon** → **Abyssal Firestorm Dragon**
- **Magmaheart Dragon** → **Inferno Sovereign Dragon**
- **Inferno Sovereign Dragon** → **Imperial Flame Dragon**
- **Imperial Flame Dragon** → **Sunscale Imperial Dragon**
- **Sunscale Imperial Dragon** → **Celestial Solar Dragon**
- **Celestial Solar Dragon** → **Sun-Crowned Dragon Emperor**
- **Sun-Crowned Dragon Emperor** → **Dragon of the First Flame**

## V4 balancing notes

- All healthy true-Dragon root forms must be **Bronze Tier or higher**.
- Race evolution is conditional and may occur earlier/later than the recommendation.
- Personal Rank remains F→Divine and is independent of species.
- Conceptual apex forms listed as **Transcendent+** must be manually placed at Transcendent, Demigod or Divine depending on how literal their Authority becomes.
- A Dragonoid bloodline may inherit this family's affinities and transformation traits without being born in the corresponding beast-form species.
