# Races and Racial Growth

Race affects **where a being starts and how its body naturally develops**.

## Race can influence

- starting attributes
- typical birth Tier
- Rank distribution
- natural stat-growth distribution
- lifespan
- mana affinity/capacity
- physical characteristics
- racial skills
- resistances
- natural evolution paths
- compatibility with classes, bloodlines and Stars

## Example: humans vs Dragonoids and Dragons

Humans normally begin in **Ordinary** Tier.

Current draconic canon:

- Healthy true **Dragons hatch at Bronze Tier or above**.
- Most **Dragonoids are born around Beginner Bronze**, with degraded individuals sometimes beginning at Advanced Iron and high/ancient lineages beginning higher within Bronze.
- Dragons emphasize overwhelming racial vessel quality and instinctive magic.
- Dragonoids emphasize civilized spellcraft, Classes and humanoid efficiency while retaining draconic bloodline potential.

This does not mean Rank and Race are the same thing. A B-Rank Dragon can begin vastly stronger than an S-Rank Human because of racial baseline, while the S-Rank Human still possesses superior Personal Rank growth efficiency.

See [[Draconic Races - Index]].

## Racial growth bias

Two beings with the same Rank and Level can gain different attributes because their species naturally distributes growth differently.

Example concept only:

- Human: balanced/adaptable
- Dragonoid: higher STR/VIT bias, strong mana tolerance
- Elf: AGI/INT/mana-control bias
- Vampire: VIT/AGI/blood-related growth
- Dragon: extremely high base vessel quality and powerful racial growth
