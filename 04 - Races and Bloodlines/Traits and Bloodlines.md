# Traits and Bloodlines

## Traits

Traits are individual characteristics recognized by the System. They may be innate, earned, evolved or granted by extraordinary events.

Traits can modify:

- growth
- affinity
- resistance
- skill behavior
- resource absorption
- perception
- Tier breakthroughs
- Class evolution

Not every person needs a Trait.

## Special Bloodlines

Bloodlines are inherited or awakened lineage powers that can affect:

- starting stats/Tier
- Rank potential
- racial evolution
- affinities
- unique skills
- Mana
- Divinity
- Class options/evolution
- Law compatibility much later

The Status Window should display **Special Bloodlines** separately from Race.

This allows a character to remain Human while carrying unusual divine, draconic, demonic or other lineage components.
