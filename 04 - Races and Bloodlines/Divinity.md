# Divinity

Divinity is a higher-order stat/energy distinct from Mana.

## Born Divine vs born Demigod

**Born Divine:** possesses innate Divinity from birth. The Divinity stat exists from the beginning even if the being cannot yet fully use or understand it.

**Born Demigod:** possesses divine heritage/potential but does not automatically begin with the same innate active Divinity reserve as a true born Divine.

This distinction is important for Alok.

## Alok

Alok is a **Divine Child** and therefore his Status contains a **Divinity** stat from the beginning.

V1 used `Divinity: 5` in his earliest sheet; the rewrite can retain or rebalance the exact starting number later.

## Uses of Divinity

Divinity will eventually influence:

- divine abilities
- divine vessel development
- resistance to higher-order power
- Law interaction
- Godhood progression
- Ninth-Star transformation

## Ninth Star

A sufficient accumulation and successful integration of Divinity with Mana is required to transform the Eight-Star distributed mana vessel into a **Full God Vessel**.

See [[Stars and Mana Cores]].
