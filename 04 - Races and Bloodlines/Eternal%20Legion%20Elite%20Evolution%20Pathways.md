# Eternal Legion Elite Evolution Pathways

## Purpose

This note replaces the old **Level-range evolution table** with the current V4 progression model.

In the current system, elite Legion members progress through three separate but interacting systems:

1. **Personal Rank** — innate growth potential.
2. **Race Evolution** — what the being physically/spiritually becomes.
3. **Class Evolution** — combat role, specialized growth, and Skills.

**Tier**, not Level range, is the primary evolutionary milestone.

Level still matters for growth and mastery, but a unit does not automatically evolve simply because it reaches a certain Level.

---

# Core Evolution Rules

## 1. Tier is the major evolutionary gate

Current Tier ladder:

1. Ordinary
2. Iron
3. Bronze
4. Silver
5. Gold
6. Platinum
7. Diamond
8. Supreme
9. Noble
10. Baron
11. Count
12. Duke
13. King
14. Emperor
15. Sovereign
16. Transcendent
17. Demigod
18. Divine

A Legion member may qualify for Race or Class evolution when:

- approaching a Tier limit
- completing a breakthrough
- achieving sufficient Class mastery
- recovering soul identity
- fulfilling lineage conditions
- receiving Soul Evolve
- obtaining compatible materials
- completing major combat or conceptual achievements

---

## 2. Race and Class are separate

Example:

**Solis**

Race:
> Basic Skeleton → Bonebound Revenant → Fleshbound Revenant → Soulborn Human → Reborn Human

Class:
> Trainee Soldier → Soldier → Veteran Soldier → Knight → Soulbound Knight → Eternal Knight

These should never be merged into one progression label.

---

## 3. Restoration is part of Alok's growth

Early in **Sovereign of the Eternal Cycle**, Alok cannot fully reconstruct a soul.

His first major manifestations begin as skeletal vessels because:

- skeletons are cheaper to construct
- bones provide the minimum stable physical anchor
- incomplete Life Authority limits biological restoration
- Death Authority is initially easier for him to express than Life Authority

As Alok develops:

- Life Authority
- Death Authority
- Soul Authority
- Mana capacity
- Divinity
- Class mastery

his manifestations become more complete.

### Planned manifestation progression

**First elite summons**
- skeletal
- incomplete bodies
- gradual restoration

**Second generation**
- partially reconstructed immediately
- much faster restoration

**Third major elite onward**
- Alok becomes capable of summoning a sufficiently intact soul in a fully restored body from the beginning

Eventually skeletal manifestation becomes an **optional low-cost combat manifestation**, rather than a limitation.

# Recommended Main Elite Roster

The original seven are still useful, but the current system benefits from one dedicated monster/beast elite.

Recommended core elites:

1. **Serena** — Vampire / Blood Sovereign path
2. **Solis** — Human Knight / Legion Commander path
3. **Valeria** — Valkyrie / Celestial Warrior path
4. **Raven** — Soul Mage / Death Arcanist path
5. **Aria** — Fairy / Life-Fey path
6. **Aurelia** — Angel / Celestial Support-Commander path
7. **Drakhar** — Dragonoid / Dragon evolution path
8. **Fenriris** — Soulbeast / Apex Monster path

The eighth elite exists to ensure Alok's Legion is not primarily humanoid.

Monsters, beasts, dragons, spirits, demons, angels, undead, and living races should all be valid Legion members.

Raven should instead become a specialist in:

- soul magic
- death magic
- curses
- ritual magic
- battlefield spellcraft
- research

He may still have necromantic knowledge, but that should be only one part of his toolkit.

# Elite Manifestation Timeline

A useful long-term narrative structure:

## First Elite — Solis

Manifested as:

**Basic Skeleton**

Reason:

Alok has almost no restoration mastery.

---

## Second Elite — Serena

Manifested as:

**Skeleton / Bloodbound Skeleton**

But her body develops faster than Solis's because Alok has already learned from his first manifestation.

---

## Third Elite — Valeria

Manifested:

# **Fully Restored**

This should be an important Class milestone.

Alok expects bones to form.

Instead:

- Soul Anchor forms
- Mana circulates
- muscles appear
- organs form
- skin reconstructs
- hair and eyes restore
- clothing/armor manifests or must be equipped separately

Alok realizes his power has evolved from:

> "summoning undead"

into:

> **restoring beings from their souls**

---

# Later Manifestation Modes

Eventually Alok should deliberately gain multiple manifestation methods.

## Skeletal Manifestation

Cheapest.

Uses:

- emergency deployment
- weak souls
- mass soldiers
- low Mana situations

Advantages:

- low Mana cost
- fast
- simple

Disadvantages:

- weakest body
- incomplete restoration

---

## Partial Restoration

Moderate cost.

Uses:

- mid-level Legion troops
- beings expected to fight immediately

---

## Complete Restoration

Expensive.

Uses:

- elites
- important souls
- intelligent Legion members
- high-potential monsters

Produces:

- complete physical body
- restored biology
- proper Mana pathways
- correct Race structure

---

## Evolved Restoration

Very expensive.

Requires:

**Soul Evolve**

Alok does not merely restore what the soul was.

He reconstructs the body according to a valid superior evolutionary path.

Example:

**Ironfang Wolf soul**

could become:

> Dire Mana Wolf

rather than returning as an ordinary Ironfang Wolf.

---

# Soul Evolve — Two Fundamental Choices

Alok should eventually discover two major uses.

## Restoration Path

> **Become what you were.**

Reconstructs:

- original Race
- original body
- original biological traits
- original compatible Skills

---

## Evolution Path

> **Become what you could be.**

Uses:

- soul potential
- materials
- Mana
- Divinity
- Class compatibility
- evolutionary lineage

to reconstruct the being along a superior path.

This is one of the defining powers of the Eternal Cycle.

---

# Class Evolution Rules for All Legion Members

Class evolution should depend on behavior.

A Skeleton Soldier who spends years defending allies may evolve toward:

> Guardian → Bastion Knight

rather than:

> Veteran Soldier → Knight

A wolf that becomes Alok's primary scout may evolve toward:

> Shadow Hunter

rather than:

> Apex Predator

A Valkyrie that becomes a commander may evolve toward:

> Valkyrie Marshal

while another Valkyrie focused entirely on dueling could evolve toward:

> Heavenpiercer.

Therefore the tables in this note are **recommended primary paths**, not immutable trees.

---

# High-Tier Philosophy

At very high Tiers, generic Class names should gradually disappear.

By:

- Sovereign
- Transcendent
- Demigod
- Divine

the strongest elites should begin developing **individualized Classes and Race evolutions** based on:

- personality
- history
- achievements
- soul identity
- relationship with Alok
- conceptual comprehension
- Legion role

For example:

Solis should not necessarily end as merely:

> Divine Paragon Knight

His eventual Divine Class may become something unique such as:

> **First Sword of the Eternal Sovereign**

or:

> **Oathkeeper at the End of the Cycle**

The same principle should apply to every major elite.

# Design Goal

Alok's Eternal Legion should eventually feel less like:

> an undead army

and more like:

> **a civilization of restored and evolving beings gathered under the Sovereign of the Eternal Cycle.**

It can contain:

- humans
- undead
- vampires
- spirits
- fairies
- angels
- demons
- dragons
- magical beasts
- monsters
- artificial life
- reborn souls
- entirely new Life/Death hybrid species

That breadth is what makes Alok fundamentally different from a conventional necromancer.
