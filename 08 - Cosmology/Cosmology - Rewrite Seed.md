# Cosmology - Rewrite Seed

The legacy cosmology is preserved in `99 - Legacy Reference`, but the rewrite should rebuild it into one coherent structure.

## Concepts worth preserving

- Life and Death as foundational/primal divine forces connected to Alok's origin
- multiple pantheons/cultures interacting with genuine higher beings
- gods with fragments/domains of larger concepts
- demons/devils as an ancient race rather than automatically evil creatures
- dragons and other high races able to rival divine beings
- the Tower as a neutral or supra-factional testing/progression structure

## Rewrite direction

Do not treat the old list of mythological names as fully locked canon. The cosmology should explain **why multiple pantheons exist, what their relationship is to Laws/Authorities, and how primal/fundamental concepts differ from ordinary divine domains.**
