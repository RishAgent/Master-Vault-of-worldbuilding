# Aeonmorta Forces — Military Structure

> [!summary]
> The **Aeonmorta Alliance** is the vast political and military order centered on the **Four Supreme Empires** tied to House Aeonmorta.
>
> Its military hierarchy uses **Tier** as the minimum public measure of realized power.
>
> Military title, Tier, Race, Star Rating, Class, and racial evolution are separate systems.

# 1. Core Political Structure

The Aeonmorta Alliance is not limited to the four Supreme Empires themselves.

It includes:

- the **Four Supreme Empires**
- subordinate empires
- vassal kingdoms
- allied royal houses
- sworn military orders
- noble territories
- Dragon and Dragonoid clans
- Angelic domains
- Valkyrie houses
- Demon and Demonoid states
- other civilizations formally placed beneath the authority or protection of the Supreme Empires

The Four Supreme Empires sit at the top of this structure.

Their authority reaches down through multiple layers of subordinate civilizations.

Because of this, restricted imperial law—especially law involving **direct royal arts, royal bloodlines, imperial inheritances, throne authority, or Aeonmorta property**—may obligate vassal forces to intervene even when no Supreme Empire is physically present.

This is why an apparent outsider publicly using a direct royal art such as **Graceful Strike** can trigger arrest, detention, or imperial investigation.

# 2. Core Military Principle

> **Tier = realized power.**
>
> **Military Rank = institutional authority.**
>
> **Race / Evolution = what the being is.**
>
> **Star Rating = Mana-Core architecture.**
>
> **Class = growth and specialization framework.**

None of these should be treated as interchangeable.

A powerful being does not automatically hold a high military office.

Likewise, a decorated officer may be stronger than the minimum Tier required for their title.

# 3. Aeonmorta Military Rank Baselines

These are **minimum Tier requirements**, not exact power caps.

| Military Standing | Minimum Tier | General Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| **Imperial Soldier** | Gold | Professional soldier of a Supreme Empire or major directly controlled force |
| **Elite Imperial Soldier** | Platinum | Veteran, specialist, royal guard candidate, elite formation member |
| **Imperial Knight** | Diamond | Formally knighted combatant trusted with independent combat authority |
| **Aeonmorta Knight** | **Supreme** | Alliance-recognized knight permitted to act under the authority of the Four Supreme Empires |
| **High Knight** | **Noble** | Senior knight; can command elite Knight formations and important operations |
| **Grand Knight** | **Baron** | Major imperial military figure capable of commanding armies, territories, or strategic forces |
| **Knight Commander** | **Count** | Commands large formations and multiple Grand Knights |
| **Imperial General** | **Duke** | Continental / kingdom-scale military authority |
| **Grand Marshal** | **King** | One of the highest active military commanders of a Supreme Empire |
| **Throne Guardian / Supreme Marshal** | **Emperor+** | Direct strategic servant of a Supreme ruler; exceptionally rare |

# 4. Important Rule — Minimum Does Not Mean Maximum

Military rank is not automatically granted by Tier.

Examples:

- A **Supreme Tier** warrior is strong enough to qualify for consideration as an Aeonmorta Knight, but is not automatically one.
- A **Baron Tier** being may never have served in the military at all.
- A veteran Grand Knight may be **Count Tier** or even **Duke Tier** while retaining the title because military appointment and personal power are separate.
- A King Tier Dragon could be a clan elder, sovereign ruler, scholar, independent climber, or Grand Marshal depending on role and recognition.

Formal military advancement may require:

- Tier qualification
- loyalty oaths
- imperial recognition
- command ability
- military achievements
- strategic competence
- service history
- legal standing
- factional approval
- specialized trials or examinations

# 5. Why Aeonmorta Knight Matters

The title **Aeonmorta Knight** is not a casual designation.

Minimum requirement:

# Supreme Tier

This means an Aeonmorta Knight is already an extremely powerful existence by ordinary Tower standards.

They are expected to be capable of:

- independent imperial missions
- representing Aeonmorta authority
- enforcing alliance law
- commanding lesser imperial soldiers
- protecting high-value nobles and imperial assets
- acting as battlefield anchors
- operating across hostile worlds and Tower regions

The title carries both military and political authority.

# 6. Draconic Members of the Aeonmorta Forces

Dragons and Dragonoids are sibling branches of the same ancient draconic ancestry.

They are not treated as:

- pet and owner
- beast and rider by default
- servant and master by default

Advanced draconic civilization recognizes Dragons and Dragonoids as:

- citizens
- soldiers
- officers
- nobles
- bonded partners
- clan elders
- rulers
- military commanders
- independent Tower climbers

## Draconic Convergence

At **Platinum Tier**, Dragon and Dragonoid development begins converging.

At **Diamond Tier**, Draconic Ascension normally completes.

From Diamond onward, Dragon-origin and Dragonoid-origin become primarily **origin designations**, because both can possess:

- humanoid forms
- true draconic forms
- advanced spellcraft
- Class compatibility
- powerful bloodline abilities
- Dragon Authority
- complete draconic manifestations

# 7. Mixed Draconic Military Structure

The Supreme Dragon Empire may contain military units composed of:

- Dragonoid-origin Ascendants
- Dragon-origin Ascendants
- full-form Dragons
- humanoid-form Dragons
- bonded Dragon–Dragonoid combat pairs
- independent Dragon officers
- Dragonoid commanders
- Dragon commanders
- mixed-race imperial formations

A Dragon may command Dragonoids.

A Dragonoid may command Dragons.

A bonded pair may share battlefield authority.

Military rank follows **institutional office and merit**, not species ownership.

# 8. Stars and Military Rank

Star Rating is **not** an automatic military qualification.

A Supreme Tier martial warrior may have relatively undeveloped Star architecture if they primarily use:

- Aura
- Qi
- martial force
- bloodline power
- physical techniques
- Laws

A Supreme Tier mage may possess a much higher Star Rating.

Therefore:

- Aeonmorta Knight does **not** mean a fixed Star count.
- Grand Knight does **not** mean a fixed Star count.
- Imperial General does **not** mean a fixed Star count.

Mage-specific offices may later have separate Star requirements.

# 12. Restricted Royal Arts and Enforcement

Direct royal inheritances of the Four Supreme Empires are politically protected.

Examples include:

- Graceful Strike
- other Aeonmorta royal techniques
- restricted imperial bloodline arts
- throne-authorized martial systems
- specific inherited Laws or royal authorities

Unauthorized public use may trigger:

- detention
- confiscation attempts
- identity verification
- investigation
- arrest
- transfer to a Supreme Empire court
- military intervention

Vassal kingdoms and subordinate empires may consider enforcement their duty under alliance law.

This creates the Floor Zero misunderstanding:

Alok uses **Graceful Strike**.

Vassal representatives assume:

- he stole it
- killed or robbed an authorized wielder
- illegally inherited restricted royal knowledge

They attempt to detain him.

Zeyria recognizes that the opposite is true:

> Alok is the lost Aeonmorta prince and therefore has direct birthright to the art.

# 13. House Aeonmorta and the Four Supreme Empires

House Aeonmorta stands at the center of the alliance created by the four Supreme Empires.

Alok is tied by blood to all four supreme ruling lines.

This means that once his identity becomes public:

- the military does not treat him as a random noble
- vassal rulers must reevaluate prior actions toward him
- imperial forces gain a legitimate basis to protect him
- use of royal techniques becomes lawful by birthright
- his existence carries enormous political weight
- attacks against him may be interpreted as attacks against the ruling bloodline itself

# 14. Design Rule for Future Army Scenes

When writing high-level Aeonmorta forces:

- use **Tier**, not Level, as the main public power indicator
- avoid numbered racial “tiers” such as Fourth-Tier Dragonoid
- use actual racial/evolutionary terminology
- keep Stars separate unless the character is specifically a mage
- preserve military rank as an institutional title
- remember rank minimums are floors, not caps
- Dragons can be officers in their own right
- Dragonoids and Dragons may both occupy the same military titles
- Baron/Count/Duke/King should feel qualitatively monstrous compared with current Silver characters
- Emperor+ military figures should remain extremely rare and politically significant
