# Bronze-to-Gold Refinement System
## Cores, Crystals, Vessel Saturation & Breakthroughs

> [!summary]
> **Bronze Tier introduces formal Vessel Refinement.**
>
> At Bronze, Levels and EXP still matter strongly, so progression uses a **hybrid framework**:
>
> **Levels + Attributes + Refinement + breakthrough resources**
>
> At **Silver Tier**, the balance changes.
>
> Ordinary Level-based growth ceases to be the primary higher-progression engine and **Refinement becomes the dominant advancement framework**.
>
> From Bronze onward, monster Cores, Mana Crystals, monster organs, blood, marrow, elemental glands, rare essences, Gate resources, and alchemical preparations become increasingly important because they directly develop the vessel toward its next substage.
>
> **Standard safe daily refinement throughput is capped at 20 standard Core-loads for the Physical Vessel and 20 standard Mana-Crystal-loads for the Mana Core.**
>
> These are separate parallel limits. Continuous access to the full daily limit is exceptional; resource scarcity, compatibility, recovery, travel, expeditions, breakthrough preparation, and Tier Tempering are what stretch higher progression across years, decades, and centuries.
>
> This note currently defines the system from **Beginner Bronze through Peak Gold** and deliberately leaves Platinum+ open for later development.

# 1. Progression Phases

## Ordinary and Iron — Level-Dominant Growth

Progression is primarily represented by:

- EXP
- Levels
- Attributes
- Classes
- training
- Skills
- Stars
- advancement materials

Cores and Crystals may still be consumed or processed, but they behave mainly as:

- permanent stat consumables
- Mana supplements
- breakthrough ingredients
- alchemical resources

There is no formal visible Vessel Refinement percentage yet.

---

## Bronze — Hybrid Progression

Upon reaching **Beginner Bronze**, the System recognizes that simple Level growth is no longer sufficient to represent the full development of the vessel.

Bronze therefore unlocks:

- Physical Vessel Saturation
- Mana-Core Saturation
- percentage-based Core absorption
- percentage-based Crystal absorption
- refinement-quality evaluation

At Bronze:

> **Levels are still important.**
>
> **Refinement begins filling the gaps Levels cannot efficiently cover.**

This is the stage relevant to many of the Crawfords.

From Silver onward:

> **Tier development is measured primarily through Refinement rather than Level.**

Levels may still exist internally as:

- legacy progression records
- Trial-generation history
- Class records
- special System rewards

but the story no longer needs to treat Level as the main advancement engine.

# 2. Why Refinement Begins at Bronze

Bronze is the first Tier where the vessel becomes developed enough that raw accumulation alone begins to produce diminishing returns.

From Bronze onward, advancement increasingly requires:

- denser Mana
- stronger biological structures
- reinforced Mana channels
- better Core stability
- higher-quality monster essence
- more complete physical reconstruction

Bronze therefore serves as the transitional Tier between:

> **Level-driven growth**

and:

> **resource-driven vessel refinement**

# 4. Overall Stage Completion

The weaker track limits total stage completion.

Example:

```text
[ ADVANCED BRONZE REFINEMENT ]

Physical Vessel:
81.60%

Mana Core:
93.40%

Overall Stage Completion:
81.60%

Status:
UNSATURATED
```

Rule:

# Overall Saturation = lower of the two primary tracks

Therefore:

- 100% Mana Core does not compensate for an incomplete physical vessel;
- 100% Physical Vessel does not compensate for an incomplete Mana Core.

Both tracks must reach saturation before normal breakthrough preparation can begin.

# 7. Core Tier-Scaling Rule

Higher Tiers require increasingly more refinement resources.

The current working major-Tier relationship is:

> **Bronze base efficiency → Silver at roughly one-third → Gold at roughly one-third again**

This allows the system to slow naturally as lifespan expands.

The exact multiplier does not need to remain mathematically fixed at Platinum+.

# 11. Refinement Resistance

The closer a vessel approaches saturation, the harder it becomes to force additional permanent improvement into the same structure.

| Current Saturation | Effective Absorption |
|---|---:|
| **0–50%** | **100%** |
| **50–75%** | **90%** |
| **75–90%** | **75%** |
| **90–99%** | **60%** |
| **99–100%** | **40%** |

This creates long-term bottlenecks at values such as:

- 96%
- 98%
- 99.2%
- 99.8%

The final fraction is intentionally difficult.

# 12A. Safe Daily Refinement Throughput

Refinement is not limited only by how many resources a cultivator owns.

The body and Mana Core can safely process only so much permanent structural change in one day.

## Standard Safe Daily Limit

For a normal Bronze+ cultivator using standard-quality, same-substage resources:

# **Physical Vessel: up to 20 standard Core-loads per day**

# **Mana Core: up to 20 standard Mana-Crystal-loads per day**

These are **separate parallel limits**.

A cultivator may therefore safely process, in the same day:

- up to 20 standard Core-loads toward Physical Vessel Saturation;
- up to 20 standard Mana-Crystal-loads toward Mana-Core Saturation.

This does **not** mean 40 resources contribute to one track.

The two tracks develop independently, and overall progression remains limited by the weaker track.

## Load Equivalents

The daily rule is measured in **standard refinement-load equivalents**, not merely literal item count.

A normal same-substage, standard-quality Core or Mana Crystal counts as approximately:

# **1 load**

More powerful materials consume more of the day's safe processing capacity.

Working guideline:

| Resource | Approximate Daily Load Cost |
|---|---:|
| Damaged / weak resource | 0.25–0.50 load |
| Standard same-substage resource | 1 load |
| High-Purity resource | ~1.5 loads |
| Pristine resource | ~2 loads |
| Boss-grade resource | ~5 loads |
| Exceptional / Unique resource | variable |
| Higher-substage resource | determined by power, preparation, and safety |

This prevents a cultivator from bypassing the daily limit simply by replacing twenty ordinary resources with twenty enormously stronger ones.

## Why the Limit Exists

Permanent refinement reconstructs:

- muscles;
- bones;
- organs;
- blood;
- connective tissue;
- Mana channels;
- the Mana Core;
- circulation pathways;
- Mana density;
- recovery structures;
- racial vessel architecture.

Too much permanent change in too little time creates structural instability.

Exceeding the safe daily load may cause:

- refinement fever;
- internal bleeding;
- Mana-channel inflammation;
- organ stress;
- Core instability;
- temporary affinity contamination;
- loss of absorption efficiency;
- permanent vessel damage;
- failed breakthroughs;
- death in severe cases.

## Going Above 20 / 20

The 20 + 20 rule is the **normal safe baseline**, not an absolute cosmic law.

Exceptional methods may temporarily raise it:

- specialized advancement arrays;
- alchemical processing;
- healers;
- racial advantages;
- regeneration;
- body-reconstruction Skills;
- superior Classes;
- Divine or bloodline effects;
- carefully diluted higher-grade resources;
- protected advancement chambers.

However:

> **Higher throughput requires proportionally greater stabilization.**

Alok and similarly abnormal beings may exceed ordinary limits, but even they cannot process unlimited permanent growth without consequence.

## Availability Is Usually the Real Bottleneck

The daily cap represents **how fast a cultivator can safely process resources if those resources already exist**.

Most cultivators do not possess enough compatible Cores and Mana Crystals to sustain 20 / 20 every day.

A normal adventurer may refine heavily for only a few days after an expedition.

A major House may maintain continuous refinement for weeks or months from stockpiles.

A Gold cultivator may possess enormous wealth yet go months or years without obtaining enough useful Gold-grade resources.

Therefore:

> **20 / 20 is a throughput ceiling.**

> **It is not assumed daily availability.**

This distinction is essential to long-term progression balance.

# 13. Lower-Grade Resource Penalties

Resource effectiveness depends on its strength relative to the user's current substage.

| Resource Relative to User | Multiplier |
|---|---:|
| Same substage | **×1.00** |
| 1 substage lower | **×0.10** |
| 2 substages lower | **×0.025** |
| 3 substages lower | **×0.005** |
| 1 full Tier lower | **×0.001** |
| 2+ full Tiers lower | **negligible / effectively zero** |

This prevents high-Tier beings from advancing efficiently by farming weak monsters forever.

| 3 substages higher | **×10** |

# 39. Relationship to Lifespan

Refinement is intentionally slow.

Higher Tiers grant the lifespan necessary to sustain that slower advancement.

The design principle is:

> **Tier grants lifespan.**
>
> **Lifespan grants time to refine the next vessel.**
>
> **Refinement grants access to the next Tier.**
>
> **The next Tier grants even greater lifespan.**

Bronze development may take years or decades.

Silver development may take decades.

Gold development may take centuries.

Higher Tiers expand into vastly longer timeframes.

# 39A. Refinement Time Framework

The daily-load rule creates a hard mechanical floor beneath progression time.

The practical same-stage resource-equivalent requirement can be divided by the normal safe processing rate:

# **Minimum processing days = Practical Standard Equivalents ÷ 20**

Because Physical Vessel and Mana-Core resources may be processed in parallel, the time is **not** divided by 40.

Both tracks must independently reach 100%.

The table below assumes:

- standard-quality same-substage resources;
- both Physical and Mana tracks receive sufficient resources every day;
- no missed days;
- no injuries;
- no supply shortages;
- no expedition downtime;
- no failed absorption;
- no breakthrough delay;
- no Tier Tempering delay.

Therefore the "Continuous 20/day Floor" is an **absolute idealized processing floor**, not a normal real-world advancement speed.

## How to Read the Calendar Ranges

The working calendar ranges are **generic balancing references**, not age requirements and not guarantees.

They assume a successful cultivator who continues making meaningful progress but does **not** have perfect uninterrupted access to suitable resources.

Real calendar time includes:

- locating compatible monsters;
- Gate access;
- expedition organization;
- travel;
- injuries and recovery;
- resource extraction;
- alchemical processing;
- Mana-Crystal scarcity;
- stockpile depletion;
- competition for rare materials;
- refinement stabilization;
- Merit requirements;
- breakthrough catalysts;
- failed breakthrough attempts;
- Tier Tempering;
- reconstruction.

A heavily resourced noble, royal prodigy, exceptional Class-holder, or major expedition leader may progress far faster than the working range.

A resource-poor cultivator may take far longer or stall permanently.

## Tier-Level Interpretation

A useful broad reading is:

| Tier | Mechanical Best-Case Meaning | Normal Story-Scale Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | individual substages can theoretically be processed in months to a few years if fully supplied | **years to decades** because suitable resources are not continuously available |
| Silver | continuous full supply still needs years per substage | **decades** because Silver progression is expedition-scale |
| Gold | even perfect full-cap processing requires many years | **centuries** because Gold resources, campaigns, stabilization, and breakthroughs are strategic-scale |

This preserves the central progression rule:

> **The body sets the maximum safe processing speed.**

> **The world sets how often that maximum can actually be used.**

## Major-Tier Breakthrough Time Is Separate

The tables above distinguish **saturation** from **advancement**.

Reaching:

- Physical Vessel 100%;
- Mana Core 100%;

does not mean immediate promotion.

Peak Bronze → Beginner Silver, Peak Silver → Beginner Gold, and Peak Gold → Beginner Platinum require Tier Tempering and full reconstruction.

Tier-breakthrough preparation may itself take years or decades because the cultivator needs materials that ordinary refinement cannot substitute for.

Thus a person may remain:

- Saturated Peak Bronze;
- Saturated Peak Silver;
- Saturated Peak Gold;

for a very long period despite being unable to gain meaningful ordinary saturation.

This is not wasted time.

It is the period in which the next vessel is prepared.

# 41. Design Rules Going Forward

The system should preserve these principles:

1. One Gate should not normally complete a substage.
2. Bronze is a Level + Refinement hybrid Tier.
3. Silver marks the shift to Refinement-dominant progression.
4. Later substages take longer than earlier substages.
5. Higher Tiers take substantially longer than lower Tiers.
6. Lower-grade resources rapidly lose usefulness.
7. Higher-grade resources are valuable but dangerous.
8. Both Physical Vessel and Mana Core must be developed.
9. Permanent Attributes contribute to saturation.
10. Equipment and temporary buffs do not.
11. Resources do not replace Merit or breakthrough qualification.
12. Major Tier breakthroughs require Tier Tempering and reconstruction.
13. Cores, Crystals, monster materials, and Gates must remain economically important.
14. The framework must naturally support years, decades, centuries, and eventually far longer progression.
15. Normal safe daily processing is **20 standard Core-loads + 20 standard Mana-Crystal-loads**.
16. The two daily limits are separate and may run in parallel.
17. Higher-quality or higher-power resources consume proportionally greater daily refinement load.
18. The 20 / 20 limit is a safe processing ceiling, not assumed daily resource availability.
19. Saturation time and breakthrough time are separate.
20. Major Tier breakthroughs may remain blocked for years or decades after 100% saturation.
