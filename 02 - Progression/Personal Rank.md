# Personal Rank

**Personal Rank measures growth potential and growth efficiency. It is not a power Tier.**

A person normally identifies current strength by Tier (for example, *Advanced Elite* or *Peak King*), not by saying they are "S-Rank strength."

## Current ladder

| Rank | General meaning |
|---|---|
| **F** | Deficient growth potential |
| **E** | Low |
| **D** | Below average |
| **C** | Average baseline |
| **B** | Talented |
| **A** | Exceptional |
| **S** | Genius |
| **SS** | Extraordinary |
| **SSS** | Monstrous / legendary potential |

## What Rank influences

- Tier-appropriate stat growth per Level
- Training efficiency
- Mana growth efficiency
- Ease/speed of advancing through Tier substages
- Ease of overcoming Tier bottlenecks
- Skill-development speed
- Potentially Star progression efficiency
- How efficiently the body converts resources and opportunities into permanent growth

## What Rank does NOT mean

- It does not directly state current combat strength.
- It does not determine current Tier.
- It does not impose a hard maximum Tier.
- A low-Rank veteran can be far stronger **right now** than a young high-Rank prodigy.

## Growth model — current direction

Use **Tier-scaled growth multiplied/modified by Personal Rank**, rather than a single fixed `+X stats per level` forever.

Conceptually:

`Tier Base Growth × Rank Efficiency × Race/Class/Trait influences + other gains`

This keeps Rank relevant when stats grow from tens to billions and beyond.

> [!warning] TBD
> Exact Rank multipliers are intentionally not locked yet. They must be balanced against the Tier table, leveling speed, racial baselines and class bonuses.

## Rank and Race

Races have different **Rank distributions**, not different definitions of Rank. An A-Rank human and an A-Rank Dragonoid both have A-grade growth potential, but the Dragonoid may begin at a much higher Tier and possess superior racial growth in physical attributes.

Rank can potentially improve through exceptional evolution, bloodline awakening, divine intervention, perfect breakthroughs or similarly rare events, but it should never be easy to grind upward.
