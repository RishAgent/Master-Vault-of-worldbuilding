# Stars and Mana Cores

Star Rating measures the **development and architecture of a being's mana system**, not their overall Tier.

## Starless

A Starless being has no completed mana circle. Magic must be gathered, shaped and produced directly through the body.

Consequences:

- inefficient mana use
- lower throughput
- greater bodily strain
- fewer consecutive spells
- limited complexity

A Starless fire user can still cast a basic **Fireball**, but doing so is inefficient.

## One Star and above

When the mana core crosses its threshold, it gains a circle and the being becomes One Star.

The circle acts as a dedicated mana-processing structure. Even when casting the **same Fireball**, a One-Star caster can cast more of them, faster and at lower mana cost than a Starless caster.

Additional circles improve:

- mana efficiency
- storage/circulation
- spell formation speed
- throughput
- stability
- simultaneous mana operations
- the complexity of magic that can be sustained

### Example spell architecture

- **Fireball — Zero Star:** basic gather → ignite → launch structure
- **Fire Blast — One Star:** higher throughput/compression and controlled release
- **Fire Bind — Two Star:** continuous manifestation, shaping and target restraint/tracking

A higher-Star caster can still use lower-Star magic; it simply becomes cheaper and easier relative to their evolved mana system.

## Stars One through Seven

Stars 1–7 develop the conventional **core + circles** architecture. Each threshold adds/refines circles and raises the level of magic the being can stably process.

## Eight Star — Body Core

Eight Star is fundamentally different from Seven Star.

At Eight Star, the old centralized mana-core architecture reaches a limit. The breakthrough transforms the **entire body into a vast distributed mana core**.

- every cell functions as part of the core architecture
- the body becomes a mana-processing/storage structure
- mana efficiency and throughput change qualitatively
- the vessel must be extraordinarily strong to survive this state

This transformation is one of the major requirements on the road to Godhood.

## Ninth Star — God Vessel

Ninth Star is another qualitative transformation rather than simply another circle.

The being must accumulate sufficient **Divinity** and successfully merge Mana and Divinity throughout the Eight-Star distributed core/body.

Successful completion creates a **Full God Vessel**.

This is why a being can possess divine power or even profound Law comprehension and still be only a **Partial God** if the Ninth-Star transformation has not been completed.

## Relationship to Tier

Stars and Tiers interact but do not replace one another.

- **Tier** = strength/quality of the vessel
- **Star** = mana architecture supported by that vessel

A stronger vessel can withstand more advanced mana architecture, while Star breakthroughs can themselves force dramatic vessel evolution.
