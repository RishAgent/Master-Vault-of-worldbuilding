# Silver-to-Gold Refinement System
## Cores, Crystals, Vessel Saturation & Breakthroughs

> [!summary]
> Beginning at **Silver Tier**, ordinary Level-based growth stops being the primary progression framework.
>
> The System reclassifies higher progression around:
>
> - **Physical Vessel Saturation**
> - **Mana-Core Saturation**
> - Cores
> - Mana Crystals
> - monster materials
> - essences
> - Stars
> - Class mastery
> - racial / bloodline development
> - Merit / qualification
> - breakthrough resources
>
> Silver is the point where raw EXP and Level gains are no longer enough to meaningfully develop the vessel.
>
> From this stage onward, a combatant must **refine the body and Mana Core directly** using compatible higher-Tier resources.
>
> This is one of the main reasons monster Cores, Mana Crystals, organs, blood, bones, marrow, glands, hides, essences, and Gate resources are so valuable.

---

# 1. Core Progression Shift at Silver

## Ordinary through Bronze

Before Silver, progression is primarily represented through:

- Levels
- EXP
- Class growth
- Attributes
- training
- Skills
- Stars
- breakthrough materials

Tier advancement still requires proper vessel development, but Levels remain one of the dominant visible progression engines.

---

## Silver and Above

Upon reaching **Beginner Silver**, the System unlocks the Higher Refinement Framework.

Working System message:

```text
[ HIGHER PROGRESSION FRAMEWORK UNLOCKED ]

Current Tier:
SILVER

Level-Based Primary Progression:
COMPLETE

New Primary Progression:
VESSEL REFINEMENT

Physical Vessel Saturation:
UNLOCKED

Mana-Core Saturation:
UNLOCKED

Higher-Tier Resource Refinement:
UNLOCKED
```

From this point onward:

> **Tier development is measured primarily through Refinement rather than Level.**

Levels may still exist internally as legacy / System history, Class records, Trial-generation records, or special progression data.

However:

- ordinary Level gains are no longer the main source of higher-Tier growth;
- the story does not need to manually track every higher Level;
- Silver+ advancement is governed by Refinement, saturation, resources, and breakthrough requirements.

---

# 2. Alok Transition Rule

Alok entered Silver while still carrying a Trial-generation Level record.

Current legacy record:

- Level 48
- Beginner Silver

This does **not** need to be erased.

Instead:

> Alok's Level becomes a legacy / historical progression record while his active Silver development is measured through Refinement.

His current Attributes can be used to calculate his starting Silver saturation.

This preserves all previous Level-based progression without requiring the higher system to continue scaling forever through only EXP.

- connective tissue

- racial body quality
- durability

# Monster Cores

- INT

- compression
- Mana-channel quality

# Mana Crystals

# 4. Overall Stage Completion

The weaker track limits total stage completion.

Example:

```text
[ BEGINNER SILVER REFINEMENT ]

Physical Vessel:
72.40%

Mana Core:
96.10%

Overall Stage Completion:
72.40%

Status:
UNSATURATED
```

Rule:

# Overall Saturation = lower of the two primary tracks

Therefore:

- 100% Mana Core does not compensate for an incomplete body;
- 100% Physical Vessel does not compensate for an incomplete Mana Core.

Both must be complete before normal breakthrough preparation can begin.

---

# 5. Why Higher-Tier Progression Uses Percentage Refinement

Raw Attribute values increase too rapidly for a permanent flat resource bonus to remain useful.

Example Tier benchmarks:

| Stage | Benchmark |
|---|---:|
| Beginner Silver | 2,500 |
| Intermediate Silver | 4,500 |
| Advanced Silver | 8,000 |
| Peak Silver | 15,000 |
| Beginner Gold | 25,000 |
| Intermediate Gold | 45,000 |
| Advanced Gold | 80,000 |
| Peak Gold | 150,000 |
| Beginner Platinum | 250,000 |

Therefore resource absorption is measured as:

# a percentage of the current substage's required refinement

rather than a fixed universal Attribute number.

This allows the same system to scale naturally upward.

# 7. Refinement Resistance

The more complete a vessel becomes, the harder it is to force additional permanent improvement into the same substage.

## Absorption Efficiency

| Current Saturation | Effective Absorption |
|---|---:|
| **0–50%** | **100%** |
| **50–75%** | **90%** |
| **75–90%** | **75%** |
| **90–99%** | **60%** |
| **99–100%** | **40%** |

This creates the common phenomenon of high-Tier combatants remaining stuck for years at:

- 96%
- 98%
- 99.2%
- 99.8%

The final fraction of saturation is deliberately difficult.

# 9. Relative Resource Strength

Resource effectiveness depends on how its substage compares with the user's current substage.

## Lower-Grade Resources

| Resource Relative to User | Multiplier |
|---|---:|
| Same substage | **×1.00** |
| 1 substage lower | **×0.10** |
| 2 substages lower | **×0.025** |
| 3 substages lower | **×0.005** |
| 1 full Tier lower | **×0.001** |
| 2+ full Tiers lower | **negligible / effectively zero** |

This prevents powerful beings from progressing efficiently by slaughtering weak monsters indefinitely.

---

# 10. Higher-Grade Resources

| Resource Relative to User | Multiplier |
|---|---:|

| 1 substage higher | **×2.5** |
| 2 substages higher | **×5** |
| 3 substages higher | **×10** |
| 1 full Tier higher | **×25** |

Example for a Beginner Silver cultivator:

| Resource | Base Completion |
|---|---:|

# 11. Absorption Danger

A resource can contain more power than the current vessel can safely refine.

Possible consequences:

- ruptured Mana channels
- organ failure
- vessel tearing
- Core cracking
- uncontrolled Mana discharge
- affinity contamination
- bloodline contamination
- failed forced advancement
- permanent injury
- death

Most cultivators safely use:

- same-stage resources,
- carefully prepared resources one substage higher.

Stronger materials often require:

- alchemical dilution
- formation arrays
- healers
- staged refinement
- specialized techniques
- racial compatibility
- long recovery periods

Alok can safely tolerate more than an ordinary cultivator because of:

- Divine Regeneration
- Three-Star architecture
- Divine Personal Rank
- Aeonmorta bloodline
- Sovereign of Eternal Cycle
- Serena's alchemy
- abnormal Mana control

But even Alok cannot ignore unlimited refinement pressure.

---

# 12. Resource Quality

Substage determines the amount of contained power.

Quality determines how much of that power is usable.

Working multipliers:

| Resource Quality | Multiplier |
|---|---:|
| Damaged / Impure | **×0.25–0.50** |
| Standard | **×1.00** |
| High Purity | **×1.50** |
| Pristine | **×2.00** |
| Boss-grade | **×5.00** |
| Exceptional / Unique | **variable — case by case** |

A Boss Core is therefore valuable not merely because of Tier but because its essence is denser, cleaner, and structurally superior.

# 13. Refinement-Grade Core Drop Rule

Not every slain monster provides a complete directly absorbable Core.

Many monsters may contain:

- damaged cores
- immature cores
- fragmented cores
- unstable Mana organs
- low-purity essence
- useful biological materials

Only a minority provide **full refinement-grade Cores**.

## Working Direct-Core Yield

For ordinary monsters:

# **~1–3%**

may produce a complete, directly usable refinement-grade Core.

This percentage varies by:

- species
- Tier
- age
- evolution
- Mana density
- method of death
- extraction quality
- Core anatomy

Some naturally core-dominant species may have much higher rates.

Some species may almost never produce a clean Core.

# 15. Mana Crystals

Mana Crystals are not assumed to exist inside every monster.

They may be obtained from:

- Gate deposits
- Mana veins
- crystal caverns
- crystallized monster organs
- elemental nodes
- Boss territories
- mineable resources
- treasure zones
- specialized species

Their primary use is:

# Mana-Core Saturation

They are therefore one of the most strategically important resources in high-Tier civilization.

# 16. Gate Balance

High-Tier Gates contain enormous monster populations.

Typical populations include approximately:

- Bronze Gate: **5,000–10,000**
- Silver Gate: **25,000–50,000**
- Gold Gate: **100,000–250,000**

This does **not** mean one combatant can clear one Gate and instantly finish a Tier.

Reasons:

1. only a small percentage produce full refinement-grade Cores;
2. loot is normally divided among many raiders;
3. Mana Crystals are separate environmental resources;
4. absorption takes time;
5. Refinement Resistance reduces late-stage efficiency;
6. strong resources may be unsafe;
7. both Physical and Mana-Core tracks must be completed;
8. breakthrough qualifications still remain.

---

# 17. Silver Gate Example

Typical Silver Gate midpoint:

# **~37,500 monsters**

If approximately **1%** produce full refinement-grade Cores:

# **~375 usable Cores**

before:

- Boss drops
- special species
- regional elites
- damaged extraction
- loot division

For a Beginner Silver cultivator, if every one of those Cores were perfectly compatible Beginner-Silver resources:

375 × 0.01%

=

# **3.75% Physical Refinement**

A real Gate contains mixed substages, so some Cores may be more valuable.

However, normal expeditions also contain:

# **100–300 raiders**

Therefore an ordinary participant may receive only a small fraction of the Gate's total resources.

A single Silver Gate should normally produce:

> meaningful progress

not:

> an entire substage.

---

# 18. Exceptional Gate Contribution

Someone such as Alok may:

- kill thousands personally,
- claim greater contribution rights,
- reach regional Bosses,
- obtain higher-substage Cores,
- use his own storage,
- recover rare materials efficiently.

Therefore he may gain far more Refinement from a Gate than an ordinary raider.

This is intended.

He should progress abnormally fast.

However:

> Even an exceptional combatant should normally require multiple major expeditions, long refinement periods, or unusually powerful resources to complete a Silver or Gold substage.

- STR
- AGI
- VIT

Examples:

## Troll-type Core

Tends toward:

- high VIT
- high STR
- low AGI

## Wolf / predator Core

Tends toward:

- high AGI
- moderate STR
- moderate VIT

## Wyvern Core

Often balanced across:

- Free AP once actually allocated
- permanent consumables
- racial awakening

# 25. Overflow / Held Growth

If one track reaches 100% before the other:

```text

100%

81%
```

additional permanent growth of the completed track does not advance the substage automatically.

The System may hold compatible overflow growth for later release after advancement.

This follows the same basic principle as previously established held Attributes.

The exact maximum safe overflow remains open for later design.

# 28. Why Cores and Crystals Become Strategically Important

Once Silver is reached, money alone cannot create advancement.

A wealthy noble can purchase resources.

But those resources must still exist.

This creates enormous demand for:

- Gates
- monster territories
- Outer Wilds
- Mana mines
- crystal deposits
- Boss hunts
- rare monster species
- elemental zones
- alchemists
- monster harvesters

Therefore higher-Tier society naturally develops entire industries around Refinement resources.

- strategic resources

- stronger monster Cores

# 31. Relationship to Lifespan

Refinement is intentionally slow.

That is why higher Tier also grants vastly greater lifespan.

Examples:

- Silver Humans: centuries
- Gold Humans: roughly one to two millennia
- Platinum Humans: several millennia
- higher Tiers: increasingly vast timescales

The system is designed around the principle:

> **Tier grants lifespan.**
>
> **Lifespan grants the time required to refine the next vessel.**
>
> **Successful refinement grants the next Tier and even more lifespan.**

# 33. Design Philosophy Going Forward

The Refinement framework should preserve the following principles:

1. **One Gate should not normally complete a substage.**
2. **Higher substages should take longer than lower substages.**
3. **Higher Tiers should take much longer than lower Tiers.**
4. **Lower-grade monsters should become increasingly inefficient.**
5. **Stronger resources should matter greatly but remain dangerous.**
6. **Permanent Attribute growth should directly contribute to vessel completion.**
7. **Both physical and magical development matter.**
8. **Resources cannot replace Merit / qualification.**
9. **Major Tier breakthroughs require more than ordinary saturation.**
10. **The system must scale naturally into years, decades, centuries, and beyond.**
