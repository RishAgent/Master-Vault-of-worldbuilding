# Levels and Experience

Levels remain part of the System, but **Level no longer defines Tier**.

## Level function

Levels represent accumulated System progression and provide opportunities for permanent growth through:

- Tier-scaled stat growth
- Class growth bonuses
- Race growth
- Trait/Bloodline modifiers
- Potential skill/class evolution triggers
- System unlocks at important checkpoints

## Initial level limit

**The old Level 20 initial limit/checkpoint is raised to Level 25.**

Level 25 is the first major System progression wall/checkpoint in the rewrite.

> [!warning] TBD
> The exact condition for surpassing Level 25 still needs to be designed. It may involve a mission, trial, class awakening/evolution, Tier requirement, Star requirement, or a combination.

## Experience sources

Possible EXP sources include:

- Monster kills
- Gate clears
- System quests and missions
- Trials
- Major achievements
- Potentially meaningful non-combat progression where appropriate

## Important rule

Two characters at the same Level can have dramatically different Tiers and stats because of Personal Rank, Race, Class, Traits, Bloodlines, resources, training history, Tier breakthroughs and other factors.
