# Level EXP Progression Table — Levels 1–50

## Purpose

This table defines EXP progression from Level 1 through Level 50.

The progression through **Level 5 remains unchanged**.

From **Level 5 onward**, EXP requirements rise much more aggressively than the previous version. This is intended to preserve the value of:

- higher-Tier monsters,
- boss kills,
- Judgment Trials,
- major quests,
- EXP multipliers,
- long-term training,
- and rare progression opportunities.

The system should allow dramatic growth without making Levels 20–25 trivial after one exceptional event.

## Progression Bands

### Levels 1–5 — Initial Awakening

These values remain canon:

- Level 1 → 2: **200 EXP**
- Level 2 → 3: **500 EXP**
- Level 3 → 4: **1,000 EXP**
- Level 4 → 5: **2,000 EXP**

Cumulative EXP to Level 5:

**3,700 EXP**

A Classless Awakened normally cannot progress beyond Level 5.

Additional EXP is banked until the Class restriction is removed.

---

### Levels 6–10 — Early Class Development

Requirements begin rising sharply once a Class is obtained.

This prevents low-level Ordinary monsters from remaining an efficient progression method.

Typical useful sources:

- Peak Ordinary monsters
- Iron monsters
- Gate elites
- early bosses
- System quests
- first Judgment rewards

Cumulative EXP to Level 10:

**73,700 EXP**

---

### Levels 11–15 — Established Combatant

Progression now requires meaningful combat volume.

Iron-Tier enemies become the standard useful source of EXP, while Bronze enemies provide high-value acceleration.

Cumulative EXP to Level 15:

**598,700 EXP**

---

### Levels 16–20 — Elite Iron / Early Bronze Progression

EXP growth becomes significantly steeper.

Typical progression sources:

- Advanced and Peak Iron enemies
- Iron elites and bosses
- Bronze monsters
- long Gate raids
- Judgment Trials
- major System objectives
- EXP amplification items

Cumulative EXP to Level 20:

**4,523,700 EXP**

Level 20 should represent a highly developed Awakened rather than an ordinary training milestone.

---

### Levels 21–25 — Elite Awakened Progression

This range is deliberately expensive.

A character should require sustained high-level combat, exceptional opportunities, or major rewards to advance consistently.

Typical sources:

- Bronze monsters
- Bronze elites and bosses
- high-risk Gates
- advanced Judgment performance
- major competitions
- rare quests
- EXP multiplier items

Cumulative EXP to Level 25:

# **30,023,700 EXP**

This keeps Level 25 prestigious even when characters have access to unusually productive Gates or Trial environments.

## EXP Banking Rule

If progression is blocked by a Level cap, missing Class, breakthrough requirement, or other System restriction:

> **EXP is banked rather than lost.**

Example:

```text
[ LEVEL CAP ACTIVE ]

Current Level: 5 / 5
Class: None

Additional EXP:
BANKED
```

Once the restriction is removed, banked EXP is applied sequentially through each Level requirement.

---

## Triple EXP Card Interaction

A Triple EXP Card seals:

- existing banked EXP,
- and all EXP earned during the next six hours.

At expiration:

> **Entire sealed EXP total ×3**

The amplified EXP remains banked if progression is still restricted.

Example:

```text
Previous Banked EXP: 27,865
Trial EXP Earned: 500,000

Sealed EXP:
527,865

After ×3:
1,583,595 EXP
```

Under this revised curve, that amount produces a major Level jump without automatically carrying an Awakened anywhere near Level 25.

---

## Why the Curve Becomes Much Steeper

The previous progression made high-volume Trial combat too powerful relative to the Level curve.

For example, once Iron monsters begin awarding hundreds of EXP each, a long Judgment run can generate hundreds of thousands or millions of EXP.

The revised curve ensures:

- hundreds of Ordinary kills matter early but fall off quickly,
- Iron monsters remain useful through the mid Levels,
- Bronze monsters become increasingly important at higher Levels,
- Triple EXP is exceptional without invalidating progression,
- Judgment runs can cause dramatic advancement without immediately reaching the current elite ceiling,
- Level 20–25 remains meaningful for veteran Awakened.

# Summon and Companion Progression Rules

## 1. Trial-Generation Level Cap

The pre-Trial Level 25 restriction applies to the Awakened themselves.

It does **not** automatically apply to independent living companions or Eternal Legion members.

Therefore:

- Alok remains capped at Level 25 until the restriction is removed.
- Alana and the other Awakened remain capped at Level 25.
- Solis can continue leveling.
- Frosty can continue leveling.
- Beast Tamer bonded beasts can continue leveling.
- Ordinary necromancer summons do **not** level independently.

Tier and vessel restrictions still apply. A summon or beast may continue earning EXP while its Attributes become held if its current vessel cannot safely express further growth.

---

## 2. Ordinary Necromancer Summons — Template Progression

Ordinary undead summons are **summoned constructs/templates**, not persistent growth-capable individuals.

They do not gain personal EXP or Levels.

Their strength improves through the summoner's:

- Summoning Skill mastery
- Class advancement
- Class evolution
- Mana control
- summon-capacity improvements
- equipment or Skill modifiers

Example:

A Shadow Necromancer may currently summon:

- **3 Advanced Iron Shadow Soldiers**

Later progression may allow:

- 5 Advanced Iron Shadow Soldiers
- 8 Peak Iron Shadow Soldiers
- a smaller number of Beginner Bronze Shadow Guards
- mixed summon types
- improved equipment
- lower upkeep
- stronger summon Skills

If a normal shadow soldier is destroyed, a later summon is a replacement generated from the current Skill template.

The individual summon does not retain personal Level history.

A second necromancer may currently summon:

- **5 Beginner Iron undead**

The same rule applies. As mastery improves, the quantity and quality of the summon template improve.

---

## 3. Beast Tamer Companions — Living Progression

Beast Tamer companions are living bonded entities.

They possess their own:

- Level
- EXP
- Attributes
- Tier
- Race
- Skills
- evolution paths
- personality

A bonded beast can naturally:

> gain EXP → Level → accumulate Attribute growth → reach a vessel limit → advance Tier → evolve

This is fundamentally different from an ordinary necromancer's template summon.

---

## 4. Bonded Beast Resonance

A Beast Tamer's growth is partially tied to the growth of their bonded beasts.

When a bonded beast gains permanent Attributes, a percentage of that growth is echoed back to the Tamer through the soul contract.

### Standard Resonance Framework

| Bond Stage | Share of Beast's Permanent Attribute Growth |
|---|---:|
| New Bond | **2%** |
| Stable Bond | **5%** |
| Deep Bond | **7.5%** |
| Soul Bond | **10%** |

The transferred growth is tracked separately as:

# **Bond Attributes**

Example:

If a beast gains:

- +100 STR
- +80 AGI
- +120 VIT
- +60 INT

and the bond transfers 5%, the Tamer gains:

- +5 STR
- +4 AGI
- +6 VIT
- +3 INT

These are permanent while the bond and beast remain intact.

They are not natural Rank growth.

---

## 5. Multiple Beast Bonds

Each bonded beast contributes its own separate Bond Attributes.

Example:

### Flame Lion
- +20 STR
- +8 VIT

### Storm Hawk
- +18 AGI
- +12 INT

### Stone Bear
- +15 STR
- +25 VIT

Total Bond Attributes:

- STR +35
- AGI +18
- VIT +33
- INT +12

If one beast dies, only the Attributes derived from that beast are removed.

---

## 6. Beast Death and Soul Backlash

If a bonded beast dies:

1. the associated soul bond collapses,
2. all Bond Attributes gained from that beast are immediately erased,
3. the Beast Tamer suffers **Soul Backlash**.

Possible effects include:

- severe soul pain
- temporary Mana instability
- reduced Mana recovery
- temporary unconsciousness
- inability to form a new contract for a period
- long-term or permanent soul injury in extreme cases

Backlash severity scales with:

- bond depth
- duration of the bond
- the beast's Tier
- the beast's importance to the Tamer's soul structure
- the amount of Bond Attributes being lost

A newly contracted low-Tier beast produces much less backlash than losing a deeply bonded high-Tier companion developed over many years.

---

## 7. Beast Tier Advancement Resonance

A full beast Tier breakthrough may generate an additional one-time Bond Resonance bonus.

This bonus should be based on:

- bond quality
- the beast's post-reconstruction Attributes
- Class mastery
- evolution quality

The exact percentage may vary by Beast Tamer Class.

All such Attributes remain tied to that specific beast and are lost if the bond is permanently destroyed.

---

## 8. Bond Traits

At deeper bond stages, a Beast Tamer may inherit minor characteristics from a beast.

Possible examples:

- enhanced smell
- low-light vision
- minor elemental resistance
- greater Mana sensitivity
- improved reflexes
- heat resistance
- cold resistance

These are **Bond Traits**, not copies of the beast's full Skills.

Rare and advanced Beast Tamer Classes may specialize in stronger trait-sharing.

---

## 9. Eternal Legion — Persistent Natural Progression

Alok's Eternal Legion follows a separate Divine progression model.

Because the Class operates through:

- Life Divinity
- Death Divinity
- Soul Authority

both living and formerly dead Legion members are persistent growth-capable beings.

Examples:

- **Solis** — formerly dead / reconstructed Legion member
- **Frosty** — living Legion member

They can personally:

- gain EXP
- Level
- accumulate Attributes
- advance Tier
- learn Skills
- evolve Race
- improve mastery
- develop individual personalities

Solis is not merely a summoned skeleton template.

Frosty is not merely a Beast Tamer-style temporary summon.

They retain identity and progression.

---

## 10. Eternal Legion Resonance

Alok does **not** use the Beast Tamer percentage-stat-sharing rule.

His existing Eternal Legion Resonance remains:

- **Every 5 Levels gained by a Legion member:** Alok gains +2 free AP
- **Every Substage advancement:** Alok gains +5 free AP
- **Every full Tier advancement:** Alok gains +10 free AP

Only genuine progression achieved after Legion recognition counts.

This keeps Beast Tamer growth and Eternal Legion growth mechanically distinct.

---

## 11. Eternal Legion Death Difference

A Beast Tamer's bond can collapse permanently when the beast dies.

Alok's Eternal Legion is fundamentally different.

Because Legion existence is anchored through Life, Death, and Soul, death does not automatically mean permanent severance.

Depending on:

- Soul Anchor integrity
- Alok's available Mana
- Divinity
- reconstruction resources
- damage to the member's soul

a Legion member may potentially be restored.

This is one of the defining Divine advantages of the Eternal Legion.

---

## 12. EXP Participation Rule for Persistent Companions

Persistent companions do not receive full EXP merely for existing nearby.

### Recommended rule

- **Direct kill:** full eligible EXP
- **Meaningful combat participation:** partial shared EXP
- **Meaningful support / assisted kill:** partial EXP based on contribution
- **No participation:** 0 EXP

This prevents passive power-leveling.

If a Class or Skill grants bonus summon EXP, that multiplier applies after the member's eligible EXP share is determined.

# Canon Lock

- Awakened remain Level-capped at **25** before the True Trials.
- Independent living beasts and Eternal Legion members are not bound by that specific generational Level cap.
- Ordinary necromancer summons do not possess independent Level progression.
- Necromancers grow summon quantity and quality through Skill mastery and Class progression.
- Beast Tamer companions level naturally.
- Beast Tamers receive a small percentage of their beasts' permanent Attribute growth through Bond Resonance.
- If a beast dies, all Attributes gained from that beast are erased.
- Beast death causes Soul Backlash.
- Alok's Eternal Legion supports true individual growth for both living and reconstructed members.
- Alok retains his separate Eternal Legion AP resonance system rather than Beast Tamer-style percentage sharing.
