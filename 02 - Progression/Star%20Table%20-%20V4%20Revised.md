# Star & Circle Progression System — V4 Canon

> [!summary]
> **Stars** measure Mana-Core architecture.
> **Core Circles** are the permanent rings formed around the Mana Core.
> **Casting Circles** are external spell structures projected by the mage.
>
> A mage's Star Rating determines both the number of permanent Core Circles and the normal maximum number of fully independent projected Casting Circles.

# 1. Core Principle

A **Star** represents a completed layer of magical architecture around the Mana Core.

Each Star improves:

- Mana compression
- Mana throughput
- spell stability
- external Mana control
- spell complexity
- simultaneous spell construction
- projected-Circle control
- Circle size
- Circle projection range
- Mana recovery efficiency
- access to higher-Circle magic

Star Rating is separate from Tier, Level, Personal Rank, Race, Class, Affinity, and raw combat ability.

# 2. Core Circles vs Casting Circles

## Core Circles

Each Star forms one permanent Circle around the Mana Core.

| Star | Permanent Core Circles |
|---|---:|
| ★ | 1 |
| ★★ | 2 |
| ★★★ | 3 |
| ★★★★ | 4 |
| ★★★★★ | 5 |
| ... | ... |
| ★ ×24 | 24 |

## Projected Casting Circles

External Casting Circles can be manifested anywhere within the mage's stable control range.

A mage can normally maintain a number of fully independent stable Casting Circles equal to their Star Rating.

Examples:

- One Star → **1 independent Casting Circle**
- Two Stars → **2 independent Casting Circles**
- Three Stars → **3 independent Casting Circles**
- Four Stars → **4 independent Casting Circles**

> [!important]
> Circle count is **not projectile count**.
>
> One Circle may stream many Mana Bullets, Blood Arrows, Lightning Zaps, Ice Spears, or other repeated/branched spells if the mage has enough throughput and control.

# 4. Star Formation Rules

Natural Star progression checks **Base / Natural INT**.

Equipment, Titles, temporary buffs, transformations, borrowed Mana, and temporary INT bonuses do not create natural Stars.

## First Star special case

- deliberate meditation may begin around **75 Base INT**
- stable artificial completion becomes much more reliable around **150+ Base INT**
- natural formation occurs around **250 Base INT**

## Second Star onward

A practical deliberate/artificial benchmark is about **75% of the natural threshold**.

# 6. Circle Size

The **Max Circle Radius** is the largest stable projected Casting Circle normally available at that Star Rating without special artifacts, formations, or Skills.

Smaller Circles are:

- faster
- cheaper
- easier to stabilize
- better for precision

Larger Circles provide:

- greater throughput
- more glyph space
- more simultaneous branches
- greater projectile density
- better support for large spells

# 7. Stable Control Radius

Stable Control Radius is the area within which a mage can maintain high-quality external Mana manipulation.

It governs:

- remote Circle placement
- active spell steering
- barriers
- spell restructuring
- Mana constructs
- controlled zones

A self-contained projectile may travel beyond this radius.

# 8. Circle Count and Throughput

Star Rating determines **independent platforms**, not shots per second.

### One-Star Serena

One independent Circle can still release:

- ~10 Blood Arrows/sec

### One-Star Alok

One Circle demonstrated:

- ~10 Mana Bullets/sec
- ~5 Lightning Zaps/sec

### Two-Star Alok

Two-Star architecture now permits:

- **2 independent projected Casting Circles**

Possible use:

**Circle A**
- Lightning Zap stream

**Circle B**
- Mana Bullet stream

Or:

- offense + defense
- two attack angles
- simultaneous elemental and general magic
- two separate targets

Actual simultaneous performance still depends on:

- concentration
- Parallel Spell Construction
- Mana recovery
- throughput
- spell complexity
- Circle size
- mastery

# 9. Major AoE Width

Major AoE Width is only a rough benchmark.

A mage can choose:

- narrow beams
- small precision spells
- concentrated projectiles
- wide battlefield effects
- barriers
- distributed micro-spells

Star Rating increases magical architecture, not just explosion size.

# 10. Star Advancement Benefits

Each Star generally improves:

- spell/Skill output
- Mana recovery
- compression
- throughput
- projection range
- independent Circle count
- maximum Circle size
- parallel construction
- access to the next Circle of magic

## Alok's established Two-Star benefits

- **All Skill Damage +20%**
- **Mana Recovery +20%**
- increased projection range
- improved higher-order Circle stability
- improved Parallel Spell Architecture
- Second-Circle magic unlocked
- independent Casting Circle capacity increased from **1 → 2**
- normal maximum Circle radius increased from **~1 m → ~2 m**
