## Current design pillars

1. **Personal Rank is potential, not current strength.** It governs how efficiently a being grows through Tiers.
2. **Tier is realized vessel strength.** Stats determine Tier rather than Level.
3. **Levels remain.** They are a progression engine, not the definition of power.
4. **Classes specialize; they do not restrict.** A fire-aligned class can still learn Nature Bind, sword skills, healing, etc. if requirements are met.
5. **Stars measure mana architecture.** Starless casting is body-produced; circles increase efficiency and complexity; Eight Star and Ninth Star are qualitative transformations.
6. **Race matters.** Different species begin at different physical baselines and have different growth tendencies.
7. **Kills can produce loot/materials**, while the System can issue quests and missions that reward progression.
8. **Divinity is a distinct stat.** A born Divine possesses innate Divinity from the beginning.

## Rewrite principle

The new framework should support the full journey from ordinary humans to gods without needing to replace the rules every time the scale increases.
