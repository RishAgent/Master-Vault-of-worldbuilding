# Felora Auction — First and Second Lot Sets

## Auction Structure

The auction is divided into escalating blocks.

- **Lots 1–10** are the opening prestige/progression block, intended to draw spending from young heirs, disciples, merchants, lower-Merit nobles, and ambitious Merit holders.
- **Lots 11–20** are the first true strategic block, attracting Great Houses, high nobles, senior Merit holders, Gold-tier cultivators, military factions, major merchants, and the Crown.

# First Set — Lots 1–10

## Lot 1 — Opening Prestige Lot
Already completed in the story.

## Lot 2 — Rare Skill Scroll
A broadly useful **Rare-grade Skill Scroll**.

Suggested example:
### Gale Step — Rare
- short-distance acceleration
- evasive movement
- directional correction
- useful for swordsmen, assassins, archers, scouts, and mages

## Lot 3 — Mana-Recovery Bracelet
Suggested working name:
### Bracelet of Flowing Wells — Rare
Possible effects:
- **+15% passive Mana recovery**
- **+5% Mana-channel stabilization while casting**

## Lot 4 — Aetherbloom / Coreberry Plant
Public identity:
### Moonwell Berry Plant
Observed properties:
- gathers ambient Mana
- produces glowing Mana-rich berries
- useful as mild cultivation food
- suitable for children and bonded beasts

True System identity:
### Aetherbloom Coreberry Vine
Hidden function:
- absorbs Monster Cores
- absorbs Mana Crystals
- processes elemental essences
- processes compatible biological progression materials
- refines them into stable edible berries

Primary beneficiaries:
- Skittles
- Dawn
- Dusk
- Nocta
- Frosty
- Ferno
- Revana

Balance:
- finite daily processing capacity
- imperfect conversion efficiency
- fruiting delay
- maturity-dependent throughput
- compatibility matters
- no free energy generation

This becomes **Alok's first genuinely desired purchase**.

## Lot 5 — Emberwake Saber
### Emberwake Saber — Rare Silver Weapon
- best for Bronze/Silver melee users
- **+5% realized melee Attack Power**
- successful slashes inflict **Ember Wound**
- Ember Wound burns for about **5 seconds**
- repeated hits refresh rather than infinitely stack

## Lot 6 — Rare Combat Beast
Suggested example:
### Stormmane Lynx
- current Tier: Bronze
- natural potential: Peak Silver
- exceptional development may allow Beginner Gold
- Wind affinity
- weak Lightning affinity
- agility, stealth, tracking, ambush specialization
- high Beast Tamer compatibility

Important:
- rare and valuable
- clearly below Ferno's exceptionally rare Duke-tier combat-beast heritage

## Lot 7 — Extended Spatial Pouch
- hundreds of cubic meters of storage
- preservation enchantments
- useful for expeditions, merchants, adventurers, nobles, and monster harvesting

## Lot 8 — Rare Class Rune
A rune granting a specific **Rare-grade Class**.

Possible Classes:
- Paladin
- Necromancer

This is different from the later Second-Class Awakening Rune.

## Lot 9 — Specialized Defensive / Utility Artifact
Suggested example:
### Mirrorleaf Mantle
- reduces incoming magical projectile damage
- can periodically distort or redirect one targeted spell
- strong but non-strategic Silver-grade defensive utility

## Lot 10 — Poison Wyvern Bloodline Organ
Suggested example:
### Venomheart Gland — Peak Silver Nightspine Wyvern
Possible uses:
- Wyvern Bloodline Elixir
- poison affinity strengthening
- venom-resistance development
- reptilian/wyvern bloodline enhancement
- assassin bloodline medicine
- Beast Tamer evolution preparations

Bloodline users are common enough that this is a rare and expensive product.

Example:
### Silver Sabre Bloodline
- improved reflexes
- agility
- stealth
- reaction speed

Lot 10 is the capstone of the opening block.

# Second Set — Lots 11–20

## Lot 11 — Stabilized Bronze Gate Core
A **House-scale strategic asset**.

Possible functions:
- anchors a permanent Bronze Gate facility
- recurring monster populations
- renewable monster materials
- Merit opportunities
- training grounds
- Mana Crystals
- herbs
- Monster Cores
- economic revenue

This is where the true auction begins.

## Lot 12 — Second-Class Awakening Rune
Permanently unlocks an additional Class slot.

Result:
- minimum **Uncommon**
- possible **Rare**
- potentially better depending on compatibility, trial performance, and user potential

Suggested mechanic:
- opens the second Class slot
- triggers an awakening trial or compatible Class selection

## Lot 13 — Permanent Refinement-Efficiency Resource
Suggested working form:
### Meridian Root of the Deep Earth

Effect:
### **+5% Permanent Refinement Yield**

If a compatible resource normally gives:
**0.100% refinement**

the user receives:
**0.105% refinement**

Applies to:
- Monster Cores
- Mana Crystals
- compatible refinement medicines derived from them

Does not automatically improve:
- Class rewards
- Divine blessings
- AP rewards
- unrelated unique artifact effects

## Lot 14 — Higher-Potential Combat Beast

- storm manipulation
- long-range vision
- pursuit specialization

Still below Ferno's long-term bloodline ceiling.

## Lot 19 — Portable Cultivation Pavilion
Suggested working name:
### Ninefold Mana Gathering Pavilion

Possible functions:
- deployable cultivation chamber
- raises ambient Mana density
- reduces Mana leakage during refinement
- stabilizes Core/Crystal absorption
- supports several cultivators simultaneously
- useful to noble Houses, academies, militaries, and expedition bases

## Lot 20 — Heaven-Tempered Ascendant Core
The centerpiece of the second set.

### Effect by Starting Stage

**Beginner Gold**
- advances to **Advanced Gold**

**Intermediate Gold**
- advances to **Peak Gold**

**Advanced Gold**
- advances to the **limit of Peak Gold**

**Peak Gold**
- greatly assists the Platinum breakthrough
- if successful, leaves the user at approximately **25% Beginner Platinum development**

### Limitations
It does not make the Platinum breakthrough automatically safe.

A Peak-Gold user must still:
- survive Tier Tempering
- complete vessel reconstruction
- stabilize the new Tier
- satisfy compatibility requirements
- successfully complete the breakthrough

Possible secondary effect:
- temporary or stage-limited refinement-efficiency improvement until Platinum

Strategic value:
- can save decades or centuries
- replaces enormous quantities of Gold-grade refinement resources
- attracts Gold nobles, Great Houses, the Crown, military powers, and ancient families

# Current Canon Decisions

- Star Formation Crystal is **removed**.
- Star formation remains tied to Tier / Mana-Core architecture and should not be sold as a simple auction shortcut.
- Ferno-class heritage remains exceptionally rare.
- Lot 14's stronger beast remains below Ferno's long-term bloodline ceiling.
- Lot 4's berry plant is especially synergistic with Skittles and Alok's stored Core/Crystal resources.
- Lot 20 is extremely powerful but does not eliminate the danger or requirements of the Platinum breakthrough.
