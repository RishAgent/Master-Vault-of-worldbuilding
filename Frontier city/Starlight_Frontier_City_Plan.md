# Starlight Frontier City Plan

## 1. Core Concept

House Starlight will expand an existing fortified settlement at the beginning of the Silver Ring into a major private frontier city-hub.

The city will become:

- House Starlight's principal outer-ring logistics center,
- a cultivation center built around a permanent Mana vein,
- a military and expedition staging point,
- the main Wolfheide launch hub for deeper Gold Ring and Platinum Frontier expeditions,
- the secured location for House Starlight's reusable Platinum Gate Core,
- and the territorial seat granted to Alok Aeonmorta as a landed Viscount.

Alok's personal Viscount standing remains valid even without territory. The city is an additional territorial grant and administrative responsibility.

---

# 2. Location

Recommended location:

> **Inner Silver Ring / start of the Silver Ring**

The location already contains limited frontier infrastructure rather than being built from nothing.

Existing structures may include:

- fortified walls,
- barracks,
- warehouses,
- convoy yards,
- a Merit Hall,
- medical facilities,
- monster-processing yards,
- communication arrays,
- supply depots,
- protected roads,
- temporary expedition housing.

House Starlight and Wolfheide will expand this Silver Ring stronghold into a permanent fortified city.

- Gold Ring resource zones,
- House Starlight resource claims,

- secured Gold routes,

- rare monster territories.

## 3.3 Platinum Frontier Access Hub

The frontier city becomes the natural Wolfheide staging location for:

- approved Platinum expeditions,
- Crown military teams,
- Great House expeditions,
- Gold-tier elites,
- foreign liaison teams,
- expedition supply and recovery,
- resource processing,
- treaty administration.

Geographic progression:

> Wolfheide interior  
> → Starlight territory  
> → Silver Ring frontier city  
> → deeper Silver Ring routes  
> → Gold Ring strongholds and resource zones  
> → Imperial secured corridor  
> → Platinum Frontier access zone

# 4. Permanent Mana Vein

As part of the Krai diplomatic settlement, the Empire permanently transfers one major Mana vein to House Starlight.

The vein is not leased.

It becomes:

> **Permanent House Starlight property**

The Empire possesses other Mana veins, so the transfer is significant without being existential to the Empire.

Working name:

> **Astral Convergence Vein**

Name remains adjustable.

---

# 5. Mana Vein Placement

Recommended structure:

The vein lies:

- beneath the city,
- beneath an adjacent mountain complex,
- or in a heavily fortified underground cultivation zone directly connected to the city.

It is protected by:

- layered Mana barriers,
- military formations,
- high-grade access seals,
- Starlight guards,
- specialized cultivation arrays,
- emergency containment systems.

The vein is a strategic House asset, not an openly accessible mine.
