# Current Canon Summary

## Locked / strongly accepted

- Stats determine Tier; Level does not.
- Tier substages: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Peak.
- Tier ladder: Ordinary → Iron → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Supreme → Noble → Baron → Count → Duke → King → Emperor → Sovereign → Transcendent → Demigod → Divine.
- Ordinary physical benchmarks: ~5 / 8 / 12 / 16.
- Iron benchmarks: ~22 / 40 / 80 / 150.
- Full current Tier table is in [[Tier System]].
- Personal Rank describes growth potential/efficiency and uses F through SSS as the current working ladder.
- Different races can be born into different Tiers. Healthy true Dragons hatch at Bronze or above; most Dragonoids are born around Beginner Bronze, making them one of the strongest naturally born civilized races.
- Classes specialize but do not prohibit off-class magic/skills.
- Levels remain.
- First progression checkpoint/cap is raised from Level 20 to **Level 25**.
- Starless magic is produced directly through the body and is inefficient.
- Mana circles increase efficiency and stable spell complexity.
- Fireball is a Zero-Star example; Fire Blast One-Star; Fire Bind Two-Star.
- Eight Star: entire body/every cell becomes part of the mana core.
- Ninth Star: Mana + sufficient Divinity are merged into a Full God Vessel.
- Kills can drop materials and sometimes System loot.
- The System can issue quests/missions that reward progression.
- Status includes Name, Race, Level, Personal Rank, Tier, Star, Traits, Special Bloodlines, Classes, Skills, Attributes, Mana and Divinity where applicable.
- Alok is a born Divine Child and therefore has a Divinity stat from the beginning.

- Dragons and Dragonoids are sibling draconic races: Dragons begin as magical beasts; Dragonoids are humanoid civilized descendants/kin. Their convergence begins at Platinum Tier and normally completes at Diamond Tier, producing Draconic Ascendants with genuine humanoid and draconic forms.
- Dragons and Dragonoids coexist as kin/partners in draconic civilizations; Dragons are not culturally treated as pets by default.
- Alok has ancestry connected to the Supreme Dragon Lineage; exact source and awakening details remain reveal-controlled.
