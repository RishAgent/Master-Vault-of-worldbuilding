# Eternal Legion — Current Elite Status and Evolution

> [!important] Canon checkpoint
> **Story date:** Early May 2024, immediately after the Loreiya arc.  
> This note tracks the four currently active elite/persistent Legion members: **Solis, Frosty, Valeria, and Lyra.**
>
> Tier, Race, Class, Level, and development stage are separate systems. **Tier is the primary vessel/evolutionary gate.** Class and Race evolution are not automatic merely because a Level is reached.

---

# 1. Core Progression Rules

## Tier Attribute Calibration

| Tier / Stage | Working Attribute Range |
|---|---:|
| Advanced Iron | ~80–139 |
| **Peak Iron** | **~140–180** |
| **Beginner Bronze** | **250+** |
| Intermediate Bronze | ~450+ |
| Advanced Bronze | ~800+ |
| Peak Bronze | ~1,500+ |

> [!note]
> **181–249 is intentionally not a normal progression band.** A Peak Iron vessel approaches its structural limit around the 140–180 range. A true Bronze breakthrough reconstructs/expands the vessel and causes a major jump into the **250+** range.

Development labels are not rigid numerical gates, but the following ranges are useful working guides inside Peak Iron:

- **Early:** ~140–150 dominant range
- **Mid:** ~150–160
- **Late:** ~160–170
- **Peak:** ~170–180 / breakthrough-ready

## Eternal Legion Resonance

Alok gains permanent free Attribute Points from genuine Legion progression:

- **Every 5 Levels gained by a Legion member:** +2 AP
- **Every Substage advancement:** +5 AP
- **Every full Tier advancement:** +10 AP

Only progression achieved after Legion recognition counts.

Persistent Legion members can gain EXP, Level independently, accumulate Attributes, improve Skill mastery, advance development stages, break through Tiers, evolve Race, evolve Class, and develop individual personalities and identities.

They do **not** function like ordinary necromancer templates.
