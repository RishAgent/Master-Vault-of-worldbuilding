# V4 Master Timeline — Simulation, Hero Exposure, Mock Trials & True Trials

> **Canon planning note:** This timeline begins from the current story date, **24 January 2022**, and organizes the full pre-Trial progression into distinct narrative phases.
>
> The structure intentionally separates:
> - simulated combat preparation
> - public hero exposure
> - criminal / covert operations
> - mock-trial escalation
> - family leave
> - final training
> - the True Trials

---

# 1. Current Date

## 24 January 2022

The first official **Simulation Trial** begins.

Team Proxy is selected as the first team to enter the simulation system and establish the opening benchmark.

At this point:

- Alok is Level 25 and Intermediate Bronze — Peak Development
- Alana is Level 25 and Beginner Bronze
- most top Awakened are at or near the pre-Trial Level cap
- WAHO still keeps most details about the coming catastrophe hidden from the general public
- the public does not yet understand the full purpose of the 201 Awakened
- astronomical anomalies are beginning to become increasingly difficult to conceal

---

# 2. Phase One — Simulation Training

## 24 January 2022 → Late May 2022

### Approximate Duration
**Four months**

### Primary Purpose
Teach the Awakened how to function as coordinated teams under increasingly complex combat conditions.

This phase focuses less on raw Level progression and more on:

- teamwork
- battlefield coordination
- communication
- command hierarchy
- Mana conservation
- target prioritization
- rescue decisions
- defensive formations
- role specialization
- retreat discipline
- endurance
- mental fatigue resistance
- adapting to unknown scenarios

Because many Awakened have already reached the temporary Level cap, development increasingly shifts toward:

- Skill mastery
- Class mastery
- Mana control
- tactical experience
- physical control
- combat technique
- team synergy

---

## Simulation Structure

Early simulations begin with:

- small monster groups
- direct combat
- simple survival objectives

They gradually evolve into:

- large enemy waves
- mixed monster armies
- boss encounters
- civilian rescue scenarios
- escort missions
- defensive objectives
- multi-stage battles
- split-team operations
- command-disruption scenarios
- resource-limited missions
- missions where killing the enemy is not the true objective

### Mental Cost

The simulation does not physically consume the user's real Mana or damage the real body.

However:

- pain feedback is simulated
- wounds feel real
- exhaustion is recreated
- sensory overload accumulates
- mental fatigue builds
- prolonged runs become psychologically exhausting

This allows the Awakened to experience repeated high-risk combat without physically destroying themselves.

---

# 3. Transition — Why WAHO Changes Strategy

## Mid / Late 2022

By this point, the secrecy surrounding Earth's situation is becoming unsustainable.

Independent astronomers, observatories, physicists, and other experts begin noticing impossible astronomical anomalies.

Possible discoveries include:

- disappearing stars
- missing distant galaxies
- unexplained void-like regions
- inconsistent deep-space observations
- large-scale celestial structures vanishing from expected locations
- independent observatories reaching similar conclusions

The evidence becomes too widespread for governments to suppress permanently.

WAHO and world governments are forced to make a strategic choice:

> Reveal the truth before the public discovers it chaotically.

However, revealing only that the galaxy is disappearing would create panic.

WAHO therefore pairs the disclosure process with the public introduction of the Awakened.

The message becomes:

> Humanity is in danger.

But also:

> Humanity has been preparing.

And:

> These young Awakened are among the people being trained to protect Earth.

---

# 4. Phase Two — WAHO Hero Exposure Program

## Mid / Late 2022 → 2023

WAHO creates a formal public-facing hero movement.

The exact public organization name can be finalized later.

Possible working names:

- WAHO Global Response Corps
- WAHO Awakened Response Division
- Global Awakened Response Initiative
- WAHO Hero Response Network

The movement remains under WAHO authority.

The Awakened are **not vigilantes**.

They operate alongside:

- police
- military
- firefighters
- disaster response agencies
- intelligence services
- hospitals
- rescue organizations
- local governments

---

# 5. Hero Exposure — Stage One: Rescue First

The first public missions are intentionally rescue-oriented.

WAHO wants the world to associate the Awakened with:

> Protection.

before:

> Destructive power.

### Typical Missions

- building collapses
- earthquakes
- floods
- fires
- landslides
- industrial accidents
- missing-person searches
- evacuation support
- infrastructure failures
- large traffic disasters
- rescue from dangerous terrain
- hazardous-environment extraction

The public begins seeing the Awakened as real people who save lives.

---

# 6. Public Image Development

As missions become public:

- rescue footage spreads
- news coverage increases
- social media follows specific Awakened
- fan communities appear
- public rankings emerge unofficially
- debates begin over whether Awakened are heroes, weapons, or both
- conspiracy theories spread
- some people become afraid of their power
- others idolize them
- politicians debate WAHO authority
- governments argue over jurisdiction
- families begin learning more about what their children were being trained for

The Awakened gradually stop being hidden assets.

They become visible symbols of humanity's preparation.

---

# 7. Hero Exposure — Stage Two: Crime Response

Once public trust is established, WAHO authorizes higher-risk missions.

The Awakened begin participating in coordinated operations against serious criminal threats.

### Possible Targets

- armed gangs
- trafficking networks
- hostage situations
- cartel infrastructure
- kidnapping rings
- contract-killing organizations
- black-market weapons networks
- illegal experimentation facilities
- organized human trafficking
- violent extremist cells
- future Mana-resource black markets

These operations remain supervised by WAHO and government agencies.

The Awakened are not simply given permission to kill criminals.

Rules of engagement still matter.

# 9. WAHO Special Operations Structure

Rather than assigning the Awakened to independent military shadow teams as in the older version, V4 uses a WAHO-controlled structure.

WAHO identifies Awakened whose abilities are suited to:

- covert rescue
- infiltration
- anti-trafficking operations
- hostage recovery
- intelligence support
- high-risk criminal raids
- counter-terror operations
- clandestine missions

This creates an internal special-response branch.

Possible working names:

- WAHO Special Response Division
- WAHO Shadow Operations Unit
- WAHO Black Response Unit
- WAHO Special Operations Directorate

Alok becomes particularly valuable because of:

- regeneration
- enhanced perception
- extreme physical ability
- lightning
- Soul-related abilities
- high survivability
- rapid combat adaptation
- his ability to operate effectively with limited support

---

# 10. Alok's First Human Kill

The Loreiyan conflict becomes a major turning point in Alok's life.

Up to this point, Alok has killed:

- monsters
- undead
- summoned creatures

But not another human being.

His first human kill should be:

- deliberate
- necessary
- emotionally significant
- not glorified
- not accidental

The preferred structure is a situation where:

- a Loreiyan operative is about to kill a hostage or innocent person
- Alok cannot safely restrain them in time
- the threat is immediate
- Alok chooses lethal force

He understands afterward that:

- the decision was necessary
- he would make the same decision again
- necessity does not make it emotionally meaningless

This marks the moment where Alok begins understanding the real burden of being responsible for lives.

# 11. Phase Three — Expanding Mock Trials

## 2023 → End of 2024

The Mock Trial program becomes increasingly realistic.

This phase should no longer feel like academy combat practice.

The Awakened gradually transition toward large-scale expeditionary preparation.

---

## Early Mock Trials

Duration:

**Hours to 1–3 days**

Focus:

- team combat
- rescue
- survival
- resource management
- command rotation
- environmental hazards

---

## Intermediate Mock Trials

Duration:

**Several days to one week**

Focus:

- multi-team missions
- separated squads
- limited communication
- scarce consumables
- civilian protection
- hostile terrain
- moving objectives
- incomplete intelligence

---

## Advanced Mock Trials

Duration:

**One to several weeks**

Focus:

- simulated city defense
- Gate Break scenarios
- long-term supply management
- casualty replacement
- base defense
- leadership loss
- moral dilemmas
- opposing intelligent factions
- missions where retreat is required
- operations where killing the enemy is the wrong strategic choice

---

## Final Mock Trial

By late 2024, the final Mock Trial may last approximately:

# **One Month**

The purpose is to recreate the psychological burden of entering an unknown Trial with no guarantee of immediate return.

# 12. Public Revelation Timeline

## 2022

WAHO publicly confirms the existence of Awakened.

The 201 begin appearing through the Hero Exposure Program.

The full cosmic threat is not yet completely revealed.

---

## 2023

Astronomical evidence becomes increasingly public.

WAHO and world governments acknowledge that Earth faces a genuine external existential threat.

The public learns that the Awakened were not created merely for disaster response.

---

## 2024

The broader truth is revealed.

This includes:

- the Void
- the disappearing galaxy
- the Tower
- the existence of the Trials
- the fact that the 201 were identified and prepared for this purpose

By this point the public has already spent years watching the Awakened save lives.

This is intentional.

WAHO wants humanity's reaction to be:

> Those are the people who have already been protecting us.

rather than:

> The government says mysterious superhumans are our only hope.

# 21. Core V4 Difference From the Original Version

## Original Concept
Awakened teams were split up and assigned to selected military units for exposure and operational experience.

## V4 Revision
The Awakened remain under **WAHO authority** and become part of a WAHO-organized international hero movement.

Military organizations still cooperate with them, but the hero structure is:

- globally coordinated
- publicly visible
- politically strategic
- rescue-oriented at first
- gradually expanded into serious crime response
- designed to build public trust before full disclosure of the coming catastrophe

This makes the Exposure Arc part of WAHO's broader psychological strategy:

> Humanity must know the danger.

But humanity must also believe that survival is possible.

The Awakened become the visible proof of that hope.
