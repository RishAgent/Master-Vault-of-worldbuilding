# Post-Connection Earth — Mana, Gates, Zones, WAHO and Progression

> [!important] Core premise
> After Earth completes the required Judgment Trial stage and officially connects to the Tower, Mana begins spreading across the planet.
>
> Earth does **not** instantly become a high-Tier magical world.
>
> It progresses through the same Tier structure used elsewhere:
>
> **Ordinary → Iron → Bronze → Silver → Gold → ...**
>
> Earth's current world Tier determines the normal Tier ceiling of naturally forming Gates, environmental Mana density, monster evolution, resource quality, and the level of danger civilization must adapt to.

---

## 1. WAHO Before Full Tower Connection

WAHO already exists before public Tower connection.

It was formed because:

- the Tower made contact with Earth
- governments were required to cooperate
- the Tower supplied controlled Gates before the Judgment Trials
- selected Awakened used these Gates for training and progression
- WAHO coordinated Gate access, research, Awakened activity and preparation for the Trials

WAHO is therefore a multinational institution created by cooperating governments under Tower pressure and necessity.

Before public connection, WAHO operates the limited Gate network available to Earth.

After public connection, it evolves into the world's dominant Awakened institution.

---

## 2. WAHO After Connection

After Earth connects to the Tower, WAHO becomes the equivalent of a global **Hunters Association / Awakened Authority**.

WAHO retains:

- institutional access to the Tower
- authority over official Tower entry
- Awakened registration
- Class certification
- Tier certification
- Gate licensing
- Gate monitoring
- Gate Break response coordination
- Awakened combat regulations
- resource exchange systems
- Judgment Fragment regulation
- intergovernmental Awakened coordination

National branches emerge in individual countries while remaining tied to the international WAHO structure.

Examples:

- WAHO Sri Lanka
- WAHO Japan
- WAHO United States
- WAHO India
- WAHO Germany

---

## 3. Earth World Tier System

Earth itself uses the same Tier structure as individuals and Gates:

**Ordinary → Iron → Bronze → Silver → Gold → ...**

Earth's World Tier affects:

- ambient Mana density
- normal natural Gate ceiling
- natural monster evolution
- available Mana resources
- Mana Crystal quality
- Core quality
- magical plant growth
- environmental mutation
- difficulty of wild zones
- speed of Awakened progression

Earth begins the public Mana Era at:

# **Ordinary World Tier**

and advances gradually as planetary Mana saturation rises.

---

## 4. Tower-Issued Gates vs Natural Gates

### Tower-Issued Gates

Before full connection:

- supplied directly by the Tower
- controlled by WAHO
- used to prepare selected Awakened
- deliberately provided progression environments
- not evidence that pre-connection Earth was naturally Iron or Bronze Tier

### Natural Gates

After connection:

- begin appearing across Earth as Mana spreads
- are influenced by Earth's World Tier
- may form in cities, wilderness, oceans, underground areas, ruins and Monster Zones
- can Break if ignored
- become a central source of EXP, materials and wealth

---

## 5. Ordinary-Tier Earth

For the first months after connection:

# **Earth remains Ordinary Tier**

This means:

- naturally forming Gates are overwhelmingly Ordinary
- Ordinary monsters dominate early wild evolution
- low-Tier Cores and Mana Crystals become common
- loot remains modest
- Awakened progression is slow
- ordinary militaries remain highly relevant
- guns, artillery, armor, drones and aircraft still matter
- dangerous ecological mutation begins but remains early-stage

A normal Awakened may need years to reach meaningful power.

---

## 6. Human Progression After Global Awakening

Once Earth connects, humans gain System access.

A newly awakened human begins at:

# **Level 1**

Without a Class, progression stops at:

# **Level 5**

To continue meaningfully, they need a Class.

Classes are not automatically granted.

---

## 7. Judgment Fragments

Classes require:

# **Judgment Fragments**

Judgment Fragments become one of the most valuable resources in the new Earth economy.

They are:

- earned through actual monster hunting
- rare
- not available from the Tower Shop
- impossible to mass-produce
- tied to real combat and progression

Working concept:

> An Awakened may need to kill **hundreds of monsters** before obtaining a Judgment Fragment.

Exact drop logic can remain partially hidden.

Possible factors:

- kill count
- contribution
- monster quality
- combat difficulty
- personal growth
- System-recognized accomplishments
- hidden Judgment criteria

Fragments may be personally used, sold, traded to WAHO, given to local governments, reserved for elite candidates, or used by guilds to recruit and develop talent.

---

## 8. Why Judgment Fragments Matter

Wealth cannot fully bypass the progression system.

A rich person can buy:

- weapons
- armor
- Mana Crystals
- training
- Gate access
- healing
- equipment

But cannot simply buy a Class from the Tower.

Judgment Fragments must come from real Awakened activity.

This makes them politically and economically priceless.

---

## 9. Classed Awakened Progression

Once an Awakened gains a Class:

- progression beyond Level 5 becomes possible
- stronger Gates provide better EXP
- better Classes and mastery produce better growth
- higher-Tier monsters provide better materials
- Tower climbing eventually becomes necessary for major advancement

The Trial Generation begins the Mana Era massively ahead of ordinary humanity because they already possess:

- higher Levels
- developed Classes
- Class Mastery
- Tier experience
- months of real combat experience
- rare equipment
- Tower knowledge
- Gate knowledge

They are Earth's first veteran generation.

---

## 10. Mana Emergence and Environmental Mutation

Mana spreads gradually across Earth and affects:

- humans
- animals
- plants
- fungi
- oceans
- forests
- underground ecosystems
- mineral deposits
- weather systems
- Gate density

Animals and plants mutate much more freely than System-structured sapient races.

This produces:

# **Mana-Evolved Species**

Examples may include:

- stronger predators
- venom-enhanced snakes
- armored crocodiles
- Mana-sensitive birds
- massive insects
- evolved canines
- enhanced elephants
- aquatic Mana predators
- magical plants
- poisonous flora
- alchemical herbs

Not every evolved animal becomes hostile.

Some remain territorial, some can be domesticated, some retain existing bonds with humans, and some may eventually become intelligent enough to receive proper System recognition.

---

## 11. Early Destruction

The first months are dangerous.

Humanity cannot contain every mutation or Gate.

Cities and towns will be lost.

Possible causes:

- Gate Breaks
- mass animal mutation
- infrastructure collapse
- monster migration
- local Mana spikes
- insufficient Awakened response
- multiple simultaneous disasters

Some cities lose districts.

Some towns disappear entirely.

Some countries adapt efficiently.

Others lose large regions.

Civilization survives, but geography and settlement patterns begin changing.

---

## 12. Protected Zones

Surviving major population centers gradually become:

# **PROTECTED ZONES**

These may include:

- major cities
- strategic towns
- ports
- industrial districts
- agricultural centers
- airports
- power infrastructure
- WAHO headquarters
- logistics hubs

Early defenses:

- concrete barriers
- steel walls
- military checkpoints
- drones
- artillery
- armored vehicles

Later defenses:

- Mana-reinforced walls
- Barrier Arrays
- Mana detection systems
- monster-warning networks
- WAHO response towers
- Mana weapon emplacements
- Awakened rapid-response teams

Protected cities eventually resemble fortified Mana-era metropolises.

---

## 13. Frontier Zones

Between safe civilization and fully lost territory are:

# **FRONTIER ZONES**

Characteristics:

- roads may still function
- civilian settlements may survive
- patrols are common
- Gate clearing is frequent
- monster attacks remain possible
- resource extraction is dangerous
- guild contracts are common
- Awakened escorts may be required

Frontier Zones become major work areas for professional Awakened.

---

## 14. Monster Zones

Collapsed or abandoned regions become:

# **MONSTER ZONES**

These may include:

- ruined cities
- abandoned suburbs
- forests
- mountains
- swamps
- industrial districts
- coastline
- islands
- underground networks
- collapsed highways
- abandoned military zones

Monster Zones are evolving Mana ecosystems, not static ruins.

---

## 15. Self-Reinforcing Monster Zones

Typical cycle:

**Gate spawns**  
→ is not cleared  
→ Gate Break occurs  
→ monsters escape  
→ creatures reproduce / migrate / establish territory  
→ deaths and Mana increase local saturation  
→ native species evolve faster  
→ rare materials begin forming  
→ more Gates appear  
→ zone becomes increasingly dangerous

A ruined city can therefore become a full magical ecosystem.

Possible features:

- monster packs nesting in apartment towers
- flying beasts roosting in skyscrapers
- Mana vines consuming roads
- evolved rats filling metro tunnels
- Gate entrances inside abandoned buildings
- dominant predators controlling entire districts
- naturally forming Mana Crystals
- valuable old-world salvage
- rare monster materials
- alchemical resources

---

## 16. Gate Breaks

Uncleared Gates can eventually:

# **BREAK**

When a Gate Break occurs:

- Gate monsters enter Earth
- civilians are threatened
- Monster Zones become stronger
- ecosystems are destabilized
- new monster populations may establish themselves

Gate Breaks are especially common in uncontrolled Monster Zones.

Protected Zones prioritize Gate clearance specifically to prevent them.

## 18. Awakened Career Ladder

A normal Awakened progression path may look like:

1. Awaken at Level 1
2. Hunt Ordinary monsters
3. Reach Level 5
4. Search for a Judgment Fragment
5. Gain a Class
6. Continue Ordinary Gate work
7. Enter Frontier Zones
8. Take higher-risk contracts
9. Progress into Iron content once Earth advances
10. Join elite guilds or WAHO special teams
11. Gain Tower access
12. Push beyond normal connected-world limitations

Strength directly affects:

- income
- survival
- status
- Gate access
- resource access
- guild rank
- Tower opportunities
- political influence

## 19. Mana Cores, Crystals and TP Economy

Monster kills and Gates provide:

- Monster Cores
- Mana Crystals
- monster materials
- magical plants
- equipment
- Skill resources
- rare drops

These can be sold directly into the Tower economy for:

# **Tower Points**

or sold domestically to:

- WAHO
- governments
- guilds
- corporations
- private buyers

for national currencies such as dollars, rupees, yen or euros.

This keeps both Tower Points and national currencies relevant.

---

## 20. Earth Reaching Iron Tier

Eventually planetary Mana density reaches the next threshold.

Earth becomes:

# **IRON-TIER WORLD**

Consequences:

- Iron Gates begin naturally forming
- Iron monsters become sustainable
- Iron Mana Crystals become accessible
- Iron Cores become more common
- loot quality rises sharply
- Awakened progression accelerates
- higher-grade equipment becomes practical
- stronger Classes become more valuable
- Monster Zones become much more dangerous

Ordinary Gates become increasingly manageable for established Awakened.

Iron threats become the new serious standard.

---

## 21. Higher World Tiers

### Bronze Earth

By the time Earth reaches Bronze:

- Bronze Gates become naturally possible
- permanent Mana ecosystems exist outside Gates
- large Monster Zones may be completely controlled by Bronze creatures
- fortress cities become normal
- protected travel corridors become essential
- powerful guilds dominate regional defense
- magical infrastructure becomes mainstream
- monster resources become central to civilization
- some wilderness regions become effectively non-human territory

Humanity remains civilized, but the world has fundamentally changed.

---

## 22. Travel in the Future

As danger increases, safe travel changes.

Possible systems:

- armored convoys
- Awakened escorts
- fortified rail corridors
- protected highways
- Mana aircraft
- WAHO transport networks
- eventually portal networks

Travel outside Protected Zones becomes increasingly dependent on Awakened security.

## 23. Reclamation Campaigns

Monster Zones are not automatically lost forever.

WAHO, governments or guild coalitions can launch:

# **RECLAMATION CAMPAIGNS**

Typical objectives:

- destroy nests
- kill dominant monsters
- clear local Gates
- suppress Gate Breaks
- secure Mana nodes
- restore roads
- establish barriers
- recover infrastructure
- rebuild settlements

Successful reclamation can restore entire regions to human control.

---

## 24. Managed Monster Zones

Some Monster Zones may eventually become too economically valuable to erase completely.

They can produce:

- Mana Crystals
- Cores
- rare herbs
- valuable hides
- alchemical blood
- evolved plant matter
- Gate materials
- rare monsters

This creates a future political and ethical conflict:

> Should humanity destroy dangerous monster ecosystems?

or

> Should controlled zones be preserved because they generate enormous wealth?

Some countries may intentionally maintain regulated hunting reserves.

---

## 25. Global Inequality

Countries will adapt differently.

Some will:

- fortify rapidly
- develop powerful WAHO branches
- create elite guilds
- stabilize Monster Zones
- profit from Mana resources

Others will:

- lose major cities
- suffer regional collapse
- depend on foreign Awakened
- lose large territories
- struggle to control Gate Breaks

This creates major geopolitical differences in the Mana Era.

## 29. World-Tier Gate Rule

> Earth's current World Tier determines the normal ceiling of naturally forming Gates.

### Ordinary Earth
- overwhelming majority of Gates: Ordinary
- rare Iron anomaly possible
- Iron anomaly = national emergency

### Iron Earth
- Ordinary Gates become common
- Iron Gates become normal serious content
- rare Bronze anomaly possible

### Bronze Earth
- Iron Gates become routine for established Awakened
- Bronze Gates become strategic threats
- rare Silver anomalies become catastrophic events

Rare over-Tier Gates remain possible, but should always be exceptional.
