> [!important] Level Credit rule
> A Guaranteed Level reward becomes a **Level Credit**.
>
> It does not forcibly advance a character through:
>
> - a Level cap
> - a Tier/stage vessel wall
> - an evolution requirement
> - an unsafe Attribute-expression limit
>
> Credits remain stored until they can be safely redeemed.
>
> This prevents a Trial reward from bypassing the setting's vessel progression rules.

|---|---:|---:|

### Rounding

The System internally retains fractional growth.

Displayed Attributes round only after accumulated fractional growth reaches a full point.

# 9. Class Evolution Rule

Class Mastery and Class Evolution are separate.

A Class can become:

**Novice → Trained → Adept → Expert → Master**

without changing its name.

A true Class Evolution requires additional conditions such as:

- sufficient mastery
- Tier
- achievements
- combat identity
- role development
- compatible Race
- special trials
- Class-specific conditions

When a Class evolves, its base growth package and available Skills improve.

Mastery of the previous Class accelerates mastery of the evolved Class but does not automatically make the new Class Master-tier.

# 27. Trial-Generation Principle

The first Judgment challengers are not normal future Awakened.

They are the **Trial Generation**.

Their progression is exceptional because:

- they entered the Judgment Trials early
- they receive Tower-wide progression rewards
- Level caps are raised through Trial completion
- they gain extraordinary amounts of EXP
- they receive large AP rewards
- they obtain Tower Points, Skills, Classes and rare materials
- they experience constant Class mastery growth

Later normal Awakened may:

- awaken at Level 1
- remain Classless
- hit the Classless Level-5 limit
- acquire a Class and continue
- eventually hit the broader Level-25 restriction
- require Tower progression to move beyond it

Therefore the Trial Generation can become historically exceptional without making their growth representative of ordinary global progression.
