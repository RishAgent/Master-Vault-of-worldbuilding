# 5. Divine Power

Alok independently fused Mana and Divinity and unlocked a new System resource.

## Divine Mana Conversion

# **1,000 Mana + 1 Divinity → 1 Divine Power**

The conversion is repeatable.

## Divine Power Stat

**Divine Power:** variable / stored resource

- Does **not** regenerate naturally.
- Must be synthesized manually.
- No hard maximum capacity has been established yet.
- Storage is currently limited practically by Alok's Core, soul, Star architecture, control, and vessel.

### Effective Resource Cost

Every **1 Divine Power** represents:

- **1,000 Mana**
- **1 Divinity**

Therefore:

- 10 Divine Power = 10,000 Mana + 10 Divinity
- 100 Divine Power = 100,000 Mana + 100 Divinity

**Rarity:** Divine  
**Classification:** Independent Divine Skill  
**Element:** Flame / Divine  
**Casting Requirement:** At least 1 active Magic Circle

### Current Beam Width

# **1 meter**

### Resource Cost

# **10 Divine Power / second**

### Base Damage

# **180,000 AP / second**

### Effects

#### Divine Flame
Carries limited Law-interference tied to:

- burning
- heat
- combustion
- thermal destruction

#### Divine Penetration
Partially bypasses:

- conventional magical resistance
- elemental defenses
- lower-order protective effects

#### Thermal Annihilation
Rapidly melts, burns, or vaporizes non-resistant matter.

#### Persistent Divine Burn
Targets directly struck receive a Divine Burn.

- cannot be reliably extinguished by ordinary water
- cannot be reliably removed by low-grade Frost Skills
- continues damaging the target after direct beam exposure ends

#### Sustained Beam
Can continue as long as Alok can maintain:

- Divine Power supply
- concentration
- Circle stability

#### Width Scaling
Current stable width is **1 meter**.

Increasing width will require:

- more Divine Power
- greater control
- stronger Star architecture
- stronger vessel support

#### Divine Overload
Prolonged use stresses:

- Casting Circles
- Mana channels
- Core stability
- current Silver-tier vessel

The Class Codex is currently treated as **Eternal Legion-exclusive for Class granting**.

## Functions

### Record
Can record Class architectures Alok encounters or acquires.

### Analyze
Can study:

- Class Mana architecture
- Skill construction
- summon systems
- contracts
- progression paths
- magical principles

### Legion Grant
Can reconstruct and grant compatible recorded Classes to recognized Eternal Legion members.

## Restriction

Alok **cannot casually grant Classes to outsiders**.

Non-Legion people still require normal Class-acquisition methods such as Judgment Fragments.

## Alok's Personal Benefit

Alok may study the **magical principles** encoded inside recorded Classes and reproduce similar magic manually without taking the Class himself.

The Codex gives him the blueprint.

It does not automatically grant mastery.
