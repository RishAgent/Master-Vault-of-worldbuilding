# 1. Core Progression Rules

## Personal Rank, Race, Class, Level and Tier are separate

Each persistent Eternal Legion member develops through interacting systems:

1. **Personal Rank** — innate growth efficiency and potential.
2. **Race** — what the being physically/spiritually is.
3. **Class** — combat/professional specialization and Class Skills.
4. **Level** — accumulated System growth.
5. **Tier / Stage** — the realized structural power of the vessel.
6. **Mastery / Achievements** — can unlock Class and Race evolution without being reducible to Level alone.

**Tier is the primary vessel gate.**

Level growth can approach a stage ceiling, but raw Level gains do not automatically force a stage or Tier breakthrough.

### Stage-expression rule

If Level growth would push a Legion member beyond the safe expression range of the current stage:

- the vessel does **not** automatically advance;
- excess growth can be **held/banked**;
- the member must still achieve appropriate vessel development, mastery, stabilization, breakthrough conditions, or reconstruction.

This rule is especially important for high-growth members such as **Valeria, Lyra, and Serena**.

Alok gains permanent free Attribute Points from genuine persistent-Legion progression:

Only progression achieved after Legion recognition counts.

Persistent Legion members:

They are **not ordinary necromancer templates**.

# 3. Manifestation / Reconstruction Rules

Alok's Eternal Cycle has progressed beyond conventional summoning.

## Early manifestation

Early Alok could only cheaply anchor souls into skeletal or incomplete vessels because:

- Death Authority was easier to express than Life Authority;
- biological reconstruction was expensive;
- Mana capacity was limited;
- Divinity was limited;
- Soul / Life / Death integration was immature.

## Current manifestation

By Trial One, Alok can reconstruct a sufficiently intact soul into a complete biological supernatural body from the beginning.

### Serena establishes the current milestone

Serena is the first major case where Alok deliberately:

- preserved an ancient coherent soul;
- asked for consent;
- selected a lower current Tier to make rebirth affordable;
- preserved S-rank potential;
- preserved historical evolutionary potential;
- preserved memories and identity;
- rebuilt complete vampiric biology;
- rebuilt Mana pathways;
- rebuilt blood-energy circulation;
- restored regeneration;
- removed artificial imprisonment defects;
- reinforced resistance to hostile control/sealing;
- integrated her with the Eternal Legion without erasing autonomy.

This is no longer "summoning undead."

It is:

> **reconstructing living beings from their souls.**
