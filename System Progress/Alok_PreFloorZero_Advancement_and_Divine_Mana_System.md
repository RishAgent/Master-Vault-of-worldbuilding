# Alok Pre-Floor-Zero Advancement and Divine Mana System

> [!summary]
> This note locks Alok's main progression during the months between Earth's return and the first Floor Zero cycle.
>
> The order is:
>
> **Silver breakthrough → late Beginner Silver → Three-Star → Flame affinity → Frost affinity → manual elemental understanding → Divine Mana creation → Divine Power stat → self-created Divine Flame and Divine Frost Skills**
>
> Alok does not spend this period primarily teaching others.
>
> He focuses on himself, his family, magical theory, and advancement.
>
> He may occasionally enter WAHO training Gates, help with dangerous situations, and casually kill low-Tier monsters in ways that become viral online.

---

# 1. Main Arc Purpose

The pre-Floor-Zero Earth period is not idle time.

It is Alok's first sustained period of self-directed magical development after Judgment.

His focus shifts from:

> surviving Trials

to:

> understanding how his power actually works.

This arc establishes Alok as more than a powerful user of inherited or System-granted abilities.

He begins becoming:

# **a creator of magical principles and Divine Skills**

---

# 2. Progression Order

The current intended sequence is:

1. advance from Bronze saturation to **Beginner Silver**
2. process some banked EXP after the cap restriction is removed
3. refine the new Silver vessel
4. reach **late Beginner Silver**
5. raise Base INT enough to begin deliberate Third-Star meditation
6. form **★★★ Three-Star**
7. integrate **Flame affinity**
8. integrate **Frost affinity**
9. refuse to depend on Flame/Frost Skill Scrolls
10. learn both elements manually
11. create ordinary elemental spell structures
12. study Mana, Divinity, and spell creation with Azrakel and Seraphae
13. independently create **Divine Mana**
14. unlock the **Divine Power** stat
15. create an independent Divine Flame Skill
16. create an independent Divine Frost Skill
17. enter Floor Zero carrying his own self-created Divine elemental magic

# 14. Divine Mana — Core Definition

# **Divine Mana**

is:

> Mana that has been successfully fused with Divinity until the Mana itself carries limited Law-interference properties.

It is not merely:

- Mana coated in Divinity
- Divinity surrounding a spell
- an ordinary divine buff

The fusion changes the quality of the Mana itself.

---

# 15. Why Divine Mana Is Different

Mana is magical energy.

Divinity is closer to:

- Authority
- concept
- Law
- divine existence

When fused correctly:

> Mana stops merely producing an effect and begins carrying a fragment of conceptual authority.

Examples:

## Ordinary Flame

Creates:

- heat
- combustion
- fire

## Divine Flame

Can interfere with:

# **the Law / concept of burning**

## Ordinary Frost

Removes:

- heat
- thermal energy

## Divine Frost

Can interfere with:

# **the Law / concept of stillness, freezing, or thermal motion**

This is why Divine Mana can affect phenomena that ordinary Mana cannot.

# 16. Normal Demigod Use of Divine Mana

Most Demigods do not independently create Divine Mana.

A common process is:

1. a Divine parent owns a Divine Skill
2. the parent grants inheritance permission
3. the Skill imprint contains the Divine conversion architecture
4. the Demigod activates the Skill
5. the Skill automatically creates the needed Divine Mana
6. the Demigod uses the effect without necessarily understanding the conversion

Thus:

> **The inherited Divine Skill creates Divine Mana for them.**

They are using a finished divine framework.

They are not necessarily creating Divine Mana independently.

# 19. Resource Distinction

Alok now has three separate energy resources.

## Mana

Used for:

- normal magic
- Skills
- reinforcement
- spell structures
- summoning
- barriers
- elemental casting

Mana regenerates naturally at a comparatively high rate.

---

## Divinity

Raw Divine essence / authority.

Used for:

- Divine reinforcement
- bloodline effects
- Divine Skills
- Divine Mana synthesis
- conceptual authority

Alok has a limited Divinity pool.

Divinity regenerates naturally, but much more slowly than Mana.

### Working recovery rate

# **~50 Divinity per hour**

This value is a current working benchmark and may scale later with:

- Tier
- Stars
- Divine bloodline maturation
- Divine Core development
- Law comprehension
- special environments
- Divine resources

---

## Divine Power

Stored usable Divine Mana.

Divine Power:

- does not regenerate naturally
- must be deliberately created
- is consumed by Divine Skills
- remains depleted until Alok synthesizes more

This makes Divine Skills powerful but limited.

---

# 20. Divine Mana Conversion Ratio

Working ideal conversion:

# **1,000 Mana + 1 Divinity = 1 Divine Power**

Example:

```text
100,000 Mana
+
100 Divinity
=
100 Divine Power
```

The resulting Divine Power represents a standardized quantity of stable Divine Mana.

---

# 21. Conversion Efficiency

The ratio above represents the ideal stable ratio.

Alok may initially be inefficient.

Example early conversion:

```text
2,000 Mana + 1 Divinity
→ 1 Divine Power
```

or:

```text
1,500 Mana + 1 Divinity
→ 1 Divine Power
```

As mastery improves:

- unstable stage: poor efficiency
- early stable stage: moderate efficiency
- mature stage: approaches ideal
- future Divine mastery: may exceed current assumptions

This gives Divine Mana synthesis its own mastery curve.

---

# 22. Divinity Is the Main Bottleneck

Mana eventually becomes abundant for Alok.

Divinity remains limited.

That means Divine Power cannot become free simply because his Mana pool is enormous.

Example:

If Alok has:

- 500,000 Mana
- 120 Divinity

his practical Divine-Power creation ceiling is constrained primarily by:

# **120 Divinity**

not by Mana.

At the ideal ratio, the theoretical maximum is:

# **120 Divine Power**

if he is willing to spend his entire usable Divinity pool.

---

# 23. Divinity Recovery

Current working natural recovery:

# **~50 Divinity / hour**

Example:

If Alok spends 100 Divinity:

- 1 hour later: ~50 restored
- 2 hours later: ~100 restored

assuming normal conditions and no penalties.

This means Divine Power is limited but not so rare that one Divine spell removes him from action for weeks.

It remains a strategic resource.

# 24. Core Divinity vs Usable Divinity

Not all Divinity should be spendable.

Separate:

## Core Divinity

Integrated permanently into:

- soul
- bloodline
- Divine foundation
- Class architecture
- inherited Divine nature

Core Divinity cannot be casually converted.

Consuming it would damage Alok's existence.

## Usable Divinity

The recoverable operational pool.

This is what can be:

- spent
- regenerated
- converted into Divine Power

This prevents Alok from accidentally consuming his own Divine foundation.

# 27. Divine Power Is Not Just Damage

Divine Power does not simply mean:

> more damage.

Its primary distinction is:

# **Law interference**

A smaller Divine spell may defeat or bypass a much larger ordinary spell if the conceptual interaction favors it.

Examples:

- Divine Flame burns things resistant to ordinary heat
- Divine Frost suppresses energy movement ordinary ice cannot affect
- Divine Lightning may interfere with magical conduction or judgment-like principles later
- Divine Life could override normally irreversible biological collapse
- Divine Death could affect beings resistant to normal death magic

Actual effects depend on Skill design and Law comprehension.
