# Attributes

## Core attributes

### STR — Strength
Physical power, force production, lifting capability, striking power, muscular output and related physical performance.

### AGI — Agility
Movement speed, reaction speed, coordination, acceleration, balance, evasive capability and nervous-system performance.

### VIT — Vitality
Durability, stamina, recovery, organ/bone/muscle resilience, life force and—at high Tiers—the vessel's ability to survive its own Mana and Divinity.

### INT — Intelligence / Mana Processing
Not ordinary IQ. INT represents supernatural mental and magical processing: mana capacity/processing, spell computation, magical perception, control and related spiritual-mental capability.

### Mana
A separate energy reserve. Mana capacity and quality are influenced by INT, Star development, Race, Class, Traits, Bloodlines and other progression.

### Divinity
A separate higher-order energy/stat. See [[Divinity]].

## Human baseline

Ordinary physical stats use the following rough anchor:

- **~5:** children, elderly, frail or physically weak people
- **~8:** average healthy adult
- **~12:** well-trained / advanced ordinary human
- **~16:** absolute peak of ordinary humanity

Stat distributions do not need to be balanced. A Peak Ordinary sprinter can have much higher AGI than STR, while an elite strength athlete can have the reverse.

## High-Tier meaning

At high progression, enormous physical stats are not just about punching harder. The body must become strong enough to contain vast Mana, Star transformations, bloodline power, and eventually Divinity. This is why physical statistics can rise into extreme values without becoming narratively meaningless.
