# Dragon Contracts

A powerful dragon does not casually surrender autonomy. The relationship between Tamer and Dragon is defined by the type of contract they forge. High-tier contracts are always mutual.

## Types of Contracts

| Contract Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Companion Contract** | Basic contract. Usually formed with lower-intelligence dragons (Iron/Bronze). Establishes hierarchy and simple communication. |
| **Battle Covenant** | Focused purely on combat synchronization. Often used by military Dragon Riders. High stat sharing, low emotional bleed. |
| **Guardian Bond** | A defensive contract where the dragon pledges to protect a specific location or person. Common with Nature/Earth dragons. |
| **Bloodline Covenant** | A generational contract. The Tamer's descendants and the dragon's descendants remain bound. Common among Imperial human families. |
| **Soul Covenant** | A deep spiritual bond. High Tamer Resonance, shared perception, and emergency power sharing. Extreme emotional backlash if one dies. |
| **True Covenant** | The highest form of mutual partnership. The Tamer and Dragon are functionally a single magical unit. Requires absolute trust. |
| **Temporary Trial Bond** | Imposed by System mechanics. Example: Rayne and Night. Bypasses normal slot limitations but is restricted by the trial's parameters. |

## Tamer Resonance

Resonance measures the depth of the connection. It is built through Bond Strength, Trust, Mana Compatibility, Emotional Resonance, and Combat Synchronization.

High Resonance unlocks:
- Superior shared mana incubation.
- Safer evolution guidance.
- Stat sharing and combined skills.
- Shared perception and telepathy.
- Emergency power sharing (e.g., Tamer using their own mana to fuel a dragon's breath attack).

## Humanoid Manifestation & Contracts

When a Gold+ rank dragon achieves [[Humanoid Form]], the contract dynamics shift:
- **Stat Sharing:** Remains active, though physical stats may compress or shift toward magical precision.
- **Riding:** Usually only possible in Dragon Form, unless the Tamer is riding a massive summoned mana-construct of the dragon.
- **Autonomy:** Highly intelligent humanoid dragons act as true partners—friends, siblings, rivals, or military allies. Treating them as "pets" breaks True Covenants.
