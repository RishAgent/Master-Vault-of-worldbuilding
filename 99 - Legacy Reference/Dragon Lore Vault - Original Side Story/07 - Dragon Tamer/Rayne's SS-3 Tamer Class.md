# Rayne's SS-3 Dragon Tamer Class

> [!rule]
> Rayne possesses the **SS-3 Dragon Tamer** class. This is an absurdly high-ranking class that grants extraordinary POTENTIAL, but it does **NOT** give automatic ownership over powerful dragons.

Rayne's advantage comes from superior knowledge, appraisal, bonding, and resource efficiency. He still needs dragons willing to bond, time, trust, and rare materials to execute his arts.

## Class Perks & Abilities

- **Draconic Evolution Sight:** Unparalleled appraisal capabilities. Reveals dormant ancestry, all evolution paths (Natural, Mutation, Transcendent), exact failure probabilities, and precise resource requirements.
- **+40% EXP Share:** Dragons under his active training receive a massive boost to their accumulated growth.
- **Hidden-Path Discovery:** Can identify Transcendent evolution branches that would be invisible even to S-Rank Tamers.
- **Advanced Incubation & Potential Enhancement:** Can dramatically alter an egg's future ceilings using far fewer resources than normally required.
- **Superior Affinity Balancing:** Can safely cultivate highly unstable multi-affinity hybrids (e.g., Night's Blue Flame / Darkness / Starlight mix).
- **Mutation Stabilization:** Can pause or correct an evolution that is mutating dangerously, saving the dragon's life.

## Covenant Slots

Rayne has **three true Dragon Tamer covenant slots**. 

> [!important] 
> His Phantom Trial Dragon, [[Night]], does **NOT** permanently use one of these slots. Night is bound by Trial mechanics, preserving Rayne's capacity for three permanent real-world partners.
