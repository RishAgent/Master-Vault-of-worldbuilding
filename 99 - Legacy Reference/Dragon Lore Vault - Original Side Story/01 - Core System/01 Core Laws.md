# Core Draconic Laws

## 1. Bloodline Rank Is Not Level

> [!rule] Absolute Core Rule
> Bloodline Rank represents inherited draconic quality. Level represents accumulated growth.

A Level 50 Iron dragon can kill a Level 10 Gold dragon. 
At comparable Level, age, combat experience, physical condition, and environmental advantage, the superior bloodline usually has the advantage. 

Bloodline is potential and quality. Level is accumulated development.

## 2. Evolution Is Conditional

Evolution is never an automatic result of reaching a certain Level. It is a biological and magical shift resulting from a combination of factors:

- Minimum Level and biological maturity
- Bloodline Purity
- Mana-core density and affinity development
- Environmental exposure and diet
- Rare resources or celestial events
- Combat achievements or near-death survival
- Bonded-Tamer influence
- Inherited memories or ancient blood
- Conceptual comprehension at extreme tiers

> [!warning] Evolution Risks
> Evolution may fail, partially succeed, mutate into an unstable form, permanently close other evolutionary routes, or spontaneously create a new lineage.

## 3. Not All Dragons Have Endless Evolution

Some dragons naturally stop at Iron-2, Bronze-1, or Silver-2. A dragon can continue gaining LEVELS even after reaching its maximum Bloodline evolution. A Level 200 Bronze dragon can therefore exist. It is simply a Level 200 Bronze dragon. See [[03 Evolution Ceilings]].

## 4. Rarity and Distribution

The population distribution strongly favors lower ranks.

| Category | Typical Ranks | Rarity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Common** | Iron, Bronze | Most wild dragons. |
| **Uncommon** | Silver | Respected predators. |
| **Rare** | Gold | Masters of regions. |
| **Exceptional** | Platinum | Apex entities. |
| **Exalted** | Diamond | Near-legends, rulers of vast territories. |
| **Civilization-Level** | Epic, Legendary | Beings that define eras or whole worlds. |
| **Multiversal Myth** | Mythical | Concepts made flesh. Unknown even to advanced societies. |

## 5. High-Purity Stage Skipping

> [!canon]
> A dragon may develop so well during egg formation/incubation that it SKIPS early stages. 
> E.g., An Ashscale Drake egg hatching directly as a Bronze-1 Flamehorn Dragon.

This can happen naturally through powerful parents, exceptional purity, ancestral awakening, or rare nesting environments. However, it is also one of the most vital capabilities of an elite Dragon Tamer. See [[Egg Engineering Loop]].
