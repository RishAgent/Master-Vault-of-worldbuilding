# Humanoid Manifestation Mechanics

> [!canon] Core Rule
> Gold-rank dragons and above may become eligible for humanoid forms. This is **NOT automatic**.

Achieving a humanoid form requires maturity, high intelligence, a stable mana core, immense body control, and sufficient mana to compress draconic mass into a smaller shape.

## Progression of Form Control

- **Gold Rank**: Capable of partial or temporary humanoid form. Usually unstable, heavily retaining draconic features (horns, tails, scales).
- **Platinum Rank**: Stable transformation. Can maintain the form indefinitely as long as mana holds.
- **Diamond Rank**: Near-perfect form control. 
- **Epic Rank**: The humanoid form retains major combat capability. An Epic dragon in human shape can still punch through mountains.
- **Legendary / Mythical Rank**: Physical form becomes highly flexible, more a matter of preference than biological limitation.

## Biology of the Humanoid Form

- **Mass Compression**: The massive bulk of a dragon is compressed into dense mana. This makes their humanoid bodies incredibly heavy and durable despite appearing normal.
- **Retained Features**: Most dragons retain slit pupils, unusual hair colors (matching their scales), elemental markings, and a heavy "draconic aura" that lesser beasts fear.
- **Combat Differences**: 
  - *Dragon Form*: Maximum raw power, armor, breath weapons, flight, mass.
  - *Humanoid Form*: Greater precision, smaller target, ability to use humanoid weapons/artifacts, fine magic control, social interaction, mana efficiency.

## Interaction with Dragon Tamers

When a dragon achieves Humanoid Form, the Tamer dynamic shifts. They cannot be treated as pets. They are highly intelligent partners.
- **Covenant Skills**: Stat sharing and Tamer skills remain active regardless of the dragon's current physical form.
- **Evolution Sight**: Rayne's [[Rayne's SS-3 Tamer Class|Draconic Evolution Sight]] can easily penetrate a humanoid transformation to see the true draconic core beneath.
