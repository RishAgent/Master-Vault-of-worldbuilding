# Evolution Resources & Tiers

Dragon Tamers require immense wealth and specific materials to enact [[Egg Engineering Loop|Egg Engineering]] or [[Bloodline Refinement]]. These are broadly categorized into Resource Tiers.

## Tier Classifications

| Tier | Rarity | Description | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Common** | Low | Readily available in nature, used for basic feeding and slight stat boosts. | Fire-aspected coal, low-grade mana crystals. |
| **Uncommon** | Moderate | Requires hunting specific low-tier monsters or harvesting in dangerous terrain. | Wyrmheart Ore, Thunder-struck wood. |
| **Rare** | High | Capable of triggering Bronze/Silver evolutions. Highly contested. | Dragon-Marrow Essence, Bloodline Fruits. |
| **Epic** | Extreme | Capable of forcing Gold/Platinum mutations. Often heavily guarded or restricted by empires. | Ancient Dragon Blood, Star-core shards. |
| **Legendary** | Mythical | Capable of birthing Diamond/Epic evolutions. Items of legend. | Heart of a fallen Leviathan, Perfected Solar Pearl. |
| **Mythic-Scale** | Conceptual | Items that defy reality, required for Transcendent Conceptual evolutions. | A crystallized timeline, the first spark of fire. |

## Categories of Use

- **Catalysts**: Items that trigger an immediate evolution attempt. Highly dangerous if the dragon isn't prepared.
- **Nutrients**: Long-term feed used to build bone, scale, and mana-core density.
- **Purifiers**: Rare items (like Moon Lotus) that strip away conflicting mana without harming the dragon. Used in Bloodline Refinement.
- **Ancestral Echoes**: Items containing the genetic or spiritual memory of an ancient dragon, used to awaken dormant traits in a diluted descendant.
