# Evolution Ceilings

> [!important] Crucial Rule
> **Not all dragons have endless evolution.** Some dragons naturally stop at Iron-2. Others at Gold-1. A dragon cannot arbitrarily reach the Mythical tier simply by leveling up or surviving a long time. 

A dragon can continue gaining LEVELS even after reaching its maximum Bloodline evolution. A Level 200 Bronze dragon can therefore exist.

To prevent the world from becoming saturated with Mythical dragons, every species has defined Evolution Ceilings.

## The Three Ceilings

### 1. Natural Ceiling
The highest rank a bloodline can normally reach through its established, common evolutionary family. This is what a wild dragon will achieve if it lives a long, successful life and gathers normal resources.

### 2. Mutation Ceiling
The highest rank reachable through rare but still biologically plausible branches. This requires unusual environmental exposure, rare dietary components, near-death survival, or the awakening of dormant, diluted ancestry.

### 3. Transcendent Ceiling
A rank reachable **only** through extraordinary intervention. This includes:
- Ancient inheritances or World-class treasures.
- Conceptual affinity awakenings.
- Extraordinary [[Dragon Tamer Overview|Dragon Tamer]] restructuring.
- Direct System blessings or Authority imprints.

> [!example]
> **Cinderback Drake**
> - **Natural Ceiling:** Iron-2
> - **Mutation Ceiling:** Bronze-2
> - **Transcendent Ceiling:** Silver-1
> 
> Even with the greatest Dragon Tamer in existence, a Cinderback Drake cannot become a Mythical conceptual entity without fundamentally destroying what it is and replacing it with something else. It is capped at Silver-1.
