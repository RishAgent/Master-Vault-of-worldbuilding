# Bloodline Purity

> [!rule] Definition
> Bloodline Purity determines how strongly a dragon expresses its CURRENT inherited lineage. It is separate from [[01 Bloodline Ranks|Bloodline Rank]].

A Gold-2 dragon with 32% Purity is a damaged or heavily diluted expression of the Gold-2 lineage. A Gold-2 dragon with 96% Purity is an extremely powerful expression of that exact same lineage.

## Purity Categories

| Purity Range | Category | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0–39%** | Degraded | Lineage is badly diluted, possibly suffering from mana conflicts or physical weakness. |
| **40–69%** | Ordinary | Standard wild expression. Functional but unexceptional. |
| **70–84%** | Strong | Excellent specimen. Often an alpha or pack leader in wild settings. |
| **85–94%** | Exceptional | Rare. Displays traits perfectly, highly resilient. |
| **95–99%** | Near-perfect | Once-in-a-generation wild specimen, or the result of intense Dragon Tamer cultivation. |
| **100%** | Perfect | Perfect current-lineage expression. |

## Effects of 100% Purity

Achieving 100% Purity does **NOT** automatically mean evolution to the next Bloodline Rank. Instead, it unlocks profound biological and magical benefits:

- Increases evolution success rates dramatically.
- Reveals hidden or dormant ancestry.
- Awakens ancient inherited traits.
- Unlocks rare mutation or transcendent evolution routes.
- Strengthens all current inherited abilities to their maximum conceptual limit.
- Guarantees the production of exceptional eggs.

## Bloodline Concentration vs. Purity

> [!rule]
> **Purity** is the lack of dilution/conflict. **Concentration** is the raw volume/density of the ancestral essence.

A dragon may have 98% purity (no conflicting bloodlines) but low ancestral concentration (the actual spark of the ancient ancestor is faint). Conversely, a dragon may have 85% purity (some mixed ancestry) but extremely concentrated ancient blood, making them volatile but powerful.

Tamers use [[Bloodline Refinement]] to increase Purity, and specific rare treasures to increase Concentration.
