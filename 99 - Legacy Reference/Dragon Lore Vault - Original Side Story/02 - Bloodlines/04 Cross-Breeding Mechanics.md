# Cross-Breeding Mechanics

> [!abstract]
> The laws governing the artificial and natural hybridization of distinct draconic elements.

## The Principle of Hybridization
In the wild, dragons of different elemental affinities rarely interact peacefully. However, through the intervention of Dragon Tamers or extreme environmental pressure, two distinct bloodlines can breed. 

When Dragon A (Affinity X) and Dragon B (Affinity Y) produce an egg, the egg undergoes intense, localized mana-friction.

### The Rank Jump
If the egg survives the conflicting mana within it (which is rare), the resulting creature is born as a **Hybrid Species**. Because it combines the genetic and magical potential of two separate lineages, a successful hybrid is almost always born **one full Tier higher** than its parents' baseline.
*   **Example**: An Iron-1 [[Ashscale Drake]] (Fire) mating with an Iron-3 [[Frostburrow Drake]] (Frost) can yield a Bronze-2 [[Boiler Drake]] (Thermal).

## Stability and Sterility
Hybrids are incredibly potent, possessing access to compound elements (like Steam, Magnetism, or Plasma) that purebloods cannot easily reach. 

However, they suffer from extreme genetic instability:
1.  **Short Evolutionary Trees**: Most hybrids only have one or two stages of evolution before their conflicting mana prevents further Ascension. They hit their Natural Ceilings very early.
2.  **Sterility**: The vast majority of 1st-generation hybrids are sterile and cannot breed to produce more hybrids. 
3.  **Ultra-Rare Multi-Hybrids**: Very rarely, a hybrid *is* fertile and breeds with a third element. These "Further Combinations" (e.g., a 3-element dragon) are so rare they are considered cryptids, often mutating into unique, solitary species with bizarre, unpredictable affinities.

## Common Tamer Combos
Tamers often utilize lower-tier dragons specifically as breeding stock to secure powerful mid-tier hybrids.
*   **Fire + Frost** = Thermal Lineage
*   **Storm + Earth** = Lodestone Lineage
*   **Fire + Nature** = Pyrophyte Lineage
