# Night

## Overview
> [!abstract]
> Night is Rayne's Phantom Trial Dragon. He is currently a Silver-3 Noctis Azurewing Wyrm with immense potential for stellar evolution.

## Bond Mechanics
Because Night is a **Phantom Trial Dragon**, he does **NOT** permanently occupy one of Rayne's three true covenant slots. This allows Rayne to dedicate immense resources to Night's growth during the Trial without sacrificing his real-world taming capacity.

## Evolution Status

**Current Target Evolution:** [[Noctis Starfire Dragon]] (Gold-1)

**Evolution Strategy:**
Night is currently undergoing a highly complex affinity balancing act orchestrated by Rayne's SS-3 Tamer class.
To evolve, Night requires:
1. Five separate nights of Moon Essence.
2. Compatible Starfire resources.
3. Darkness resources.
4. Blue Flame resources.
5. Impeccable Bloodline Purity maintenance.
6. A specific evolution catalyst.

> [!warning] Affinity Danger
> Night's emerging Starlight affinity is volatile. If Starlight becomes too dominant, he will suffer biological instability. If Darkness collapses, the Starfire evolution will fail, potentially mutating him into an Abyssal form instead. Rayne must carefully regulate these inputs.
