## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Eternal Chorus Dragon is a creature of pure harmonic resonance. Its song is so profoundly powerful that it can rewrite the emotional state and collective willpower of entire civilizations.

## Appearance
Sleek and highly reflective, its body looks like polished silver and crystal. Resonant cavities line its neck and chest, acting as organic amplifiers. When it sings, its body vibrates, shedding light in kaleidoscopic patterns corresponding to the pitch and tone of its melody.

## Abilities
- **Civilization's Anthem**: Sings a frequency that blankets a vast region, forcefully implanting euphoria, utter despair, or absolute loyalty into the minds of millions.
- **Shatter-Pitch Breath**: A concentrated beam of sound waves that perfectly matches the resonant frequency of any target, instantly atomizing solid matter.
