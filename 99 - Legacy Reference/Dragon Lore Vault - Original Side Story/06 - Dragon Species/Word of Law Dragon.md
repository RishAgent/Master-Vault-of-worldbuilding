# Word of Law Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The ultimate, terrifying conclusion of artificial dragon engineering. An entity of pure logic and magic that seeks to impose absolute order on reality.

## Appearance
It does not look like a living creature. It appears as a massive, floating, interlocking puzzle box of geometric crystal and glowing golden runes, occasionally shifting into a vaguely draconic shape.

## Abilities
- **Axiom**: It can declare a "Law" (e.g., "Fire does not burn here," or "Gravity is reversed"). As long as it has the mana to sustain the calculation, the universe must obey the Law.
- **Absolute Nullification**: By perfectly calculating the mana frequency of an incoming attack, it can generate an exact counter-frequency to erase the attack from existence instantly.
