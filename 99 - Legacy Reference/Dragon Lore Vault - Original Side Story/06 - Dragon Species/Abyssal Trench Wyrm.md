## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Abyssal Trench Wyrm is a terrifying deep-sea predator adapted to the crushing pressures of the ocean floor. It utilizes hypnotic bioluminescent lures to draw massive prey into the pitch-black depths before striking.

## Appearance
A serpentine monstrosity with no wings, its body is highly flexible and coated in a slimy, pressure-resistant membrane over inky-black scales. Along its spine and jutting from its jaw are fleshy appendages that glow with an eerie, rhythmic blue-green bioluminescence. It has no visible eyes, relying entirely on sensory pits and vibrations.

## Abilities
- **Bioluminescent Lure**: Projects hypnotic light patterns that entrance marine life and even unwary sailors, drawing them inevitably toward its jaws.
- **Crushing Coils**: Can wrap its serpentine body around prey, constricting with force sufficient to crumple steel submarine hulls as if they were tin cans.
