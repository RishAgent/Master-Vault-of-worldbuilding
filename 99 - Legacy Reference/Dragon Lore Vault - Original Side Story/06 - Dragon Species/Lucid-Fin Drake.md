## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A bizarre, semi-ethereal aquatic drake that stalks the shipping lanes of the open ocean. It feeds on the vivid dreams of sleeping sailors, occasionally dragging their minds into chaotic underwater dreamscapes.

## Appearance
The Lucid-Fin Drake looks like a cross between a serpent and an eel, with a bioluminescent hide that shimmers with impossible, shifting colors that strain the waking eye. Its fins are vast, flowing, and semi-translucent, resembling drifting silk in the water. As it swims, it leaves a trail of faint, glowing mist that smells faintly of ozone and sleep-incense.

## Abilities
- **Slumbering Wake**: As the drake swims alongside a ship, its bioluminescence and rhythmic movements project a hypnotic field, forcing a supernatural drowsiness upon any crew members who observe it.
- **Dream-Dive**: It can physically breach the boundary between reality and the dreamscape of a sleeping victim nearby, consuming their nightmares and leaving them in a state of profound, exhausting paralysis upon waking.
