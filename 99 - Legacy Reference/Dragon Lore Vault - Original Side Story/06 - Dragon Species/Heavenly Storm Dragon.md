# Heavenly Storm Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A disaster-class dragon that does not ride the storm—it *is* the storm. Its mere awakening can cause continental hurricanes.

## Appearance
An elongated, serpentine Heavenly Dragon with six limbs and no physical wings. It flies purely through levitation and wind mastery. Its scales are blinding white and crackle with continuous, contained lightning.

## Abilities
- **World-Cell Generation**: Can spawn a supercell thunderstorm capable of covering a country in hours.
- **Divine Lightning**: Breathes white lightning that carries the 'Light' affinity, making it purifying and capable of incinerating dark mana or curses instantly.
