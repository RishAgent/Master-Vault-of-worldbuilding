## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Somnium Dragon is a profoundly sapient creature that resides primarily within the collective unconsciousness. It wields the power to fashion persistent pocket dimensions out of dream-stuff, often trapping the minds of the unwary.

## Appearance
Its physical form is highly unstable in the waking world, appearing as a shifting kaleidoscope of iridescent colors and translucent scales that seem to blur at the edges. It possesses grand, sweeping wings that look like star-filled night skies, and its eyes hold the mesmerizing depth of a lucid dream.

## Abilities
- **Dreamscape Forging**: Can create permanent enclaves within the Dream Realm. These pocket dimensions operate on rules dictated entirely by the dragon, serving as its true lair and sanctuary.
- **Mind Tether**: Capable of putting physical beings into a deep coma, pulling their consciousness into its dreamscape where they can be interrogated, consumed, or studied.
