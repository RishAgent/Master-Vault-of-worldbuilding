# Wildfire Drake

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A fast, aggressively spreading drake that leaves a trail of unextinguishable fire wherever it runs.

## Appearance
A lean, four-legged drake. Its scales are made of black obsidian and petrified wood. Its mane and tail are literal trails of continuous flame.

## Abilities
- **Blaze Trail**: It moves at incredible speeds, and the ground it touches instantly ignites, creating walls of fire to trap its prey.
- **Thermal Updraft**: Uses the heat from its own fires to create wind currents, allowing it to jump massive distances despite lacking wings.
