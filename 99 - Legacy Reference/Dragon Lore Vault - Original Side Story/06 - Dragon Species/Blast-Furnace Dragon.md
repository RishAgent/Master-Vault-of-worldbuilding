# Blast-Furnace Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A living siege engine. Its internal temperature is so high that it constantly vents black smoke from gaps in its armor to avoid exploding.

## Appearance
A heavily armored True Dragon. Its wings are rigid, making it a poor flyer, used mostly for gliding or intimidation. It has prominent vent-like structures along its spine.

## Abilities
- **Smog Screen**: Can vent blinding, toxic black smoke from its spine to cover a massive area.
- **Slag Breath**: Breathes a continuous stream of semi-molten iron slag that hardens as it cools, trapping enemies.
- **Overheat**: Temporarily boosts its physical speed and strength by risking internal core damage.
