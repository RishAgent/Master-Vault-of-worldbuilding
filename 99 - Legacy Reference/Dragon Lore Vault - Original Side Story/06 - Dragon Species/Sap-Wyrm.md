# Sap-Wyrm

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A small, incredibly sticky wyrm that relies on trapping its prey rather than overpowering it.

## Appearance
A legless wyrm that looks like a giant, moving blob of golden-brown tree sap. 

## Abilities
- **Sticky Trail**: Leaves a trail of fast-hardening sap wherever it slithers.
- **Sap Spit**: Spits a glob of glue-like substance to bind a target's legs or wings.
