## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Glacial Epoch Dragon is an Emperor-class entity that serves as a walking extinction event. Its mere awakening can trigger full-blown ice ages across entire planets.

## Appearance
Vastly larger than a mountain range, it looks like a living glacier of impossibly dense, blue-white ice. Its majestic wings span horizons, and every exhale seeds the atmosphere with endless blizzards. It moves with a slow, godlike grace, entirely unbothered by the trivialities of lesser beings.

## Abilities
- **Epoch Genesis**: By unleashing its full power, it plunges the global climate into an immediate and devastating ice age, freezing oceans and continents alike.
- **Absolute Annihilation Breath**: A beam of cold so profound that it permanently shatters the atomic structure of whatever it hits, erasing it from existence entirely.
