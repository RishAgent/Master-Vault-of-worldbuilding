# Shard-Glass Drake

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A fragile but incredibly elusive subterranean drake that uses its perfectly flat scales to bounce light and confuse predators.

## Appearance
A slender, angular drake. Its scales look like shattered mirrors glued back together. In the dark, it is invisible; in the light, it is blinding.

## Abilities
- **Refraction Flare**: Absorbs ambient light and fires it back as a blinding, concentrated laser.
- **Mirror Image**: Uses light manipulation to project up to three false copies of itself in its immediate vicinity.
