## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Gravecrown Dragon represents an absolute mastery over the veil between life and death. Acting as a sovereign of the dead, it perpetually sustains and commands a massive, standing army of undead minions.

## Appearance
This terrifying entity appears as a draconic avatar of the reaper. Its flesh is translucent, revealing a skeleton forged of dark, soul-infused metal. A literal crown of ghostly, wailing flames floats above its horned head. Its wings are composed entirely of dense, suffocating shadows rather than physical membranes.

## Abilities
- **Legion of the Damned**: Radiates a permanent aura that effortlessly reanimates any corpse within several miles, integrating them instantly into a hive-mind army under its telepathic control.
- **Soul Sever**: Its claws can physically grasp and sever the connection between a living body and its soul, killing mortals instantly without leaving a physical wound.
