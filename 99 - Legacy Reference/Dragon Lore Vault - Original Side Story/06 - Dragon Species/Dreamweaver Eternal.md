# Dreamweaver Eternal

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The absolute apex of the Dream lineage. A Dreamscape Titan that has merged completely with the Dream Realm, becoming its living architecture. Every dream that every creature has ever had, across all worlds, occurs within this dragon's body. It is the stage upon which the subconscious performs.

## Appearance
It has no singular appearance because it *is* every appearance. It is the shifting landscape of all dreams simultaneously. When it chooses to manifest a "body," it appears as a dragon woven from aurora and starlight, its form constantly dissolving and reforming as the dreams of billions of creatures shift around it.

## Abilities
- **Dream Sovereignty**: Total, absolute dominion over the Dream Realm. It can enter, alter, or destroy the dreams of any sentient being across any world simultaneously.
- **Manifest Dream**: Can pull any dream — any concept that has ever been imagined by any mind — into physical reality permanently. If someone has dreamed of a perfect weapon, this dragon can make it real.
- **Nightmare Imprisonment**: Can trap any being in an eternal, inescapable dream. The victim's physical body withers while their consciousness loops through customized nightmares forever.
