# Noctis Azurewing Wyrm

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A highly mobile, nocturnal wyrm that utilizes intense blue fire and darkness to hunt. Known for its incredible aerial acceleration and potential for stellar evolution.

## Appearance
Sleek, elongated wyrm body with two powerful rear legs and expansive, dark-blue wing-arms. Scales resemble midnight blue obsidian. Their manes and wing-edges constantly emit a faint, heatless blue aura in the dark.

## Size
Building-sized when fully grown.

## Abilities
- **Azure Flame Orb**: Condenses extremely hot, concentrated blue fire into explosive orbs rather than a continuous breath.
- **Night Veil**: Bends darkness and ambient shadows around itself for near-perfect aerial camouflage at night.
- **Aerial Acceleration**: Can use micro-bursts of blue flame to propel itself at supersonic speeds for short distances.

## Combat Style
Hit-and-run aerial bombardment. It uses Night Veil to remain unseen, drops Azure Flame Orbs, and uses Aerial Acceleration to dodge retaliation.

## Tamer Compatibility
Extremely loyal but hard to impress. They require a Tamer who understands nocturnal rhythms and can supply extremely pure fire and dark mana.

## Tamer Development Opportunities
A skilled Tamer can guide the Noctis Azurewing toward the highly volatile **Noctis Starfire Dragon** evolution by balancing its Blue Flame and Darkness while carefully introducing Starlight. If the balance fails, the dragon will mutate into an Abyssal Azure Dragon, losing its celestial potential entirely.
