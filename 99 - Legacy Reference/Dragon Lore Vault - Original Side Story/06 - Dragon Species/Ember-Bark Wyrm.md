# Ember-Bark Wyrm

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A parasitic hybrid that requires a forest fire to hatch. It is literally made of burning wood.

## Appearance
A small wyrm that looks like a charred, glowing log. The cracks in its "bark" reveal the intense fire burning inside.

## Abilities
- **Spontaneous Combustion**: Can ignite its own wooden armor to drastically increase its physical damage, at the cost of its own health.
- **Ash Cloud**: Exhales thick, choking ash to blind enemies and hide its movements.
