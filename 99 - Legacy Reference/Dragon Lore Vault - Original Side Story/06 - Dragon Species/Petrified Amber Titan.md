# Petrified Amber Titan

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> If an Amber Tomb Dragon survives long enough under the intense pressure of tectonic plates, the amber crystallizes into diamond-hard petrified wood/amber. It regains mobility as a walking mountain.

## Appearance
A colossal, lumbering Titan whose body is composed entirely of petrified, glowing amber and compacted bedrock. 

## Abilities
- **Tectonic Stomp**: By slamming its massive legs into the ground, it can trigger localized earthquakes that shatter cities.
- **Resin Core**: Its heart is a core of pure, liquid time magic. If the armor is somehow breached, this core instantly crystallizes whatever strikes it, freezing the attacker in time forever.
