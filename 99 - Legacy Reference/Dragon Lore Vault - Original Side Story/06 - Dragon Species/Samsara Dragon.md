# Samsara Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A dragon that has transcended the binary of Life and Death entirely. It does not grant life or deal death — it controls the *wheel of reincarnation* itself, choosing what every soul becomes in its next life. It is judge, jury, and ferryman.

## Appearance
An Ancient Dragon with two perfectly mirrored halves. Its left side is a blossoming garden of living flesh, flowers growing from its scales, eyes bright with new life. Its right side is skeletal, wreathed in ghostly blue flame, its eye socket a portal into the afterlife. Where the two halves meet, reality shimmers and distorts.

## Abilities
- **Karmic Judgment**: Can read the complete karmic history of any soul and assign its next incarnation. It can condemn a hero to be reborn as an insect, or elevate a worm to be reborn as a king.
- **Wheel Turn**: Can forcibly trigger the death and immediate reincarnation of any living being, resetting them to a new body with no memories. This is not killing — the soul continues. It is simply *moved*.
- **Deathless**: Cannot be permanently killed. If destroyed, it reincarnates within 7 days in a new body, retaining all memories and power. The only way to stop it is to destroy the concept of reincarnation itself.
