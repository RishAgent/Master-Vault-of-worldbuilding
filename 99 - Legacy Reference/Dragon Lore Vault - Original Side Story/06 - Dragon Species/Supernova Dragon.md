# Supernova Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A Stellar Corona Dragon that has consumed so much stellar energy that its own core has become a self-sustaining fusion reactor. It doesn't harness the sun — it *is* a sun. When it dies, it detonates with the force of a supernova, sterilizing entire solar systems.

## Appearance
A blinding, impossibly radiant Ancient Dragon. Its body is a contained stellar explosion — roiling plasma constrained into a draconic silhouette by its own gravitational field. Its wings are solar flares, each one large enough to engulf a moon. It cannot be looked at directly; observers are blinded permanently within seconds.

## Abilities
- **Solar Core**: Its internal temperature exceeds 15 million degrees. Physical contact is impossible — matter sublimates into plasma miles before reaching its body.
- **Coronal Mass Ejection**: Can launch continent-sized waves of superheated plasma at targets, scorching entire hemispheres of a planet.
- **Supernova Death**: Upon death, its core collapses and detonates in a stellar explosion. The blast radius encompasses an entire star system. This makes killing it effectively a suicide mission for entire civilizations.
