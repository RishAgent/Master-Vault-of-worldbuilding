## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Hadal Abyss Dragon rules the most lightless, crushing depths of the world's oceans. It is an Emperor Dragon of absolute pressure and void, entirely alien to the surface world.

## Appearance
Its leviathan form is largely composed of living, pressurized darkness. It has no eyes, navigating via omnipresent sensory control over the water itself. Faint, ghostly bioluminescence outlines its colossal, serpentine body, which is covered in scales that can withstand the weight of entire oceans.

## Abilities
- **Abyssal Crush**: Increases the local atmospheric or water pressure to hadal levels, instantly flattening mountains and crushing armies into paste.
- **Voidwater Breath**: Exhales a stream of super-dense, light-devouring water that annihilates matter and creates localized black holes.
