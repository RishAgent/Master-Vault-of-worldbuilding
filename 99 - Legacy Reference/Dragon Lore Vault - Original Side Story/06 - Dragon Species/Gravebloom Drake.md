# Gravebloom Drake

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A bizarre, terrifying, yet entirely natural drake that metabolizes decay. It is the beginning of the delicate Life/Death duality lineage.

## Appearance
A heavily built, wingless drake. Its scales are dull bone-white and obsidian black. Strange, bioluminescent pale flowers—Graveblooms—grow directly out of the soil trapped in the crevices of its armor. 

## Size
Rhino-sized.

## Anatomy
Slow and deliberate. Its blood is a thick, sap-like substance that rapidly coagulates and sprouts vegetation when exposed to air.

## Abilities
- **Corpse Moss**: Can rapidly grow necrotic moss over fresh kills to begin the digestion process externally.
- **Decay Resistance**: Entirely immune to rot, disease, and standard death-mana curses.
- **Death Sense**: Can perfectly locate the dying or recently deceased within a massive radius.

## Combat Style
Attrition. It is slow but nearly impossible to kill with conventional magic. It outlasts opponents, letting its ambient necrotic aura weaken them while it regenerates via Corpse Moss.

## Tamer Compatibility
Extremely difficult. Most Tamers are repulsed or actively poisoned by its presence.

## Tamer Development Opportunities
Requires a Tamer who understands perfect equilibrium. If fed too much Life mana, it dies of cancerous overgrowth. If fed too much Death mana, it rots into a mindless undead. Perfectly balancing both forces evolves it into the much more powerful **Nethergarden Dragon**.
