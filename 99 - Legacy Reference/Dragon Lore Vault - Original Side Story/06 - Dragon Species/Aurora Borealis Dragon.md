# Aurora Borealis Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A dragon of such immense size and power that its very existence generates the planetary auroras. It swims through the planet's magnetic field rather than the air.

## Appearance
Massive beyond comprehension, but possessing almost zero physical mass. It looks like a ribbon of dancing, colored starlight and plasma stretching across the sky.

## Abilities
- **Magnetic Annihilation**: Can flip the localized magnetic field to crush or repel entire armies.
- **Solar Flare Breath**: Unloads the raw, unfiltered radiation of a star. Bypasses physical armor completely.
- **Intangibility**: Weapons pass straight through its plasma body unless enchanted with incredibly high-tier Darkness or Space magic.

## Lore
Often worshipped as gods of the sky. They are entirely aloof from terrestrial concerns unless the planet itself is threatened.
