# Worldsong Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A dragon whose voice is indistinguishable from the fundamental frequency of the world. It can alter reality simply by singing a different truth.

## Appearance
A majestic, multi-winged True Dragon covered in feathers of spun gold and silver. It is constantly surrounded by a halo of visible, vibrating sound waves.

## Abilities
- **Reality Rewrite**: By singing the "Chord of Fire," it can ignite an ocean. By singing the "Chord of Peace," it can force an entire battlefield into a deep sleep. 
- **Soul Resonance**: Can speak directly to the soul of any creature, bypassing all mental defenses.

## Lore
They are the ultimate diplomats and historians of the draconic world. It is said that when a Worldsong Dragon dies, the planet itself goes silent for a day.
