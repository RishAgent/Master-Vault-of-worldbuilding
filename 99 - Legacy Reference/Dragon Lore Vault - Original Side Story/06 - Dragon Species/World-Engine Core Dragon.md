# World-Engine Core Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A dragon that acts as the beating mechanical heart of a volcanic region. It does not just consume metal; it manufactures it internally at a molecular level.

## Appearance
An incredibly massive Emperor Dragon. Its scales are no longer just armor; they resemble impossibly complex, interlocking gears, plates, and exhaust vents running on pure magma-blood.

## Abilities
- **Gravity Well**: It can manipulate local gravity to pull massive chunks of ore directly into its maw, or crush enemies under their own weight.
- **Manufactory**: It can regurgitate fully formed, perfectly tempered weapons or structures made from unknown, indestructible alloys.
