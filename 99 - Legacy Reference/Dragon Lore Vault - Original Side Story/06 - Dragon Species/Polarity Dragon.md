# Polarity Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A dragon that has mastered magnetism to such a degree that it borders on gravity manipulation. 

## Appearance
An Emperor Dragon entirely stripped of organic-looking features. It resembles a construct of floating, superconducting rings and jagged metal plates revolving around a core of pure lightning.

## Abilities
- **Orbital Bombardment**: Can pull meteorites from the upper atmosphere down to the battlefield using localized magnetic attractors.
- **Magnetic Repulsion Field**: A passive shield that violently reflects all metal and kinetic attacks.

## Tamer Compatibility
Considered a strategic superweapon. Tamers use them to lift entire castles into the sky.
