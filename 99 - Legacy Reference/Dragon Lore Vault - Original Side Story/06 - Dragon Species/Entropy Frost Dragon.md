# Entropy Frost Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> An incomprehensibly rare mutation of the Glacial Epoch Dragon. It no longer merely freezes water or air — it freezes *entropy itself*, halting all thermodynamic processes in its domain. Where it dwells, time ceases to have meaning.

## Appearance
An impossibly still Emperor Dragon. Its body appears to be carved from a single perfect crystal of absolute-zero ice. No light reflects off it — photons simply stop moving when they reach its surface. It exists as a permanent silhouette against reality.

## Abilities
- **Entropy Lock**: Can permanently freeze a localized area of spacetime, trapping anything within in an eternal, unchanging moment. Not even Mythical-tier dragons can break free without conceptual-level power.
- **Heat Death Aura**: A passive field that drains all kinetic energy from matter within a continental radius. Fires die, hearts stop, winds cease. The world around it becomes perfectly still.
- **Temporal Stasis Breath**: Its breath weapon doesn't freeze the body — it freezes the *timeline* of whatever it hits. The target experiences no time passing and cannot be interacted with until the dragon releases them.
