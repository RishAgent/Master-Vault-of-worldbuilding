## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Rime-Crown Dragon is a majestic and highly territorial drake that claims dominion over high mountain peaks. It establishes its reign by creating elaborate ice crowns on the highest summits, projecting an aura of freezing cold to deter intruders.

## Appearance
This drake possesses a sleek, angular body covered in scales that resemble frosted glass, gleaming with an internal blue light. A jagged crest of unmelting ice protrudes from its skull, resembling a frozen crown, which grows larger as the dragon ages. Its breath exhales as a continuous mist of biting frost.

## Abilities
- **Summit Coronation**: Breathes a specialized frost that rapidly crystallizes into architectural ice structures, used to build its crowns and fortify its nest.
- **Glacial Aura**: Passively lowers the temperature in its immediate vicinity to well below freezing, causing moisture in the air to instantly freeze into deadly icicles.
