## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Reef Emperor Dragon is a sprawling aquatic titan that serves as a mobile habitat for thousands of marine species. It commands the very ecosystems that have rooted and grown upon its colossal back over centuries.

## Appearance
A majestic True Dragon with a broad, flat back that is entirely encrusted with vibrant, living coral reefs, anemones, and kelp forests. Its natural scales are an emerald green and deep oceanic blue, but are rarely seen beneath the calcified layers of the reef. It has paddle-like limbs and a massive rudder-tail.

## Abilities
- **Ecosystem Symbiosis**: Can command the myriad of venomous creatures, stinging polyps, and marine predators that live on its body to attack threats, functioning like a living aircraft carrier of the sea.
- **Calcifying Breath**: Breathes a hyper-saturated alkaline burst that instantly calcifies in the water, turning enemies into statues of living coral and rock.
