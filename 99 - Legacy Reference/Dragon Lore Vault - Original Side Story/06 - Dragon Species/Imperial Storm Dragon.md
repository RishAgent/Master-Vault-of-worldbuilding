# Imperial Storm Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The absolute ruler of the skies. It dictates the weather of the hemisphere it resides in.

## Appearance
A massive, heavily armored Emperor Dragon. Its scales are deep thunderhead-blue, and its horns form a jagged, lightning-bolt crown.

## Abilities
- **Sky's Decree**: An Authority skill. It can command the sky to be clear or to unleash devastation, and the world obeys. No lesser wind or lightning magic can be cast in its presence without its permission.
- **Spatial Step**: Uses lightning to tear holes in space, allowing it to instantly teleport anywhere within its storm system.
