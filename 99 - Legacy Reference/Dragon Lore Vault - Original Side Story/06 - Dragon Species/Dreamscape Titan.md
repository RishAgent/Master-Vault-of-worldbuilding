## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Dreamscape Titan is an Emperor Dragon that controls the entire Dream Realm of a planet. It dictates the subconscious fears and aspirations of all sentient life.

## Appearance
Its body is incomprehensibly massive, formed entirely of shifting thought-matter and conceptual energy. It has no fixed physical shape, appearing differently to every observer based on their deepest desires and worst nightmares. 

## Abilities
- **Reality Override**: Temporarily overwrites physical reality with the rules of the Dream Realm, making the impossible possible and turning physical laws into mere suggestions.
- **Nightmare Core Breath**: Unleashes a wave of pure conceptual terror that fundamentally breaks the minds of entire continents, leaving them in eternal, waking nightmares.
