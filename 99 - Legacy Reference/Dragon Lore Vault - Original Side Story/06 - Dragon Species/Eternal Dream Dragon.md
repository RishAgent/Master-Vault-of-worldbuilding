# Eternal Dream Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A creature that exists almost entirely outside of physical reality. It slumbers in the astral plane, and its dreams shape the probability of the waking world.

## Appearance
Incomprehensible in the physical realm. If forced to manifest, it appears as an ever-shifting, kaleidoscopic ribbon of starlight, eyes, and iridescent mist.

## Abilities
- **Lucid Reality**: The dragon treats reality as a dream it is having. It can alter physics, summon objects from nothing, or erase people from existence by simply "waking up" from the part of the dream where they existed.
- **Time Dilation**: Because time is subjective in a dream, it can trap enemies in mental loops that last millennia while only a second passes in reality.
