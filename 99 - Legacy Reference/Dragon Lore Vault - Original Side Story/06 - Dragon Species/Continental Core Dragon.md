## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Continental Core Dragon is not just a beast that lives in the earth; it is the earth. This Emperor Dragon literally serves as the geological core and anchor of a continent.

## Appearance
Its true size is difficult to measure, as it is fused with the continental plates above it. Its exposed segments are forged from super-compressed planetary core material and liquid iron. Its heart beats in tandem with the seismic activity of the planet, glowing with intense, world-forging heat.

## Abilities
- **Continental Uplift**: Can sink or raise entire landmasses at will, reshaping the geography of the planet on a whim.
- **Worldbreaker Breath**: Exhales a catastrophic surge of planetary core energy that fractures tectonic plates and splits continents in half.
