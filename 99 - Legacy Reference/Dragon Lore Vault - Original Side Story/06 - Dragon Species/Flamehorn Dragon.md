# Flamehorn Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A robust and aggressive True Dragon that marks the transition from terrestrial scavengers to aerial predators in the Fire/Imperial line.

## Appearance
Red and black scales that ripple like cooling lava. It possesses a pair of distinctive, thick, back-swept horns that constantly radiate intense heat and glow orange-white.

## Size
Large horse to small house size.

## Anatomy
Has achieved True Dragon physiology with four legs and large, bat-like wings capable of sustained flight. 

## Abilities
- **Sustained Fire Breath**: Can unleash a continuous stream of true fire, unlike the ash-clouds of its predecessors.
- **Flame Horn Charge**: Focuses immense heat into its horns to melt through rock and armor during a physical charge.
- **Natural Flight**: Achieves true flight, utilizing thermal updrafts to soar for days.

## Combat Style
Prefers aerial superiority. It dives to strike with Flame Horn Charge or circles out of reach while bathing targets in Sustained Fire Breath.

## Tamer Compatibility
Excellent. They are the backbone of many draconic cavalries due to their loyalty and relatively straightforward elemental needs.

## Tamer Development Opportunities
A Tamer can manipulate their diet to push them toward **Magmaheart Dragon** (by feeding them extreme-density Earth/Fire ores) or risk a demonic mutation into **Infernal Horn Dragon** by exposing them to corrupted mana.
