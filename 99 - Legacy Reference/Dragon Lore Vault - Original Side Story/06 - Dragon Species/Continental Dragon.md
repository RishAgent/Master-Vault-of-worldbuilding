## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Continental Dragon is a behemoth of absurd proportions, so vast that sleeping individuals are frequently mistaken for hills or small mountains. Its very heartbeat resonates through the bedrock of continents.

## Appearance
Its scale is incomprehensible, possessing a dense hide indistinguishable from ancient, moss-covered bedrock and mountainous terrain. Trees and vegetation often grow on its dormant body. When it moves, entire forests are uprooted by the shifting of its stony, slate-grey scales. Its eyes burn like deep underground magma chambers.

## Abilities
- **Continental Shift**: By bracing itself and exerting its full strength, it can physically alter the landscape, creating ravines, shifting riverbeds, or forcing up new mountainous terrain over hours of effort.
- **Gravity Well**: Can vastly increase the gravitational pull in its immediate vicinity, grounding flying adversaries and crushing lesser foes under their own weight.
