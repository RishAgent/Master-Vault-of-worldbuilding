# Storm Eternal

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The rarest mutation of the Storm lineage. A Tempest Archon Dragon that was struck by its own lightning so many times that its body converted entirely to living plasma. It no longer generates storms — it IS the atmosphere's wrath given permanent, sentient form.

## Appearance
A colossal dragon made entirely of self-sustaining lightning and plasma, wrapped in a permanent supercell thunderstorm that acts as its "body." Its eyes are ball lightning. Its roar is a continuous sonic boom. Rain falls from its wings like blood.

## Abilities
- **Atmospheric Sovereignty**: Total control over every weather system on the planet simultaneously. It can make it rain on one continent while creating a drought on another, all as a casual thought.
- **Plasma Form**: Its body is non-physical lightning. Physical attacks pass through it harmlessly. Only grounding-based or earth-element attacks can disrupt its form.
- **Gigavolt Breath**: A breath weapon that delivers the equivalent electrical discharge of a billion lightning bolts in a single, focused beam. Can vaporize a mountain range.
