## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Tectonic Drake is a colossal, subterranean powerhouse that thrives within the crushing depths of active fault lines. Its mere movement through the earth's crust can trigger localized earthquakes and minor eruptions.

## Appearance
Heavily armored with thick, interlocking plates of basalt and obsidian, this drake resembles a walking mountain ridge. Its legs are stumpy but obscenely muscled, designed for digging and pushing through solid rock. Veins of glowing magma pulse beneath its armor plates, illuminating its path in the deep dark.

## Abilities
- **Fault Fracture**: Strikes the ground with its massive tail or claws, sending directed kinetic shockwaves through the earth that can sunder fortifications or swallow enemies in fissures.
- **Magma Bore**: Exhales a concentrated stream of superheated, pressurized lava that can melt through solid bedrock in seconds, allowing for rapid subterranean tunneling.
