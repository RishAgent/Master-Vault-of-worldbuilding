## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Solar Flare Dragon is a blindingly radiant predator of the upper atmosphere. It harnesses the full spectrum of solar radiation, weaponizing the deadly light and heat of the sun itself.

## Appearance
Its scales are non-existent, replaced by a solid membrane of contained, swirling plasma. It resembles a localized star given draconic shape, radiating intense heat and blinding light. Coronary loops of magnetic energy flare off its back and tail constantly.

## Abilities
- **Radiation Burst**: Emits an omnidirectional pulse of high-frequency gamma and ultraviolet radiation, instantly incinerating unshielded targets and heavily mutating survivors.
- **Plasma Breath**: Exhales a torrent of stellar plasma that rivals the surface temperature of the sun, melting through anything it touches.
