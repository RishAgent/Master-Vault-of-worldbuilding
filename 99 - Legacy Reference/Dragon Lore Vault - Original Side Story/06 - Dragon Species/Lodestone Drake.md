# Lodestone Drake

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A heavy drake that generates a constant magnetic field, inadvertently pulling loose metal debris toward itself.

## Appearance
A squat, powerful drake covered in metallic spikes. It often has swords, armor plating, and rusted shields stuck to its body, acting as makeshift armor.

## Abilities
- **Magnetic Pull**: Can magnetize a target, pulling them violently toward its jaws.
- **Repel**: Can violently reverse its polarity to blast away metal weapons. Excellent against knights and armored infantry.
