# Celestial Solar Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A disaster-class entity. It does not generate fire; it is a miniature sun encased in draconic form.

## Appearance
Its physical scales have mostly burned away, replaced by an outline of blinding, contained plasma. Its wings leave trails of solar wind.

## Abilities
- **Supernova**: Can detonate its own outer layer of plasma to create a localized nuclear explosion, regenerating shortly after.
- **Drought Aura**: Merely flying over a region will boil lakes and wither forests within hours.
