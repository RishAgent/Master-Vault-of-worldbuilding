## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A heavily armored drake possessing an incredibly dense, spherical carapace. It primarily navigates the treacherous inclines of its mountainous habitat by curling into an unstoppable boulder to crush threats.

## Appearance
Despite its name, the Stoneshell Dragon remains biologically classified as a highly specialized drake. It possesses a uniquely domed back covered in interlocking, hyper-dense basalt plates. Its limbs and tail are relatively stubby, designed to tuck perfectly into grooved recesses along its belly. When fully curled, it forms a nearly seamless, multi-ton sphere of jagged stone.

## Abilities
- **Avalanche Roll**: By curling into a defensive sphere, the drake can hurl itself down mountain slopes at terrifying speeds, gaining momentum to become an unstoppable kinetic projectile that can flatten entire forests or crush predators.
- **Seismic Slam**: By rearing up and slamming its immense mass into the ground, it can generate localized tremors strong enough to shatter stone and knock heavily armored foes off their feet.
