# Dragon of the First Flame

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The ultimate culmination of the Fire lineage. It is not just a dragon that uses fire; it is the concept of temperature and combustion made flesh.

## Appearance
It changes depending on the viewer. Sometimes it is a vast, cooling cinder; other times it is a blinding white-hot serpentine form that stretches across the horizon.

## Abilities
- **Conceptual Burn**: Its flames can burn things that are not physical—it can burn time, it can burn memories, it can burn distance.
- **Spark of Genesis**: Can breath life into inanimate ash, instantly creating lower-tier Fire dragons from nothing.
