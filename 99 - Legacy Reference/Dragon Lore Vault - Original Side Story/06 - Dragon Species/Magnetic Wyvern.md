# Magnetic Wyvern

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A wyvern that doesn't use wind to fly. It flies by constantly repelling itself against the magnetic field of the planet.

## Appearance
A sleek, jagged wyvern made of polished dark metal. Electricity constantly arcs between its wingtips.

## Abilities
- **Railgun Spit**: Can pick up rocks or metal chunks in its mouth, accelerate them using magnetic rails along its jaws, and fire them at supersonic speeds.
- **Levitation**: Can hover perfectly still in the air without flapping its wings, making it a perfectly stable aerial weapons platform.
