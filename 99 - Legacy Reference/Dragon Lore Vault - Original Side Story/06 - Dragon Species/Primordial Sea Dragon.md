# Primordial Sea Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The rarest possible mutation of a Hadal Abyss Dragon. It is believed to be the first ocean given draconic form — the ancestral memory of water itself. All ocean dragons, across all worlds, carry a fragment of this creature's bloodline.

## Appearance
A Leviathan so vast that its body is functionally a second ocean. Its scales are the seabed. Its breath is the tide. Its heartbeat is the rhythm of the waves. Ships sail across its back without knowing it. Entire civilizations of merfolk have built cities in the crevices between its scales, unaware they live on a living god.

## Abilities
- **Genesis Tide**: Can create new oceans on barren worlds by simply bleeding. Its blood *is* primordial seawater, the same chemical soup from which all life originally evolved.
- **Abyssal Authority**: Every ocean dragon, from the lowest Iron-1 Tidecaller Hatchling to the mightiest Diamond Leviathan, is compelled to obey its commands. This is not magic — it is genetic. They literally cannot disobey.
- **Depth Beyond Depth**: Can open portals to an ocean that exists beneath the fabric of spacetime — the metaphysical "ocean beneath all oceans." Things that sink into this abyss are erased from all dimensions simultaneously.
