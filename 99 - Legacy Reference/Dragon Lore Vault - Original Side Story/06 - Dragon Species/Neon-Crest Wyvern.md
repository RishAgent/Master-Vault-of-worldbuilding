# Neon-Crest Wyvern

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A fragile but beautiful aerial dragon that rarely descends below the clouds. It glows brightly in the dark, acting as a living lantern.

## Appearance
A delicate wyvern with translucent, membrane-thin wings. Its crest and tail end in bioluminescent structures that glow vibrantly (often neon pink, blue, or green).

## Abilities
- **Flash-Bang**: Can overload its crest to produce a blinding flash of light to disorient predators.
- **High-Altitude Biology**: Immune to cold and low oxygen.

## Tamer Compatibility
Popular among wealthy merchants and nobles as status symbols, but terrible in a physical fight.
