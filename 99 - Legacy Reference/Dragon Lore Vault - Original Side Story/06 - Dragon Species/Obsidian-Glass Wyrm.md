# Obsidian-Glass Wyrm

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> An incredibly rare, unnatural "Further Combination" hybrid. Born from the impossible union of a Magma-Root hybrid and a Mirror bloodline dragon. 

## Appearance
A serpentine wyrm made entirely of liquid obsidian that constantly cools into razor-sharp, reflective glass scales, before melting again. 

## Abilities
- **Void Reflection**: Because it combines heat, earth, and mirror magic, it doesn't just reflect light—it reflects kinetic energy into a localized void dimension, making it entirely immune to physical force.
- **Glass-Cannon**: Despite its defensive hax, its physical body is extremely brittle. If hit by a sonic or resonance attack (which it cannot reflect), it will shatter instantly.

## Lore
It exists as proof that cross-breeding can go beyond two elements, though the results are almost always sterile anomalies that defy traditional evolutionary trees.
