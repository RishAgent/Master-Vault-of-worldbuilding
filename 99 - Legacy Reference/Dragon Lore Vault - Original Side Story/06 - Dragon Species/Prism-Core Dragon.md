# Prism-Core Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A master of illusion and magic reflection. They are highly sought after by wealthy nobles to act as anti-magic guardians for their vaults.

## Appearance
A True Dragon whose wings are composed of hundreds of floating, unconnected crystal prisms held together by a magnetic/magical field.

## Abilities
- **Spell Reflection**: Can physically angle its crystalline wings to bounce low-to-mid tier spells back at the caster.
- **Prismatic Ray**: Fires a beam of light that splits into seven different elemental damages.
- **Optical Camouflage**: Perfect invisibility by bending light entirely around its body.
