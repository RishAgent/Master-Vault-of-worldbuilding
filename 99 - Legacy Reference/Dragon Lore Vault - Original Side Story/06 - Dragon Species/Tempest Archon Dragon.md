## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Tempest Archon Dragon is a colossal apex predator that embodies the unyielding fury of the atmosphere. Known as the stormiest dragon alive, its very existence generates permanent hurricane systems wherever it roams.

## Appearance
This massive True Dragon boasts scales that resemble jagged, overlapping shards of electrified glass. Arcs of chain lightning constantly course through its body, illuminating the deep indigo and violet hues of its hide. Its wingspan is unimaginably vast, creating gale-force winds with every beat, while its eyes burn like the eye of a tempest.

## Abilities
- **Eye of the Storm**: Generates and maintains a permanent Category 5 hurricane around itself, rendering it virtually untouchable by conventional means.
- **Lightning Breath**: Exhales a hyper-concentrated beam of plasma that strikes with the force of a million simultaneous lightning bolts, ionizing the air and vaporizing targets.
