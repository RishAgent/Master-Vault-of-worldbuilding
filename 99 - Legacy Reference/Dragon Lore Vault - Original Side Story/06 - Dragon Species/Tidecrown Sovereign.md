## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Tidecrown Sovereign is a majestic oceanic deity that commands the very currents and tides. It is a terrifying force of nature capable of summoning tsunamis and sinking armadas with a flick of its tail.

## Appearance
An elegant, elongated True Dragon with scales that shift in color from azure blue to seafoam green, mimicking the changing ocean depths. Its crest resembles a crashing wave, and its wings are webbed with a tough, transparent membrane that propels it through water faster than a torpedo. Water constantly orbits its body in zero-gravity spheres.

## Abilities
- **Tidal Domination**: Can manipulate ocean currents and water pressure on a massive scale, creating whirlpools capable of swallowing galleons or summoning devastating tsunamis against coastal targets.
- **Hydro-Cannon**: Condenses thousands of gallons of water into its maw before firing it as a hyper-pressurized beam capable of slicing through mountains and obliterating naval vessels.
