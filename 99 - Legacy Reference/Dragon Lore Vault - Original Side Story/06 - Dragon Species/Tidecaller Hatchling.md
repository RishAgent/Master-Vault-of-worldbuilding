## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A tiny, highly territorial amphibious drake that thrives in the harsh environment of coastal tidal pools. Despite its diminutive size, it possesses a surprising degree of control over small bodies of water and acts as an aggressive ambush predator.

## Appearance
Measuring no larger than a medium-sized dog, the Tidecaller Hatchling possesses sleek, rubbery skin instead of traditional scales, allowing for frictionless movement through water. Its coloration mimics the mottled patterns of coastal rocks and sand, offering perfect camouflage in shallow pools. Thin, webbed fins extend from its forelimbs and tail, enabling rapid bursts of aquatic speed, while its jaws are lined with needle-like teeth designed to pierce crustacean shells.

## Abilities
- **Tidal Surge**: The hatchling can manipulate the water within its immediate vicinity to create forceful, localized currents, capable of knocking prey off balance or propelling itself out of danger.
- **Crushing Jaws**: Despite its size, its jaw muscles are heavily developed, allowing it to easily shatter the hardened carapaces of crabs and coastal mollusks.
