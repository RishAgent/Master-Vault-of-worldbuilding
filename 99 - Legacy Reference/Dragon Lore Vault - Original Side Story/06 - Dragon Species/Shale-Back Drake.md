## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A robust, burrowing drake whose hide is layered with compressed sedimentary rock. It relies on ambush tactics, lying completely still to mimic natural stone formations before striking at passing prey.

## Appearance
The Shale-Back Drake is heavily built, with thick, muscular forelimbs ending in spade-like claws optimized for excavating hard earth. Its scales are entirely replaced by overlapping, jagged plates of hardened shale and sedimentary stone, granting it exceptional durability but limiting its flexibility. Its head is blunt and heavily armored, often used as a battering ram to widen tunnels.

## Abilities
- **Sedimentary Camouflage**: By pressing itself flat against canyon walls or cave floors, the drake becomes virtually indistinguishable from natural rock, masking both its visual profile and thermal signature.
- **Tremorsense**: Lacking acute eyesight, the drake relies on specialized sensory organs along its underbelly to detect the minute vibrations of footsteps through solid stone up to a mile away.
