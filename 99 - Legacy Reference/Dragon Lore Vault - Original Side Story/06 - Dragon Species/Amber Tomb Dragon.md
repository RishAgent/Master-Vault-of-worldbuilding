# Amber Tomb Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A dragon that has willingly entombed itself in a mountain-sized block of indestructible magical amber. It sacrifices all mobility for absolute immortality and defense.

## Appearance
Cannot be directly seen. It exists inside a massive, glowing golden amber structure that looks like a geometric mountain.

## Abilities
- **Absolute Stasis**: The amber prevents time from advancing inside it, making the dragon immune to aging, poison, or magical decay.
- **Resin Tsunami**: Can flood the surrounding miles of terrain with rapid-hardening sap to permanently trap invaders.
- **Guardian Aura**: Projects a massive aura of calming, restorative nature mana that causes ecosystems to flourish around its tomb.

## Tamer Compatibility
Cannot be truly tamed once it reaches this stage. It is treated more like a geographical landmark or a patron deity by local druids and elves.
