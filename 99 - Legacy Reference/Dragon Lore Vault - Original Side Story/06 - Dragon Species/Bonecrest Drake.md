## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A highly adaptable scavenger that supplements its natural defenses by physically integrating the bones of its meals into its own skeletal structure. It thrives in environments saturated with decay and the remnants of violence.

## Appearance
A hulking, low-to-the-ground drake, the Bonecrest has patchy, dull-grey scales that are often overshadowed by jagged plates of assorted bone jutting from its spine and limbs. Through a grim biological process, it calcifies and fuses the remains of its prey directly onto its own hide, creating a jagged, asymmetrical armor. Its snout is elongated and powerful, designed to snap femurs and pry open ribcages with ease.

## Abilities
- **Osteo-Assimilation**: The drake possesses highly specialized stomach acids that partially dissolve consumed bone, which is then rapidly secreted and fused with its outer skeletal plates to reinforce its armor.
- **Necrotic Bite**: Bacteria thriving in the drake's mouth, combined with its latent death affinity, make its bite highly infectious, causing rapid necrosis in living tissue.
