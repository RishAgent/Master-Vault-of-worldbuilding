## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A haunting creature that haunts sites of great slaughter, feeding not just on decaying flesh but on the lingering spiritual trauma of the dead. It serves as a grim custodian of ancient battlefields.

## Appearance
The Tombstone Drake is a gaunt, heavily armored beast whose thick, rectangular dorsal plates strongly resemble weathered grave markers. Its scales are ashen grey and perpetually cold to the touch. It has no visible eyes; instead, its sensory organs are specialized pits along its snout that detect the subtle thermal and ethereal shifts of lingering souls and rotting flesh.

## Abilities
- **Spirit Siphon**: The drake can inhale the spectral residue left behind by violent deaths, converting this sorrow and trauma into potent physical stamina and regenerative energy.
- **Grave's Chill**: It naturally radiates an aura of profound, despair-inducing cold. Living creatures that approach too closely suffer from sudden lethargy, dread, and a rapid drop in core body temperature.
