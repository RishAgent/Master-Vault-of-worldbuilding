# Reed-Horn Wyvern

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A highly social, flock-based wyvern that communicates through complex musical chords generated by its hollow horns.

## Appearance
Feathered wyverns with distinct, long, hollow horns that have multiple holes along the side, resembling a flute or reed instrument.

## Abilities
- **Sonic Burst**: Can exhale sharply through its horns to create a concussive blast of sound.
- **Flock Resonance**: When flying in a group, their combined horn-songs can shatter stone or drive predators insane.

## Tamer Compatibility
Excellent for Tamers who want a scouting flock. The Tamer can learn to decipher their musical notes to gather intelligence over long distances.
