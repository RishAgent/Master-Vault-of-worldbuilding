# Eternal Winter Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A creature that embodies the freezing of all things. It brings not just cold, but the absolute cessation of molecular movement.

## Appearance
A massive, jagged Ancient Dragon covered in armor of unbreakable True Ice. It exudes an aura of cold so intense that the air around it turns into snow instantly.

## Abilities
- **Absolute Zero Breath**: A breath weapon that doesn't just freeze water, but freezes mana and kinetic energy. Things struck by it become so brittle they shatter into dust.
- **Glacial Stasis**: It can freeze itself or others in a block of ice where time does not pass, allowing it to survive world-ending cataclysms by simply skipping them.
