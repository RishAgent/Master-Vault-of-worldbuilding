## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A small, wingless frost wyrm that uses ambient atmospheric moisture to continually generate its own defensive plating. It is most active during freezing dawns, hunting among frosted alpine vegetation.

## Appearance
Serpentine and slender, the Dewscale Wyrm possesses translucent, pale blue scales that glisten with a perpetual sheen of moisture. As temperatures drop, this moisture freezes into a beautiful, fractal-patterned armor of solid ice that thickens along its spine and flanks. When its ice armor is fully formed, the wyrm resembles a crawling, articulated icicle.

## Abilities
- **Frost Armor**: The wyrm naturally exudes an aura of extreme cold, freezing the morning dew and ambient moisture directly onto its scales to create a regenerative layer of hard ice armor.
- **Glacial Spit**: It can expel a concentrated stream of supercooled liquid that instantly freezes solid upon contact with air or a warm-blooded target, restricting movement.
