# Pyrophyte Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A walking ecological disaster. It turns any environment it inhabits into a desolate ash-waste.

## Appearance
A large True Dragon. Its wings resemble the skeletal, burning branches of a dead tree. Magma drips constantly from its maw, acting as a twisted version of tree sap.

## Abilities
- **Magma Roots**: It can plunge its claws into the earth, injecting magma into the soil to erupt as volcanic geysers beneath its enemies.
- **Ash Resurgence**: If killed, its body turns entirely to ash. If that ash is not completely scattered or purified by water magic, it will regrow from a single ember within a month.
