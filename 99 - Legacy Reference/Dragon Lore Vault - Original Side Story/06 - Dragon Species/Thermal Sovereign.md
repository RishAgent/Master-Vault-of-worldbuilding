# Thermal Sovereign

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The absolute limit of the Fire/Frost hybrid line. It has perfectly stabilized the conflicting mana, allowing it to control the boiling point of the environment around it.

## Appearance
A majestic True Dragon whose scales are translucent, showing the violently swirling red and blue mana streams circulating through its veins. It is constantly surrounded by a thick, obscuring mist.

## Abilities
- **Boiling Blood**: It can instantly raise the temperature of the moisture in the air to boiling, scalding entire armies just by standing near them.
- **Flash Freeze**: Conversely, it can instantly halt the molecular movement of the vapor it produces, dropping the temperature locally to create razor-sharp ice constructs from the mist.
