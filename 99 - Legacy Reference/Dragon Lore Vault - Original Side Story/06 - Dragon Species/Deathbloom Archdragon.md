## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Deathbloom Archdragon is a terrifying arbiter of mortality, holding mastery over the delicate balance of existence. It possesses the sovereign authority to permanently kill an immortal being or permanently resurrect the fallen.

## Appearance
Its body is a stark dichotomy; one half is comprised of lush, blooming flora and radiant, golden scales, while the other is skeletal, wreathed in ash and necrotic fungi. An aura of wilting and blooming flowers perpetually surrounds it, signifying the unending cycle of life and death it governs.

## Abilities
- **Mortal Decree**: Can sever a being's connection to life completely, bypassing resurrection magic, or restore a destroyed soul to a new, permanent body.
- **Cycle's Breath**: A dual-stream breath attack that simultaneously rots physical matter to dust while wildly overgrowing biological material until it ruptures.
