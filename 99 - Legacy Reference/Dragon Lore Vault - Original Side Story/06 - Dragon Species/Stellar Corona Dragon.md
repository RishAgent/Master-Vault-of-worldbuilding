## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Stellar Corona Dragon is a cosmic Emperor Dragon that makes its lair within the burning coronas of stars. It wields the catastrophic power of nuclear fusion and stellar death.

## Appearance
A titanic entity of blinding, pure white plasma and dark matter. It possesses a crown of erupting solar flares and wings that span tens of thousands of miles. Its very presence distorts gravity and emits heat capable of melting terrestrial planets.

## Abilities
- **Supernova Trigger**: Can destabilize a star, forcing it into a premature supernova, or unleash a miniature supernova explosion from its own body.
- **Fusion Core Breath**: Exhales a hyper-dense beam of raw nuclear fusion that punches clean through planets and disintegrates matter at the subatomic level.
