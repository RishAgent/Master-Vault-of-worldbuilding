# Abyssal Firestorm Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The absolute zenith of a demonic mutation within the Fire lineage. It combines corrupted flames with hurricane-force winds to spread devastation globally.

## Appearance
A monstrous, six-winged True Dragon. Its scales are obsidian black and bleed dark red, demonic fire. Its mere presence causes the air pressure to drop, signaling the arrival of a firestorm.

## Abilities
- **Hellfire Hurricane**: It can spin its six wings to generate a continent-spanning hurricane composed entirely of unextinguishable, demonic fire.
- **Mana Combustion**: It can ignite the ambient mana in the air around an opponent, causing their own spells to detonate in their face.
