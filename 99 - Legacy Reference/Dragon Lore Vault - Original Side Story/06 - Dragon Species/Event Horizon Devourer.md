# Event Horizon Devourer

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A mutation caused by the Darkness affinity utterly consuming the Stellar affinity. It is no longer a creature of starlight; it is a living black hole.

## Appearance
An impossibly long wyrm whose body is entirely invisible. It can only be seen by the gravitational lensing and the accretion disk of crushed matter and blue fire spinning around its "head."

## Abilities
- **Singularity Core**: It pulls everything within a massive radius toward its mouth. Escaping requires spatial magic or speeds approaching light.
- **Matter Eradication**: Anything it consumes is completely removed from existence, breaking the laws of conservation of mass.
