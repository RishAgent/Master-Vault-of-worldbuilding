## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A highly aggressive aerial and aquatic predator that forms a symbiotic relationship with toxic marine life. It dominates shallow tropical waters, using its venomous natural weaponry to subdue larger prey.

## Appearance
The Coral-Spine Wyvern features broad, leathery wings capable of folding tightly against its body to allow for streamlined diving. Along its back and tail, a vibrant, symbiotic coral reef actually grows directly out of its flesh. This living ridge is highly toxic, sporting jagged edges and stinging polyps that deter larger predators. Its scales are typically a mottled blend of vivid turquoise, pink, and orange.

## Abilities
- **Venomous Barbs**: The coral growths on its spine constantly secrete a potent neurotoxin. By thrashing its tail, the wyvern can snap off these razor-sharp coral fragments, embedding venomous shrapnel into attackers.
- **Hydro-Propulsion**: Using specialized gills and its muscular wings, it can forcefully expel water to launch itself out of the ocean at terrifying speeds, transitioning seamlessly from swimming to flight.
