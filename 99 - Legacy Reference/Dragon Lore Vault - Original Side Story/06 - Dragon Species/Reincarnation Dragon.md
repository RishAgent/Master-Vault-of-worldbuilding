## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Reincarnation Dragon is an Emperor Dragon that commands the ultimate cosmic loop. It possesses the terrifying power to reset the life-cycle of an entire species, deciding their evolutionary fate.

## Appearance
A majestic, terrifying entity possessing six wings of alternating pure light and absolute void. Its body constantly dissolves into dust and reforms from blooming energy in a never-ending cycle. A massive halo of souls orbits its horned head.

## Abilities
- **Species Reset**: Can forcibly regress an entire civilization or species back to its primordial state, wiping its history and evolution from the world.
- **Samsara Breath**: An attack that doesn't just kill, but forces the target through thousands of agonizing lifetimes in a fraction of a second, destroying their soul through sheer existential exhaustion.
