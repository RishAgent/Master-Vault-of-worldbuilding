# Ashscale Drake

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A very common, low-tier volcanic scavenger. They are the bottom of the draconic food chain in fire biomes, relying on thick hides and sheer numbers.

## Appearance
Squat, heavy-bodied drakes without wings. Their scales are a dull, matte grey, resembling cooled volcanic rock, with faint orange glows between the joints when angry.

## Size
Dog to horse-sized.

## Anatomy
Wingless. Heavy digging claws. A specialized respiratory system that safely filters toxic ash.

## Abilities
- **Cinder Breath**: Exhales a cloud of blinding, choking hot ash rather than true flame.
- **Heat Hide**: Highly resistant to ambient heat and low-tier fire magic.
- **Stone Grip**: Claws designed to grip sheer volcanic rock.

## Combat Style
Prefers to flee or blind enemies with ash. In packs, they will swarm and bite, using their internal heat to burn whatever they latch onto.

## Ecology
Scavengers. They follow larger Fire Dragons to eat the charred remains of their kills, and consume mineral ash to strengthen their scales.

## Evolution Requirements
Requires prolonged exposure to high-density fire mana, usually requiring them to migrate closer to active lava flows, to evolve into an Emberclaw Drake.

## Tamer Development Opportunities
A high-ranking Tamer can use an Ashscale egg to practice basic [[Bloodline Refinement]], pushing its purity to 99% and skipping the Iron-2 stage entirely to hatch a [[Flamehorn Dragon]] (Bronze-1).
