## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A rare, fragile drake born only in environments saturated with raw magical energy. Its scales naturally arrange themselves into primitive, flickering proto-runes that occasionally trigger unpredictable magical effects.

## Appearance
The Glyphling Drake has a wiry, energetic build with scales that shift in color depending on the local mana density. Across its flanks and wings, clusters of luminescent scales form shifting geometric patterns that vaguely resemble ancient arcane script. These proto-runes pulse with an erratic inner light, flaring brightly when the drake is agitated or feeding on ambient mana.

## Abilities
- **Arcane Discharge**: When threatened, the drake can rapidly channel the latent energy in its proto-runes to unleash a chaotic burst of raw magical force, though it cannot control the exact nature or element of the blast.
- **Mana Sight**: Its eyes lack pupils, glowing with a solid, pale light that allows it to visually perceive the flow of mana in the environment, easily locating hidden fonts or enchanted objects.
