# Logos Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A transcendent mutation of the Omniscience Construct Dragon that has moved beyond merely *storing* spells to *being* the fundamental language of magic itself. It is the living grammar of the System. When it speaks, reality parses its words as commands.

## Appearance
It has no fixed physical form. It manifests as a colossal, serpentine arrangement of burning golden runes that constantly rearrange themselves into new configurations. Each configuration is a different spell. Observers who stare at it too long find the runes burned into their retinas, granting — or cursing — them with involuntary spellcasting.

## Abilities
- **Word of Creation**: Can speak new physical laws into existence. If it says "gravity reverses here," gravity reverses. This is not an illusion or a force — the fundamental constant is *rewritten*.
- **Erase**: Can speak a thing's True Name backwards, causing it to be retroactively removed from history. The target didn't die — it simply never existed.
- **Living Spellbook**: Any mage who bonds with a Logos Dragon gains access to every spell that has ever been or will ever be cast, but the cognitive load of this knowledge drives most to madness within hours.
