# Worldspine Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The absolute apex of the Earth lineage. A Continental Core Dragon that has sunk so deep into the planet's mantle that it has fused with the planetary core itself. It IS the planet's spine — the geological engine that drives all tectonic activity. If it dies, the planet's core goes cold and all geological activity ceases forever.

## Appearance
It cannot be "seen" in any conventional sense. It is a dragon-shaped mass of compressed iron and nickel at the center of the planet, superheated to 6,000°C, spinning at differential rates to generate the planet's magnetic field. When it rarely surfaces — which causes extinction-level geological upheaval — it appears as a dragon made of molten iron, taller than mountain ranges, dragging continents behind it.

## Abilities
- **Tectonic Command**: Controls every earthquake, volcanic eruption, and continental drift on the planet. All geological activity is its heartbeat.
- **Magnetic Fortress**: Generates the planet's magnetic field. It can collapse this field at will, exposing the planet to lethal solar radiation within hours.
- **Core Eruption**: Can force the planet's core material to the surface, creating a supervolcanic eruption capable of triggering a mass extinction event.
