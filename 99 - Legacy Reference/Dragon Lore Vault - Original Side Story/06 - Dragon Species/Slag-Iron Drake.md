# Slag-Iron Drake

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A heavy, lumbering drake that consumes raw ore. Often hunted for the high-quality refined metal it leaves behind in its droppings.

## Appearance
Looks like a blocky, wingless lizard made of rusted iron plates. Its mouth glows with internal heat when it opens it. 

## Size
Rhino-sized.

## Abilities
- **Molten Bite**: Can bite through solid rock and most low-tier armor by superheating its jaws.
- **Plate Armor**: Its scales are literal iron, providing excellent physical defense but making it incredibly slow.

## Tamer Compatibility
Excellent for defensive vanguards, but their diet makes them incredibly expensive to feed.
