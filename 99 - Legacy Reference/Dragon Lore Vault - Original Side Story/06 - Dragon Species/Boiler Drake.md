# Boiler Drake

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A short, extremely dense drake that constantly vents scalding steam from vents on its back to prevent its internal organs from detonating.

## Appearance
Looks like a cross between a fiery lizard and an iron stove. Its scales are metallic and grey, heavily scarred by the intense heat and cold warring inside it.

## Abilities
- **Scald Blast**: Breathes a cone of superheated vapor that bypasses physical armor and severely burns the respiratory system.
- **Overpressure Dash**: Uses its steam vents to propel itself forward in short, explosive bursts of speed, completely ignoring its heavy weight.
