# Fractal Glass Sovereign

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A master of defensive geometry. It exists simultaneously in multiple places by reflecting its own mass across minor spatial folds.

## Appearance
A True Dragon whose body resembles a perfectly cut, multi-faceted diamond. Its form is constantly shifting in a fractal pattern, making it impossible to determine its true size or location.

## Abilities
- **Fractal Presence**: It can attack from five different angles simultaneously, as each reflection possesses physical weight for a fraction of a second.
- **Absolute Refraction**: Completely immune to all directed energy and magic attacks, bouncing them harmlessly away in fractal patterns.
