## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Worldmantle Dragon is a terrestrial titan that lives within the planet's mantle, swimming through oceans of magma. Its colossal body is quite literally composed of shifting tectonic plates, giving it unimaginable durability.

## Appearance
It looks less like a biological creature and more like a geographic feature come to life. Its scales are vast slabs of basalt and obsidian, glowing from within with veins of molten rock. When it moves, the earth grinds and trembles, and its physical form constantly reforms as the "plates" on its back subduct and erupt.

## Abilities
- **Tectonic Shift**: Can cause massive earthquakes and volcanic eruptions by simply adjusting its posture or shifting its tectonic scales.
- **Mantle Breath**: Unleashes a continuous torrent of superheated core magma, capable of melting through the strongest magical barriers and reducing entire cities to slag.
