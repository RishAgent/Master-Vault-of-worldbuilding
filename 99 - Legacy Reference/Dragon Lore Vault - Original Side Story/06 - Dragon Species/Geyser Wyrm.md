# Geyser Wyrm

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A long, serpentine hybrid that lives mostly underground or in deep boiling lakes, surfacing only to ambush prey with jets of high-pressure water.

## Appearance
A pale, eyeless wyrm. Its body is smooth to allow it to slip through rock fissures, and its mouth is designed like a pressure nozzle.

## Abilities
- **Water Cannon**: Can compress boiling water in its throat and fire it at speeds capable of cutting through solid steel like a waterjet cutter.
- **Thermal Vision**: Totally blind, but can see the heat signatures of all living creatures in a miles-wide radius.
