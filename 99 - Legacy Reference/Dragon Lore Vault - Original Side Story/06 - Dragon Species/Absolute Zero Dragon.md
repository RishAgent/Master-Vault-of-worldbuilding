## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Absolute Zero Dragon is an apocalyptic entity of cold, capable of reducing environmental temperatures to a state where molecular motion ceases. Within its domain, the sheer lack of thermal energy practically freezes time itself.

## Appearance
Its body is a mesmerizing array of hyper-dense, translucent ice crystals that refract light into pale, ghostly auroras. The dragon appears almost ethereal, with an anatomical structure that defies standard biology. Frost cascades from its maw like a continuous mist, and its mere presence causes the air to shatter and splinter.

## Abilities
- **Thermal Void**: Drains all heat from the surrounding area, approaching absolute zero and halting all chemical and physical reactions.
- **Time-Freeze Domain**: By completely arresting molecular motion within a certain radius, it effectively freezes time for anything caught in its grip.
