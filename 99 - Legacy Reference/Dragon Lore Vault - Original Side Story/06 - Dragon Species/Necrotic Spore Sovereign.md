# Necrotic Spore Sovereign

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A civilization-ending threat. It is the apex of the Fungal family before it ascends into a true mythic plague. 

## Appearance
A bloated, floating monstrosity held aloft by pockets of necrotic gas. Its body is covered in massive, pulsing fungal structures that constantly rain toxic spores.

## Abilities
- **Spore Hivemind**: Every creature infected by its spores becomes part of a hivemind controlled by the Sovereign. It can see through their eyes and detonate their bodies remotely.
- **Absolute Decay**: An aura that rusts metal, rots wood, and turns stone to dust within miles of its presence.
