# Sunscale Imperial Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A majestic, prideful dragon whose scales literally shine like the midday sun. They demand tribute from lesser creatures.

## Appearance
An Emperor Dragon body type: possessing six limbs (four legs, two massive wings) and a literal crown of hardened, glowing horns that resemble a sunburst. 

## Abilities
- **Solar Flare**: Breaths highly concentrated Light/Fire magic that burns at the temperature of a star's surface.
- **Aura of the Sovereign**: A passive aura that forces weaker monsters and humans to kneel under the weight of its Authority affinity.
