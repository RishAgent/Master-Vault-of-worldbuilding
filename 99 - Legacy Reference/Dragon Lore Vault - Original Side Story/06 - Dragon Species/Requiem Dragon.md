## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Requiem Dragon is a somber drake that acts as a conduit between the realms of the living and the dead. Its haunting, melodic roars possess the eerie ability to temporarily animate the recently deceased.

## Appearance
Its body is gaunt and skeletal, tightly wrapped in leathery scales the color of old bone and dried blood. Ethereal, wispy tendrils of soul-stuff leak from the gaps in its armor. Its vocal cords are highly developed, vibrating visibly in its neck when it prepares to unleash its signature song.

## Abilities
- **Dirge of Awakening**: Sings a localized requiem that forcefully tethers departing souls back to their bodies, raising them as temporary, mindless undead thralls under its loose control.
- **Soul Syphon**: Can breathe a cone of spectral mist that drains the vitality of living creatures, converting it into temporary sustenance and accelerating decomposition.
