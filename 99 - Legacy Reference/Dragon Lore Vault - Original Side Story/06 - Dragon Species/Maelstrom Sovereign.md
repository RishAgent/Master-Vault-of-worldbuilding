## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Maelstrom Sovereign is the unquestioned ruler of the open oceans. It commands entire ocean gyres, warping the currents of the world to create inescapable whirlpools of doom.

## Appearance
Its sleek, hydrodynamic body is covered in bioluminescent, sapphire-blue scales that shimmer like the sun reflecting off the deep sea. It has multiple sets of fin-like wings that propel it through water with terrifying speed. Its tail ends in a crescent fluke that can generate tidal waves with a single flick.

## Abilities
- **Gyre Command**: Controls major oceanic currents, allowing it to summon colossal whirlpools that can swallow armadas whole.
- **Crushing Depths Breath**: Exhales a high-pressure jet of hyper-compressed water that can slice through enchanted armor and stone like a surgical blade.
