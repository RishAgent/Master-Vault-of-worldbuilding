## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Akashic Library Dragon is a living archive of magical history. Its mind is an endless repository containing a perfect copy of every spell ever cast in the universe.

## Appearance
Its scales resemble ancient, inscribed parchment, heavily etched with glowing runes of unfathomable complexity. It lacks traditional eyes; instead, its head is adorned with floating geometric glyphs that perceive the world through raw magical data. Its wings are spectral, formed from swirling letters and incantations.

## Abilities
- **Omni-Cast**: Can simultaneously cast hundreds of different spells it has cataloged from history without needing somatic or verbal components.
- **Runic Unraveling**: Emits a localized pulse that deconstructs any incoming spell into base mana, rendering the dragon immune to almost all magical assaults.
