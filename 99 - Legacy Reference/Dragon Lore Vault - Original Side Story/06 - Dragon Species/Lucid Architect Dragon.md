## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Lucid Architect Dragon blurs the line between reality and the subconscious. It possesses the terrifying ability to draw constructs, creatures, and logic from the dream realm and make them physically real for short periods.

## Appearance
Its form is highly unstable, shifting slightly in texture and color as if viewed through a heat mirage. Its wings are made of nebular gas and starlight, and its eyes contain entire spinning galaxies. It moves with a surreal grace, leaving a trail of impossible geometric shapes in its wake.

## Abilities
- **Dream Manifestation**: Can rip nightmarish entities or impossible architecture directly from the minds of nearby beings and force them into physical existence.
- **Slumber Breath**: A wave of somnolent mist that forcibly severs a target's consciousness from their body, trapping them in a comatose state while the dragon devours their dreams.
