## Summary
> [!abstract]
> The Blizzard Tyrant Dragon is the undisputed apex predator of the polar regions, possessing the terrifying power to command extreme winter weather. Its presence alone brings about unnatural, devastating blizzards that can freeze entire armies.

## Appearance
A sleek, aerodynamic True Dragon coated in jagged, diamond-hard ice scales that reflect light blindingly. Its wings are massive and semi-translucent, trailing perpetual flurries of snow. Two massive, swept-back horns of pure permafrost adorn its regal head, channeling the bitter winds.

## Abilities
- **Absolute Zero**: Breathes a concentrated beam of absolute cold that instantly flash-freezes anything it touches, shattering magical wards and molecular structures alike.
- **Whiteout Domain**: Passively generates a miles-wide blizzard wherever it flies, reducing visibility to zero, dropping temperatures to lethal levels, and masking its devastating dive-bomb attacks.
