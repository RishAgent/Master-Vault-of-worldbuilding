## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A relentless apex predator of the frozen wastes that brings the storm with it wherever it flies. Its mere presence drastically alters local weather patterns, ensuring its prey is weakened by the cold before it even strikes.

## Appearance
The Hoarfrost Wyvern is a majestic, terrifying sight, with vast wings covered in razor-sharp, crystalline plumage rather than leathery skin. Its body is lean and predatory, colored in blinding whites and deep glacial blues. A perpetual mist of freezing air billows from its maw and the joints of its wings, leaving a trail of instant frost wherever it touches down.

## Abilities
- **Perpetual Blizzard**: The wyvern's internal core is so cold that it passively generates a localized blizzard within a 50-meter radius, significantly reducing visibility and rapidly draining the heat from any living creature trapped within.
- **Shatter-Roar**: It can unleash a high-frequency, freezing roar that flash-freezes ambient moisture into deadly shrapnel, capable of lacerating foes and shattering brittle, frozen armor.
