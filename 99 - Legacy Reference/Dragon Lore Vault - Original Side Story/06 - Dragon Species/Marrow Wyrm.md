## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A highly disturbing subterranean predator that hollows out graves from below. It possesses a terrifying command over the bones it consumes, using them to create macabre, twitching constructs.

## Appearance
The Marrow Wyrm is a massive, eyeless subterranean serpent covered in sickly, pale-yellow scales that resemble aged teeth. Its head is a terrifying array of grinding mandibles designed specifically to pulverize stone coffins and crush bone into a fine paste. It secretes a thick, black, tar-like substance from its pores, which acts as a conduit for its necrotic magic.

## Abilities
- **Skeletal Puppetry**: By regurgitating its necrotically-charged tar onto scattered bones, the wyrm can spontaneously animate them into jagged, aggressive skeletal constructs that swarm intruders or serve as decoys.
- **Marrow Drain**: Its bite features specialized hollow fangs that instantly liquefy and drain the bone marrow of living targets, causing immediate, excruciating structural weakness in the victim's skeleton.
