# World-Ash Dragon

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A Pyrophyte Dragon that has consumed enough organic matter to stabilize its burning form into pure, indestructible carbon (diamond and graphite).

## Appearance
A terrifying Emperor Dragon that looks like it is carved from a single, massive piece of black diamond. It no longer burns outwardly; all the heat is trapped inside its core. 

## Abilities
- **Diamond Skin**: Its carbonized body is practically immune to all physical and magical attacks. 
- **Volcanic Winter**: Its mere presence fills the sky with so much ash that it blocks out the sun, plunging entire regions into a permanent, freezing dark.
