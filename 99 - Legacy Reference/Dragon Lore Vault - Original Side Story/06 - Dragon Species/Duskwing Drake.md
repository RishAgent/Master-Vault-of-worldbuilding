## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A small, nocturnal predator that hunts primarily by pacifying its prey through specialized soporific pollen. It bridges the gap between the physical and ethereal by feeding on the latent dream energy of those it puts to sleep.

## Appearance
The Duskwing Drake possesses a slender, almost fragile skeletal structure covered in scales that shift between deep violet and midnight blue. Its wings are disproportionately large, resembling those of a moth more than a traditional dragon, and are coated in a fine, shimmering powder. Its eyes are large and bulbous, designed to draw in every mote of available light in near-total darkness.

## Abilities
- **Soporific Dust**: By vigorously flapping its wings, the drake releases a cloud of potent, sleep-inducing pollen. Prolonged exposure causes profound lethargy and eventually deep slumber in most biological creatures.
- **Dream Siphon**: When resting near a sleeping creature, the drake can passively absorb fragments of its dreams, converting the ethereal energy into physical sustenance.
