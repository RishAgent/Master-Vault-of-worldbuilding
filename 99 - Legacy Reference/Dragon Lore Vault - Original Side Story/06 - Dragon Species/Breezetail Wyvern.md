# Breezetail Wyvern

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> A small, incredibly agile wyvern. While fragile, its sheer numbers and speed make it a nuisance in windy regions.

## Appearance
Slender, built entirely for aerodynamics. Covered in extremely fine, overlapping scales that resemble feathers from a distance. Usually pale blue, white, or gray. 

## Size
Large eagle to condor-sized.

## Anatomy
Wyvern body type: two rear legs ending in sharp talons, and two large wing-arms. Very light bone structure.

## Abilities
- **Glide**: Masterful manipulation of air currents allows them to stay aloft with almost zero effort.
- **Gust Claw**: Can compress wind around their talons to extend their slashing range.
- **Keen Sight**: Excellent vision for spotting small prey from high altitudes.

## Combat Style
Hit and run. They cannot take a hit. They dive, slash with Gust Claw, and use the wind to accelerate away immediately.

## Ecology
Flock hunters. They swarm larger prey and strip it bare, or harass larger dragons into dropping their kills.

## Tamer Compatibility
Easy to tame due to their simple needs, but difficult to use in serious combat due to their fragility.

## Tamer Development Opportunities
Excellent subjects for speed-training. A Tamer who can consistently expose them to severe weather can trigger their evolution into a **Galecrest Wyvern**.
