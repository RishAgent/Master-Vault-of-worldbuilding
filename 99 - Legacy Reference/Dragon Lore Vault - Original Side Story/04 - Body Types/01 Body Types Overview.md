# Dragon Body Types

Bloodline and affinities often dictate a dragon's body plan. The classification of a dragon informs its combat style, ecological niche, and physical capabilities. 

> [!note] 
> Size is completely independent of Body Type or Bloodline Rank. A True Dragon could be the size of a dog, while a Drake could be the size of a mountain.

## Common Classifications

- **True Dragon**: Four limbs plus wings. The standard, highly adaptable apex body plan.
- **Drake**: Terrestrial dragon, usually wingless. Often heavily armored or built for sprinting/burrowing.
- **Wyvern**: Two rear legs and wing-arms. Specialized for aerial dominance.
- **Wyrm**: Elongated dragon, sometimes entirely limbless, resembling a massive serpent.
- **Lindwyrm**: Elongated serpentine body with two forelimbs. Often amphibious or subterranean.
- **Amphiptere**: Winged serpentine dragon with few or no legs.
- **Leviathan**: Gigantic aquatic draconic lineage. Built for crushing oceanic pressures.
- **Burrow Dragon**: Subterranean specialist, often with shovel-like claws or drill-horns.
- **Shell Dragon**: Heavily armored turtle-like draconic body.

## Specialized & Archetypal Classifications

- **Feathered Dragon**: Scales mixed with or entirely replaced by mana-feathers (often seen in Wind, Light, or Celestial lineages).
- **Ancient Dragon**: A long-lived lineage characterized by deep inherited memory and immense ambient mana capacity.
- **Imperial Dragon**: An Authority-bearing ruler lineage. Often possesses a physical "crown" of horns or scales.
- **King / Emperor Dragon**: A lineage that can biologically and magically suppress or command lesser dragons of its element.
- **Titan Dragon**: Defined by siege-scale mass, incredible density, and unparalleled durability.
- **Astral Dragon**: Adapted to stellar mana, vacuum, zero-gravity, or deep space environments.
- **Spirit Dragon**: Partially or wholly spiritual body, able to bypass physical barriers.
- **Conceptual Dragon**: A Mythical entity interacting directly with the fundamental laws of reality. Its body is often a manifestation of its concept rather than flesh and blood.
