# Ocean / Leviathan Family

## Summary
> [!abstract]
> Colossal aquatic lineages that rule the oceans. They evolve to withstand crushing abyssal pressures and eventually gain spatial authorities to move bodies of water.

## Ancestry & Origins
Evolved in the deepest trenches where physical mass and water-mana density were the only things that mattered for survival.

## Typical Affinities
Water, Ice, Wind. Higher tiers unlock Darkness (Abyssal branch), Space (Leviathan branch), and Authority.

## Body Type Trends
Starts as agile aquatic Wyrms, evolving into massive, heavily armored Leviathans capable of swallowing ships whole.
