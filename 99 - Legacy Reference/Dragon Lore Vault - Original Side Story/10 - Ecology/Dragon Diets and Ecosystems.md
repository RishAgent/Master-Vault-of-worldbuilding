# Dragon Diets and Ecosystems

Dragons are not merely apex predators; they are often the keystone species that define entire biomes. A dragon's Bloodline Rank and Affinity heavily dictate its ecological role.

## Diets

Different lineages consume vastly different materials to fuel their immense mana cores:
- **Carnivores**: Standard drakes and wyverns. They hunt large beasts.
- **Lithovores**: Earth/Metal dragons that consume ores, gems, and bedrock.
- **Mana-Eaters**: Higher-tier dragons often bypass physical food entirely, absorbing ambient mana, sunlight, starlight, or raw elements (e.g., magma).
- **Concept-Eaters**: Extremely rare. Dream dragons may consume memories; Void dragons consume spatial distance; Death dragons consume the conceptual end of life.

## Ecological Impact

Dragons warp their environments:
- **Ashscale Drakes** act as volcanic scavengers, preventing lava tubes from clogging by eating cooled magma rock.
- **Verdant Dragons** act as forest stabilizers. Their dung and shed scales act as hyper-fertilizer, causing rapid jungle growth.
- **Storm Dragons** alter regional weather patterns simply by existing. A roosting Thunderjaw Wyvern can cause localized endless thunderstorms.
- **Leviathans** manage oceanic mana pressure. Without them, deep-sea trenches would rupture with uncontrolled water-mana.
