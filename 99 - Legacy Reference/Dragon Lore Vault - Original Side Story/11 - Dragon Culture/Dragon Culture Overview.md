# Dragon Culture Overview

> [!abstract]
> For intelligent dragon societies (usually Gold rank and above), survival is not enough. They develop complex cultures based on lineage, power, and territorial history.

## Intelligence Thresholds

- **Iron**: Animalistic to highly trainable.
- **Bronze**: Advanced social intelligence (pack tactics, rudimentary communication).
- **Silver**: Near-human intelligence when mature; capable of understanding complex concepts.
- **Gold**: Fully sapient. capable of speech, complex reasoning, and long-term planning.
- **Platinum+**: Highly sophisticated, often possessing ancient wisdom and massive egos.

## Cultural Elements

### Language
Dragons communicate through several layers:
1. **Instinctive Calls**: For basic threats and mating (Iron/Bronze).
2. **Draconic Body Language**: Horn angles, tail movements, mana pressure flares.
3. **Mana Resonance**: Telepathic or empathic projection (Silver+).
4. **True Draconic Language**: Spoken by Gold+. A rumbling, resonant tongue.
5. **Authority Speech**: Used by Imperial/Emperor dragons; inherently forces compliance in weaker beings.

### Hoards
Different dragons hoard different items based on their affinities, not just gold:
- **Fire**: Heat crystals, magma-forged weapons.
- **Soul/Dream**: Memories, emotionally significant artifacts.
- **Crystal**: Rare unblemished gemstones.
- **Storm**: Highly conductive metals (copper, silver).
- **Ancient**: Knowledge, tablets, historical records.

### Social Structures
- **Bloodline Houses**: Strict hierarchies based on Purity and Rank. A Gold-1 is subservient to a Platinum-1 of the same family.
- **Matriarchies/Patriarchies**: Often dictated by the strongest living elder.
- **Duels**: Formalized combat to settle territory or mating disputes without destroying the environment.
