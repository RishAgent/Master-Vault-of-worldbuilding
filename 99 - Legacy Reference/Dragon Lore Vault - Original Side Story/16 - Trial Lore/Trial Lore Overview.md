# Trial Lore Overview

> [!abstract]
> The System occasionally subjects individuals to Trials. Within these spaces, normal rules of reality, time, and draconic ecology can be temporarily suspended or altered.

## Phantom Trial Dragons

During a System Trial, a Tamer may be temporarily bound to a **Phantom Trial Dragon** (e.g., [[Night]]). 

### Mechanics of a Phantom Bond
- **Slot Preservation**: It does not consume one of a Tamer's true real-world covenant slots.
- **Accelerated Growth**: Time and resources within a Trial are often distorted. A Tamer can push a Phantom Dragon through evolutions that would take decades in the real world in a matter of months.
- **Data Retention**: While the physical dragon might be tied to the Trial instance, the *knowledge* the Tamer gains regarding that specific evolutionary pathway is permanently retained. 

## Trial Environments

Trials can simulate impossible evolutionary conditions:
- **Perfect Celestial Alignment**: Creating the conditions for a Mythical evolution that hasn't occurred naturally in ten thousand years.
- **Mana Density Extremes**: Environments where the air is literally liquid mana, forcing rapid biological adaptation or death.

> [!warning]
> The consequences of failing to balance a dragon's affinities inside a Trial are just as lethal as the real world, though the Tamer themselves might only face a score penalty rather than physical death.
