# Dragon Civilizations

> [!abstract]
> High-tier dragons (Platinum, Diamond, Epic) do not just exist in the wild; they organize and build civilizations that rival or surpass humanoid empires.

## Types of Civilizations

### Dragon Cities
Massive metropolises built into mountainsides or floating islands, scaled for True Dragons and Humanoid-manifested dragons alike. Architecture is often shaped by earth/metal dragons rather than built with tools.

### Sky Kingdoms
Ruled by Storm, Wind, and Celestial families. Accessible only to those capable of sustained flight or spatial teleportation.

### Volcanic Empires
Militaristic societies ruled by Imperial Fire lineages. Often expansionist and highly structured.

### Ocean Leviathan Courts
Deep-sea civilizations built around hydrothermal vents, governed by strict laws of pressure and silence.

### Dimensional Dragon Realms
Pockets of folded space maintained by Void or Space dragons, serving as neutral ground or hidden sanctuaries for ancient lineages.

## Interspecies Relations

Dragon civilizations interact with humanoids in various ways:
- **Hostility**: Viewing humanoids as pests or food.
- **Coexistence**: Mixed cities where humanoid-form dragons and humans trade freely.
- **Worship**: Primitive humanoid societies worshipping local Diamond-rank dragons as gods.
- **Dragon Merchants**: Dragons using humanoid forms to access humanoid economies for rare evolution resources.
- **Dragon Tamer Politics**: Elite Tamers acting as diplomats between human empires and dragon courts. Forced contracts are highly illegal and punishable by death in advanced dragon societies.
