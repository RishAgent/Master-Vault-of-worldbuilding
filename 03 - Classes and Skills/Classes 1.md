# Classes

## Core Concept

A **Class** represents the path a character is developing through their actions, talents, affinities, combat style, personality, achievements, and choices.

Classes do **not** determine what a person is allowed to do.

A character may learn and use any skill they are physically, magically, or mentally capable of using, even if that skill falls outside their Class.

A Fire-oriented Class can still use Nature Bind.
A swordsman can still learn healing magic.
A mage can still learn physical reinforcement or weapon skills.

The difference is efficiency.

> **Skills determine what a person can do.**
>
> **Class determines what they are especially good at becoming.**

---

## What a Class Provides

A Class may provide some combination of:

- bonus stat growth
- preferred stat distribution
- improved growth for related skills
- reduced mana or stamina costs for compatible abilities
- increased effectiveness for compatible abilities
- unique passive effects
- access to Class Skills
- access to Class Evolutions
- improved compatibility with certain weapons or magic
- special combat mechanics
- unique breakthroughs
- evolutionary paths

Classes should influence progression without becoming hard restrictions.

---

## Classes and Level Growth

Personal Rank determines the character's overall growth potential and efficiency.

Race provides natural growth characteristics.

Class determines how part of that growth is specialized.

Example:

### Iron Vanguard

A physically oriented Class may naturally favor:

- STR
- VIT
- AGI

and provide less natural INT growth.

### Flame Arcanist

A magical Class may favor:

- INT
- AGI

while granting bonuses to fire-related magic and spell development.

Two characters with the same Rank and Race may therefore develop very differently because of their Classes.

---

## Classes and Affinity

**Affinity and Class are separate systems.**

Affinity is an innate property of the mana core that converts neutral mana into elemental mana and grants increased effectiveness, resistance, and adaptability toward that element.

A person does not need a matching Class to possess or use an Affinity.

Examples:

- Sword Saint + Lightning Affinity
- Guardian + Earth Affinity
- Flame Arcanist + Nature Affinity
- Assassin + Wind Affinity

A matching Class and Affinity can create strong synergy, but a mismatch is not inherently bad and may even lead to unusual Class Evolutions.

---

## Skill Freedom

Classes do not lock skills.

A character can learn a skill if they meet its requirements.

Possible requirements may include:

- sufficient stats
- sufficient Star rating
- enough mana
- appropriate weapon
- suitable body
- compatible race
- compatible bloodline
- required knowledge
- prerequisite skills
- specific achievements
- special materials
- System rewards

A Class may make some skills easier to learn or stronger to use, but it does not normally prevent unrelated skills.

---

## Class Skills

Some abilities may be tied directly to a Class.

These are known as **Class Skills**.

Class Skills may be:

- granted when obtaining the Class
- unlocked at certain Levels
- unlocked at certain Tiers
- earned through Class Quests
- created through repeated use and specialization
- unlocked after fulfilling hidden conditions
- gained during Class Evolution

Class Skills tend to synergize strongly with the Class.

However, ordinary Skills remain separate and may be learned freely.

---

## Obtaining a Class

Classes are not necessarily chosen from a simple list.

The System evaluates the individual and may consider:

- Race
- Personal Rank
- Affinities
- Traits
- Bloodlines
- Stats
- Skills
- combat style
- achievements
- repeated behavior
- choices
- training
- profession
- Tier
- Star rating
- previous Class development

The System then offers Classes compatible with what the individual has become.

This means two people with similar abilities may receive completely different Classes.

---

## First Class Awakening

The first Class Awakening should occur after the character has had enough time to establish a path.

Current working checkpoint:

> **Level 25**

Level 25 is the new early progression milestone replacing the old Level 20 limit.

At or around this point, the System may evaluate the character and provide Class options.

This is not necessarily automatic.

Certain individuals may:

- awaken earlier
- awaken later
- receive hidden Classes
- fail to qualify immediately
- unlock unique Class Trials
- receive only one compatible Class
- receive an unusually large number of choices

---

## Class Evolution

Classes are not permanent static labels.

As a character develops, the Class can evolve.

Evolution may be influenced by:

- Level
- Tier
- Star rating
- Skill mastery
- Affinity
- Traits
- Bloodline awakenings
- Race evolution
- achievements
- Class Quests
- major battles
- Law comprehension
- Divinity
- choices made by the character

Example:

> Flame Initiate  
> → Flame Adept  
> → Inferno Magus  
> → Lord of the Burning Sky

Another person beginning with a similar Class may evolve differently:

> Flame Initiate  
> → Flameblade  
> → Ashen Knight  
> → Solar War Saint

Class evolution therefore reflects the individual's actual path.

---

## Branching Evolutions

A Class may have multiple possible evolutionary branches.

Example:

### Warrior

Possible developments:

- Guardian
- Berserker
- Duelist
- Knight
- Weapon Master

A Guardian who develops strong Earth Affinity and defensive Skills might evolve toward:

> Stone Guardian  
> → Adamant Bastion  
> → Mountain Sovereign

The Class system should reward what the character actually does rather than force everyone through the same progression tree.

---

## Unique Classes

Rare individuals may develop Classes that do not belong to standard progression lines.

Unique Classes may emerge from:

- unusual combinations of Affinities
- rare Traits
- special Bloodlines
- impossible achievements
- divine heritage
- racial mutations
- unusual Skill combinations
- hidden System conditions

Unique Classes should be rare and meaningful.

They should not automatically be superior in every situation.

---

## Class Rarity

Classes may have rarity or quality grades.

Possible working structure:

- Common
- Uncommon
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary
- Mythic
- Unique
- Divine

This is **not the same as Personal Rank or Tier**.

Class rarity describes how unusual, complex, or powerful the Class path itself is.

A low-Rank individual could theoretically obtain a very rare Class.

A high-Rank individual could begin with a simple Class and later evolve it into something extraordinary.

This system is still open for balancing.

---

## Multiple Classes

Current preferred rule:

A person normally possesses **one primary Class**.

Instead of collecting many unrelated Classes, compatible paths should usually:

- branch
- merge
- evolve
- create hybrid Classes

Example:

Instead of possessing:

- Swordsman
- Lightning Mage
- Wind Mage

a character may eventually develop:

> Stormblade

This keeps Status windows readable and prevents Class accumulation from becoming excessive.

Exceptional circumstances may allow:

- a Secondary Class
- a Profession Class
- a Divine Class
- a unique dual-Class state

but these should be uncommon.

---

## Professions

Non-combat development may use **Professions** rather than combat Classes.

Examples:

- Blacksmith
- Alchemist
- Enchanter
- Healer
- Rune Scholar
- Merchant
- Architect
- Chef

A Profession can develop alongside a primary Class without counting as a second combat Class.

Professions may also evolve.

Example:

> Blacksmith  
> → Mana Smith  
> → Runeforger  
> → Divine Artificer

---

## Class Quests

The System may issue quests related to Class development.

Examples:

- learn a required Skill
- defeat a certain type of enemy
- master a weapon
- survive a dangerous environment
- protect someone
- create a new technique
- reach a specific Tier
- reach a specific Star rating
- defeat an opponent without using a favored ability
- complete a hidden achievement

Class Quests may reward:

- Class Skills
- Class Evolution
- stat growth
- Traits
- items
- titles
- new evolutionary branches

---

## Classes and Tier

Tier measures the current strength and quality of the character's vessel.

Class does not directly determine Tier.

However, Class growth bonuses can help a character reach Tier stat thresholds faster.

A high-Tier character may still possess a relatively simple Class if they have not developed it.

A lower-Tier character may possess an exceptionally rare or powerful Class but lack the stats to fully exploit it.

---

## Classes and Stars

Star rating measures mana-core development and determines the complexity of magic the caster can efficiently support.

Class does not replace Star progression.

A Class may:

- improve mana efficiency
- improve certain types of spells
- help stabilize higher-Star magic
- unlock specialized spell forms

but it does not automatically increase the character's Star rating.

---

## Classes and Divinity

At higher stages, Classes may begin interacting with Divinity, Laws, Domains, and Authorities.

Certain Classes may evolve into Divine Classes.

However, extremely powerful beings may eventually transcend ordinary Class definitions.

A god should not remain limited by a mortal Class label simply because the System once classified them that way.

Possible late-stage progression:

> Class  
> → Advanced Class  
> → Mythic/Divine Class  
> → Divine Path  
> → Law/Domain specialization  
> → Authority

This is a conceptual direction rather than a locked progression formula.

---

## Alok

Alok's Class development should be unusual because of his:

- Divine Child status
- Divinity from birth
- hidden heritage
- unusual Bloodlines
- multiple Affinities
- Soul-related abilities
- eventual connection to Life, Death, and Existence

His first Class should **not immediately reveal everything about his true nature**.

The System may:

- struggle to classify him
- offer unusual choices
- produce hidden results
- generate a unique Class
- create a Class that evolves far beyond its original form

His Class progression should foreshadow what he will eventually become without immediately revealing the full truth.

---

## Design Rules

1. Classes specialize; they do not imprison.
2. Skills remain broadly learnable.
3. Affinity is independent from Class.
4. Personal Rank remains the measure of growth potential.
5. Tier remains the measure of realized vessel strength.
6. Star rating remains the measure of mana-core development.
7. Race, Bloodline, and Traits remain independent systems.
8. Classes should evolve based on what characters actually do.
9. Hybrid concepts should usually become evolved Classes rather than endless Class stacking.
10. Class evolution should feel earned.
11. Rare Classes should be meaningful, not automatically unbeatable.
12. At the highest levels of existence, Classes may eventually be transcended.
