# Alok Elemental Confluence Spellbook
## Eight Affinities — Standalone and Combination Spells

> [!summary]
> After successfully integrating **Light, Lunar, Starlight, and Wind** into his existing elemental framework, Alok now possesses eight stable affinities:
>
> - Blood
> - Lightning
> - Flame
> - Frost
> - Light
> - Lunar
> - Starlight
> - Wind
>
> His Mana Core and three permanent Circles can now support **true elemental confluence**, where multiple affinities are woven into one spell structure rather than merely cast side-by-side.
>
> Current working outputs below are intended for **Beginner Silver / ★★★ Three-Star Alok**.
>
> Affinity bonuses are already included in the listed working damage values unless otherwise stated.

# 2. Elemental Interaction Changes

## Light

Light has been altered by later affinity integration.

It now naturally carries:

- Lunar undertones
- Stellar properties
- stronger purification potential
- flexible radiant expression

Alok may emphasize different aspects of the same Light affinity:

- golden radiance
- silver Lunar radiance
- star-filled celestial radiance

## Wind

Wind acts as more than a standalone element.

It can function as:

- accelerator
- carrier
- compression medium
- propagation enhancer
- elemental amplifier
- movement enhancer

Wind naturally improves the battlefield utility of many of Alok's other elements.

# 16. Combination Spell Philosophy

Combination spells are not simply Spell A + Spell B.

One affinity modifies the fundamental behavior of another.

Examples:

- **Wind + Flame:** Wind accelerates combustion, increases spread, and shapes the Flame body.
- **Wind + Lightning:** Wind creates propagation pathways and improves chain reach.
- **Lunar + Frost:** Lunar suppresses turbulence, stabilizes freezing, and interferes with Mana circulation.
- **Starlight + Lightning:** Starlight creates a stable penetration path and improves compression and accuracy.
- **Blood + Lightning:** Blood provides an internal conductive route.

This is the core identity of Elemental Confluence.

# 21. Canon Locks

- Alok currently possesses eight stable affinities: Blood, Lightning, Flame, Frost, Light, Lunar, Starlight, and Wind.
- Light now carries Lunar and Stellar properties.
- Wind naturally enhances the movement and propagation of other elements.
- Two-affinity spell construction is stable.
- Three-affinity spell construction is possible.
- Four-affinity spell construction is currently unstable.
- Eightfold Confluence is currently catastrophically unsafe.
- Combination spells weave affinities into one structure rather than merely casting separate spells together.
- Listed damage values already include current affinity effects.
- Do not reapply Lightning +120% or other affinity bonuses to listed final working values.
- Divine Skills remain conceptually superior to ordinary combination spells.
