# Valeria — Eternal Legion Tier Evolution

> [!summary]
> **Personal Rank:** A  
> **Current Tier:** Peak Bronze  
> **Current Race:** Restored Valkyrie  
> **Current State:** Advancement-ready — awaiting Silver breakthrough  
> **Legion Status:** Persistent Eternal Legion member  
> **Primary Identity:** Luminous aerial vanguard, protector, future battlefield commander

---

# 1. Core Concept

Valeria's path is built around:

- Valkyrie restoration
- aerial warfare
- luminous Mana
- protection
- frontline combat
- command

Her growth should increasingly express the identity of a true high-order Valkyrie rather than being defined by Level or fixed Class stat packages.

---

# 2. Current Tier

# Peak Bronze

Valeria has reached the final Bronze substage and completed the development needed to prepare for Silver.

Current state:

# Advancement-ready.

She is awaiting the proper Silver breakthrough, stable reconstruction, suitable resources/catalysts if required, and Alok's support where appropriate.

---

# 3. Current Race

# Restored Valkyrie

Bronze marked the restoration of Valeria into a true Valkyrie body rather than an incomplete shade/initiate state.

Current characteristics include:

- stable humanoid Valkyrie physiology
- strong luminous Mana compatibility
- aerial combat potential
- high physical/magical balance
- natural battlefield awareness
- strong resistance to corruption
- protective instincts toward allies

---

# 4. Core Racial Traits

## Valkyrie Physiology

Supports aerial mobility, physical combat, Mana reinforcement, high endurance, supernatural reactions, and weapon synchronization.

## Luminous Mana

Valeria naturally expresses radiant/luminous Mana suited to offense, anti-corruption, barriers, ally protection, and battlefield illumination.

## Aerial Combat Instinct

Her body and Mana pathways are naturally compatible with three-dimensional combat.

## Guardian Instinct

Valeria's evolution strongly favors formation protection, interception, holding battle lines, shielding allies, and counterattacks.

# 6. Silver Breakthrough

Valeria's next evolution is:

# Restored Valkyrie → Valkyrie Warrior

The Silver breakthrough should emphasize:

- stronger aerial movement
- denser luminous Mana
- improved weapon reinforcement
- stronger barriers
- greater battlefield mobility
- higher resistance to corruption
- better command presence
- stronger ally-protection effects

The exact abilities can be defined when the breakthrough occurs.

---

# 7. Long-Term Identity

Valeria should evolve toward an:

# aerial / celestial battlefield commander.

Her identity should combine personal combat ability, protective magic, mobility, battlefield leadership, luminous warfare, and Valkyrie heritage.

At higher Tiers, her exact evolution should increasingly depend on achievements and her own identity rather than a rigid predetermined tree.
