# Eternal Legion — Tier-Based Elite Progression

> [!summary]
> This note replaces the older Level/stat-package-heavy Legion progression model for the major persistent characters.
>
> Going forward, **Personal Rank and Tier are the primary progression references**.
>
> - **Personal Rank** = innate potential / quality.
> - **Tier** = realized vessel power and evolutionary stage.
> - **Race evolution** is tied primarily to Tier breakthroughs.
> - Skills, affinities, mastery, soul quality, and achievements still matter, but they do not need fixed per-Level stat packages.
>
> Exact Attributes, Level-growth tables, Class stat bonuses, and automatic AP packages are removed from these character progression notes.

# 2. Tier-First Progression Rule

Persistent Legion members are no longer tracked primarily through Level-derived stat packages.

Their growth should instead be written through:

- Tier
- substage
- vessel saturation
- Mana-Core saturation where applicable
- racial evolution
- affinity development
- soul development
- Skill mastery
- combat experience
- breakthrough resources
- achievements
- Eternal Cycle reinforcement where appropriate

A character can become stronger within the same Tier without requiring a rigid numerical Level package.

---

# 3. Bronze → Silver Rule

For major Legion members at Peak Bronze:

- reaching Peak Bronze does **not** automatically grant Silver;
- the vessel must be ready;
- refinement/saturation must be complete where applicable;
- racial and soul structures must be stable;
- a Silver breakthrough must still occur.

### Advancement-ready

**Solis** and **Valeria** are ready to prepare for their Silver breakthroughs.

### Not yet advancement-ready

**Lyra** is Peak Bronze but remains **unsaturated** and must continue strengthening/refining her vessel before attempting Silver.

# 5. Design Principle

The Eternal Legion is not a collection of disposable summons.

Its major members are persistent people and living beings whose evolution should increasingly reflect:

- identity
- history
- personality
- soul
- race
- relationships
- achievements
- affinities
- combat style
- personal choice

Tier determines the scale of the vessel.

What they become within that scale should remain individual.

---

# 6. Canon Locks

- Remove fixed Class-stat bonuses from Legion character progression notes.
- Remove mandatory per-Level Attribute-growth packages from these notes.
- Personal Rank remains relevant.
- Tier is the main realized-power reference.
- Race evolution is primarily tied to Tier.
- Serena is currently **S Rank / Beginner Silver**.
- Lyra is currently **S Rank / Peak Bronze / unsaturated**.
- Solis is currently **B Rank / Peak Bronze / awaiting Silver advancement**.
- Valeria is currently **A Rank / Peak Bronze / awaiting Silver advancement**.
- Frosty and Ferno retain their dedicated evolution notes.
- Future demon racial progression should use the same **Tier-first** design philosophy.
