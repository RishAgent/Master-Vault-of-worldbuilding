# Flame Lion — Eternal Legion Evolution Line

> [!summary]
> **Current Race:** Flame Lion Cub  
> **Personal Rank:** A  
> **Current Tier:** Peak Iron  
> **Legion Status:** Bound to Alok's Eternal Legion  
> **Primary Affinity:** Fire  
>
> **Leveling develops the current form.**  
> **Tier advancement evolves the race.**

# 1. Current Identity

The Flame Lion hatched from the second magical beast egg Alok had kept in storage.

It emerged already at:

- **Personal Rank:** A
- **Tier:** Peak Iron
- **Race:** Flame Lion Cub
- **Legion:** Eternal Legion
- **Bond:** Soul-bound to Alok

Current characteristics:

- small, roughly house-cat sized
- warm, fluffy body
- early ember-like mane
- playful and highly affectionate
- instinctively recognizes Alok's family
- quickly bonded with Frosty
- enjoys climbing its parents

# 2. Core Racial Traits

## Flameborn Physiology

Natural Fire-aligned magical-beast body with:

- strong heat resistance
- natural Fire affinity
- improved resistance to hostile flame
- high physical durability
- strong compatibility with Fire reinforcement

## Ember Mane

A developing mane of living Mana-flame.

Current properties:

- warm rather than dangerously hot
- instinctive and emotion-responsive
- can flare during excitement or combat
- harmless to recognized family

## Predator's Body

Enhances:

- strength
- agility
- reactions
- claws
- fangs
- scent
- hearing
- balance
- predatory instincts

## Heat Immunity

Ordinary fire and heat are largely harmless.

Higher-grade magical, conceptual, or Divine flames may still affect it depending on Tier and quality.

## Familial Flame Recognition

The Flame Lion instinctively recognizes beings accepted through its Soul Bond and family bond.

Its passive flames do not harm recognized family unless deliberately overridden.

Recognized family currently includes:

- Alok
- Alana
- Serena
- Lyra
- Frosty
- Solis
- Valeria
- accepted Eternal Legion family

This allows Frosty to safely:

- climb it
- ride it
- wrestle with it
- remain in direct contact with its flames

# 3. Growth Principle

The Flame Lion has two progression layers.

## Level Growth

Leveling improves:

- Attributes
- physical maturity
- flame intensity
- Mana capacity
- Skill mastery
- current racial abilities

## Tier Evolution

Crossing a major Tier causes a racial evolution.

New forms may grant:

- stronger racial Attribute growth
- new Fire properties
- improved regeneration
- larger true body size
- new combat Skills
- elemental authority

> [!important]
> **Personal Rank and racial evolution are separate.**
>
> The Lion is already A Rank, but its Race continues evolving as its vessel advances.

# 6. Bronze Evolution — Embermane Flame Lion

## Tier
**Bronze**

The cub becomes a true combat juvenile.

Expected changes:

- grows toward medium-dog size
- mane becomes visibly composed of living flame
- claws and fangs strengthen
- flame control becomes deliberate

## Abilities

### Ember Claws
Fire-coated claws with greater penetration and burning wounds.

### Flame Pounce
Explosive Fire-assisted acceleration and leap power.

### Ember Roar
Short-range cone of flame, pressure, and sound shock.

### Flamecoat
Controlled flames surround the body.

Effects:

- damages nearby enemies
- improves close-range defense
- remains harmless to family

### Predator's Flame Sense
Detects:

- heat
- Fire Mana
- living-body warmth
- nearby Mana signatures

# 7. Silver Evolution — Blazefang Lion

**Silver**

## Abilities

# 11. Supreme Evolution — Heavenflame Lion

## Tier
**Supreme**

The evolved flame becomes:

# **Heavenflame**

Potential properties:

- burns physical matter
- damages Mana barriers
- destabilizes weaker spells
- consumes compatible elemental Mana
- damages Mana constructs directly

It does **not yet** automatically burn souls, concepts, or Laws.

## Abilities

### Heavenflame Maw

### Heavenfire Territory

### Skyfire Step
Stable aerial movement through controlled flame.

### Heavenflame Armor
Dense living fire behaves as magical armor.

# 13. Compact Form

Starting around **Gold or Platinum**, the Flame Lion should gain:

# **Compact Form**

Allows it to compress its true body into a smaller juvenile companion form.

Purpose:

- indoor mobility
- cuddling
- climbing Alok
- playing with Frosty
- sleeping with family
- preserving its affectionate cub behavior

Compact Form does **not** lower its real Tier.

> [!important]
> Even at extremely high Tiers, the Lion should remain capable of returning to a small, adorable family form.

# 14. Relationship with Frosty

Frosty and the Flame Lion bond almost immediately.

Their elemental contrast is playful rather than hostile.

Frosty:
- Water
- Ice
- minor Life

Flame Lion:
- Fire
- Heat
- magical-beast physiology

Because of Familial Flame Recognition:

- the Lion's fire does not hurt Frosty
- Frosty can ride it safely
- they can wrestle and play
- accidental steam clouds are common
- future combination attacks are possible

Potential family behavior:

- Frosty riding on its back
- Frosty sitting on its head
- Flame Lion chasing Frosty
- Frosty cooling it after overuse
- both climbing Alok simultaneously

# 15. Family Behavior

The Flame Lion remains affectionate regardless of combat growth.

Current behaviors:

- climbs Alok
- sleeps against family
- plays with Frosty
- accepts Lyra's feline affection
- recognizes Alana and Serena as family
- seeks physical contact
- softens its flames around family
- becomes protective if family is threatened

Core identity:

> **A terrifying magical predator in battle.**
>
> **A warm, affectionate cub at home.**
