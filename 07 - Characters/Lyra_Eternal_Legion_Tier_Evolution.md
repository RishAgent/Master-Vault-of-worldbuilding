# Lyra — Eternal Legion Tier Evolution

> [!summary]
> **Personal Rank:** S  
> **Current Tier:** Peak Bronze  
> **Current Race:** Ascendant Genesisborn  
> **Current State:** Unsaturated — not yet ready for Silver advancement  
> **Legion Status:** Voluntary persistent Eternal Legion member  
> **Primary Identity:** Adaptive Mana combatant, morphogenic striker, Alok's wife

---

# 1. Origin

Lyra was originally designated **V25**.

She was engineered as a weapon rather than raised as a person.

After her creator activated the termination protocol, Alok preserved her and integrated her into the Eternal Legion.

The reconstruction preserved her soul, memories, personality, and freedom while removing malicious control architecture, termination mechanisms, and forced behavioral overrides.

Her future evolution should never become another externally imposed design.

---

# 2. Current Tier

# Peak Bronze

Lyra has advanced rapidly because of her S-Rank potential and adaptive physiology.

However:

# she is not yet saturated.

Her Peak Bronze status means she has reached the final Bronze substage, but her vessel still needs further refinement before attempting Silver.

She currently needs continued development through:

- physical-vessel refinement
- Mana-Core refinement
- combat adaptation
- stable Mana circulation
- morphogenic control
- permanent growth
- suitable Bronze-grade Cores and Mana Crystals

Alok should **not** force her into Silver early.

---

# 3. Core Racial Traits

## Genesisborn Physiology

A living adaptive body capable of rapidly responding to Mana, injury, combat pressure, environmental stress, and repeated attack patterns.

## Adaptive Bio-Mana Architecture

Lyra's body acts as a living Mana interface.

It can temporarily generate combat structures such as:

- blades
- hooks
- tendrils
- defensive plates
- shields
- ranged emitters

## Predatory Sensory Suite

Enhanced hearing, scent, motion detection, target tracking, and threat recognition.

## Enhanced Regeneration

Lyra can use Mana to accelerate healing and reconstruct damaged adaptive tissue.

## Morphogenic Combat Adaptation

Her body can change combat configuration rapidly rather than relying on one fixed weapon style.

| **Platinum+** | Self-Determined | No fixed racial tree |

---

# 5. Silver Evolution

When Lyra eventually reaches full Peak Bronze saturation and successfully advances:

# Ascendant Genesisborn → High Genesisborn

Expected themes:

- greater Mana-body integration
- stronger regeneration
- more complex generated weapons
- faster adaptation
- better ranged/close-range switching
- improved sensory processing
- stronger control over biological restructuring

The exact Silver abilities should be defined when the breakthrough happens rather than preloading an inflexible list.

---

# 6. Freedom Principle

Lyra's evolution should be shaped by her own choices, combat style, relationships, emotions, achievements, preferred weapons, Mana development, and identity as a person rather than a product.

Aloris designed V25.

# Lyra decides what comes next.

---

# 7. Eternal Legion Bond

Lyra remains permanently linked to the Eternal Legion, but Alok deliberately preserves her autonomy.

- telepathic communication is available
- passive thought access is disabled
- deep mental access requires permission
- emotional freedom is unrestricted
- cognitive freedom is unrestricted
- disagreement and refusal are permitted
- affection is never forced
