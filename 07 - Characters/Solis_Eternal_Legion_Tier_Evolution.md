# Solis — Eternal Legion Tier Evolution

> [!summary]
> **Personal Rank:** B  
> **Current Tier:** Peak Bronze  
> **Current Race:** Fleshbound Revenant  
> **Current State:** Advancement-ready — awaiting Silver breakthrough  
> **Legion Position:** First persistent Legion member  
> **Primary Identity:** Frontline knight, disciplined soldier, future Legion commander

---

# 1. Core Concept

Solis's progression is fundamentally a:

# restoration path.

He began as an undead skeletal existence.

As his Tier rises, Alok's Eternal Cycle gradually restores body, flesh, biological structure, soul-body integration, human appearance, and eventually full living humanity.

At the same time, Solis develops from a basic soldier into Alok's long-term knight and battlefield commander.

---

# 2. Current Tier

# Peak Bronze

Solis has completed the major Bronze-stage development needed for the next breakthrough.

Current state:

# Advancement-ready.

He is waiting for the correct opportunity/resources/process for:

# Silver.

This should be treated as a true evolutionary reconstruction rather than a simple increase in combat numbers.

---

# 3. Current Race

# Fleshbound Revenant

At Bronze, Solis has regained far more humanoid structure than his earlier skeletal form.

Current characteristics should include:

- stable humanoid frame
- reconstructed musculature/flesh
- greatly improved physical coordination
- strong durability
- reduced conventional undead weaknesses
- increasingly natural facial expression
- stronger soul-body synchronization

He is close to human—but not fully restored yet.

---

# 4. Core Traits

## Soulbound Frame

Solis's body remains anchored directly to his restored soul structure, giving high structural stability, strong endurance, reliable weapon control, and compatibility with continued reconstruction.

## Disciplined Combat

Solis specializes in swordsmanship, formation combat, defensive positioning, battlefield discipline, command execution, and protecting weaker Legion members.

## Persistent Revenant Physiology

He remains difficult to disable through injuries that would incapacitate a normal human.

This trait should gradually transform as he returns to complete biological life.

# 6. Silver Breakthrough

Solis's next major evolution is:

# Fleshbound Revenant → Soulborn Human

The Silver breakthrough should restore:

- fully natural external human appearance
- more complete organs and biological systems
- greatly improved soul-body synchronization
- normal warmth and circulation
- more natural sensation
- stronger recovery
- further reduction of undead vulnerabilities

He is still not completely biologically restored until Gold.

---

# 7. Gold Restoration

At Gold:

# Soulborn Human → Fully Reborn Human

This is the completion of Solis's original restoration arc.

Everything afterward represents evolution beyond restored humanity rather than merely recovering what was lost.

---

# 8. Long-Term Identity

Solis should gradually become:

- Alok's First Knight
- disciplined battlefield leader
- commander of persistent Legion forces
- protector of the household
- symbol of what the Eternal Cycle can restore rather than merely resurrect
