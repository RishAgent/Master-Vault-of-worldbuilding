# Serena — Eternal Legion Tier Evolution

> [!summary]
> **Personal Rank:** S  
> **Current Tier:** Beginner Silver  
> **Current Race:** High Pureblood Vampire  
> **Former Name:** Mercy Adulia  
> **Legion Status:** Voluntary Eternal Legion member  
> **Primary Identity:** Blood combatant, blood mage, alchemist, Alok's wife
>
> Serena was reconstructed by Alok directly from her preserved soul into a complete living vampiric body. She is not an undead summon.

---

# 1. Current Identity

Serena is an ancient vampire whose soul survived long after her original body was lost.

Alok reconstructed her while preserving:

- memories
- personality
- soul identity
- S-Rank potential
- vampiric bloodline
- historical evolutionary potential
- autonomy

Her current Silver vessel is a continued restoration of the powerful existence she once was.

---

# 2. Current Tier

# Beginner Silver

Serena is currently one of the two confirmed Silver-tier members of the traveling group alongside Alok.

Her Silver advancement represents:

- stronger Pureblood physiology
- denser blood-energy circulation
- more stable regeneration
- stronger Mana pathways
- greater blood control
- improved supernatural senses
- stronger resistance to hostile blood or soul interference

---

# 3. Core Racial Traits

## Pureblood Physiology

Serena possesses a complete living vampiric body with:

- supernatural strength and speed
- high regenerative capacity
- enhanced senses
- blood-energy circulation
- strong Mana compatibility
- high resistance to ordinary disease and biological deterioration

## Blood Affinity

Blood is Serena's defining magical and racial affinity.

She can use it for:

- offensive constructs
- weapon reinforcement
- ranged attacks
- barriers
- sensing
- regeneration support
- alchemical applications

## Vampiric Regeneration

Serena can rapidly recover from injuries while sufficient Mana, blood-energy, vitality, and biological material remain available.

## Predatory Senses

Enhanced:

- hearing
- smell
- movement tracking
- blood detection
- life-signature awareness

## Ancient Soul

Serena's soul is far older and denser than her current reconstructed vessel.

This gives her unusual composure, combat memory, magical understanding, resistance to weak soul interference, and long-term evolutionary potential.

# 5. Gold Restoration Milestone

Serena's historically recovered state was:

# True Pureblood Vampire — Gold

Gold therefore represents a major restoration point.

Reaching Gold should feel less like Serena becoming something entirely new and more like reclaiming the full quality of what she once was—before eventually surpassing it.

# 6. Character Identity

Serena's bond with Alok developed through soul proximity, trust, emotional transparency, his refusal to dominate her, and his willingness to restore her as a person rather than use her as a weapon.

She should remain composed, affectionate, teasing when comfortable, highly knowledgeable, dangerous in combat, and technically skilled in magic and alchemy.
