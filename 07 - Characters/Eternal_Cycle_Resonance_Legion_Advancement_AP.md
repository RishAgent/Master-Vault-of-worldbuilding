# Eternal Cycle Resonance — Legion Advancement AP

> [!summary]
> Alok gains **Attribute Points (AP)** when a genuine member of the **Eternal Legion** advances to a new **Tier or substage** after becoming a recognized Legion member.
>
> This system does **not** grant AP for ordinary Levels.
>
> Rewards scale dramatically with Tier.

---

# 1. Core Rule

Alok receives AP when a true Eternal Legion member achieves a genuine advancement.

Valid advancement events include:

- Tier breakthrough
- Intermediate substage
- Advanced substage
- Peak substage

The reward depends on the Tier reached.

> **No Level-based AP.**

# 3. Tier Breakthrough Rule

The **Beginner** column represents the reward for entering that Tier.

Example:

> Peak Bronze → Beginner Silver

Reward:

# **+50 AP**

This is treated as one advancement event.

---

# 4. No Double-Dipping

A Tier breakthrough does not grant both:

- the previous Tier's Peak reward, and
- the new Tier's Beginner reward

for the same advancement event.

Example:

> Advanced Bronze → Peak Bronze

Reward:

# **+10 AP**

Later:

> Peak Bronze → Beginner Silver

Reward:

# **+50 AP**

The Silver breakthrough does not also grant another Peak Bronze reward.

---

# 5. No Retroactive AP

Advancements achieved before a person becomes a recognized Eternal Legion member do not generate AP retroactively.

Example:

A person joins the Legion while already at:

> Beginner Silver

Alok receives:

# **0 AP**

for their existing Beginner Silver state.

If that member later advances to:

> Intermediate Silver

Alok receives:

# **+25 AP**

---

# 6. Legion Membership Requirement

The advancement must belong to a **true recognized Eternal Legion member**.

## Counts

Examples include:

- Alana
- Serena
- Lyra
- Frosty
- Ferno
- Nocta
- restored people or creatures formally inducted into the Legion
- Aeonmorta retainers formally integrated into Alok's Eternal Legion

## Does Not Count

The following do not generate Legion advancement AP unless formally elevated into true Legion membership:

- Shadows
- Shadow Golems
- ordinary Mana constructs
- temporary summons
- auxiliary skeletons
- Shadow Lich skeleton auxiliaries
- Crawfords
- ordinary allies
- contracted helpers who are not Legion members

# **+40 AP**

---

# 8. Scaling Philosophy

The system deliberately scales aggressively.

At low Tier:

- rewards are useful but modest

At Silver and Gold:

- Legion progression becomes a meaningful source of Alok's personal growth

At very high Tier:

- advancement of even one powerful Legion member becomes enormously valuable

This reflects the Sovereign principle that:

> **Alok grows as his Legion grows.**

However, the system only rewards genuine advancement.

It cannot be exploited through:

- temporary stat boosts
- fake breakthroughs
- Level grinding
- repeated regression and restoration
- summon creation

---

# 9. Design Purpose

Eternal Cycle Resonance reinforces the class fantasy of the **Sovereign of Eternal Cycle**.

Alok is incentivized to:

- develop Legion members
- help them evolve
- provide resources
- improve their Classes
- help them break through safely
- build a powerful civilization rather than hoard strength entirely for himself

Their growth becomes part of his growth.

---

# 10. Canon Locks

- No Level-based Legion AP.
- AP is granted only from genuine Tier or substage advancement.
- Rewards use the table in this note.
- Beginner values represent Tier-breakthrough rewards.
- No retroactive AP.
- No double-dipping at Tier breakthrough.
- Alana counts as Eternal Legion.
- Formally integrated Aeonmorta retainers count.
- Shadows do not count.
- Shadow Golems do not count.
- Auxiliary skeletons do not count.
- Crawfords do not count.
- Temporary stat boosts do not trigger AP.
- Genuine Legion growth is the intended source of these rewards.
