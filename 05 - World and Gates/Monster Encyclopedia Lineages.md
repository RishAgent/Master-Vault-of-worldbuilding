# Monster Encyclopedia — Ordinary, Iron & Bronze Tier
## Evolutionary Lineages — Revised V2

> [!info] Core Rule
> **Tier represents realized power.**
>
> **Evolution represents species quality, racial potential, and structural advancement.**
>
> A monster may sometimes remain in the same evolutionary form while reaching a higher substage, but major species evolutions usually raise its minimum and maximum potential significantly.

**Tier:** Intermediate Ordinary  
**Typical EXP:** 10 – 20 EXP

# 12. Boss Classification Rule

Evolution and Boss status are separate.

For example:

**Goblin King — Peak Bronze**
may normally grant:

**3,250 – 5,000 EXP**

But:

**Goblin King — Bronze Gate Boss**
may grant:

**10,000 – 18,000+ EXP**

The Boss modifier reflects:
- Superior individual development
- Larger Mana Core
- Extra Skills
- Territorial authority
- Unique mutations
- Stronger bloodline
- System classification
- Encounter difficulty

# 13. Soul-Based Evolution Principle

Soul-based summon evolution should use the established racial line rather than simply adding raw Attributes.

Example:

**Regular Goblin Summon**
→ Medium Goblin
→ High Goblin
→ Goblin Elite
→ Goblin Chief
→ Goblin Champion
→ Goblin King

Each evolution may reconstruct:
- Body
- Mana Core
- Race
- Potential
- Intelligence
- Affinity
- Skills
- Natural weapons
- Growth rate

Possible requirements:
- Compatible souls
- Soul quantity
- Soul quality
- Tier threshold
- Level threshold
- Core quality
- Special materials
- Affinity
- Skill mastery
- Achievement conditions
- Authority/Class effects

> [!important]
> The summon retains continuity of identity.
>
> It is not replaced by a different creature.
>
> The same summon becomes a higher existence.

---

# 14. Design Rule — Evolution vs Tier

A racial evolution sets a **typical power floor and potential ceiling**, but does not hard-lock exact strength.

Example:

A newly evolved **Goblin Elite** may begin around Beginner or Intermediate Iron before growing into its typical Advanced-Iron range.

An exceptional Goblin Elite might eventually touch Peak Iron without evolving.

Likewise, a freshly evolved Goblin Champion might enter Beginner Bronze before maturing toward Intermediate Bronze.

Therefore:

**Tier = current realized power.**

**Evolution = quality of existence and future potential.**

This distinction is especially important for Alok's future summons.
