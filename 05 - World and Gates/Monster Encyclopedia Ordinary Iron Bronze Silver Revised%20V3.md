# Monster Encyclopedia — Ordinary, Iron, Bronze & Silver Tier
## Evolutionary Lineages — Revised V3

> [!info] Core Rule
> **Tier represents realized power.**
>
> **Evolution represents species quality, racial potential, and structural advancement.**
>
> A monster may remain in the same evolutionary form while advancing through substages, while major racial evolutions often increase its natural power floor and future ceiling.

---

# 1. EXP Classification Rule

Monster EXP uses only:

- Beginner
- Intermediate
- Advanced
- Peak
- Boss

There is **no universal Elite EXP classification**.

> [!important]
> Names such as **Goblin Elite** or **Orc Elite** may still exist as racial/evolution names.
>
> They do **not** create a separate EXP category. Their EXP is determined by their actual Tier and substage.

Boss classification remains separate from normal Tier/stage progression.

## Bronze-Tier Monsters — Revised

Bronze begins a steeper EXP economy.

The base target rises by approximately **50% per substage**.

| Stage | Typical EXP |
|---|---:|
| Beginner Bronze | **2,000 – 3,000** |
| Intermediate Bronze | **3,000 – 4,500** |
| Advanced Bronze | **4,500 – 6,750** |
| Peak Bronze | **6,750 – 10,000** |
| Bronze Boss | **25,000 – 60,000+** |

### Bronze progression anchors

Lower-bound stage progression:

**2,000 → 3,000 → 4,500 → 6,750**

This is approximately:

**×1.5 per stage**

The upper ranges allow:

- stronger species
- superior bloodlines
- better Skills
- better Mana Cores
- exceptional racial bodies
- monsters near the next substage

without introducing a separate Elite category.

## Silver-Tier Monsters — New

From Bronze onward, a full major-Tier increase uses a much larger EXP jump.

The same-stage Silver baseline is approximately **10× Bronze**.

This mirrors the V4 vessel scale, where equivalent Silver Attribute benchmarks are roughly ten times the Bronze benchmarks.

| Stage | Typical EXP |
|---|---:|
| Beginner Silver | **20,000 – 30,000** |
| Intermediate Silver | **30,000 – 45,000** |
| Advanced Silver | **45,000 – 67,500** |
| Peak Silver | **67,500 – 100,000** |
| Silver Boss | **250,000 – 600,000+** |

### Silver progression anchors

Lower-bound stage progression:

**20,000 → 30,000 → 45,000 → 67,500**

Again:

**×1.5 per stage**

Major Tier transition:

**Beginner Bronze 2,000 → Beginner Silver 20,000**

approximately:

# **×10 EXP**

# 3. EXP Scaling Philosophy

The EXP curve should encourage challengers to fight monsters appropriate to their own progression.

A Level 25–30 Awakened should not efficiently advance by farming thousands upon thousands of Iron monsters.

Likewise, once Silver enemies become survivable, they should become far more efficient sources of progression than mass-killing low Bronze monsters.

## Working rule from Bronze onward

### Substage increase
Approximately:

# **×1.5 EXP**

per major substage.

### Full Tier increase
Approximately:

# **×10 EXP**

for the equivalent substage.

This produces a clean future framework if retained:

- Beginner Bronze: ~2,000
- Beginner Silver: ~20,000
- Beginner Gold: theoretical ~200,000

Only Bronze and Silver are currently locked by this note.

---

# 4. Why Bronze Needed Higher EXP

The current Level curve becomes extremely expensive after Level 20.

Examples:

- Level 20 → 21: **2,200,000 EXP**
- Level 25 → 26: **12,000,000 EXP**
- Level 29 → 30: **35,000,000 EXP**

Using the old Bronze values, advanced characters would require unreasonable kill counts even when fighting monsters of a meaningful Tier.

The revised system makes Bronze valuable during Bronze-era progression while still strongly incentivizing Silver enemies once characters approach the Level 25–30 range.

## Approximate Level 25 → 26 comparison

EXP required:

**12,000,000**

Approximate kills if every kill awarded the midpoint of its range:

| Enemy | Approx. Midpoint EXP | Approx. Kills |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beginner Bronze | 2,500 | ~4,800 |
| Intermediate Bronze | 3,750 | ~3,200 |
| Advanced Bronze | 5,625 | ~2,134 |
| Peak Bronze | 8,375 | ~1,433 |
| Bronze Boss | 42,500 | ~283 |
| Beginner Silver | 25,000 | ~480 |
| Intermediate Silver | 37,500 | ~320 |
| Advanced Silver | 56,250 | ~214 |
| Peak Silver | 83,750 | ~144 |
| Silver Boss | 425,000 | ~29 |

> [!note]
> Actual progression also includes:
>
> - quests
> - Trial rewards
> - bosses
> - EXP multipliers
> - group contribution
> - Class effects
> - major achievements
>
> Therefore raw monster-kill counts are only a balancing reference.

# 16. Draconic Balance Reference

True Dragons are not ordinary low-Tier monsters.

Healthy true Dragons typically hatch around **Bronze Tier or above**.

Working draconic birth baselines:

| Draconic Lineage Quality | Typical Hatch Tier |
|---|---|
| Weak / degraded true Dragon | Beginner Bronze |
| Common Dragon | Beginner–Intermediate Bronze |
| Greater / High lineage | Intermediate–Advanced Bronze |
| Ancient / Imperial lineage | Advanced–Peak Bronze |
| Primordial / Supreme lineage | Peak Bronze–Beginner Silver |

> [!important]
> A Dragon is usually **overbuilt for its Tier**.
>
> At equal Tier and similar Personal Rank, it may have:
>
> - higher STR/VIT
> - stronger natural armor
> - larger Mana capacity
> - breath weapons
> - elemental resistance
> - flight
> - superior combat mass
>
> Therefore a wild Dragon encountered as an enemy may award toward the upper end of its stage range or receive Boss classification if the encounter warrants it.

Draconic race status and Boss status remain separate.

# 17. Boss Classification Rule

Boss status is separate from race and ordinary substage.

A Boss may possess:

- superior individual development
- larger or denser Mana Core
- additional Skills
- territorial authority
- unique mutations
- stronger bloodline
- command over subordinate monsters
- System encounter designation
- enhanced regeneration
- special phases / mechanics

## Boss EXP

| Tier | Typical Boss EXP |
|---|---:|
| Ordinary | 60 – 100+ |
| Iron | **1,000 – 2,500+** |
| Bronze | **25,000 – 60,000+** |
| Silver | **250,000 – 600,000+** |

Exceptional world bosses, Trial bosses, Sovereign monsters, and unique named entities may exceed these ranges dramatically.

# 18. Boss Multiplier Philosophy

Boss EXP is intentionally much higher than ordinary same-Tier monsters.

A useful working expectation from Bronze onward is roughly:

# **~3× to 6× a strong normal Peak-stage monster**

depending on:

- encounter complexity
- number of phases
- subordinate mobs
- special mechanics
- regeneration
- territorial advantage
- rarity
- System classification

Bosses therefore remain major progression events.

# 19. Soul-Based Evolution Principle

Soul-based summon evolution should follow racial/evolution lines rather than simply adding raw Attributes.

Example:

**Regular Goblin Summon**  
→ Medium Goblin  
→ High Goblin  
→ Goblin Elite  
→ Goblin Chief  
→ Goblin Champion  
→ Goblin King

Possible reconstruction factors:

- compatible souls
- soul quantity
- soul quality
- Tier threshold
- Level
- Core quality
- affinity
- materials
- Skills
- achievements
- Authority/Class effects

> [!important]
> The summon retains identity continuity.
>
> It is not replaced by another creature.
>
> The same being evolves into a higher existence.

---

# 20. Evolution vs Tier

A racial evolution defines a typical power floor, structural quality, and potential ceiling.

It does not hard-lock exact Tier.

Example:

A newly evolved Goblin Elite may initially express Beginner/Intermediate Iron before maturing toward its typical Advanced-Iron range.

An exceptional Goblin Elite may approach Peak Iron before evolving.

Likewise:

A newly evolved Goblin Champion may enter Beginner Bronze before maturing toward Intermediate Bronze.

Therefore:

# **Tier = current realized power**

# **Evolution = quality of existence and future potential**
