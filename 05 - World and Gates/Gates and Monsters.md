# Gates and Monsters

Gates remain a major source of monsters, resources, danger and progression, but the V1 gate-rank/level mapping must be rebuilt around the new **Tier + Rank + Level** framework.

## Retained principles

- Gates vary dramatically in scale and danger.
- Higher Gates can contain altered spaces/world-sized environments and time dilation.
- Gate Breaks release monsters into the outside world.
- Released monsters can be especially dangerous.
- Gates can contain functioning ecosystems, not just linear dungeons.
- Monster evolution should feel biological/magical where possible.

## Legacy monster pool to mine

Goblin, Kobold, Slime, Wild Hound, Cave Snake, Forest Sprite, Stone Imp, Bat, Orc, Bugbear, Skeleton, Lizard, Harpy, Warg, Ogre, Giant Spider, Lizardman, Troll, Wyvern, Shadow Mage and others.

## Preferred bestiary direction

The V1 **Frost Lupinid** line is a good model for future monsters because it contains:

- mana-driven physiology
- environmental adaptation
- social/pack behavior
- evolutionary stages
- intelligence divergence
- tactical differences between stages

See [[Bestiary]] in the legacy folder for the complete original Frost Lupinid concept.
