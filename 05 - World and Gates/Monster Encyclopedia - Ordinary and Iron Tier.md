# Monster Encyclopedia — Ordinary & Iron Tier

> [!info] System Rule — Monster Rewards
> Monster EXP is determined by Tier, substage, species, individual strength, mutation, elite/boss status, relative strength, and contribution.
>
> The EXP values below are **typical base rewards**.
>
> Random System rewards such as Skill Scrolls, Equipment, Random Draw Tickets, special Consumables, Achievement rewards, and Judgment Fragments are **not guaranteed** and are therefore not listed as standard drops.

---

# 2. ORDINARY-TIER MONSTERS

## Lesser Goblin

**Tier:** Ordinary  
**Typical EXP:** 10 EXP

### Description
Small humanoid monsters with weak bodies but high aggression. They commonly gather in groups and compensate for poor individual strength through numbers, crude weapons, traps, and ambushes.

### Combat Characteristics
- Low STR
- Moderate AGI
- Poor VIT
- Basic tool use
- Packs of 5–20 are common
- Usually flee when isolated

### Harvestable Materials
- Goblin Teeth
- Goblin Nails
- Toughened Skin
- Small Mana Gland
- Crude Weapons

### Possible Core
**Ordinary Goblin Core**

### Possible Mana Crystal
Small Ordinary Mana Crystal

Typical Tower Value: **5 – 15 TP**

# 8. IRON-TIER GATE POPULATION

A typical **Iron-Tier Gate** contains approximately:

**~1,000 monsters**

This is a benchmark, not a fixed rule.

Population varies according to:
- Gate size
- Terrain
- Ecosystem
- Mana density
- Reproduction rate
- Species composition
- Age of the Gate

A normal Iron Gate usually contains:
- Large numbers of Ordinary-Tier monsters
- Significant numbers of Iron-Tier monsters
- Several elites
- One or more regional leaders
- Usually one main Gate Boss

An Iron-Tier Gate does **not** contain 1,000 Iron-Tier monsters.

The Gate is classified Iron because its overall ecosystem, dominant threats, Mana density, and highest expected danger fall within the Iron range.

# 10. BOSS REWARD RULES

Bosses have significantly higher chances of generating random System rewards.

Possible random rewards include:
- Skill Scrolls
- Equipment
- Consumables
- Tower Points
- Random Draw Tickets
- Rare crafting materials
- Achievement rewards
- Judgment Fragments

Judgment Fragments remain **rare even from bosses**.

# 11. MONSTER CORES

Monster Cores are not guaranteed from every creature.

| Monster Classification | Typical Core Chance |
|---|---:|
| Weak Ordinary | Very Low |
| Regular Ordinary | Low |
| Strong Ordinary | Low – Moderate |
| Ordinary Elite | Moderate |
| Beginner Iron | Moderate |
| Intermediate Iron | Moderate |
| Advanced Iron | Moderate – High |
| Peak Iron | High |
| Iron Elite | Very High |
| Iron Gate Boss | Nearly Guaranteed |

Core quality depends on:
- Tier
- Mana density
- Species
- Core condition
- Purity
- Mutation
- Affinity
- Harvest quality

---

# 12. MANA CRYSTALS

Mana Crystals may be found:
- Inside monster bodies
- Near monster nests
- In Mana veins
- In caves
- Near Gate cores
- Around high-density Mana zones
- As random System rewards

They are not guaranteed from every kill.
