# Starlight Competition — Post Round I Master Plan

## 1. Current Position

Round I is complete.

Locked result:

- **1,104 contestants** entered Round I
- **552** advanced through Ownership Flags
- **72** received Exceptional Advancement
- **624 contestants** remain

Alok's team recorded the highest Team Competition Merit.

Alok personally received approximately:

> **68,000,000 Individual Competition Merit**

Krai, Kade, and the Imperial-aligned participants involved in the illegal camp attack have been removed from competition.

Krai is currently under restricted noble custody inside Starlight territory while the legal, political, and diplomatic consequences are processed.

> **Beginner Bronze**

The next treatment is no longer primarily suppression.

> **Controlled parasite destruction and resource reclamation**

> **Destroy the parasite, keep the useful refinement.**
