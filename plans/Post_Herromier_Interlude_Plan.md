# Post-Herromier Interlude Plan

## Purpose

This interlude bridges the end of the Herromier Arc and the beginning of the Starlight Arc.

It will:
- temporarily leave Felora after the investigation;
- establish Lanthisa as Alok's long-term interworld hub;
- grow the Eternal Legion through Gate training;
- stockpile large amounts of unspent AP;
- advance Alok through Silver-tier refinement;
- awaken his four dormant bloodlines;
- accelerate Nocta's natural evolution into sapience;
- clear the Tower's first ten Beginner Floors;
- unlock the hidden Treasury of the Gods;
- introduce Athena and Hermes under revised divine relationship rules;
- introduce Alok's grandparents properly;
- give Alok Dusk and Dawn;
- return to Felora substantially stronger while very little Feloran time has passed;
- end with the reveal that Alana is pregnant.
