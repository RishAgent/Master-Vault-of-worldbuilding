# Starlight Competition Power Ceiling and Qualification Structure

> [!summary]
> The Starlight Grand Competition is open to entrants under 35, but Feloran progression, EXP requirements, refinement resources, travel logistics, Outerwild danger, and the scarcity of high-Tier combat environments naturally keep the field far below Silver.
>
> **Peak Bronze is the practical ceiling for normal under-35 prodigies.**
>
> **Advanced Bronze is already elite-finalist territory.**
>
> **Silver under 35 is a historic-level anomaly.**
>
> **Alok at age 26 and Intermediate Silver is completely outside normal Feloran expectations.**

---

# 1. Core Design Principle

Feloran advancement cannot be accelerated by wealth alone.

A top noble House can provide:

- Monster Cores
- Mana Crystals
- alchemical support
- equipment
- tutors
- protected hunting access
- military escorts
- Gate permissions
- advancement facilities
- breakthrough resources

But wealth cannot directly provide:

- accumulated EXP
- genuine combat experience
- personal monster kills
- verified Merit
- years of survival in dangerous environments
- safe access to enough high-Tier enemies

This distinction is critical.

A noble heir may possess the resources required to refine quickly in theory, but still lack the combat progression and experience needed to advance.

---

# 2. Core and Crystal Absorption Limit

From Bronze onward, vessel refinement progresses in parallel with combat growth.

A person may safely absorb at most:

# **20 Monster Cores per day**

and

# **20 Mana Crystals per day**

under normal compatible conditions.

These limits apply separately to:

- Physical Vessel refinement through Cores
- Mana-Core refinement through Crystals

However, this is a **maximum processing ceiling**, not a normal daily supply.

Most people cannot obtain enough compatible resources to maintain this rate continuously.

Even major noble Houses cannot casually provide 20 suitable Cores and 20 suitable Crystals every single day for years.

---

# 3. Why Wealth Does Not Remove the Resource Bottleneck

A theoretical year at maximum refinement throughput requires:

- **7,300 usable Monster Cores**
- **7,300 usable Mana Crystals**

for one person.

That is an enormous strategic resource burden.

Obtaining this supply requires:

- Gates
- monster hunts
- Outerwild expeditions
- crystal mines
- Mana deposits
- harvesting teams
- alchemists
- extraction specialists
- military escorts
- logistics
- storage
- transport
- replacement equipment
- medical support

Not every monster produces a complete refinement-grade Core.

Not every Mana Crystal is compatible, high enough grade, safe to absorb, or available for private use.

Resources are also divided between expedition members, military stockpiles, noble House reserves, markets, alchemical processing, breakthrough stockpiles, and other heirs and retainers.

Therefore even a top noble House cannot treat high-grade refinement resources as infinite.

---

# 4. Outerwild Logistics

The Outerwilds are the main source of sustained high-Tier progression resources and combat environments.

Serious expeditions require:

- hundreds of fighters in major operations
- Bronze and Silver officers
- healers
- scouts
- mages
- barrier specialists
- harvesters
- alchemists
- logistics personnel
- supply wagons or storage artifacts
- fortified camps
- replacement equipment
- medical reserves

Travel itself is a major cost.

From major cities or the capital:

- reaching the kingdom frontier may take weeks or months
- secured Ring routes still require organized movement
- reaching productive hunting regions takes additional time
- expeditions may remain outside for months or years
- returning with resources takes further time

The expedition must also survive monster attacks, weather, terrain, resource scarcity, damaged equipment, injuries, deaths, failed hunts, migration changes, and supply interruptions.

This makes high-Tier advancement a long logistical project rather than a simple purchasing decision.

---

# 5. Kingdom-Border Limitation

Within stabilized kingdom territory:

- Ordinary monsters are common
- Iron monsters are common combat threats
- Bronze monsters exist in managed or remote areas
- Silver monsters are rare exceptional threats

There are almost no naturally spawning Silver Gates inside normal kingdom borders.

Therefore a person seeking sustained late-Bronze and Silver-grade EXP cannot remain safely around the capital.

They eventually need:

- Outerwild expeditions
- Silver Ring hunting
- rare exceptional Gates
- frontier campaigns
- Crown military operations
- dangerous monster territories

This strongly limits how quickly even noble prodigies can advance.

---

# 6. EXP Is a Major Bottleneck

Bronze progression is not refinement-only.

Bronze remains a hybrid stage using:

# **Levels + EXP + Attributes + Refinement + breakthrough resources**

A wealthy heir cannot sit inside an estate consuming Cores and Crystals until they become Silver.

They still require sustained combat progression.

As Levels rise:

- EXP requirements increase sharply
- low-Tier monsters become inefficient
- Bronze monsters become necessary
- later progression increasingly favors Silver enemies and bosses
- high-risk Gates and major objectives become important

This forces serious prodigies into real combat environments.

---

# 7. Alok as the Calibration Benchmark

Alok provides a useful upper benchmark.

From approximately age 16 to 26, he required nearly ten years of continuous high-intensity progression to move from around Intermediate Iron through Beginner Silver.

During that period he had advantages most Felorans do not:

- three Classes
- exceptional combat ability
- constant Gates
- repeated monster battles
- elixirs
- advancement consumables
- rare rewards
- unusually high EXP acquisition
- Core and Crystal refinement operating in parallel from Bronze onward
- superior survivability
- exceptional combat opportunities

Even with these advantages, the progression required years of constant danger and combat.

Therefore a normal noble prodigy cannot replicate Alok's progression curve simply through wealth.

---

# 8. Under-35 Natural Power Ceiling

The Starlight Competition should reflect these constraints.

| Candidate Type | Plausible Tier |
|---|---|
| Respectable professional entrant | Advanced Iron – Peak Iron |
| Talented noble | Peak Iron – Beginner Bronze |
| Strong noble heir | Beginner Bronze |
| Major-House prodigy | Intermediate Bronze |
| Exceptional kingdom talent | Advanced Bronze |
| Extreme royal / national prodigy | Advanced Bronze – low Peak Bronze |
| Once-in-a-generation young monster | Peak Bronze |
| Silver before 35 | Historic-level anomaly |
| **Alok, age 26** | **Intermediate Silver** |

---

# 9. Peak Bronze Meaning

A Peak Bronze combatant under 35 should already be extraordinary.

Such a person likely:

- awakened young
- began serious combat in adolescence
- entered Gates for years
- spent long periods on frontier expeditions
- consumed enormous quantities of refinement resources
- possesses a high-quality Class
- has elite instructors
- has noble, royal, military, or Great-House backing
- has personally survived thousands of monster encounters
- has accumulated significant Merit

Peak Bronze at 33–34 should be enough to make someone a famous young talent across a kingdom.

---

# 10. Advanced Bronze Meaning

Advanced Bronze should be the normal high-end level for serious finalists.

An Advanced Bronze entrant under 35 is already nationally talented, heavily resourced, experienced in real combat, and likely sponsored by a noble House, military institution, academy, or major Merit organization.

This Tier should remain prestigious.

---

# 11. Silver Under 35

A Human reaching Silver before 35 is not merely talented.

They are an extreme anomaly.

To do so they would need some combination of:

- extraordinary Personal Rank
- rare Class
- exceptional bloodline
- early awakening
- enormous resource backing
- years of Outerwild experience
- high-grade Gate access
- unusually high survival rate
- strong EXP acquisition
- exceptional Merit accumulation
- safe access to Silver-grade breakthrough materials

A Silver contestant under 35 should therefore be treated as a historic-level figure.

By default:

# **The Starlight Competition has no ordinary Silver candidates.**

If a Silver candidate is introduced later, it must be explicitly justified as an extreme exception.

---

# 12. Alok's Position

Current Alok:

- Age: **26**
- Tier: **Intermediate Silver**
- Crown Champion standing
- three extraordinary Classes
- abnormal racial and bloodline structure
- Three-Star Core
- extensive Gates, Trials, battles, and high-Tier combat experience
- true combat power far above ordinary Intermediate Silver

His presence in an under-35 Feloran competition is not simply impressive.

It is structurally absurd by local standards.

When officials eventually verify:

> **Age: 26**
>
> **Tier: Intermediate Silver**

the reaction should reflect the enormous amount of time, resources, EXP, combat, survival, Merit, refinement, and breakthrough preparation normally required to reach that point.

---

# 13. Competition Qualification Structure

The first registration phase should not begin with a full Merit Crest read.

Instead:

## Stage 1 — Physical Qualification

Candidates approach a testing pillar.

The pillar measures raw physical force from a direct strike.

Purpose:

> determine whether the applicant meets the minimum combat standard required to proceed to official registration.

The pillar is not a precise Tier detector.

Performance varies based on:

- STR specialization
- Class
- race
- body type
- permanent bloodline traits
- physical refinement
- technique
- ordinary permitted Mana reinforcement

The pillar exists only to remove obviously unsuitable entrants.

---

# 14. Pillar Restrictions

The strike should prohibit:

- weapons
- offensive Skills
- temporary potions
- external buffs
- outside assistance

Permanent vessel characteristics remain valid.

---

# 15. Merit Crest Verification

Only after the candidate passes the pillar test is the Merit Crest formally checked.

The verification may reveal:

- Name
- Age
- Tier
- citizenship / kingdom affiliation
- recognized social standing
- registered Classes
- competition eligibility

This is the first natural point where Alok's age of 26 becomes officially visible during the competition process.

His Crown Champion standing may already be visually recognizable from the physical Crest before this point.

---

# 16. Regional Registration Structure

The competition is held in the Starlight capital.

However, the kingdom is too large for every applicant to travel directly to the capital just to attempt entry.

Therefore:

- registration and first-phase qualification may occur at approved Viscounty-level cities
- major Merit Halls may host testing pillars
- successful applicants receive official competition qualification credentials
- qualified candidates then travel toward the Starlight capital

Airships from major Viscounty cities may reach the capital in approximately:

# **1–2 weeks**

Normal humans traveling overland may require:

# **3–4 months**

from distant regions.

Merit holders can travel significantly faster, but distance remains important.

---

# 17. Capital Tournament Field

Working target:

# **128 capital competitors**

Approximate power distribution:

- **30–40** Advanced Iron / Peak Iron
- **45–50** Beginner Bronze
- **20–25** Intermediate Bronze
- **10–15** Advanced Bronze
- **3–5** Peak Bronze
- **0 ordinary Silver**
- **Alok — Intermediate Silver**

Exact numbers remain flexible by scene needs.

Some lower-Tier contestants may perform above expectation through:

- Stars
- Classes
- affinities
- techniques
- racial advantages
- Beast contracts
- equipment
- tactical specialization

---

# 18. Strongest Normal Candidates

The strongest expected contenders before Alok's full registration should generally be:

- Peak Bronze royal heirs
- Peak Bronze Great-House prodigies
- Advanced Bronze elite Beast Tamers
- Advanced Bronze military geniuses
- high-Bronze foreign princes and princesses
- highly refined Peak Bronze candidates nearing Silver preparation

One top candidate may be:

- Age: 33–34
- Tier: Peak Bronze
- nearly saturated
- preparing for Silver
- high-rank Class
- excellent bloodline
- years of Outerwild experience
- significant Merit
- major national reputation

Such a person should be viewed as one of the greatest young talents of their generation.

---

# 19. Competition Narrative Effect

Before Alok's full registration:

> Peak Bronze is frightening.

After Alok's record is read:

> Age 26.
>
> Intermediate Silver.

The reaction should not merely be:

> "He is stronger."

The reaction should be:

> "How did he accumulate enough experience, resources, Merit, combat history and refinement to reach that point before thirty?"

This preserves the weight of Felora's progression system.

---

# 20. Canon Locks

- Bronze progression uses EXP and refinement in parallel.
- Maximum normal daily absorption is **20 Cores + 20 Mana Crystals**.
- This is a processing ceiling, not guaranteed supply.
- High-grade resource acquisition is heavily constrained by Outerwild logistics.
- Top noble Houses can fund progression but cannot eliminate time, distance, danger, EXP, Merit, or experience requirements.
- Silver Gates are extremely rare inside stabilized kingdom territory.
- Serious late-Bronze progression requires frontier / Outerwild exposure.
- Peak Bronze under 35 is extraordinary.
- Advanced Bronze is already elite-finalist territory.
- Silver under 35 is a historic-level anomaly.
- The Starlight Competition should normally contain **no Silver candidates besides Alok**.
- Alok is **26 and Intermediate Silver**.
- His true strength remains far above ordinary Intermediate Silver.
- Qualification begins with a physical testing pillar.
- Merit Crest verification occurs only after passing the physical test.
- Regional qualification may occur in Viscounty-level cities.
- The main tournament takes place in the Starlight capital arena.
- Working capital field size is approximately **128 competitors**.
