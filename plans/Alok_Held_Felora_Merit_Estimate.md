# Alok — Held Felora Merit Estimate

## Current Working Total

Alok's **last publicly known / previously audited Felora Lifetime Merit** after the demon-rift incident and investigation was:

# **21,842,500 Merit**

However, Alok's Merit Crest continues to record valid kills outside Felora.

This includes:

- Alok's own kills
- kills made by his Shadows
- kills made by recognized members of the Eternal Legion
- kills performed through properly linked Legion authority
- valid Gate kills outside Felora
- valid Tower Trial kills where the slain entity can be mapped to Felora's Tier framework

Because Felorans do not understand the existence of other dimensions, Lanthisa, the Tower, or external Gate worlds, the Merit total recorded in Alok's crest will appear impossible unless he provides a believable local explanation.

# 1. Merit Economy Reference

Felora uses fixed monster Merit by Tier and substage.

| Tier | Beginner | Intermediate | Advanced | Peak |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Iron | 100 | 250 | 500 | 1,000 |
| Bronze | 2,500 | 5,000 | 10,000 | 25,000 |
| Silver | 50,000 | 100,000 | 250,000 | 500,000 |
| Gold | 1,000,000 | 2,500,000 | 5,000,000 | 10,000,000 |

There is no relative-strength multiplier.

Merit is awarded according to the defeated target's recognized Tier/substage.

# 7. Why Alok Should Not Immediately Visit a Merit Hall

From Felora's perspective, only roughly **two days** have passed since Alok left the forest beyond Herromier.

If he immediately synchronizes his Merit Crest and reveals:

**21,842,500 → 1,006,617,500**

the increase would be extremely difficult to explain.

It would imply approximately:

# **984,775,000 new Merit**

earned in less than two Feloran days.

That would create immediate attention from:

- the Crown
- local nobles
- military authorities
- Merit officials
- guilds
- intelligence networks
- powerful Houses

Alok therefore has a strong reason to delay synchronization.

Working plan:

Alok returns to Felora but does **not** immediately enter a Merit Hall.

He spends several days or a few weeks staying relatively quiet in:

# **Wolfheide**

During this period he can:

- rest
- train
- reconnect with the group
- avoid formal Merit inspection
- let rumors settle
- prepare for a believable Merit explanation
- plan the next Outerwilds expedition

The Merit remains stored in the crest but unrevealed.

# 9. Outerwilds Cover Story

After the low-profile period, Alok can deliberately spend several weeks hunting in the Outerwilds.

Best target region:

# **Silver Ring**

This creates a believable public explanation for his enormous Merit increase.

Important prior knowledge already exists among Feloran authorities:

- Alok possesses a very large Shadow force
- Viscount-level reports already mention **300+ Shadows**
- his summons can fight independently
- he has demonstrated large-scale battlefield capability
- his group contains multiple powerful companions
- he is known to hunt aggressively and efficiently

Therefore, authorities do not need to believe Alok personally killed every monster.

They can reasonably conclude:

> Alok entered the Silver Ring with hundreds of Shadows and spent weeks conducting large-scale extermination hunts.

That explanation is extreme—

but plausible within Felora's existing understanding of him.

# 10. Merit Reveal Strategy

Recommended sequence:

**Stage 1 — Return quietly**

Do not visit a Merit Hall.

**Stage 2 — Remain in Wolfheide**

Several days to a few weeks.

**Stage 3 — Enter the Outerwilds**

Spend several weeks in or near the Silver Ring.

Actually hunt enough Silver monsters to support the story.

**Stage 4 — Allow witnesses**

Do not completely conceal the Shadow army.

Let:

- adventurers
- patrols
- merchants
- scouts
- guild parties

occasionally witness large Shadow deployments or monster kills.

This creates independent testimony.

**Stage 5 — Synchronize Merit**

Only after the Outerwilds expedition should Alok enter a Merit Hall.

At that point, the enormous number is shocking but no longer temporally impossible.
