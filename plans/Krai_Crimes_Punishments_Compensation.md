# Krai Crisis — Crimes, Punishments and Compensation Settlement

## 1. Core Political Situation

Krai is the **Tenth Imperial Prince of the Empire**.

He is **not part of Wolfheide's royal family** and has no blood claim, familial relation, or domestic princely authority within Wolfheide.

His first major offense took place during the Starlight Competition. After that incident:

- Krai was disqualified from the competition.
- He was placed under restricted noble custody inside Starlight territory.
- His political privileges were curtailed.
- The Empire was formally informed.
- The Empire sent a senior Imperial representative to Wolfheide.
- That representative was tasked with managing the diplomatic damage, ensuring Krai complied with restrictions, preventing further misconduct, and assuring Wolfheide that the Empire regarded the matter seriously.

Krai later violated those restrictions and organized a second, much more serious operation.

The second incident therefore proves:

> **pattern, escalation, motive, intent, repeated abuse of Imperial authority, and failure of prior Imperial assurances.**

This gives Wolfheide major diplomatic leverage over the Empire.

---

# 2. First Incident — Competition Conspiracy

## 2.1 Main Objective

Krai and his aligned nobles attempted to unlawfully seize two extremely valuable magical-beast assets connected to Alok Aeonmorta and House Starlight:

- **Nocta** — true dragon, intelligent bonded being, Alok's daughter and combat asset.
- **Ferno** — Blazefang Lion carrying the evolutionary lineage toward a **Solar Flame Lion**, a powerful and strategically valuable beast lineage.

The operation was not simple theft. It involved magical coercion, competition interference, abuse of noble and Imperial authority, and an organized attack within an official royal competition.

## 2.2 Charges from the First Incident

### Conspiracy to Steal Nocta
- attempted seizure of a protected bonded magical beast,
- attempted seizure of a rare true dragon,
- attempted seizure of a sentient being,
- attempted seizure of a strategic combat asset,
- interference with a voluntary family/beast bond.

### Conspiracy to Steal Ferno
- attempted theft of a rare magical-beast bloodline,
- attempted seizure of Solar Flame Lion evolutionary potential,
- attempted seizure of a strategic combat resource connected to House Starlight and Wolfheide's rising young hero.

### Forced Beast Covenant Conspiracy
The conspirators prepared and intended to use **Forced Beast Covenants** to:
- override existing loyalty,
- sever or bypass voluntary bonds,
- force control over intelligent or bonded beasts,
- convert them into faction assets.

This is treated as attempted magical enslavement / forced contracting.

### Dream-Mist Reliquary
Charges include:
- unlawful use of a restricted magical artifact,
- use of mind/awareness-altering effects during an official competition,
- magical incapacitation to facilitate coercion and theft.

### Tampering with Competition Monitoring
Charges include:
- obstruction of official oversight,
- deliberate suppression of evidence,
- interference with Crown-authorized monitoring,
- conspiracy to hide the attack.

### Organized Attack on an Official Competition Camp
Charges include:
- organized violence against contestants,
- endangerment of supervisors,
- endangerment of protected beasts,
- disruption of a royal competition,
- use of political influence to manipulate competition conditions.

### Recruitment and Corruption of Nobles and Contestants
Krai used his status and faction to recruit lesser nobles and aligned contestants, including Kade Hruise.

Charges include:
- conspiracy,
- corruption of participants,
- inducement to betray competition obligations,
- abuse of Imperial authority,
- political coercion where applicable.

# 4. Second Incident — Premeditated Assassination Attempt
