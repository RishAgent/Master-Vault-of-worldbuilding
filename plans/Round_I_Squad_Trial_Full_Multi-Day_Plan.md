# Round I Squad Trial — Full Multi-Day Plan

## 1. Core Round Structure

Round I takes place inside a large prepared training Gate.

- **1,104 contestants**
- **276 squads**
- **4 contestants per squad**
- **138 Ownership Flags**
- exactly half the squads can hold a Flag at the end
- **552 contestants** advance automatically through Flag possession
- **72 Exceptional Advancements** are selected from eliminated contestants
- **624 contestants** advance to Round II

Each squad enters the same enormous Gate environment but is transported to a randomized deployment area.

Each squad receives:

- one official supervisor
- a standard camp package
- a Camp Core
- a map crystal
- emergency supplies
- water purification equipment
- perimeter markers
- one Competition Merit network connection

The trial lasts **five days**.

---

# 2. Competition Merit and Flag Claiming

Each supervisor carries a special **Competition Merit Crest** linked to the registered Merit Crests of the four contestants.

The system records:

- monster kills
- damage contribution
- defense
- healing
- barriers
- battlefield control
- command
- major assistance
- support contribution

The final blow alone does not determine Merit.

Each contestant has:

- **Individual Competition Merit**

The squad also has:

- **Team Competition Merit**

A squad requires:

# **100,000 Team Competition Merit**

to unlock and claim an Ownership Flag.

There are exactly:

# **138 Ownership Flags**

distributed throughout the Gate.

Once a squad claims a Flag and installs it in its Camp Core:

- the camp becomes an **Owned Camp**
- defensive functions activate
- storage and warning formations activate
- the squad becomes a **provisional qualifier**

A Flag-holding squad cannot challenge another Flag-holding squad.

A flagless squad may issue a formal challenge to a Flag-holder.

A Flag cannot simply be stolen without a legal challenge.
