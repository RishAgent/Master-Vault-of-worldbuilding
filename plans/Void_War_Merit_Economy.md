# Void War Merit Economy

> [!summary]
> The **Void War Merit system** is separate from normal Tier progression and separate from Felora's Merit economy.
>
> - **Tier** measures realized combat power.
> - **Void Rank** measures recognized wartime contribution, trust, authorization and battlefield standing.
> - **Void Merit** is both the progression resource and the spendable currency.
> - Spending Void Merit can remove stars and may eventually reduce active rank.
> - A high Tier does not automatically grant a high Void Rank.
> - High Void Rank does not override inadequate Tier for a battlefield.
>
> The Void War is intentionally slow to progress through. Even extremely powerful combatants are expected to accumulate Merit over long campaigns.

---

# 1. Entry Requirement

Official Void War participation normally begins around:

- **Intermediate Gold** — exceptional / supervised entry
- **Advanced Gold** — normal frontline entry
- **Peak Gold** — experienced frontline combatant

A Gold-tier combatant is already capable of defeating very large numbers of normal monsters.

Void creatures are substantially stronger, more dangerous and harder to kill than ordinary monsters of comparable Tier.

Therefore, Void Rank advancement must still require sustained service across many battles rather than a handful of kills.

---

# 2. Rank Structure

Each Void Rank contains **five stars**.

Progression:

**☆ → ★ → ★★ → ★★★ → ★★★★ → ★★★★★**

Reaching ★★★★★ makes the combatant **eligible** to attempt promotion, provided all other requirements are satisfied.

Promotion requires:

1. sufficient current Void Merit
2. minimum Tier requirement
3. promotion qualification / mission
4. required campaign experience at higher ranks

An established Void combatant with sustained active-front experience.

A recognized frontline veteran.

A strategic combatant trusted against major Void infrastructure.

A major frontline veteran and battle leader.

A combatant trusted with planetary-scale fronts.

# 5. Current Merit Determines Stars

Void stars are based on **current unspent Void Merit**.

Example for Void Slayer:

| Current Void Merit | Status |
|---:|---|
| 0–999,999,999 | ☆☆☆☆☆ |
| 1B–1.999B | ★☆☆☆☆ |
| 2B–2.999B | ★★☆☆☆ |
| 3B–3.999B | ★★★☆☆ |
| 4B–4.999B | ★★★★☆ |
| 5B+ | ★★★★★ |

Spending Merit can immediately remove stars.

---

# 6. Historical Rank vs Active Rank

The System tracks:

- **Current Rank**
- **Current Stars**
- **Highest Rank Achieved**
- **Lifetime Void Merit Earned**
- **Current Spendable Void Merit**

Lifetime Merit is archival only.

It does not qualify a character for rank and cannot be spent.

---

# 7. Merit Spending and Star Loss

Void Merit is both progression resource and war currency.

Buying resources reduces current Merit.

If Merit falls below a star threshold:

**the star disappears immediately.**

---

# 8. Rank Grace / Merit Deficit

Dropping below the minimum active Merit threshold does not cause instant battlefield demotion.

Instead, the combatant enters a temporary grace period.

Suggested duration:

**30–90 days**

During this period:

- current rank remains temporarily recognized
- promotion attempts are blocked
- some restricted purchases are blocked
- some command missions become unavailable
- Merit must be restored before the deadline

If the deficit is not repaired:

**active rank is reduced**

Historical rank remains recorded.

# 10. Merit Scaling Principle

Normal same-Tier Void kills should contribute only a small fraction of a star.

A star should usually require:

- hundreds of high-end same-Tier kills
- thousands of lower-substage same-Tier kills
- long campaigns
- strategic objectives
- survival and contribution across many engagements

Void Rank represents **war history**, not a single successful battle.

# 13. Merit Contribution Pool

Kill Merit is not automatically granted entirely to the final attacker.

Contribution may include:

- damage
- killing blow
- defense
- healing
- barriers
- control
- command
- sealing
- rescue
- scouting
- intelligence
- logistical contribution
- corruption suppression

# 17. Promotion Requirements

Every promotion requires:

## Merit
Current Rank at:

**★★★★★**

## Tier
Minimum physical/spiritual capability for the next operational level.

## Qualification
A dedicated promotion mission, examination or battlefield certification.

## Experience
At higher ranks, verified campaign history is mandatory.

A combatant cannot farm safe enemies indefinitely and gain strategic authority.

# 21. Canon Identity

The Void War Merit system should remain:

- slow
- harsh
- war-focused
- spendable
- strategically meaningful
- resistant to farming
- meaningful even at Divine Tier

A high Void Rank should represent:

> **A history of war, not merely power.**

A high Tier says:

> **You are strong enough.**

A high Void Rank says:

> **The war trusts you.**
