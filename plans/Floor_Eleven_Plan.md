# Floor Eleven Plan

> [!summary]
> **Floors 1–10 form the Beginner Stratum.**
>
> Clearing Floor 10 does **not** grant immediate unrestricted access to Floor 11.
>
> A Player instead becomes a **Floor 11 Candidate** and must pass the universal **Multiversal Learning Path Qualification**.
>
> The Qualification is a public multiversal competition held once every **two years**, with a total duration of approximately **six months**.
>
> Passing **Stage I** is the universal certification required for Floor 11 eligibility.
>
> Later stages exist for academy scouting, prestige, recruitment, specialization, and elite selection.
>
> After qualification, each academy or learning pathway may impose its own additional admissions process.
>
> **Aeonmorta Academy** is one of the highest-tier institutions and conducts its own elite selection among invited candidates.
>
> Alok, as an Aeonmorta prince, may bypass the academy's entrance test through royal enrollment authority, but the current plan is for him to **participate anyway**.

---

# 1. Floor 11 Core Identity

Floor 11 is not primarily another combat floor.

It marks the transition from:

- survival,
- raw progression,
- beginner combat trials,
- and direct Tower clearing,

into:

- education,
- comprehension,
- specialization,
- research,
- mastery,
- independent application,
- and long-term path development.

The design principle is:

> **Floors 1–10 prove that you can climb.**
>
> **The Qualification proves that you are ready to learn.**
>
> **Floor 11 determines what you become.**

Floor 11 should deliberately slow down the extreme speed of the Beginner Floors.

It is not intended to be cleared in days or weeks.

A normal certified learning pathway may last roughly **3–5+ years**, depending on the institution, discipline, and individual.

---

# 2. Floor 10 Completion Status

After clearing Floor 10, the Player receives:

## **Beginner-Stratum Graduate — Floor 11 Candidate**

This status means:

- Floors 1–10 are permanently cleared.
- The Player is eligible to enter the next Multiversal Learning Path Qualification cycle.
- Floor 11 itself remains locked until Stage I certification is earned.
- If the Player misses a cycle, the Beginner-Stratum clear does not expire.
- They may wait for a later Qualification cycle.

Some Players intentionally delay participation to improve Tier, Stars, Class quality, education, equipment, specialist skill, or social backing.

---

# 3. Multiversal Learning Path Qualification

The Qualification is a massive open competition observed across the multiverse.

## Frequency

**Once every two years.**

## Total Duration

**Six months.**

## Multiversal Visibility

The event is publicly observed by:

- academies,
- sects,
- divine orders,
- noble Houses,
- military institutions,
- research institutions,
- crafting schools,
- private Masters,
- and other Tower-recognized learning pathways.

For many candidates, this is the first time their name becomes known outside their home world.

---

# 4. Three-Stage Structure

The open competition has **three major stages**.

Only **Stage I** is mandatory for Floor 11 certification.

Stages II and III exist for recruitment, prestige, specialization, academy invitations, and elite recognition.

---

# 5. Stage I — Qualification

Stage I is the actual universal gate to Floor 11.

It tests whether a candidate possesses sufficient foundations to begin advanced multiversal learning.

It should not be purely combat-based.

Possible tested areas:

- Mana control,
- combat fundamentals,
- adaptability,
- reasoning,
- comprehension,
- survival judgment,
- Class understanding,
- resource use,
- decision-making,
- learning under pressure,
- foundational theory,
- and practical application.

The Tower may adapt portions of Stage I to the candidate's Class, Rank, Tier, affinities, profession, or chosen path.

Passing Stage I grants:

[
FLOOR 11 ENTRY QUALIFICATION

Status:

**CERTIFIED**

You may now enroll in a Tower-recognized academy, Master-path, order, institution, or equivalent certified learning pathway.
]

A candidate does **not** need to continue into later stages in order to enter Floor 11.

---

Stage II is optional for Floor 11 access, but nearly every ambitious candidate continues because this is where institutions begin serious scouting.

The Showcase is divided according to path and discipline.

Possible categories:

- Combat
- Magic
- Summoning
- Beast Mastery
- Soul Studies
- Healing
- Alchemy
- Crafting
- Engineering
- Formations
- Command
- Strategy
- Exploration
- Scholarship
- Archery
- Elemental specialization
- Research
- Support
- Mixed disciplines

Candidates may enter multiple categories where appropriate.

Academy invitations begin arriving here.

Example:

[

Institution:
XXXX

XXXX

Scholarship:
XXXX

]

---

# 7. Stage III — Crown Phase

Stage III is the prestigious final phase.

It is not required for Floor 11 certification.

It exists for reputation, elite recruitment, rewards, academy competition, and public recognition.

Possible events:

- combat brackets,
- team scenarios,
- civilization simulations,
- expedition Trials,
- boss raids,
- research challenges,
- creation contests,
- strategic warfare,
- command exercises,
- mixed-path missions,
- survival exercises,
- and specialist finals.

This is the phase most heavily watched across the multiverse.

---

# 8. Six-Month Schedule

## Months 1–2
**Stage I — Qualification**

- Massive candidate population.
- Highest elimination volume.
- Universal Floor 11 certification occurs here.

## Months 3–4
**Stage II — Multiversal Showcase**

- Specialized categories.
- Academy scouting intensifies.
- Invitations begin arriving.
- Candidates start becoming publicly known.

## Months 5–6
**Stage III — Crown Phase**

- Elite finalists.
- High-profile events.
- Academy recruitment competition.
- Major rewards and prestige.
- Public multiversal attention peaks.

After Stage III, academy-specific admissions and enrollment periods begin.

---

# 10. Academy Admission Differences

## Ordinary Academies
May require only Stage I certification and basic entry requirements.

## Strong Academies
May require interviews, placement tests, discipline-specific assessment, or minimum Stage II performance.

## Elite Academies
May require invitation, Stage II results, internal examinations, sponsorship, or direct recommendation.

## Top Multiversal Institutions
May require exceptional performance, rare qualifications, lineage compatibility, high Rank, unusual Classes, and a second internal selection competition.

# 11. Aeonmorta Academy

The **Aeonmorta Academy** is one of the highest-tier multiversal institutions.

It exists to identify and develop exceptional individuals rather than mass-enroll students.

Possible student groups include:

- Aeonmorta bloodline members,
- allied bloodlines,
- invited outsiders,
- extreme geniuses,
- rare races,
- divine descendants,
- high-potential specialists,
- and candidates who distinguish themselves during the Qualification.

The academy is located within a family-controlled Aeonmorta / draconic realm.

---

# 12. Aeonmorta Selection

Outside candidates normally need:

1. Stage I certification.
2. An Aeonmorta invitation.
3. Completion of the Aeonmorta Selection.

Example:

[
AEONMORTA ACADEMY

You have been nominated for admission.

Invitation Status:

**Selection Candidate**

Admission requires completion of the Aeonmorta Selection.
]

The Selection may test:

- combat,
- learning speed,
- path depth,
- Mana control,
- creativity,
- will,
- adaptability,
- leadership,
- specialist expertise,
- and future potential.

# 14. Alok and the Open Competition

Alok participates in the Multiversal Learning Path Qualification even though his name is already known.

The event becomes the first time the broader multiverse sees him in a learning and evaluation context rather than purely as a Tower combat record holder.

His participation draws attention because of:

- his age,
- Divine Rank,
- Three-Star Core,
- Floor 1–10 record,
- unusual Classes,
- Shadow army,
- Divine Growth artifacts,
- multiracial vessel,
- and Champion of Judgment status.

The competition should demonstrate that Alok is not merely strong.

He is also adaptive, analytical, creative, technically talented, and exceptionally fast at learning.

# 15. Floor 11 Mastery Rule

Floor 11 cannot be passed through raw information alone.

The required progression is:

# **Theory → Practice → Independent Application / Creation → Final Validation**

The following do **not** automatically count as mastery:

- copied memories,
- implanted knowledge,
- borrowed understanding,
- externally supplied answers,
- raw information downloads,
- or someone else completing the work.

The candidate must internalize and demonstrate comprehension.

Alok already possesses abnormal authority over Souls, Shadows, restoration, life, death, racial structures, constructs, and Legion members.

> **Why does this work?**

rather than merely:

> **Can I do it?**

# 19. No Conventional Floor 11 Boss

Floor 11 does not require a traditional final boss.

Its challenge is:

- surviving the learning pathway,
- mastering the chosen discipline,
- completing institutional requirements,
- proving independent competence,
- and satisfying Tower validation.

The academy's graduation standard and the Tower's recognition function as the Floor 11 clear condition.

---

---

# 25. Why the Open Competition Stays

The universal competition remains part of the system because it:

- gives Floor 11 a universal threshold,
- allows unknown geniuses from obscure worlds to be discovered,
- creates a major multiversal public event,
- explains academy scouting,
- gives Alok a public benchmark,
- creates recruitment politics,
- and makes Floor 11 feel like a true progression boundary.

The system therefore has two layers:

# **Universal Qualification**
+
# **Institution-Specific Admission**
