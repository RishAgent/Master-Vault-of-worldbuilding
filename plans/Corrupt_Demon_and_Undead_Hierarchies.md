# Corrupt Demon and Undead Hierarchies

> [!summary]
> This note defines the broad **power hierarchy of corrupt / wild demons** and the matching broad hierarchy used for **undead threats**.
>
> These classifications are intended to scale beyond the Herromier incident into future regional, kingdom, empire, and Tower-level conflicts.
>
> **Corrupt demons are not the same thing as the civilized Demon races and states of the Tower.**
>
> Civilized demons possess nations, culture, law, military institutions, bloodlines, nobility, ordinary civilians, and legitimate rulers.
>
> Corrupt demons are destructive demonic beings shaped by unstable demonic essence, hostile realms, corruption, predation, ritual contamination, or similar forces.
>
> A being's **Tier** remains the true measure of realized power.
>
> Names such as **Imp**, **Demon**, **High Demon**, **Demon Noble**, **Demon Lord**, and **Demon King** are broad corruption/evolution classifications rather than substitutes for Tier.

---

# 1. Core Design Rule

The corrupt demon hierarchy should feel increasingly organized and dangerous as Tier rises.

Low-Tier corrupt demons behave more like:

- predators
- packs
- raiders
- instinctive destroyers
- semi-intelligent monsters

Higher-Tier corrupt demons increasingly possess:

- full intelligence
- demonic magic
- command ability
- domains
- armies
- complex curses
- dimensional movement
- ritual knowledge
- strategic goals
- the ability to conquer and administer corrupted territory

By the upper tiers, a corrupt demon is no longer merely a monster.

It can become a **kingdom-scale or empire-scale threat**.

---

# 2. Corrupt Demon Hierarchy

| Minimum Tier / Stage | Broad Classification | General Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Iron** | **Imp** | Lowest stable corrupt demon class |
| **Bronze** | **Lesser Demon** | Fully developed low-tier demon |
| **Silver** | **Demon** | True corrupt demon with mature demonic abilities |
| **Peak Silver** | **High Demon** | Elite Silver demon capable of commanding lesser demons |
| **Gold** | **Greater Demon** | Major regional demonic threat |
| **Peak Gold** | **Elite Demon** | Exceptional Gold demon; army-leading combatant |
| **Platinum** | **Demon Noble** | High demonic existence with territory/command authority |
| **Intermediate Platinum** | **Demon Lord** | Major ruler/commander of corrupted demonic forces |
| **Diamond** | **Demon King** | Kingdom- or multi-kingdom-scale corrupt demon ruler |

These are **minimum broad classifications**, not rigid species names.

A demon may possess a more specific racial/evolutionary name while still being categorized under one of these standings.

Example:

> Bloodhorn Ravager  
> Tier: Advanced Bronze  
> Broad Classification: Lesser Demon

---

# 3. Iron Tier — Imp

Iron demons are the lowest stable form of corrupt demonic life normally recognized as true demons.

Typical characteristics:

- small to human-sized bodies
- claws, horns, tails, fangs, or other unstable demonic traits
- low intelligence to basic sapience
- pack behavior
- crude demonic fire or corruption
- strong aggression
- little strategic discipline

Possible species / variants:

- Ash Imp
- Blood Imp
- Feral Imp
- Rot Imp
- Ember Imp
- Winged Imp
- Shadow Imp
- Bone Imp

A Peak Iron Imp may be drastically more dangerous than a Beginner Iron Imp while remaining broadly classified as an Imp.

---

# 4. Bronze Tier — Lesser Demon

At Bronze, corrupt demons become substantially more complete.

Typical characteristics:

- mature humanoid or monstrous bodies
- proper demonic Mana circulation
- stronger regeneration
- basic curse techniques
- formed weapons or natural weapons
- elemental/demonic attacks
- greater intelligence
- capacity to command Imps
- specialization into distinct combat roles

Possible variants:

- Horned Ravager
- Demonic Brute
- Blood Stalker
- Infernal Houndmaster
- Abyssal Marauder
- Corruption Hexer
- Flame Demon
- Shadow Demon
- Soul Leech

A Peak Bronze Lesser Demon may command dozens or hundreds of Iron Imps depending on intelligence and circumstances.

---

# 5. Silver Tier — Demon

Silver marks the point where the corrupt being is recognized simply as a true **Demon** rather than a lesser form.

Typical characteristics:

- mature demonic body
- high intelligence
- powerful demonic Mana
- advanced curses
- regeneration
- battlefield command
- dimensional or shadow movement in some bloodlines
- possession or contract abilities in some variants
- capable of directing Bronze and Iron demons
- potentially capable of creating or sustaining local corruption zones

A Silver Demon is already a serious regional threat.

## Herromier Use

The primary corrupt demon commander in the Herromier incident should be:

# Beginner Silver Demon

It works alongside the:

# Beginner Silver Lich

The Demon commands corrupt demonic forces and supports the corruption veil.

The Lich commands undead and stabilizes the Death-aligned invasion structure.

Neither needs to be inherently subordinate to the other.

---

# 6. Peak Silver — High Demon

Peak Silver demons represent the elite of the Silver demonic hierarchy.

Typical characteristics:

- command over large demon formations
- stronger corruption fields
- significant curse mastery
- advanced possession or contract techniques
- intelligent tactical command
- ability to threaten major cities
- ability to dominate weaker demonic species
- capacity to act as regional commanders beneath stronger demon rulers

---

# 7. Gold Tier — Greater Demon

Gold-tier corrupt demons are no longer local threats.

They can influence entire regions and potentially threaten small kingdoms if left unchecked.

Typical characteristics:

- large demonic domains or corruption zones
- command over thousands of lesser demons
- advanced demonic spellcraft
- high-level curses
- powerful regeneration
- dimensional travel or realm interaction
- advanced contracts
- strategic intelligence
- possible ability to establish permanent demonic nests or breaches

---

# 8. Peak Gold — Elite Demon

Peak Gold demons are exceptional battlefield monsters and commanders.

Typical characteristics:

- army-leading authority
- extreme combat specialization
- powerful personal domains
- elite demonic bloodlines or mutations
- capacity to destroy fortified cities
- ability to command Greater Demons or large numbers of lower demons
- enough intelligence to lead campaigns

---

# 9. Platinum Tier — Demon Noble

At Platinum, corrupt demons begin displaying something resembling a true ruling hierarchy.

The word **Noble** here is a corrupt-demon classification and does not imply recognition by civilized demon states.

Typical characteristics:

- possession of territory
- personal demonic armies
- stable corruption domains
- powerful bloodlines
- servants and subordinate demons
- sophisticated ritual magic
- long-term strategic planning
- ability to establish demonic strongholds

A Demon Noble can become a national-security threat.

---

# 10. Intermediate Platinum — Demon Lord

Demon Lord is reserved for beings that have moved beyond merely being powerful demons and now function as major rulers or commanders.

Minimum intended point:

# Intermediate Platinum

Typical characteristics:

- command over multiple Demon Nobles or equivalent forces
- large permanent armies
- control of extensive corrupted territory
- advanced dimensional and ritual magic
- large-scale curses
- realm breach creation
- high strategic intelligence
- ability to wage organized war against kingdoms

A Demon Lord can plausibly become the central antagonist of a kingdom-scale arc.

> [!important]
> This corrupt-demon title should not automatically be treated as equivalent to legitimate civilized demon titles.
>
> A civilized Demon Lord may receive that title through bloodline, culture, office, military standing, or recognized rulership.
>
> A corrupt Demon Lord receives the label because of its evolutionary power and command over corrupted demonic forces.

---

# 11. Diamond Tier — Demon King

Diamond marks the current planned upper classification of this corrupt hierarchy.

Typical characteristics:

- command over vast demonic armies
- multiple Demon Lords beneath them
- control over kingdoms or large corrupted realms
- capacity to destabilize multiple nations
- powerful dimensional authority
- large-scale realm breaches
- sophisticated war strategy
- ability to sustain long campaigns
- enormous corruption domains

A Demon King should be treated as:

# a multi-kingdom or empire-level threat

depending on the exact substage, bloodline, army, realm support, and political situation.

Future hierarchy above Diamond can be designed later when required.

---

# 12. Important Distinction — Civilized vs Corrupt Demons

The setting must never imply:

> demon = evil

Civilized demons remain a legitimate Tower race.

Examples include:

- Qureza
- Baneroth
- Demonoid retainers
- citizens of the Supreme Demon Empire
- allied or vassal demon states

Corrupt demons are a separate threat category.

Possible origins include:

- hostile demonic realms
- corrupted Mana environments
- failed bloodlines
- uncontrolled evolution
- ritual creation
- demonic parasites
- ancient curses
- dimensional contamination
- wild demonic ecosystems

Alok's demonic ancestry comes from legitimate supreme demon bloodlines and should not be conflated with corruption.

---

# 13. Corrupt Demon Army Structure

A large corrupt demonic army may naturally organize approximately as:

Demon King  
→ Demon Lords  
→ Demon Nobles  
→ Elite / Greater Demons  
→ High Demons  
→ Demons  
→ Lesser Demons  
→ Imps

This is not a mandatory military rank system.

It is an ecological / power hierarchy.

---

# 14. Herromier Demonic Forces

For the Herromier arc, only the lower portion of the hierarchy should appear.

Recommended maximum:

# Beginner Silver Demon

Possible force composition:

- numerous Iron Imps
- Bronze Lesser Demons
- a limited number of stronger Bronze elites
- one Beginner Silver Demon acting as demonic commander

No High Demon, Greater Demon, Demon Noble, Demon Lord, or Demon King should appear during Herromier.

---

# 15. Undead Hierarchy

The undead use a similar broad power hierarchy.

However, **species and role remain separate from classification**.

For example:

A Lich is a type of undead.

A Death Knight is a type of undead.

A Revenant is a type of undead.

Their Tier determines where they sit in the broader undead hierarchy.

This allows:

> Beginner Silver Lich  
> Broad Classification: High Undead

without requiring every Silver undead to be a Lich.

---

# 27. Herromier Three-Phase Attack

## Phase One — Beast Horde

The cult releases/manipulates accumulated monsters.

Main composition:

- large numbers of Iron monsters
- Bronze monsters
- several stronger Bronze beasts
- possible Peak Bronze apex monsters

Purpose:

- exhaust defenders
- create casualties
- damage defenses
- generate corpses
- create fear
- accumulate Death Mana

## Phase Two — Corrupt Demon Assault

After the beast phase weakens Herromier, corrupt demons enter openly.

Main forces:

- Iron Imps
- Bronze Lesser Demons
- stronger Bronze demonic variants

Command:

# Beginner Silver Demon

The Demon controls the demonic side of the assault and helps maintain the corruption veil.

## Phase Three — Death Breach

The prepared Death-aligned realm breaches Felora.

Undead begin entering through the Gate.

Main forces may include:

- Lesser Undead
- Greater Undead
- armored undead
- revenants
- undead mages
- corpse beasts
- stronger Bronze undead elites

Command:

# Beginner Silver Lich

Broad Classification:

High Undead

The Lich works alongside the Beginner Silver Demon.

The Demon handles:

- corruption
- demon forces
- fear pressure
- demonic reinforcement
- cult empowerment

The Lich handles:

- undead command
- Death Mana
- corpses
- soul anchoring
- Gate stabilization

The final phase therefore becomes a combined:

# Demon + Undead assault

rather than simply a second monster wave.

# 28. Future Scaling Principle

The Herromier incident introduces only the lowest portion of both hierarchies.

Future arcs may escalate naturally:

Herromier  
→ Silver Demon / Silver Lich

Regional conflict  
→ High Demon / Undead Commander

Kingdom conflict  
→ Greater Demon / Undead Lord

Major kingdom / small empire conflict  
→ Elite Demon / Elite Undead

National or multi-national crisis  
→ Demon Noble / Undead Noble

Kingdom-scale war  
→ Demon Lord / Death Lord

Multi-kingdom / empire-scale war  
→ Demon King / Undead King

# 29. Canon Locks

- Corrupt demons are distinct from civilized demon races.
- Demons as a race are not inherently evil.
- Iron corrupt demons are broadly classified as Imps.
- Bronze corrupt demons are Lesser Demons.
- Silver corrupt demons are Demons.
- Peak Silver corrupt demons are High Demons.
- Gold corrupt demons are Greater Demons.
- Peak Gold corrupt demons are Elite Demons.
- Platinum corrupt demons are Demon Nobles.
- Intermediate Platinum corrupt demons may qualify as Demon Lords.
- Diamond corrupt demons may qualify as Demon Kings.
- The hierarchy may expand above Diamond later.
- Herromier introduces corrupt demons only up to Beginner Silver.
- The Herromier demonic commander is a Beginner Silver Demon.
- The Herromier undead commander is a Beginner Silver Lich.
- Lich is a species/type, not the universal name for all Silver undead.
- Undead broad hierarchy is separate from undead species.
- The Herromier attack has three major combat phases: Beast Horde → Corrupt Demons → Undead Gate / combined final assault.
- Corrupt demons and undead will remain important future antagonistic forces capable of scaling to kingdom, multi-kingdom, and empire-level threats.
