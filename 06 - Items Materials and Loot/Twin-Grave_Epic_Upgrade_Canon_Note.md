# Gravefire Stacking Rule

Every Gravefire projectile that successfully hits a target applies its own independent **15-second Gravefire Burn**.

Gravefire does **not** merely refresh a single burn effect.

Examples:

- 1 projectile hit = 1 active Gravefire Burn
- 2 projectile hits = 2 active Gravefire Burns
- 10 projectile hits = 10 active Gravefire Burns

Each barrel applies its own burn independently.

Repeated trigger pulls can therefore build multiple simultaneous Gravefire stacks on the same target.

There is currently **no inherent Twin-Grave stack limit**.

Practical limits are:

- Alok's Mana reserve
- fire rate
- accuracy
- target durability
- target resistance
- the target's ability to remove, suppress, or extinguish Gravefire

# New Epic Ability — Gravefire Collapse

[ NEW EPIC WEAPON ABILITY UNLOCKED ]

## GRAVEFIRE COLLAPSE

Twin Grave may forcibly collapse all active Gravefire instances currently burning within a single target.

All remaining Gravefire damage is dealt immediately.

### Requirements

**Minimum Active Gravefire Stacks:** 2

**Activation Cost:** 500 Mana

**Cooldown:** 30 seconds

### Effect

- Consumes all active Gravefire Burns on the selected target.
- Each consumed Burn deals **100% of its remaining damage immediately**.
- The consumed Burns then end.
- Gravefire Collapse does **not** create extra damage beyond the damage already stored in the active Burns.
- Its purpose is to convert Twin Grave's remaining damage-over-time into immediate burst damage.

### Example

If Alok lands 10 Epic Rapidfire projectiles on a target, those 10 projectiles carry a maximum combined Gravefire reserve of:

**31,752 AP × 10 = 317,520 AP**

If activated immediately, **Gravefire Collapse** can discharge up to:

**317,520 AP**

at once, in addition to the impact damage already dealt by the shots.

# Epic Weapon Identity

Twin Grave's combat identity at Epic rarity is:

**Standard Form**
- heavier immediate burst
- more Mana-efficient
- strong precision damage
- ideal for headshots, weak points, and controlled kills

**Rapidfire Form**
- ×5 firing speed
- lower damage per trigger
- rapidly accumulates large numbers of Gravefire stacks
- ideal for large monsters, bosses, regeneration-heavy enemies, and armor breaking

**Gravefire Collapse**
- converts stacked burn damage into immediate burst
- especially effective against regenerating or heavily armored Silver-tier enemies
