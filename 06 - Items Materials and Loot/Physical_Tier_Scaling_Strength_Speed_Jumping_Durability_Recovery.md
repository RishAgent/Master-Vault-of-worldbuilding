# Physical Tier Scaling — Strength, Speed, Jumping, Durability and Recovery

> [!important] Core Principle
> **Tier measures realized vessel development.**
>
> Level and Tier are separate.
>
> A Level 30 Beginner Bronze is not physically equal to a Level 30 Intermediate or Peak Bronze.
>
> High VIT does **not** automatically grant true Regeneration.

---

# 1. Human and Tier Benchmarks

The current working stat benchmarks are:

| Tier | Beginner | Intermediate | Advanced | Peak |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Ordinary | 5 | 8 | 12 | 16 |
| Iron | 22 | 40 | 80 | 150 |
| Bronze | 250 | 450 | 800 | 1,500 |
| Silver | 2,500 | 4,500 | 8,000 | 15,000 |

Practical interpretation:

| Vessel | Typical Stat Range | Physical Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Normal adult | ~8 | Ordinary human |
| Trained / athletic adult | ~10–12 | Fit soldier / athlete |
| Peak human | ~16 | Absolute mundane human elite |
| Beginner Iron | ~22 | Clearly superhuman |
| Intermediate Iron | ~40 | Low superhuman |
| Advanced Iron | ~80 | Powerful superhuman |
| Peak Iron | ~150 | Extreme pre-Bronze |
| Beginner Bronze | ~250 | Heavy superhuman |
| Intermediate Bronze | ~450 | Battlefield-scale superhuman |
| Advanced Bronze | ~800 | Major superhuman asset |
| Peak Bronze | ~1,500 | Monster-level humanoid |
| Alok, saturated Bronze | ~1,800 | Beyond normal Peak Bronze entry |

> [!note]
> These are **benchmarks**, not a requirement that all four attributes be equal.
>
> Specialized builds remain valid.

# 2. STR — Controlled Lifting Strength

STR does not convert linearly into kilograms.

Tier reconstruction improves force transmission, tendon integrity, skeletal load tolerance, muscular efficiency, and Mana-assisted vessel cohesion.

| STR | Approx. Controlled Maximum |
|---:|---:|
| 8 | ~60–100 kg |
| 12 | ~120–200 kg |
| 16 | ~350–500 kg |
| 22 | ~600–900 kg |
| 40 | ~1.5–2 tonnes |
| 80 | ~3–5 tonnes |
| 150 | ~7–10 tonnes |
| 250 | ~12–18 tonnes |
| 450 | ~25–35 tonnes |
| 800 | ~50–70 tonnes |
| 1,500 | ~90–120 tonnes |
| 1,800 | ~120–150 tonnes |

These represent controlled lifting or pushing feats, not maximum striking output.

---

# 3. Striking Force

A strike is more destructive than a simple lifting comparison because it combines STR, acceleration, body mass, technique, AGI, kinetic reinforcement, and Mana reinforcement where applicable.

## Beginner Bronze

A serious physical strike can:

- smash concrete walls
- badly deform cars
- crack reinforced masonry
- throw multi-ton monsters
- bend substantial steel components

## Intermediate Bronze

Can:

- flip heavy vehicles
- move loaded trucks
- crater concrete
- deform armored vehicles
- grapple creatures weighing dozens of tonnes

## Advanced Bronze

Can:

- break major reinforced structures
- demolish portions of buildings
- rip through heavy structural materials

## Peak Bronze

Can:

- throw vehicles effortlessly
- move objects near or above 100 tonnes
- punch through substantial reinforced concrete
- rip apart conventional steel structures
- wrestle enormous Bronze monsters
- create major impact craters

## Alok — Saturated Peak Bronze

At roughly ~1,800 physical stats, ordinary human infrastructure is fragile around him if he stops restraining himself.

# 4. AGI — Speed and Reaction

AGI includes acceleration, reaction speed, perception, balance, coordination, direction changes, and full-body movement speed.

| AGI | Approx. Short-Burst Movement |
|---:|---:|
| 8 | ~25–30 km/h |
| 12 | ~35 km/h |
| 16 | ~45 km/h |
| 22 | ~55–65 km/h |
| 40 | ~90–110 km/h |
| 80 | ~160–200 km/h |
| 150 | ~280–350 km/h |
| 250 | ~450–600 km/h |
| 450 | ~750–950 km/h |
| 800 | ~Mach 1.2–1.7 |
| 1,500 | ~Mach 2.5–3.5 |
| 1,800 | ~Mach 3–4 burst territory |

> [!important]
> **Natural movement is not the same as movement Skills.**
>
> Thunderstep, magical reinforcement, barrier launches, teleportation-like Skills, flight, and similar techniques can exceed raw AGI movement.

Reaction speed is also faster than full-body travel.

These are **unassisted physical jumps**.

No Skills, flight, Thunderstep, barrier platforms, air steps, or magical propulsion.

| Physical Level | Vertical Jump | Running Horizontal Jump |
|---|---:|---:|
| Normal adult ~8 | ~0.4–0.6 m | ~2–4 m |
| Athletic ~12 | ~0.7–0.9 m | ~5–7 m |
| Peak Ordinary ~16 | ~1–1.2 m | ~8–10 m |
| Beginner Iron ~22 | ~1.5–2 m | ~10–15 m |
| Intermediate Iron ~40 | ~3–4 m | ~18–25 m |
| Advanced Iron ~80 | ~6–8 m | ~30–45 m |
| Peak Iron ~150 | ~10–15 m | ~50–70 m |
| Beginner Bronze ~250 | ~20–30 m | ~60–90 m |
| Intermediate Bronze ~450 | ~35–55 m | ~100–150 m |

## Environmental Consequence

High-Tier beings cannot always launch at full force from ordinary surfaces.

A Peak Bronze making a maximum jump from asphalt may crack or crater the launch point.

Experienced Champions instinctively restrain their output in civilian environments.

# 6. VIT — Durability and Vessel Cohesion

VIT should not be interpreted as literal physical density.

A Bronze person does not suddenly weigh several tonnes.

Instead, VIT increases:

- biological cohesion
- skeletal strength
- connective tissue durability
- muscle integrity
- organ resilience
- shock resistance
- blood-loss tolerance
- stamina
- natural healing efficiency
- Mana-assisted structural stability

Their skin may still feel like skin under normal touch.

The extreme durability appears when force is applied.

---

# 7. Durability by Tier

## Ordinary

Normal human durability.

## Iron

Increasingly superhuman.

By Peak Iron:

- bones are extremely difficult to break
- blunt force that would kill a human may be survivable
- ordinary humans struggle to harm them physically

## Beginner Bronze

A typical Beginner Bronze is:

- difficult to cut with ordinary knives
- highly resistant to mundane blunt trauma
- able to survive vehicle impacts
- able to survive multi-story falls
- highly resistant to ordinary handgun fire

High-powered rifle rounds may still injure depending on location, ammunition, armor, specialization, and VIT.

## Intermediate Bronze

- handguns are close to irrelevant
- ordinary rifles may bruise, break skin, or penetrate shallowly
- heavy rifles and explosives remain dangerous

## Advanced Bronze

Standard firearms are generally poor weapons.

Meaningful threats include anti-materiel weapons, heavy explosives, Mana weapons, and high-Tier Awakened attacks.

## Peak Bronze

- ordinary small arms are effectively background threats
- large military weapons remain relevant
- conventional force needs scale rather than merely caliber

---

# 8. VIT Is Not Regeneration

This is a hard distinction.

## VIT

VIT improves the body's ability to:

- survive damage
- stabilize itself
- clot wounds
- repair existing tissue
- recover from exertion
- heal fractures
- recover damaged organs

## Regeneration

Regeneration can actively reconstruct:

- destroyed organs
- missing tissue
- broken structures during combat
- severed or lost limbs
- catastrophic injuries

Core rule:

> **VIT preserves and repairs. Regeneration reconstructs.**

---

# 9. Natural Recovery Times

The following assumes no healing Skills, no potions, no healer, no regeneration ability, and adequate food, Mana, and rest.

## Ordinary Human

- bruises / cuts: days
- fractures: weeks to months
- major organ injury: dangerous or permanently disabling
- severed limb: does not regrow

## Iron

- minor cuts / bruises: hours to ~1 day
- muscle strains: ~1–3 days
- moderate wounds: several days
- simple fracture: ~1–3 weeks
- major fracture: several weeks
- serious organ injury: weeks to months

Lost limb: **No natural regrowth.**

## Beginner Bronze ~250 VIT

- small cuts: minutes to hours
- bruising: hours
- moderate muscle tear: ~12–36 hours
- deep clean wound: ~1–3 days
- simple fracture: ~3–7 days
- complex fracture: ~1–3 weeks
- moderate organ injury: several days to weeks
- major organ trauma: weeks and potentially lethal

## Intermediate Bronze ~450 VIT

- superficial injuries: minutes
- moderate wounds: hours to ~1 day
- severe muscle tear: ~1–2 days
- simple fracture: ~1–3 days
- complex fracture: ~4–10 days
- moderate organ damage: ~2–7 days
- severe organ damage: ~1–3 weeks

## Advanced Bronze ~800 VIT

- minor wounds: minutes
- moderate wounds: a few hours
- major muscle damage: ~12–24 hours
- simple fractures: ~1–3 days
- major fractures: several days
- organ injury: ~1–7 days depending on severity

## Peak Bronze ~1,500 VIT

- cuts / bruises: minutes
- major flesh wounds: several hours
- severe muscle damage: hours to ~1 day
- simple fractures: ~12–24 hours
- major fractures: ~1–3 days
- organ trauma: hours to several days depending on severity

# 10. Destroyed Organs and Fatal Injury

High VIT does not mean immortality.

Most Champions still die if they suffer:

- total brain destruction
- decapitation
- complete heart destruction
- catastrophic blood loss before stabilization
- massive full-body destruction

A damaged organ may heal.

A completely annihilated organ may exceed normal VIT-based repair unless enough structure remains or advanced healing is available.

This is one of the major ways Alok's Regeneration remains exceptional.

---

# 11. Lost Limbs

High VIT does not normally regrow a completely missing limb.

If a Bronze warrior loses an arm:

- the wound closes quickly
- blood loss is controlled faster
- the remaining tissue heals cleanly
- the arm does **not** naturally grow back

Possible restoration methods:

- Regeneration Skill
- Life magic
- powerful healer
- regeneration elixir
- racial regenerative ability
- advanced magical medicine
- artificial magical prosthetic
- Tier-breakthrough reconstruction
- rare Tower resources

---

# 12. Reattachment vs Regrowth

Reattaching a severed limb is much easier than creating a new one.

If the limb is recovered quickly, a sufficiently strong healer can potentially reconnect:

- bone
- arteries
- veins
- nerves
- muscle
- Mana channels

Battlefield protocol for high-Tier teams:

> **Recover and store severed body parts immediately.**

Inventory storage can become extremely valuable for preserving amputated limbs for later reattachment.

---

# 13. Tier-Breakthrough Reconstruction

Major Tier breakthroughs reconstruct and refine the vessel.

A missing limb may potentially be restored during a major breakthrough if:

- the System retains the original body template
- sufficient Mana is available
- sufficient biological / magical resources are supplied
- the breakthrough is powerful enough
- the individual survives the process

This should be possible but exceptional.

It is **not** guaranteed natural limb regeneration.

---

# 14. Stamina and Physical Recovery

VIT also affects stamina and fatigue recovery.

## Beginner Bronze

Can perform strenuous activity for many hours.

A hard physical fight may require roughly 30–90 minutes for major stamina recovery, assuming Mana-channel or mental fatigue is not the main limitation.

## Intermediate Bronze

Can operate at high intensity for many hours.

A few hours of sleep can provide recovery comparable to much longer mundane rest.

## Peak Bronze

Can remain physically active for extreme durations.

However, high VIT does not eliminate:

- sleep deprivation
- neural fatigue
- Mana-channel exhaustion
- psychological stress
- emotional exhaustion
- Overdrive damage

Alok's Frost Island collapse is an important example.

Even a powerful vessel still eventually requires sleep and recovery.

# 15. Falling Durability

Approximate uncontrolled fall tolerance:

| Tier | Typical Fall Tolerance |
|---|---:|
| Ordinary | ~3–6 m can seriously injure |
| Peak Ordinary | ~8–12 m may be survivable but dangerous |
| Beginner Iron | ~15–25 m |
| Intermediate Iron | ~30–50 m |
| Advanced Iron | ~70–100 m |
| Peak Iron | ~150–200 m |
| Beginner Bronze | several hundred meters |
| Intermediate Bronze | ~0.5–1 km depending on landing |
| Advanced Bronze | potentially kilometers |
| Peak Bronze | terminal velocity is no longer inherently lethal |

At high Bronze, landing surface and body orientation matter more than simple height.

A Peak Bronze can still break bones, suffer internal injury, and crater the impact surface.

Alok's Regeneration makes such falls substantially less dangerous to him than to a normal Peak Bronze.

---

# 16. Civilian-Life Restraint

Bronze and higher beings must consciously regulate their strength in ordinary environments.

Experienced Judgment Champions have years of control.

They can safely:

- shake hands
- hug civilians
- sit in ordinary furniture
- ride normal vehicles
- hold children
- interact with doors and appliances

But this is learned restraint.

A newly advanced Bronze may accidentally:

- break handles
- crack floors
- bend objects
- damage vehicles
- injure people

Alok's control is exceptionally refined.

---

# 17. Returning Champion Physical Context

Most returning Earth Champions are Level 30, but Tier remains the better physical comparison.

Current rough distribution:

- majority: **Beginner Bronze**
- substantial minority: **Intermediate Bronze**
- small handful: **Advanced Bronze**
- Alok: **Peak Bronze saturated / Silver advancement unlocked**
- Serena: **Beginner Silver exception**

This means a normal returning Champion is already massively beyond mundane humanity.

Even Beginner Bronze Champions are effectively:

> **humanoid armored combat assets with superhuman reactions, magic, and years of battle experience.**

---

# 18. Alok Special Case

Alok's physical scaling is not representative of normal Bronze.

He possesses:

- ~1,800 Peak-Bronze saturation stats
- Divine personal rank
- Two-Star Mana Core
- Regeneration
- extraordinary Mana control
- advanced combat experience
- multiple Classes
- Eternal Legion
- movement Skills
- magical reinforcement

Most importantly:

> **His Regeneration allows active reconstruction that ordinary Champions cannot replicate.**

An injury that would permanently cripple another Bronze fighter may be temporary for Alok if he has enough Mana and remains alive long enough to regenerate.

# 19. Canon Locks

- Tier measures realized current vessel power.
- Level and Tier are separate.
- Intermediate Ordinary is the practical healthy-human baseline.
- Peak Ordinary represents mundane human physical limits.
- Bronze is a major qualitative vessel threshold.
- STR is not a linear kg conversion.
- AGI includes reaction, perception, coordination, and movement.
- raw jumps are distinct from magical movement Skills.
- high-Tier jumping can damage mundane terrain.
- VIT increases vessel cohesion, durability, stamina, and natural healing.
- VIT does not proportionally increase body weight.
- VIT does not equal Regeneration.
- high VIT can rapidly heal damaged tissue.
- high VIT does not naturally regrow completely lost limbs.
- reattachment is easier than regrowth.
- major Tier breakthroughs may exceptionally restore missing anatomy.
- high VIT does not remove the need for sleep.
- brain destruction, decapitation, complete heart destruction, and catastrophic blood loss remain lethal to most non-regenerators.
- Alok's Regeneration remains a distinct and extraordinary advantage.
- experienced Champions instinctively restrain their physical output around civilians and mundane infrastructure.
