# Mana Crystal Conversion Table

## Purpose

Mana Crystals are crystallized Mana resources obtained from monsters, Gate deposits, Mana veins, treasure areas, harvesting, quest rewards, and certain magical objects.

They are distinct from **Monster Cores**.

- **Mana Crystal** = condensed Mana resource / crafting / conversion material.
- **Monster Core** = biological or magical core harvested from a monster.
- Monster kills do **not** guarantee either resource.
- Crystal value is determined by **Tier, size, purity, and Mana density**.
- The Tower can assign an exact conversion value in Tower Points (TP).

## Crystal Quality

The values above are ranges because two crystals of the same Tier are not necessarily equal.

The Tower evaluates:

- **Tier**
- **Purity**
- **Size**
- **Mana Density**
- **Stability**
- **Special elemental or conceptual properties**

Example:

```text
[ MANA CRYSTAL ]

Name: Iron Mana Crystal
Tier: Iron
Purity: 63%
Mana Density: Moderate
Tower Conversion Value: 91 TP
```

A poor Iron Mana Crystal may be worth roughly **50 TP**, while an unusually pure one may approach **150 TP**.

---

## Drop and Harvest Rules

Mana Crystals are **not guaranteed monster drops**.

Possible sources include:

- Monster drops
- Mana veins
- Gate mineral deposits
- Elites and bosses
- Harvesting Mana-rich organs or growths
- Quest rewards
- Hidden treasure zones
- Dungeon / Gate reward chests
- Dismantling magical objects
- Refining lower-grade Mana material

Monster Cores are tracked separately.

This prevents a single kill from automatically satisfying every material requirement.

## Breakthrough Relevance

Example Beginner Iron breakthrough requirement:

```text
[ BREAKTHROUGH QUEST ]

The First Step Beyond

Combat Requirement:
- Kill 10 Iron-Tier monsters
  OR
- Kill 50 Ordinary-Tier monsters

Material Requirement:
- Monster Cores ×5
- Mana Crystals ×10
```

The materials must be personally earned where the System specifies this.

Purchased materials may fail to qualify for breakthrough conditions.

---

## Economic Intent

The early economy should remain meaningful.

A rough early comparison:

- ~10 Ordinary Mana Crystals ≈ 1 Minor Mana Potion
- ~3–7 Iron Mana Crystals ≈ 1 standard Mana Potion
- ~7–20 Iron Mana Crystals ≈ 1 Major Mana Potion

This keeps Gate hunting profitable without making early monster farming instantly trivialize the Tower Shop.

---

## High-Tier Economy

From **Transcendent Tier upward**, Tower Point values are best treated as **nominal conversion values** rather than normal market prices.

At those levels, civilizations may prefer:

- Direct barter
- Contribution authority
- Territory
- Divine materials
- Laws / concepts
- Unique artifacts
- Political or military exchange
- Specialized high-order currencies

A Divine Mana Crystal may have a Tower conversion value, but almost nobody sensible would sell one for ordinary shopping currency.

---

## Progression Philosophy

**Ordinary / Iron**
- Pocket economy
- Beginner materials
- Consumables
- Basic crafting

**Bronze / Silver**
- Serious adventuring income
- Equipment improvement
- Skill acquisition
- Breakthrough resources

**Gold / Diamond**
- Significant wealth
- High-level equipment
- Major formations
- Strategic materials

**Supreme – King**
- Strategic resources
- Organization / kingdom-scale assets

**Emperor+**
- Civilization-scale resources
- Ordinary monetary value becomes secondary
