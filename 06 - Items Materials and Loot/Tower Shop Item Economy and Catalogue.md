# Tower Shop Item Economy & Example Catalogue

## Core Rule

Tower Shop pricing depends on:

1. **Grade**
2. **Utility**
3. **Rarity**
4. **Power**
5. **Tier / Star requirement**
6. **Permanent vs temporary effect**
7. **Whether the item grants progression, combat strength, survival, or convenience**

Grade determines the **general ceiling**, not a fixed price.

A Common-grade object can cost only a few Tower Points or reach as high as roughly **25,000 TP** if it has exceptional utility.

This includes **Skills**.

---

# 1. Grade Pricing Bands

| Grade | Typical Price Band | General Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Common | 5 – 25,000 TP | Beginner gear, basic Skills, potions, utility items, ordinary training resources |
| Uncommon | 25,000 – 250,000 TP | Strong early progression, improved equipment, specialized Skills, superior consumables |
| Rare | 250,000 – 2,500,000 TP | Powerful Skills, high-value equipment, major recovery items, specialized progression resources |
| Epic | 2,500,000 – 25,000,000 TP | Elite equipment, class-defining Skills, rare advancement resources |
| Legendary | 25,000,000 – 250,000,000 TP | Exceptional artifacts, major inheritance Skills, extremely rare progression items |
| Mythic | 250,000,000 – 2.5 Billion TP | Civilization-level treasures, bloodline items, extraordinary Skills and artifacts |
| Divine | 2.5 Billion+ TP | Divine materials, god-level Skills, conceptual artifacts; often not truly obtainable with TP alone |

> Prices can exceed these bands for unique or unusually powerful items.
>
> Some items may also require Tier, Star Rating, Class, Affinity, achievements, or Shop authority in addition to Tower Points.

# 3. Skills

Skill Scrolls are priced by:

- Grade
- Mana efficiency
- Attack / defense power
- Versatility
- Star requirement
- Elemental rarity
- Whether the Skill scales strongly
- Whether it is passive or active
- Whether it has movement, healing, or control utility

A Skill can be Common and still cost close to **25,000 TP** if it is unusually useful.

# 9. Special Restrictions

Certain items may not be freely purchasable even if the user has enough Tower Points.

Possible requirements:

```text
Tier Requirement
Level Requirement
Star Rating Requirement
Class Requirement
Affinity Requirement
Achievement Requirement
Tower Authority
Regional Availability
Limited Stock
Quest Completion
Personal Rank Requirement
```

Examples:

- Judgment Fragments are normally **earned**, not freely purchased.
- Class-awakening resources may require achievements.
- High-grade bloodline items may require compatibility.
- Divine items may require non-monetary exchange.

# 10. Early-Story Economic Baseline

For Alok's current stage, **5,000 TP** should feel significant.

It is enough to buy:

- many basic potions,
- a Common weapon,
- a training manual,
- utility equipment,
- or part of the cost of a stronger Common Skill.

It is **not** enough to freely purchase every useful Common Skill.

Example prices:

```text
Mana Iron Sword ............... 2,500 TP
Lightning Zap Skill Scroll .... 8,000 TP
Minor Mana Potion ............... 100 TP
Minor Health Potion ............. 100 TP
Minor Mana Shield ............ 18,000 TP
Danger Sense ................. 24,000 TP
```

This gives Common Grade a wide but useful economic range:

- cheap consumables,
- ordinary tools,
- starter equipment,
- expensive utility,
- and valuable foundational Skills.
