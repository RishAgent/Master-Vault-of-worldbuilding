# Tower Shop Item Economy & Catalogue

> [!important] Economy Revision
> This revision assumes that experienced Awakened can accumulate **very large Tower Point reserves** over years of Gates, Judgment rewards, achievements, sales, and Trial activity.
>
> Alok's earlier Judgment reward included **46,000 Gold Coins**, with:
>
> **1 Gold Coin = 5,000 TP**
>
> Total gross value:
>
> **46,000 × 5,000 = 230,000,000 TP**
>
> Therefore, meaningful Skills, equipment, draw tickets, advancement items, and higher-grade treasures must be priced high enough that hundreds of millions of TP represent major wealth without trivializing the Tower economy.

---

# 1. Core Economic Rules

Tower Shop pricing depends on:

1. **Grade**
2. **Utility**
3. **Rarity**
4. **Power**
5. **Tier / Star requirement**
6. **Permanent vs temporary effect**
7. **Scalability**
8. **Whether it grants direct progression**
9. **Whether it provides survival, information, convenience, or combat power**
10. **Availability / stock**
11. **Achievement or authority requirements**

Grade determines a broad economic band, not a fixed price.

Two Epic items can differ enormously in price if one is a conventional weapon and the other is a permanent growth artifact.

# 3. Revised Item Grade Ladder

The old seven-grade structure is expanded because the Tower spans civilizations whose resources vary far beyond ordinary mortal equipment.

| Grade | Typical Price Band | General Role |
|---|---:|---|
| **Common** | 100 – 100,000 TP | Basic consumables, starter gear, foundational Skills, everyday Tower utility |
| **Uncommon** | 100,000 – 1,000,000 TP | Strong low-tier gear, refined Skills, quality survival equipment |
| **Rare** | 1,000,000 – 10,000,000 TP | Serious combat gear, strong Skills, advanced recovery and progression support |
| **Epic** | 10,000,000 – 100,000,000 TP | Elite equipment, class-defining Skills, powerful growth items |
| **Legendary** | 100,000,000 – 1,000,000,000 TP | Exceptional artifacts, inheritances, major advancement resources |
| **Mythic** | 1 Billion – 10 Billion TP | Civilization-famous treasures, bloodline resources, extraordinary artifacts |
| **Ancient** | 10 Billion – 100 Billion TP | Relics of extinct or ancient high-order civilizations; highly restricted |
| **Relic** | 100 Billion – 1 Trillion TP | Strategic artifacts whose value exceeds ordinary adventurer economies |
| **Primordial** | 1 Trillion – 100 Trillion TP | World-shaping or lineage-origin treasures; often unique |
| **Divine** | 100 Trillion TP+ / special exchange | God-level artifacts, Laws, concepts, divine bloodlines, world authority |
| **Unique / Unclassified** | No fixed band | System exceptions, one-of-a-kind objects, Trial artifacts, conceptual items |

> [!note]
> **Unique** and **Unclassified** are descriptors rather than normal linear grades.
> A Unique item may be weak but irreplaceable, or more valuable than a Divine artifact.

# 4. Economic Interpretation by Wealth

## Early Awakened
**10,000 – 500,000 TP**

Can afford:
- Common consumables
- basic equipment
- basic Skills
- survival utility

## Established Awakened
**500,000 – 10,000,000 TP**

Can afford:
- strong Common / Uncommon equipment
- selected Rare items
- meaningful Skills
- serious reserves of consumables

## Elite Earth Awakened
**10,000,000 – 100,000,000 TP**

Can afford:
- Rare equipment comfortably
- selected Epic purchases
- large potion reserves
- advanced Trial preparation

## Extremely Wealthy Individual
**100,000,000 – 1,000,000,000 TP**

Can access:
- several Epic items
- perhaps a Legendary item
- expensive draw tickets
- major advancement resources

This is where Alok's early pre-True-Trial wealth sits.

He is extremely wealthy by Earth's standards, but not remotely rich by the standards of mature Tower civilizations.

# 13. Trial Shop Rules

The Stage Shop and Tower Shop use the same underlying currency:

# **Tower Points**

No separate expendable Trial-token economy is required unless a later Trial specifically introduces a temporary scoring mechanic.

Inside a Trial:
- EXP comes from recognized kills.
- Mana Crystals may drop or be harvested.
- Monster Cores may drop or be harvested.
- System Drops roll independently.
- Biological materials are separate.
- Hidden treasures may be kept.
- Items and materials may be sold or converted into TP.
- Achievements and milestones can directly award TP.

A Trial may additionally track contribution score, faction reputation, completion rank, or hidden objective score, but these do not automatically replace TP as the spendable Tower currency.

# 15. Restrictions

Having enough TP does not guarantee purchase access.

Possible restrictions:
- Tier Requirement
- Level Requirement
- Star Requirement
- Class Requirement
- Race Requirement
- Affinity Requirement
- Achievement Requirement
- Tower Authority
- Trial completion
- limited stock
- regional availability
- personal Rank
- compatibility
- contribution threshold

Examples:
- Judgment Fragments are normally earned, not freely purchased.
- Class-awakening resources may require achievements.
- bloodline items require compatibility.
- Ancient, Relic, Primordial, and Divine items may require authority or non-TP exchange.
- unique artifacts may never appear in ordinary Shop stock.

# 17. Economic Philosophy

## Common / Uncommon
Everyday adventurer economy.

## Rare
Serious professional progression.

## Epic
Elite individual assets.

## Legendary
National / organization-level treasures on a young world.

## Mythic
Civilization-famous resources.

## Ancient
Treasures with historical or extinct-civilization value.

## Relic
Strategic assets.

## Primordial
World-order / lineage-origin treasures.

## Divine
Conceptual, divine, or civilization-defining assets.

At sufficiently high Grades, TP becomes only one component of value.

Trade increasingly shifts toward:
- direct barter
- rare materials
- Laws
- authority
- territory
- bloodlines
- divine resources
- political exchange
- favors
- inheritance access
- contribution rights
