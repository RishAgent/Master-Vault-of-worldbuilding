# Monster Core Conversion Table

## Purpose

Monster Cores are biological or magical cores harvested from monsters.

They are distinct from **Mana Crystals**.

- **Mana Crystal** = condensed Mana resource / crafting / conversion material.
- **Monster Core** = biological or magical core harvested from a monster.
- Monster kills do **not** guarantee either resource.
- Core value is determined by **Tier, stage, size, purity, integrity, Mana density, and special properties**.
- The Tower can assign an exact conversion value in Tower Points (TP).

Monster Cores are normally more valuable than equivalent Mana Crystals because they contain both usable Mana and part of the monster's biological, elemental, or magical imprint.

As a general rule:

> **A Monster Core is worth roughly 2× an equivalent Mana Crystal of comparable Tier and quality.**

## Core Quality

The values above are ranges because two Monster Cores of the same Tier are not necessarily equal.

The Tower evaluates:

- **Tier**
- **Monster Stage**
- **Purity**
- **Size**
- **Mana Density**
- **Integrity**
- **Stability**
- **Affinity**
- **Species rarity**
- **Mutation / evolution status**
- **Special elemental or conceptual properties**

Example:

```text
[ MONSTER CORE ]

Name: Advanced Iron Goblin Elite Core
Tier: Iron
Stage: Advanced
Purity: 71%
Integrity: Excellent
Mana Density: High
Affinity: None
Tower Conversion Value: 226 TP
```

A poor Iron Monster Core may be worth roughly **100 TP**, while a high-quality or unusual one may exceed **300 TP**.

## Stage-Based Value Within a Tier

The Tier determines the broad value band.

The monster's Stage helps determine where the Core normally falls within that band.

### Example — Iron-Tier Monster Cores

| Monster Stage | Typical Core Value |
|---|---:|
| Beginner Iron | 100 – 130 TP |
| Intermediate Iron | 130 – 180 TP |
| Advanced Iron | 180 – 240 TP |
| Peak Iron | 240 – 300 TP |
| Iron Elite | 300 – 500+ TP |
| Iron Boss | 500 – 1,000+ TP |

Elites and bosses may exceed the normal Tier range because of superior Mana Density, purity, evolved biology, rare affinities, or unique properties.

## Drop and Harvest Rules

Monster Cores are **not guaranteed monster drops**.

Possible outcomes after a monster kill include:

- No Monster Core
- Monster Core only
- Mana Crystal only
- Both a Monster Core and Mana Crystal
- Either resource plus a separate System drop

Monster Cores and Mana Crystals are rolled separately.

### Suggested Core Drop Chances

| Enemy Type | Typical Monster Core Chance |
|---|---:|
| Normal Monster | 20 – 30% |
| High-Stage Monster | 30 – 45% |
| Elite | 60 – 75% |
| Boss | 90 – 100% |

These are guidelines rather than absolute rules.

Special species, mutated monsters, unique creatures, summoned beings, artificial constructs, and Trial-specific enemies may use different drop rules.

## Difference Between Monster Cores and Mana Crystals

### Mana Crystal

A Mana Crystal is primarily a **condensed Mana resource**.

Typical uses:

- Mana conversion
- powering equipment
- enchantment
- crafting
- breakthroughs
- Mana arrays
- quest requirements
- Tower Point conversion

### Monster Core

A Monster Core contains both Mana and part of the monster's **biological or magical identity**.

Typical uses:

- breakthroughs
- monster-derived equipment
- alchemy
- Skill evolution
- bloodline development
- summoning
- elemental crafting
- enchantment
- research
- evolution materials
- beast taming
- artificial cores
- Tower Point conversion

This additional biological or magical imprint is why Monster Cores are normally more valuable than equivalent Mana Crystals.

## Affinity and Special Property Modifiers

Monster Cores can possess affinities or traits that increase their value beyond the normal Tier range.

Examples:

- **Lightning Core**
- **Blood Core**
- **Fire Core**
- **Ice Core**
- **Shadow Core**
- **Poison Core**
- **Space Core**
- **Dragon Core**
- **Undead Core**
- **Regeneration Core**

A specialized buyer may value such a Core significantly above its normal Tower conversion value.

Example:

```text
[ MONSTER CORE ]

Name: Peak Iron Lightning Wolf Core
Tier: Iron
Stage: Peak
Affinity: Lightning
Purity: 82%
Mana Density: High
Integrity: Perfect
Tower Conversion Value: 418 TP
```

Although standard Iron Cores average **100 – 300 TP**, the rare affinity and exceptional quality push this one beyond the normal range.

## Breakthrough Relevance

Monster Cores are commonly used in breakthrough and vessel-reconstruction quests.

Example:

```text
[ BREAKTHROUGH QUEST ]

Tempering the Iron Vessel

Material Requirements:
- Iron-Tier Monster Core ×50
- Iron Mana Crystal ×20
- Bronze-Tier Monster Core ×1
- Bronze Mana Crystal ×1
- Perfect Orc Bone Set ×1
- Ironblood Moonflower ×1
```

Where a breakthrough specifies personal acquisition, purchased Cores may receive reduced evaluation or fail to satisfy the requirement entirely.

Material quality can also influence breakthrough evaluation.

The System may consider:

- Core Tier
- Core Stage
- Purity
- Integrity
- Mana Density
- Whether it was personally acquired
- Combat performance during acquisition
- Tier disparity
- Resource efficiency

---

## EXP and Resource Separation

EXP and loot are separate systems.

- **EXP is guaranteed** when a valid monster kill is recognized.
- **Monster Cores are not guaranteed.**
- **Mana Crystals are not guaranteed.**
- **System drops use separate loot rolls.**
- **Biological harvesting is separate from System drops.**

This prevents one kill from automatically providing every progression resource.

---

## Judgment Trial Application

Inside the Trials of Judgment, defeated monsters can provide several independent reward streams:

1. **EXP**
2. **Monster Cores**
3. **Mana Crystals**
4. **System Drops**
5. **Biological Materials**
6. **Rare Trial-Specific Resources**

Deeper Chambers and stronger enemies increase:

- expected EXP
- Core quality
- Crystal quality
- material rarity
- Tower Point value
- probability of rare System drops

This makes Inventory expansion especially valuable during long Judgment runs.

## Economic Intent

Monster Cores should remain more valuable than Mana Crystals without replacing them.

A useful rule of thumb:

- **Mana Crystal** = general-purpose Mana resource
- **Monster Core** = specialized biological / magical progression resource

The approximate **2× value relationship** keeps the economy easy to calculate while preserving meaningful differences between the two resources.

---

## High-Tier Economy

From **Transcendent Tier upward**, Tower Point values are best treated as **nominal conversion values** rather than normal market prices.

At those levels, civilizations may prefer:

- direct barter
- contribution authority
- territory
- divine materials
- bloodlines
- rare concepts / laws
- unique artifacts
- political or military exchange
- specialized high-order currencies

A Divine Monster Core may have a Tower conversion value, but almost nobody sensible would sell one for ordinary shopping currency.

## Progression Philosophy

**Ordinary / Iron**
- Beginner progression
- Basic crafting
- Breakthrough resources
- Alchemy
- Early income

**Bronze / Silver**
- Serious adventuring income
- Equipment advancement
- Skill development
- Class and breakthrough materials

**Gold / Diamond**
- Major wealth
- High-level equipment
- Evolution resources
- Strategic crafting

**Supreme – King**
- Organization / kingdom-scale assets
- High-order biological resources
- Strategic evolution materials

**Emperor+**
- Civilization-scale resources
- Divine and conceptual applications
- Ordinary monetary value becomes secondary
