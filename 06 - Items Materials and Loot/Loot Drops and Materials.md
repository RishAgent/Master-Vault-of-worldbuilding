# Loot Drops and Materials

Kills can produce **useful physical materials and System-mediated drops**.

## Natural/material drops

Depending on species and condition, defeated monsters may leave:

- hides/scales/fur
- claws/fangs/horns
- bones
- blood
- organs/glands
- venom/toxins
- elemental organs
- cores/mana stones
- soul-related materials where applicable
- usable meat or alchemical components

These materials can support crafting, enchanting, alchemy, trade and progression.

## System drops

Some kills—especially elites, bosses, rare monsters or achievement kills—may also generate:

- equipment
- consumables
- skill scrolls
- class-related items
- rare crafting materials
- treasure chests/caches
- System currency
- special keys/tokens

Drop chances should depend on threat, rarity, contribution and context rather than every monster exploding into game loot.
